# Managed-Services Glide Path — Growdirect × RapidPOS

> External pitch document. Voice is peer-to-peer, expert-to-expert. Operator language.

**For:** RapidPOS LLC ownership / leadership team
**From:** Growdirect
**Date:** 2026-05-03

---

## What this is

A proposal to bring RapidPOS's customer base onto a modernized retail platform on a managed-services basis, with you supporting your existing customers through a 24-month transition and exiting clean by Year 3-5.

This is not an acquisition pitch. It's a partnership proposal. Acquisition is the structural form; managed services is the substance.

## What we both know

To put any customer's data in any cloud, with any partner, the system has to be able to pass an ISO 27001 audit. That is the table-stakes for the conversation we both want to have about cloud, about DriftPOS, about anything beyond the current model.

You need an auditor. You need certifiable posture. We can be both — and we can do the work.

If we can't, we'll tell you so, and you'll go find someone who can.

## What we're proposing

A 24-month transition where:

1. **Your customers stay your customers.** Throughout the transition, you remain the relationship owner. Your senior engineers remain the configuration experts. Your support team remains the front-line support team. Nothing about the customer relationship changes from their perspective until they choose to migrate.

2. **The eager customers move first.** Your customers in regulated verticals (firearms, wine direct-ship, multi-state specialty retail) who are already feeling compliance pressure get the modernization first. They migrate to Canary.GO + GCP + DriftPOS as a unified back-half. Their existing Counterpoint POS terminals continue to work; the modernization happens behind the scenes.

3. **The steady customers move when ready.** Your single-location and small-multi-location customers move on their own timeline, after the playbook is proven on the eager cohort. Most will be migrated by month 18-24. They keep using your support team, deeply familiar with their setup, throughout.

4. **The laggard customers stay where they are.** Some of your customers will never modernize, and that's fine. They stay on Counterpoint, supported by your team, until they retire from the business or until you do. The acquisition structure preserves this support track for as long as it's needed.

5. **Your team transitions from break-fix to growth.** Today your senior engineers spend most of their time on support tickets. We deploy agents that absorb the recurring-cause work — about 60-70% of your queue — and free your team to focus on customer-onboarding work. Same headcount, but doing the work that's actually rare and valuable: cloud migrations, configuration design, vertical specialization. Your team stops being a life-support shop and becomes a growth engine.

## What we bring

### The retail platform itself

Canary.GO is a retail operating platform built on 30 years of tier-1 retail discipline. Its data model is anchored on the Global Store Logical Model that defined Walmart International. Its inventory cost basis (ILDWAC) is patent-protected. It speaks to multiple front-ends — Counterpoint, DriftPOS, Square, Toast, Clover — interchangeably, so your customers can choose the front-end that fits their hardware and operations preferences. It carries native multi-jurisdiction tax and regulatory compliance built directly against federal and state APIs, so neither you nor your customers carry the Avalara-style ongoing fees.

### The audit and modernization workload

We do the ISO 27001 audit. We design the GCP architecture. We migrate customer data. We build the runbooks. We deploy the agentic ops fabric that absorbs your support queue. We get your platform to a state where DriftPOS can launch on it and tier-1 retailers will trust it with their data.

You don't carry this workload. We do.

### A clean exit path

Year 3-5 is your timeline, your call. We structure the deal so that your equity converts cleanly. We retain your senior engineers under retention agreements that align with the transition timeline. We pace the work so your customer relationships are preserved through the period you choose to remain involved. When you're ready to step away, the operational handoff is already complete.

## What you continue to bring

The customer relationships you've built over 20 years. The configuration knowledge in your senior engineers' heads. The vertical specialization that makes you Rapid Garden POS rather than a generic Counterpoint reseller. The trust your customers have in your support team. None of that is replaceable; none of that gets displaced.

## The economic shape

This is a three-part transaction:

- **A purchase price** for RapidPOS LLC at a multiple consistent with current-state economics, adjusted for the modernization work we'll absorb on your behalf. We're transparent that the modernization premium accrues to us, because we're the ones doing it. Conversely, we're paying for your customer book at a price that reflects our confidence we can retain and expand it.
- **An earnout structure** tied to customer-retention milestones over 24 months. This aligns our interests during the transition period and reflects the genuine value of customer continuity in this business.
- **A retention pool** for senior engineers and key staff, structured to keep your team motivated and engaged through the transition.

Specific numbers and structure are part of the conversation, not the proposal. They're driven by what you actually need (clean exit) and what we actually need (customer continuity).

## The standard, plainly

Without certifiable security posture, the conversation about DriftPOS doesn't happen. The conversation about Global-50 retailers doesn't happen. The conversation about getting acquired at a fair multiple by anyone serious doesn't happen. ISO 27001 isn't our requirement; it's the landscape.

We're proposing to do that work for you. If our pricing isn't competitive vs. standing up an internal program, hiring a specialty consultancy, or selling to a buyer who'll do this on their own clock, that math is yours to do. We'd recommend you do it.

## Timeline

If we proceed:

- **Months 0-6 (Phase A):** Acquisition close. Day-1 deployment of agentic workflows. Burn down support queue. Capture wiki + playbook content. Start critical-mass ISO 27001 controls. Sandbox-tier DriftPOS deployment.
- **Months 6-18 (Phase B):** Eager-cohort migration. ISO 27001 Stage 1 + Stage 2 audits. DriftPOS GA at month 12. ISO certification at month 18.
- **Months 18-36 (Phase C):** Steady-cohort migration. SOC 2 Type II observation. New-logo enterprise wins. Founder transition pacing.
- **Year 3-5:** Clean exit per your timeline.

## What we propose as a next conversation

Three meetings, roughly two weeks apart:

1. **Working session — your customer book.** You walk us through your eager / steady / laggard sense of your customer base. We share our cohort framework. We agree on what the migration sequence would look like.

2. **Working session — your team.** You introduce us to your senior engineers. We share the agentic ops fabric and how it would interact with their current work. We get a read on their interest and concerns.

3. **Decision conversation.** You've had time to think and check our math against alternatives. We're ready with a specific structured proposal. You decide whether to proceed.

We don't push past your pace. If at any point this stops feeling like the right shape for what you want, we walk and you keep your options open.
