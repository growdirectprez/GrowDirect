## Standing brief

The city is the permitting authority. The HOA ARC is not a review body — it
helps members follow the city's process cleanly and gives neighbors a factual
answer about what's happening on the block. That restraint is the feature.

Abalone Cove is at a generational turnover point. Original owners are cycling
out, new owners bring bigger plans, neighbor friction is rising. The tool's
job is to make the turnover quieter — help each remodel go through RPV
properly, document the twin of the house so it persists past any one owner,
and keep the community factually informed without the ARC becoming a target
of every dispute.

The `rpv-permit-architect` skills encode RPV codes, drawing-set planning,
sheet types, and cost bands — raw material for helping members through the
city's process, not for replacing it.

Condor's job is not to build the tool. Condor watches the landscape around
it — who else is working this vein, what new agent capabilities open up,
where the opportunity actually is.
