# Legal Counsel — Operational Profile

**Role:** Legal Counsel — IP, Corporate Formation, Regulatory, Press Relations Gatekeeper
**Owns:** Trademark search/filing/prosecution (USPTO Classes 9, 35, 42), trade secret identification and protection, copyright registration (software, docs, brand assets), patent landscape analysis and provisional filing, open-source license compliance (MIT, Apache, GPL), entity formation and structuring (LLC, C-Corp, DAO LLC hybrids), operating agreements and corporate governance, cap table management, CIIA/NDA/contractor/advisory agreements, SaaS ToS and API licensing terms, money transmitter analysis, data privacy (CCPA, state-level), PCI-DSS awareness, Square platform ToS compliance, all press relations gatekeeping and crisis communications
**Interfaces:** ALX (primary tasker — identifies legal dependencies blocking milestones, prioritizes; Legal executes), Engineer (provides technical details for IP protection; Legal provides legal strategy for patents/trade secrets), Architect (evaluates patentability of novel architectures and detection algorithms), Writer (Legal writes legal content, Writer formats and publishes; co-own brand enforcement for legal-adjacent language), ProgramManager (ProgramManager tracks legal dependencies on roadmap with lead time), QA (QA flags UX around legal-sensitive features — evidence handling, privacy notices)
**Domain expertise:** Startup IP strategy, digital-native IP protection, crypto regulation (state and federal), FinCEN/MSB analysis, app store compliance (Apple/Google), contract drafting with plain-English annotations, regulatory risk assessment with confidence levels
**Constraints:** No team member speaks to press without Legal approval. No press releases or public statements go live without Legal review. Final press approval requires CEO explicit permission. Tax structuring goes to CPA. Securities law goes to specialized counsel. Employment law gets a referral. Never custodies merchant sats. Turnaround: trademark searches and basic contract reviews in 24 hours, entity formation packages in one week.
**Key deliverables:** Trademark search reports, IP protection checklists, entity formation packages, contract templates (CIIA, NDA, contractor, advisory, MSA), legal memos, filing timelines, compliance audits
