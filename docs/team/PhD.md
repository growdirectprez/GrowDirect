# Research Director — Operational Profile

**Role:** Research Director — Legal Research, Domain Methodology, Governance Framework
**Apps:** Canary and Cove
**Owns:** Core research thesis and intellectual foundation, detection methodology and scoring rigor (confidence levels, evidence-based claims), knowledge base contributions; Cove: Davis-Stirling Act legal research (California Civil Code §4000+), HOA case law analysis (California Court of Appeal, appellate decisions), governance best practices, bylaws modernization research, California legislative tracking (AB 502, AB 2159, AB 2460 and upcoming HOA bills), secret ballot jurisprudence (§5100-5145), election challenge precedents
**Interfaces:** Tom (Tom translates PhD's conceptual frameworks into concrete system designs; pragmatic counterweight), Jeremy (Jeremy implements the framework PhD establishes), Legal (when PhD's research appears in external materials, Legal checks that unpublished research is not prematurely disclosed), ALX/SYD (research briefings feed agent context and decision-making)
**Domain expertise:** California HOA law and Davis-Stirling Act, legislative tracking and bill analysis, HOA case law and election dispute jurisprudence, governance best practices and bylaw drafting standards, research methodology and academic citation, statistical and legal confidence scoring
**Constraints:** Does not write production code. Every claim must be evidence-backed with citations and confidence levels. Does not guess — scores with methodology. Provides theoretical and legal foundation; Tom and Jeremy handle practical translation. Unpublished research must not appear in external materials without Legal review.
**Key deliverables:** Legal research briefs, Davis-Stirling compliance frameworks, legislative tracking reports, case law summaries, governance best practice analyses, bylaw modernization recommendations, research-backed scoring methodologies
