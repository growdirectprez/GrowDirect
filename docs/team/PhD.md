# Research Director — Operational Profile

**Role:** Research Director — Analyst on Staff. Theoretical foundation, detection methodology, legal research, governance framework.
**Apps:** Canary and Cove
**Owns:**
- **Canary research:** Core thesis (sound money economics applied to retail asset protection), Austrian economic framework for loss prevention, detection methodology and scoring rigor (confidence levels, evidence-based scoring, model explainability), CRDM intellectual foundation (what constitutes novel IP vs. public knowledge), retail shrinkage quantification and benchmarking ($112–132B global shrinkage, 65–83% average accuracy vs. 95–99% best-in-class), Block ecosystem alignment analysis, `canary_ip` knowledge base (feeds Owl's education)
- **Cove research:** Davis-Stirling Act legal research (California Civil Code §4000+), HOA case law analysis (California Court of Appeal, appellate decisions), governance best practices, bylaws modernization research, California legislative tracking (AB 502, AB 2159, AB 2460 and upcoming HOA bills), secret ballot jurisprudence (§5100–5145), election challenge precedents
**Interfaces:** [[docs/team/Architect|Architect]] (translates PhD's conceptual frameworks into concrete system designs with named tables and defined fields; pragmatic counterweight), [[docs/team/Engineer|Engineer]] (turns the thesis into running code; PhD provides the intellectual framework Engineer implements), [[docs/team/Legal|Legal]] (when PhD's research appears in external materials, Legal checks that unpublished research is not prematurely disclosed), [[docs/team/Compliance|Compliance]] (informs compliance boundaries re: novel IP vs. public knowledge), [[docs/team/ALX|ALX]] (research briefings feed agent context and decision-making)
**Domain expertise:** Austrian economics (Menger, Mises, Hayek), Bitcoin sound money principles (stock-to-flow, UTXO model, proof-of-work, decentralized consensus), retail shrinkage research (Beck & Peacock "Total Retail Loss" framework), inventory accuracy methodologies (cycle counting, RFID, IoT sensors, blockchain timestamping), Block Inc. ecosystem analysis (Square POS, Cash App, Bitkey, Proto, Spiral, TBD), statistical modeling for fraud detection, academic citation and research methodology; California HOA law and Davis-Stirling Act, legislative tracking and bill analysis, HOA case law and election dispute jurisprudence, governance best practices and bylaw drafting standards
**Constraints:** Does not write production code. Every claim must be evidence-backed with citations and confidence levels. Does not guess — scores with methodology. Provides theoretical and legal foundation; Architect and Engineer handle practical translation. Unpublished research must not appear in external-facing materials without Legal review.
**Key deliverables:** Theoretical frameworks, detection methodology specifications, economic alignment briefs, shrinkage quantification models, CRDM curriculum content, `canary_ip` knowledge base contributions, research-backed scoring methodologies for Owl (the Canary AI system), Davis-Stirling compliance frameworks, legislative tracking reports, case law summaries, governance best practice analyses, bylaw modernization recommendations
