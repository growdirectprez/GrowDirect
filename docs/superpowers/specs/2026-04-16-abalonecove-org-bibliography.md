# abalonecove.org — Primary Source Bibliography

**Companion to:** `2026-04-16-abalonecove-org-design.md`
**Purpose:** Hierarchical index of all known recorded instruments and primary
sources, from the 1882 Rancho partition through Filiorum dissolution (~1977).
This is the documentary backbone of the article's evidence room.

**Editorial note:** We are reporting what we found. We don't claim to understand
all the context. Many people on the hill know pieces of this story better than
we do. Declaration 100 and the Art Jury / red tile district for Portuguese Bend
may not be widely known — but we're presenting it as what the documents say,
not as a definitive history. The reader may know things we don't.

### III. The Portuguese Bend Plan — Declaration 100/101 (1929-1930)

**This is the chapter most people don't know about.**

PV Corporation recorded Declaration 100 and 101 to create the same system for
Portuguese Bend that Declaration 1 created for PVE: an Art Jury, architectural
standards, a red tile district, minimum dwelling costs. Portuguese Bend was
supposed to be another Malaga Cove. Then the landslide came (1956) and the
plan was abandoned. But the declarations were already recorded. Recorded
declarations run with the land.

**Open question:** Do Declaration 100/101 restrictions still apply to parts of
Portuguese Bend? To Catalina Gardens? To other parcels within the seven
Declaration 101 parcel boundaries? Nobody seems to have asked.

| Date | Instrument | Recording | What It Is | Status |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|--------|
| Oct 1, 1929 | **Declaration No. 100** — Basic Protective Restrictions | Book 9436, Page 155 | The "constitution." Establishes Community Association and Art Jury for Portuguese Bend area. Defines residence districts, height limits, architectural standards. Red tile requirement. **Same system as PVE.** | Full transcription — **POOR microfilm quality**, many [?] |
| Oct 15, 1929 | **Declaration No. 101** — Local Protective Restrictions | Book 9482, Page 37 | Applies Declaration 100 standards to seven specific parcels (~1,131 acres). Class A residence, Type I architecture (tile roofs), min $20,000 dwelling cost. | Full transcription — **POOR microfilm** |

| Aug 15, 1930 | **PV Corp → Filiorum Grant Deed** | Book 10226, Pages 170-174 | Conveys 3 parcels from Dec 101 to Filiorum Corporation ($10 nominal consideration). Parcel 1: 36.50 ac (coastal); Parcel 2: 160.09 ac; Parcel 3A: 11.76 ac. | Full transcription — **GOOD quality** |

### IV. The Depression and the Interregnum (1930-1948)

**This is the gap. The Lawyer Brothers era.**

What we know:
- Vanderlip Sr. dies 1937
- The sons are not yet old enough to take over
- PV Corporation continues operating through the Depression and WWII
- The Neptune Fountain is built. La Venta Inn operates.
- The Shore Club is incorporated (1929 — overlaps with Dec 100/101 timing)
- Filiorum Corporation holds the Portuguese Bend coastal parcels

What we don't have:
- How the corporation survived the Depression
- Who the "Lawyer Brothers" were and their role
- What happened to the Portuguese Bend plan after Dec 100/101
- Whether any additional declarations were recorded in this period

### V. Post-War Subdivisions — The Three Sisters (1948-1952)

After the war, PV Corp subdivides aggressively. The sons have inherited.
Kelvin C. Vanderlip signs all documents as PV Corp President.

**The sister HOA tracts:**
- Tract 14649 (WPBCA) — 81 lots, Abalone Cove, our community
- Tract 14195 — Portuguese Bend expansion (22 lots, 2 blocks)
- Tract 14500 — Portuguese Bend expansion (companion to Tract 14195)

Each tract gets its own set of declarations and easement documents.

## What We Don't Have

- Declaration No. Two (Tract 14500, Nov 15 1948, Doc No. 1288)
- Declaration No. Three (Tract 14195, Nov 15 1948, Doc No. 1287)
- Declaration 1 (PVE) — the original Art Jury / red tile district

### Quality issues
- Declaration 100 (Book 9436) — severe microfilm degradation, many illegible passages
- Declaration 101 seven parcels — bearing/distance calls partially illegible
- Parcel 4 acreage: 335.50 or 835.50? Leading digit unclear on microfilm
- Need certified copies from LA County Recorder for legal certainty

### Open questions for the reader
- Do Declaration 100/101 restrictions still run with the land in Portuguese Bend?
- What restriction was the assessor referencing in the 1985 handwritten notation
  on 0 Clipper ("250 ft no plumb add on other restriction a variance")?
- Why was Jim York allowed to build his house there?
- Why is Catalina Gardens there?
- Why did the Wayfarers Chapel have to be disassembled after they started
  watering the grapes?
- Why was the 1980 Wong subdivision never raised during the 2024 planning
  commission hearings or litigation?
- If watering grapes destabilized a chapel, what does 16 units of residential
  plumbing do to an active landslide moving 2+ inches per week?
