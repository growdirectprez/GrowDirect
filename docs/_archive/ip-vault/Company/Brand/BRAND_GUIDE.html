Usage Guidelines

Do

Use the dark background version (#0F1923) as the primary. It's the hero.

Don't

Place the icon on busy photography or colored backgrounds without a container.

Do

Maintain clear space equal to the bird's head radius around the logo on all sides.

Don't

Rotate, stretch, add drop shadows, or change the bird's colors.

Do

Use the favicon version (with red alert dot) for browser tabs and app icons.

Don't

Use the full lockup below 120px wide — switch to the icon-only version.
