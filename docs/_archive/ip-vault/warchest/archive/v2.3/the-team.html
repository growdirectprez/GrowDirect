Operating Principles

1. Factory Process is the standard — no sprint starts without a spec, no story ships without QA sign-off 2. Bitcoin-native is non-negotiable — every design decision assumes cryptographic proof 3. Platform-agnostic from day one — canonical transaction schema before any second POS integration 4. Open source is our positioning — transparency is the brand; expertise is the moat 5. Do it right, do it once — no retrofitting certification requirements 6. Simple for retail — if a store manager can't understand it, simplify 7. Ship daily — something commits every day

The founder has been here before. He knows what a proper factory looks like, what happens when it breaks down, and what it takes to build it right. Canary is the continuation.
