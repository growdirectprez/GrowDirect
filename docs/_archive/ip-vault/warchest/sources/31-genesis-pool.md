## 10 Million Ordinals. From 0.1 BTC. From the Founder's Mining Reward.

The Genesis Pool is the founding act. Ten million satoshis — exactly 0.1 BTC, earned as a mining reward from F2Pool — inscribed as permanent assets on the Bitcoin time chain. Not purchased on an exchange. Not allocated from a treasury. Earned through proof-of-work by the founder and immediately deployed as the canonical notarization namespace.

The provenance is recorded on-chain with cryptographic certainty. The mining reward traces to F2Pool at a specific block height. The UTXO traces to the founder's personal wallet. The inscription transactions form an unbroken lineage from mining reward to canonical asset. A competitor can purchase Bitcoin and inscribe — but their provenance traces to an exchange, not a mining pool. Proof-of-work origin cannot be manufactured retroactively.

## The Capital Allocation Argument

Three options exist for a mining reward:

**Option A — Sell it.** One-time liquidity. The Bitcoin leaves custody. The mining was the business.

**Option B — Hold it.** No revenue. Appreciation only. Passive treasury position.

**Option C — Inscribe it.** Perpetual validation revenue. The Bitcoin stays on chain. The passive holding transforms into productive infrastructure.

GrowDirect chooses Option C. The 0.1 BTC does not leave the chain. It transforms. Before inscription: a passive treasury holding worth its market price. After inscription: a productive infrastructure asset — a canonical notarization protocol generating validation revenue on every future call, indefinitely.

The Bitcoin quantity is unchanged. The form has changed. Passive has become productive.

## Accounting Treatment

Every satoshi spent on inscription is a capital expenditure producing a permanent asset. The cost basis at current rates — 1 to 3 sat/vbyte for protocol inscriptions — is negligible. The asset classification is digital infrastructure: comparable to IP address space, spectrum licenses, or domain names. It does not depreciate. It requires zero maintenance. It generates revenue per validation at zero marginal cost.

Traditional SaaS: monthly subscription, revenue stops at cancellation. The Genesis Pool: event notarized once, validation revenue every time anyone proves it happened, for the lifetime of the inscription — which is the lifetime of Bitcoin.

## The Moat Within the Moat

The Genesis Pool is not just an asset. It is a narrative. The founder earned the Bitcoin through proof-of-work. The founder deployed it as the foundation of a permanent notarization protocol. The blocks are written. The keys are held. The origin story is on the chain.

No institutional investor, no corporate treasury, no competitor starting from zero can replicate this origin. They can inscribe. They cannot fabricate the provenance. The Genesis Pool is GrowDirect's founding inventory — and the founder's proof-of-work is the receipt.
