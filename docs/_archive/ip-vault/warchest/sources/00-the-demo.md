## The Live Demo

The investor sees this. The founder says what is below each beat. Everything on screen is real — not mocked, not seeded, not simulated.

### Beat 1: The Boot

The founder pulls a thumb drive from his pocket. Plugs it in. The Canary stack boots. PostgreSQL, Valkey, the API gateway, the detection engine, the evidence store — all from a single device.

*"Everything you are about to see runs from this thumb drive. No cloud. No server room. This is the entire system."*

### Beat 2: The Trigger

The founder opens the Square app on his phone. Processes a refund — real money, real merchant account, real Square webhook. The webhook fires within seconds.

*"I just refunded a dollar on my own Square account. That event is now in the pipeline."*

### Beat 3: The Chirp

The founder's phone chirps. A plain-language alert: the refund, the amount, the register, the time.

*"That is a Chirp. Plain language. Not a dashboard. Not a report. It tells me exactly what happened and exactly what to do."*

### Beat 4: The Seal

The founder opens the evidence store. The event is there — hashed, sealed, INSERT-only, chain-linked to the previous entry.

*"The event is sealed. Hash-chained. Append-only. Nobody can alter it, delete it, or pretend it did not happen. Not me. Not Square. Nobody."*

### Beat 5: The Inscription

The Merkle root inscribes to the Bitcoin time chain. The founder shows the transaction ID, the block number, the block explorer URL.

*"That is a Bitcoin Ordinal inscription. The notarization is now on the most secure computational network ever built. It will be there forever."*

### Beat 6: The Receipt

The founder shows the merchant receipt — event hash, chain position, inscription ID, Bitcoin block, Merkle proof path.

*"The merchant gets this receipt. They can independently verify it. They do not need to trust us. The math is the proof."*

- A verifiable receipt returned to the merchant

Nothing was simulated. Everything ran from a thumb drive. The system is real.
