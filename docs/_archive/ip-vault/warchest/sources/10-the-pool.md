## Layer 1: The Asset

GrowDirect uses its Bitcoin treasury to mint a pool of Ordinals on the Bitcoin base layer. Every Ordinal in the pool is a permanent, immutable slot on the Bitcoin time chain. It exists forever. It cannot be altered. It cannot be taken away. It does not require a server to remain valid. Bitcoin has never gone down since January 3, 2009.

This is not infrastructure spend. This is capital deployment into permanent assets.

Every sat spent minting an Ordinal is an asset on GrowDirect's balance sheet — permanently. Unlike AWS compute spend, which vanishes when the instance stops, every inscription purchased generates validation revenue indefinitely. The treasury is not burning capital. It is converting Bitcoin into permanent revenue-generating assets on the time chain.

## The Genesis Pool

The first 10,000,000 Ordinals are minted from the founder's personal first pool reward — 0.1 BTC from F2Pool. Not outside capital. Not a treasury allocation. Proof-of-work earned by the founder and immediately deployed as permanent assets on the time chain.

The provenance is recorded on-chain with cryptographic certainty: mining reward from F2Pool at a specific block height, to the founder's personal wallet with a traceable UTXO, to the inscription transactions with unbroken lineage. A competitor can purchase Bitcoin on an exchange and inscribe — but the provenance traces to Coinbase or Binance, not a mining pool. Proof-of-work origin cannot be manufactured retroactively.

The first 10 million inscriptions establish GrowDirect's canonical authority before any competitor can occupy the same block space.

## The Accounting Treatment

Traditional SaaS capital allocation: spend on servers, the server depreciates, the spend is gone. GrowDirect's capital allocation: spend on inscriptions, the inscription exists permanently, the spend becomes a balance sheet asset that generates revenue on every future validation call.

Before inscription: 0.1 BTC is a passive treasury holding. After inscription: the Genesis Pool is a productive infrastructure asset — canonical notarization protocol with perpetual revenue optionality. The Bitcoin quantity is unchanged. The satoshis have been transformed from passive to productive.

The comparison to IP address space is direct. IP ranges were just numbers until the internet needed addresses. Then they became infrastructure. Then they became valuable. Then they became essential. The people who claimed them early — when they looked like nothing — owned the foundation. Ordinal ranges are the same play. GrowDirect's Genesis Pool is not 10 million JPEGs. It is 10 million address slots in the verification layer of the post-AI internet.
