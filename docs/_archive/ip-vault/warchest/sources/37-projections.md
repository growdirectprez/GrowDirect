## The Numbers

All projections assume constant BTC/USD pricing. No cryptocurrency appreciation is modeled. Revenue figures are in USD equivalents. These are conservative baseline projections — not targets, not promises.

## Year 1: Foundation (2026)

Build the product. Certify on the Square Marketplace. Acquire the first 15 to 25 paying customers.

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Entity formation. Infrastructure deployment. Square developer certification. |
| Q2 | SaaS MVP with Square integration. Onboard 3-5 pilot merchants (beta). |
| Q3 | Launch paid tiers. Convert pilots. Acquire 5-10 new paying customers. |
| Q4 | Product iteration. Push to 15-25 paying customers. |

**Year 1 revenue estimate:** $8,000-$9,800 (SaaS subscriptions ramping Q3-Q4, plus supplementary Lightning routing fees and staking rewards).

Year 1 is a build year. Revenue starts Q3. The treasury provides full operational runway.

## Year 2: Growth (2027)

Scale to 50-100 customers. Add a second POS integration. Optimize operations.

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Add Shopify POS integration. Refine detection engine. |
| Q2 | Scale to 50 customers. Implement automated onboarding. |
| Q3 | Advanced features — trend analysis, weekly digests, PDF reports. Third POS integration. |
| Q4 | Push to 75-100 customers. Content marketing launch. |

**Year 2 revenue estimate:** $74,400-$90,900 (average 60 customers at $39/month blended average, plus supplementary income).

Year 2 is where the SaaS economics start working. At 60+ customers paying $39/month average, revenue covers all costs and generates real margin.

## Year 3: Scale (2028)

200-400 customers. Multiple POS integrations. First full-time hire.

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Add Toast, Lightspeed integrations. Launch self-serve onboarding. |
| Q2 | Scale to 200 customers. Tiered analytics. |
| Q3 | First full-time hire (support/dev). Expand Lightning channel network. |
| Q4 | Push to 300-400 customers. Evaluate enterprise pilot tier for small chains. |

**Year 3 revenue estimate:** $302,400-$365,400.

## Three-Year Summary

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|
| Year 3 paying customers | 200 | 400 |
| Year 3 ARR | ~$297,000 | ~$356,000 |
| 3-year cumulative revenue | ~$384,800 | ~$466,100 |
| 3-year net position | +$136,300 | +$328,000 |

**At scale (1% Square penetration):** 45,000 merchants at $39/month = $21M ARR. This is 1% of the addressable base. The market supports 5-10x this number.

## What the Numbers Do Not Show

The financial model projects a capital-efficient bootstrap path to profitability. But the real value is not in the subscription revenue — it is in the data asset. Every merchant who connects feeds the CRDM. Aggregated, anonymized transaction patterns across thousands of merchants create a retail intelligence layer that does not exist today for the SMB segment. The subscription revenue funds operations. The data asset creates enterprise value.
