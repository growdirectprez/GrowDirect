# The Financial Model

> *"Capital-efficient. Total infrastructure costs under $150/month. The seed treasury funds 2+ years of operations even with zero revenue."*

---

## Revenue Architecture

GrowDirect generates revenue from three streams. The SaaS subscription is the primary engine. Lightning liquidity and staking provide supplementary yield on idle treasury capital.

### Stream 1: SaaS Subscriptions (Primary)

Monthly subscription for LP analytics. Merchants subscribe through the Square Marketplace — the distribution channel they already use for payroll, loyalty, and inventory add-ons.

| Tier | Monthly Price | What's Included | Target Segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $19/mo | 8 core detection rules (payment + cash drawer). Today's View. Chirps. Process wizard. | Single location, <$50K/mo revenue |
| **Professional** | $39/mo | All 26 rules. Full wizard engine. Scorecards. 90-day data retention. | 1-3 locations, <$200K/mo revenue |
| **Business** | $79/mo | Everything in Pro + multi-location. Benchmarking. Extended retention. API access. Priority support. | 3-10 locations, advanced analytics |

**Blended average: $39/month** across tiers, based on merchant distribution patterns in the Square ecosystem.

### Stream 2: Lightning Liquidity Provision (Supplementary)

The treasury deploys BTC into strategically positioned Lightning Network channels. Routing fees generate passive income on capital that would otherwise sit idle.

| Scenario | Est. Annual Return |
|---|---|
| Passive, basic management | 0.5-1% on deployed capital |
| Active management, good peers | 1-3% |
| Optimized (Year 2+, higher volume) | 2-5% |

Returns improve as the SaaS customer base grows — the node routes payments for its own customers, creating a self-reinforcing liquidity position.

### Stream 3: Validator Staking (Supplementary)

Treasury assets deployed as blockchain validators generate staking rewards that compound and accrue to the treasury.

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Infrastructure cost per merchant | $2-4/mo | Webhook ingestion, storage, compute |
| Gross margin | 85-95% | SaaS-standard at scale |
| CAC (Square Marketplace) | Near zero | Organic discovery, no paid acquisition |
| LTV driver | Retention | Merchants who see value in month 1 don't churn |
| Assumed annual churn | 15% | Conservative for vertical SaaS |
| Payback period | < 1 month | At near-zero CAC |

The distribution model is the key differentiator. Square Marketplace handles discovery, billing, and merchant trust. There is no sales team. No implementation. No training. A merchant connects their Square account via OAuth and sees live Chirps within hours.

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low customer acquisition | Revenue doesn't cover costs | Lean operations; treasury provides 2+ year runway |
| POS API changes | Product breaks | Diversify across POS platforms; abstract translation layer |
| BTC price decline | Treasury value drops | Revenue model works at any BTC price; costs are USD-denominated |
| Lightning fee compression | Supplementary income drops | Routing is supplementary, not primary revenue |
| Competition from enterprise vendors | Market pressure | Speed, cost, and Bitcoin-native positioning are differentiators |
| Regulatory changes | Entity structure risk | Wyoming has strengthened DAO laws consistently |

## What the Numbers Don't Show

The financial model above projects a capital-efficient bootstrap path to profitability. But the real value isn't in the subscription revenue — it's in the data asset.

Every merchant who connects feeds the CRDM. Aggregated, anonymized transaction patterns across thousands of merchants create a retail intelligence layer that doesn't exist today for the SMB segment. This data asset compounds with every merchant, every transaction, every detection event.

The subscription revenue funds operations. The data asset creates enterprise value.
