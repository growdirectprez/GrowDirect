Layer 5: The Mining Moat

Traditional mining: compete for block rewards, sell the Bitcoin, the coins leave, the mining was the business.

The GrowDirect play: the mining reward never leaves. It becomes the asset.

The 0.1 BTC from F2Pool is not income — it is raw material. Fed into the inscription engine, it comes out as 10 million permanently addressed slots on the chain. The Bitcoin is still on the chain. It just has a new form. It has gone from passive treasury holding to productive infrastructure — a canonical notarization namespace generating validation revenue indefinitely.

Three options exist for a mining reward: sell it for one-time liquidity, hold it for appreciation only, or inscribe it for perpetual validation revenue while the Bitcoin stays on chain. GrowDirect chooses the third option. The capital allocation is not a trade. It is a transformation.

The Fee Window

Inscription costs are a function of block space demand. That window is not permanent.

As Bitcoin adoption accelerates — Layer 2 networks, institutional custody, Lightning settlement — block space becomes more contested. Fees rise structurally. Each halving cycle compresses the fee floor upward as miners rely increasingly on transaction fees rather than block rewards. Each competing protocol tightens availability further.

At current rates, a minimal inscription costs approximately 200 satoshis — under twenty cents. At 50 sat/vbyte sustained average, a bootstrapped competitor without existing treasury and pool relationships cannot economically establish a competing notarization protocol from zero. At 100 sat/vbyte, it becomes economically irrational to try.

The estimated point of no return: 12 to 24 months from genesis. By that intersection — accumulated history plus network effect plus rising fees — the cost to replicate GrowDirect's position from zero crosses the threshold of economic rationality for new entrants.

The play: mint the range before the window closes. Hold the keys. The range is on the chain forever regardless of what happens to fees afterward.

Vertical Integration

If GrowDirect establishes a relationship with a major mining pool — or leases hash rate — the model upgrades permanently. When the pool wins a block, GrowDirect's inscription transactions go in first, at cost, into a block the company helped produce. Zero fee market competition. Cost approaches zero. The queue runs forever.

The provenance chain becomes unbroken: hash rate to block production to inscription to key custody to validation revenue. All GrowDirect. All permanent.
