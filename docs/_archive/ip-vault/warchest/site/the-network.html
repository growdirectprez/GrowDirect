Layer 6: The Platform

Sometimes you do not want to validate against someone else's registry. You want your own keys. Your own range. Your own canonical authority. Your own royalty stream. elJeffe gives you the protocol to do exactly that.

| Level | What You Own | What You Pay elJeffe | |---|---|---| | Basic merchant | Notarized receipts | Sats per validation | | Treasury merchant | Own Ordinals, own keys | Royalty on validations | | Full node | Own registry, own gate, own revenue | Protocol royalty only | | Enterprise | Private canonical authority | License + royalty |

Every level is more powerful than the last. Every level still flows royalties back to elJeffe.

The Counter-Intuitive Network Effect

Most protocols extract value as adoption grows. The platform becomes the toll booth. The toll rises. Users have no alternative. The platform wins by making its users dependent.

elJeffe distributes power as adoption grows. A basic merchant pays per validation. A treasury merchant holds their own keys and earns on their own validations. A full node operator runs their own registry and collects their own revenue — paying only a protocol royalty. The more powerful the user becomes, the more the network benefits.

The protocol that wins is the one that makes its users more powerful than its competitors' users. The royalty compounds regardless of which level the user occupies, because every tier still validates against the elJeffe canonical record or runs the elJeffe protocol.

From Product to Protocol

The progression from basic merchant to enterprise represents the full maturation of elJeffe from a product into a protocol. At the basic tier, it is a service — GrowDirect notarizes your events, you pay to validate them. At the enterprise tier, it is infrastructure — you run your own canonical authority using the elJeffe protocol, and the protocol royalty flows back to the network.

This is the same progression that turned HTTP from a document transfer protocol into the foundation of the internet economy. The protocol creators did not capture the value — but the early infrastructure holders did. GrowDirect holds the genesis pool. The protocol royalty is the capture mechanism.
