
## Gold Standard Validation List — Real Data Hunt

When we get real merchant data flowing, these are the patterns that prove Canary's value. Finding even ONE of these in production data is gold for demos, case studies, and investor conversations.

### C-502 POST_VOID — The Money Walk
- **Pattern:** Completed sale → voided after customer leaves → merchandise gone, no payment
- **Hunt for:** Gap > 15 min between payment completion and void. Same cashier doing multiple post voids. Post voids near end of shift.
- **Gold hit:** A cashier with 3+ post voids in a week, all > $50, all within last hour of shift

### C-009 SQUARE_DELAY_HOLD — Square Already Flagged It
- **Pattern:** Square's own risk models held a payment for review
- **Hunt for:** Payments where Square held AND the merchant forced through anyway. Repeat holds on the same card or cashier.
- **Gold hit:** A location with high hold rates vs others — indicates something at that register is triggering Square's models

### C-001 HIGH_VALUE — Big Ticket Anomaly
- **Hunt for:** Transactions 3x+ the merchant's average, especially manual-entry cards
- **Gold hit:** A $500 sale at a farmers market that averages $25/transaction

### C-003 RAPID_FIRE — Velocity Abuse
- **Hunt for:** Same register, multiple transactions in < 60 seconds
- **Gold hit:** 5 transactions in 2 minutes, all refunds, same cashier

### C-101 EXCESSIVE_REFUNDS — Return Fraud
- **Hunt for:** Refund rate > 15% for a single cashier vs team average
- **Gold hit:** One cashier at 22% refund rate while the rest of the team is at 3%

### C-401 DISCOUNT_ABUSE — Sweethearting
- **Hunt for:** Excessive discounts applied by one cashier, especially % discounts on high-value items
- **Gold hit:** A cashier applying 50% discounts to friends/family — discount rate 4x team average

### Validation Discipline
- When real data flows, run weekly scan across all rules
- Any hit that matches these signatures → screenshot it, document it, save the transaction chain
- One real-world catch is worth more than 100 test scenarios for demos and sales
