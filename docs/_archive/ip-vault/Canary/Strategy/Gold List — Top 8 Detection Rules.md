# Gold List — Top 8 Detection Rules\n\nThe quick hits. Each one is a conversation-ender with the merchant. No jargon, no models — just "here's what happened, here's when, here's who."\n\n> **Adoption play:** You don't sell AI. You sell "here's the $200 your cashier walked out with last Tuesday." The AI is invisible — it's just the thing that found it.\n\n---\n\n## 1. C-502 — POST_VOID\n**The Story:** "Your cashier voided a $200 sale after the customer left with the merchandise."\n**Why It's Gold:** Undeniable. Timestamp proves it. The longer the gap between sale and void, the more suspicious.\n**Key Metric:** Time between payment completion and void (`cancel_context.gap_seconds`)\n**What To Look For:** Same cashier doing multiple post voids, especially near end of shift.\n\n## 2. C-301 — OFF_CLOCK_TRANSACTION\n**The Story:** "Someone ran a $150 payment and they weren't even clocked in."\n**Why It's Gold:** Ghost employee. No legitimate explanation for processing payments when you're not working.\n**Key Metric:** Payment timestamp vs timecard open/close\n**What To Look For:** Repeated pattern, specific employee, specific time windows.\n\n## 3. C-004 — AFTER_HOURS_TRANSACTION\n**The Story:** "A $300 sale at 2 AM. You close at 10 PM."\n**Why It's Gold:** Owner knows instantly if that's legit. One question: "Were you open?" If no — problem.\n**Key Metric:** Transaction time vs configured business hours\n**What To Look For:** Recurring after-hours activity, specific employees, high-value items.\n\n## 4. C-201 — EXCESSIVE_DISCOUNT\n**The Story:** "This employee averages 40% discounts. Everyone else averages 8%."\n**Why It's Gold:** Sweethearting — giving product away to friends and family. The comparison to peers makes it obvious.\n**Key Metric:** Average discount % per employee vs store average\n**What To Look For:** One employee consistently above threshold, discounts on high-value items, specific time patterns.\n\n## 5. C-001 — RAPID_REFUND\n**The Story:** "Refund issued 3 minutes after the sale, same cashier."\n**Why It's Gold:** Classic cash-out scheme. Ring it up, pocket the cash, refund the card. The speed is the tell.\n**Key Metric:** Time between original sale and refund\n**What To Look For:** Same employee on both sale and refund, pattern of rapid refunds, amounts that add up.\n\n## 6. C-101 — NO_SALE_ABUSE\n**The Story:** "Cash drawer opened 12 times with no sale in one shift."\n**Why It's Gold:** Skimming. The drawer shouldn't open without a transaction. Every open is either a mistake or money moving.\n**Key Metric:** No-sale drawer opens per shift per employee\n**What To Look For:** One employee way above average, specific time windows, cash variance at end of shift.\n\n## 7. C-008 — MANUAL_ENTRY_SPIKE\n**The Story:** "15 keyed-in card numbers in one shift. Store average is 2."\n**Why It's Gold:** Card-not-present fraud. Someone's typing in stolen card numbers. The spike vs baseline is unmistakable.\n**Key Metric:** Keyed-in transactions per shift vs store average\n**What To Look For:** Single employee with dramatic spike, transactions that later get disputed, specific card BINs.\n\n## 8. C-204 — UNTENDERED_ORDER\n**The Story:** "Order created, items rung up, never paid for. 3 hours ago."\n**Why It's Gold:** Merchandise walked out the door with no payment. Could be a forgotten tab — or could be theft.\n**Key Metric:** Time since order creation with no payment\n**What To Look For:** Specific employees, high-value items, orders near shift end.\n\n---\n\n## The Adoption Ladder\n\n| Stage | What We Say | What Happens |\n|---|---|---|\n| **1.
