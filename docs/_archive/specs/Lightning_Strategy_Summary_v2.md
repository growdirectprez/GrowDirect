## Core Strategic Positioning (CORRECTED)

**WE ARE NOT:**
- Competing with payment processors or banks
- Building custody solutions
- Positioning as anti-bank disruptors

**WE ARE:**
- **Block ecosystem partners** evangelizing Bitcoin to SMBs  
- Leveraging existing Bitcoin infrastructure (Lightning, BTCPay, LNURL) to provide loss prevention tools
- Helping merchants who don't know about digital privacy threats, chargeback fraud, and cybersecurity risks
- Providing **real security tools**: BOLO proximity network, Fox case management, Owl analytics
- Building **network partnerships** via ALTO model (LP providers serving merchant clusters)

---

## Lightning Integration: Three Use Cases

1. **Spam-Resistant Access (L402):** API calls cost 1-10 sats, making DDoS economically prohibitive
2. **Passwordless Auth (LNURL):** Wallet = identity, eliminating password breach risk
3. **Network Participation (BOLO Staking):** Merchants stake ~1000 sats to join proximity alert network, false reports = stake slashing

## Legal Risk Assessment

| Risk Category | Level | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Money Transmitter (State) | **LOW** | Non-custodial architecture, peer-to-peer payments |
| FinCEN / Federal MSB | **LOW** | Canary sells software, doesn't custody funds |
| State Crypto Regs | **MEDIUM** | Geo-block NY (BitLicense cost ~$500k), launch TX/WY/FL |
| Square Platform ToS | **LOW** | Not competing with payment processing, adding anti-spam layer |
| App Store (iOS) | **LOW** | Precedent exists (SaruTobi game approved 2025) |

**CRITICAL RED LINE:** Never custody merchant sats. Triggers money transmitter licensing in all 50 states.

## Messaging Guidelines

### Merchant-Facing (Plain English)
**NEVER SAY:** "Bitcoin," "Lightning Network," "satoshis," "crypto," "blockchain"  
**ALWAYS SAY:** "Instant micro payments," "pay only what you use," "no chargebacks," "your wallet, your money"

**Example pitch:**  
> "Canary uses instant micro payments—fractions of a penny per transaction—so you only pay for fraud protection when you need it. No monthly fees. No chargebacks. Your money, your control."

### Investor-Facing (Strategic)
**Positioning:** "Block ecosystem partner helping SMBs navigate Bitcoin-native future"  
**Not:** "Payment processor disruptor"

**Key Points:**
- Bitcoin alignment with Block Inc. (8,584 BTC on balance sheet, $241B processed annually)
- Micropayment economics impossible for credit cards (sub-cent transactions unprofitable)
- Network effects via BOLO proximity (more merchants = better fraud detection)
- Educational mission: retailers don't know about chargeback fraud, credential stuffing, settlement delays

## Conclusion

Canary's Lightning strategy is **legally defensible, technically feasible, and strategically aligned with Block's Bitcoin-native vision**. We are NOT attacking banks. We ARE educating merchants about real threats and providing real tools (BOLO, Fox, Owl) using real Bitcoin infrastructure.

**Our competitive edge:** Merchant empowerment via pay-per-use micropayments, community protection networks, and professional LP partnerships—all powered by Lightning rails that complement Square's payment infrastructure.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with Lightning integration. Maintain strict non-custodial compliance. Act as Block ecosystem evangelists, not payment processor competitors.
