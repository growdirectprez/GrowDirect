# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)

**Plain-English Summary:** This addendum explains exactly what data we touch during a product demo, why we touch it, how long we keep it, and your rights under California privacy law (CCPA). It attaches to the Beta Tester Agreement. Short version: we only use your data for the demo, we delete it within 30 days, and you can ask us to delete it sooner.

---

**DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM**

*Addendum to the Beta Tester Agreement between GrowDirect LLC and Participant*

**Effective Date:** Same as the Beta Tester Agreement

---

## 1. Scope

This Addendum governs GrowDirect's processing of Participant's business data during the Beta Program. It supplements (and where conflicting, supersedes) the data handling provisions in the Beta Tester Agreement.

## 2. Data We Process

| Data Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction data | Sales amounts, timestamps, payment methods, refund records, item-level details | Participant's POS system (demo environment or sample export) |
| Employee identifiers | First names, employee IDs, shift assignments | As provided by Participant for demo purposes |
| Location data | Store name, address, register identifiers | Participant-provided |
| Usage telemetry | Feature interactions, navigation paths, session duration | Product instrumentation during demo |
| Feedback | Verbal and written comments, suggestions | Participant-provided |

**What we do NOT collect:** Social Security numbers, personal financial account numbers, dates of birth, home addresses of employees, or any data not directly related to business transaction monitoring.

## 3. Purpose Limitation

All data processed under this Addendum will be used **exclusively** for:

- Demonstrating Product functionality to Participant
- Evaluating Product performance and accuracy
- Improving Product features based on real-world data patterns

GrowDirect will **not** use Participant data for: marketing, resale, profiling of individuals, credit decisions, employment decisions, or any purpose beyond product evaluation and improvement.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored on access-controlled, encrypted systems
- Access is limited to GrowDirect personnel directly involved in product development and evaluation
- GrowDirect maintains reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- **Retention period:** Data will be retained only for the duration of the Beta Program
- **Deletion commitment:** All Participant data will be **permanently deleted within thirty (30) calendar days** after the Beta Program ends or Participant withdraws
- **Early deletion:** Participant may request deletion at any time by emailing GrowDirect. GrowDirect will complete deletion within **ten (10) business days** of receiving the request
- **Confirmation:** GrowDirect will provide written confirmation of deletion upon request

## 6. CCPA Compliance

As a California business, Participant and its employees have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA. GrowDirect acknowledges:

- **Right to know:** Participant may request a summary of what personal information GrowDirect has collected, the categories, and the purposes
- **Right to delete:** Participant may request deletion of personal information at any time (see Section 5 above)
- **Right to opt out of sale:** GrowDirect **does not sell** personal information. Period.
- **No discrimination:** GrowDirect will not discriminate against Participant for exercising any CCPA rights
- **Service provider classification:** For CCPA purposes, GrowDirect acts as a **service provider** processing data on Participant's behalf solely for the business purposes described in this Addendum

## 7. Subprocessors

GrowDirect will not engage any third-party subprocessors to process Participant data without prior written notice to Participant.

## 8. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting Participant's data, GrowDirect will notify Participant within **seventy-two (72) hours** of becoming aware of the breach, including a description of the data affected and the steps being taken to address it.
