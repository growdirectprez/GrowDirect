# SCOUTING CHECKLIST — In-Person Merchant Visit

**Read this in the car before you walk in.**

**Target:** Specialty coffee merchant, Torrance CA area
**Visit Type:** Recon / soft invite for guided demo
**Date:** By Thursday, February 27, 2026

---

## THE MINDSET

You're buying coffee and having a conversation. You're not selling anything. You're not making promises. You're exploring whether this merchant might be a good fit to preview something you're building. That's it.

---

## WHAT YOU CAN SAY

- "We're building a tool that helps small businesses spot transaction problems — things like cash discrepancies, unusual refund patterns, stuff that's hard to catch manually."
- "We're looking for a few local businesses to try an early version and tell us what they think."
- "It works with Square. We'd just show you how it looks on your phone using sample data — nothing connects to your live systems unless you want it to later."
- "There's no cost, no commitment. We just want honest feedback from someone who actually runs a business like this."
- "We're a small company based here in Southern California."
- "The demo takes about an hour. We walk you through it. You tell us what makes sense and what doesn't."
- You CAN mention GrowDirect by name (that's the entity).
- You CAN describe what the product does in general terms (transaction monitoring, loss prevention, anomaly detection).
- You CAN say the product works with Square POS.

---

## WHAT YOU CANNOT SAY

- **Do NOT name the product** (the product name is confidential at this stage).
- **Do NOT promise any specific features** — "it will do X" creates implied obligations. Say "we're working on" or "we're exploring."
- **Do NOT promise launch dates, pricing, or availability.** Not even "it'll be free." Say "we haven't finalized that yet."
- **Do NOT claim the product catches theft or identifies specific employees doing wrong.** Say "it flags patterns that might be worth looking into."
- **Do NOT offer to connect to their live Square account during this visit.** The demo uses sample data.
- **Do NOT discuss other merchants we're talking to** (even if there aren't any yet — the principle stands).
- **Do NOT leave any documents behind on this visit.** Docs come at the formal demo, not the recon.
- **Do NOT make verbal agreements.** If they say "yeah, let's do it," say "great — we'll send you a short agreement by email so everything's clear for both of us."

---

## WHAT TO OBSERVE

While you're there, note the following (mental notes are fine — don't pull out a clipboard):

- [ ] How many registers / POS terminals do you see?
- [ ] Are they using Square (look for the Square reader or terminal)?
- [ ] How many employees are working?
- [ ] Does the owner seem to be on-site and hands-on?
- [ ] What's the general vibe — busy, relaxed, stressed?
- [ ] Are there multiple locations mentioned on signage or menus?
- [ ] Is there a tip jar or digital tipping on the terminal?
- [ ] What's the average transaction look like (coffee + pastry = $8–15 range)?

---

## IF THEY SAY YES

1. Thank them. Say you'll follow up by email with a short agreement and some details.
2. Get their **email address** and **best phone number**.
3. Confirm the best day/time for a one-hour walkthrough (target: Monday, March 3).
4. Do NOT hand them any documents at this point.
5. When you get back to the car: text the team with the contact info and confirmed interest.

---

## IF THEY SAY NO OR MAYBE

- "No problem at all. If you change your mind, here's my card." (Have a GrowDirect card or just give your name and email.)
- Do NOT push. Do NOT follow up more than once unless they re-engage.
- "Maybe" is fine. Say "No rush — happy to circle back whenever it works for you."

---

## RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR

If any of these come up, just be polite and leave. Do not engage further:

- They immediately ask for equity, revenue share, or partnership terms
- They want you to sign something before the conversation
- They're hostile toward technology or POS systems
- They mention active litigation involving their POS or employees

---

## AFTER THE VISIT

- Report back to the team: interested / not interested / maybe
- If interested: share contact info so we can send the legal pack (NDA + Beta Agreement + DPA + Waiver)
- If not interested: log it and move to the next lead
