SCOPE AND DESCRIPTION
Vendor Management incorporates the setup and approval of vendors who range from first time supplier of product to full marketing and logistics partners. The terms and service standards that are set up provide the basis for measuring compliance.

KEY METRICS
Time to set up approval of a vendor and set them up to start buying 
Effectiveness is measured by vendor compliance measures and profitability
The purpose of Vendor Management is to facilitate streamlined but thorough approval of vendors and to provide easy interpretation of terms and conditions so that they can be used for purchase orders. Vendor compliance measurment is also provided by Vendor Management.

Vendor Management Process Introduction
Vendor Management is not a continuous process in all areas, but rather components of the process interact with other major processes to accomplish: 
Planning - The sourcing component of Merchandise Planning
Buying and receiving merchandise
Performance Monitoring and control of the vendor relationship
The chart to follow illustrates these relatuonships.
The major point s of interaction are with:
Merchandsie Planning
Purchase Order Mangement
PO Issue
PO Tracking
PO Receiving
Performance Monitoring
One of the focuses of the preliminary recommendation is on the framework of moving towards more vendor logistics partnerships when they are available and if the economic evaluation of the supply chain costs indicate a partnership is desirable
The other focus is to have information recording, analysis and reporting capability to support the overall relationship with the vendor by measuring compliance

Partnership operations are determined by joint evaluation of supply chain economics and range from the minimum to the highest levels as illustrated by the following list:
EDI transactions with product availability and delivery standards
The above plus retail forecast
The above plus vendor managed inventory (with or without store drop shipment)
The above plus Planogram Management
The above plus marketing / promotion and market research
Note that planograms and marketing partnerships may already be done independent of the logistics partnership.
The use of vendor planograms is an outgrowth establishing the assortment policy (i.e., will categories be sole sourced). In the case where a category is sole sourced, the vendor can become the category manager subject to meeting performance objectives.
Process Recommendations (Continued)
Performance monitoring should include all facets of the supply chain inventory management process for the vendor, for example:
Order processing errors
Delivery/pick-up delays
Order completeness
Invoice errors
Profitability
Vendor allowance management is a special case of performance monitoring. It accumulates all sales and purchases against the terms and allowances and accounts for all amounts due to the retailer.
Business reviews should be conducted at least annually. The extent of the review depends upon the 
partnership
For non-partnership vendors the review should be about performance plus:
Vendor plans, new products, promotions, etc.
Determine the future of the relationship
Grow to partnership status
Increase business without partnership status
Decrease business
Terminate relationship
For partnership vendors it is more of an ongoing process. The periodic formal review is more like a joint planning session subject to performance. Partnership vendors face the same determination of future relationships.
There is an import management component to all functions.

Product development applies to private label sourcing. Vendor produces samples for testing and evaluation.
Product evaluation includes private label as well as existing products that may have been acquired for the following:
Internal testing and evaluation
Test marketing
Performance evaluation should include at least one of the following:
Performance in a test
Annual performance of a vendor being considered a 
Performance with another division
Sales performance (competitive data, third party marketing information)
Supply chain economics evaluation should use a merchandise contribution margin model to determine the best buying method and mode of flow from source to store.
Based on the analysis, the terms, conditions and performance standards are negotiated and submitted for approval. Approval triggers an agreement:
Standard agreement for non-partners
Partnership agreement covering all elements of the relationship
All approved terms and conditions are maintained in the database to control purchase orders issued the vendor. Some terms may be overridden at the PO level, (e.g., promotional terms and allowances)
Items selected from the vendor are entered into the item database.

Key Changesy
Versus Current Process
Accelerates the promotion and development of vendor logistics partnerships:
When they are available
When they provide mutual benefit
Facilitates capturing the broad range of terms and conditions associated with expanded vendor relationships so that they can be more easily used to produce accurate and timely POs and to measure compliance.
Accelerates vendor compliance implementation, recognizing some of Staples
Versus Best Practice Process
Scales back the speed of identifying and arranging logistics partnerships recognizing the following:
The need to identify Staples
Vendor readiness
Jointly determine of how to take cost out of the supply chain and reduce order-to-available-to-sell cycle times
Benefits
Increased product contribution to profit margin and reduced order-to-available for sale cycle times
Ability to issue more timely and accurate POs because terms are easily interpreted and costs and dates can be set more accurately
Improved compliance measurement will support increasing business with high performance vendors and decreasing business with or eliminating low performance vendors
