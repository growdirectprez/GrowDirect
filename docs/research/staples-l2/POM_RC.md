Process Scope and Description
PO Management incorporates the initiation, costing, approval, issuance and receiving of vendor purchase orders. Order Monitoring is used in conjunction with PO Management to track the status of a vendor order at any stage in the pipeline and to project the impact on expected receiving and in store dates.

Purchase Order Management Process Recommendationst
Suggested vendor orders are generated by one of the supply chain management order generation methods or entered manually
Within this process, based on item physical characteristics, vendor terms and allowances, and vendor delivery history, the order should be modified to meet these joint requirements (if feasible). The modifications to the order include:
Master case rounding
Unit load rounding (palletizing)
Transportation rounding (Truckload, Railcar, Container)
Reaching
Adjusting order quantities to match coordinated pick up date(s)
Based on the vendor terms and level of discount / allowance / rebate achieved, the Purchase Order should be costed at the vendor level. Other non-vendor costs that make up the total landed cost are estimated. These costs include:
Freight for collect vendors
Duty, customs, and commissions for direct imports

this Purchase Order only
Purchase Order dates should be set based on when the product needs to be available for sale in stores, processing times, and freight terms
For Partnership Vendors, availability dates should be based on service standards. For Non-Partnership Vendors availability should be set based on lead time history.
