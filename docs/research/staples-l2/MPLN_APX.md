Planning Process Guide--Store Clusters
Objective
Store profiling maximizes sales volume by optimally matching each store
s merchandies mix to its customer. More specifically, the goal is to enhance the existing planning from chain level average store concept into planning by store profile attributes, minimizing store allocation/replenishment decision criteria.
Action Steps
Obtain measurable data for each store including, for example:
Store name and number, sales volume and selling square footage
Location, population demographics, climatic data and store type
For each major merchandise category analyze the following using class and department:
Annual sales volume by month and turnover
Margin performance
Assemble the data collected in steps 1 and 2 into a spreadsheet
Group stores together based on similar performance and profile each group
Segment the stores into various groups based on the examination of both qualitative and quantitative inputs. Adjust and iterate as needed. Test and refine.
Document the output
Name and define the store profiles
Identify guidelines for classifying new stores
Develop assortment plans that support store clusters and performance goals
Planning Process Guide--Merchandise Assortment Planning
Objective
To develop and maintain a well-balanced merchandise presentation. Assortment plans can be developed for any merchandise level in the merchandise hierarchy (department, category, class, sub-class, vendor).
Action Steps
Define groupings of stores (store profiles) through analysis.
Specify minimum rations between class, colors, styles or sizes for planning, inventory analysis and distribution control purposes.
Develop a baseline assortment then verify by store profile cluster to ensure that stores within a particular profile receive similar assortments. To this additional merchandise assortment levels can be applied as required depending upon merchandise presentation objectives and store needs.
Develop assortment plan in units and dollars so that impact on store plan and planogram can be determined for integration into the overall plan.
Develop meaningful partnerships
Planning Process Guide--Strategic Resource Plan
Objective
To identify key resources/vendors that can support the merchandise assortment plan and exploit vendor partnering opportunities. A well conceived/integrated resource/vendor plan is a key enabler for enhanced vendor management.
Action Steps
Review historical information to determine key vendors.
Identify key vendors/resources that support the merchandise plan.
Identify sales potintial and receipt requirements in units and dollars.
Develop/maintain mark-up targets.
Determine vendor participation/share in advertising expenses, rebates, etc.
Communicate your commitment to maintaining a partnership with your vendor(s).
Develop (in consultation with logistics) a logistics strategy for the vendor.
