Construction photographs, ~1950–1952. Tract 14649, Lot 69, Portuguese Bend, Rancho Palos Verdes. Scanned from original Kodak Safety Film negatives and contact sheets.

The House Today

25 Sea Cove Drive as it stands. The original mid-century post-and-beam structure with pool addition. Exposed beam overhangs, sliding glass doors, chimney — all original to the ~1950 construction visible in the negatives below. PV hills behind.

1 — Rear of house from pool. Sliding glass doors, pergola structure on left, pool with slide. The hills of Portuguese Bend behind.
2 — Covered patio with exposed post-and-beam ceiling. Pool edge at left. The same beam structure visible in the 1950s framing photos.
3 — Front corner. Original chimney, exposed beam overhang, stepping stones and rock landscaping. PV hills and open land behind.
4 — Full rear glass wall. Sliding doors spanning the width of the house. Satellite dish, pool. Hillside community visible above.
5 — Rear elevation. Louvered vent between glass panels, flat roof with dark fascia, power lines, pool. Original proportions intact.
6 — Pool slide and rear wing. Pergola/cabana structure on left. The glass-walled living spaces open directly to the pool deck.
7 — Under the covered patio looking toward pool and slide. Exposed beam rafters, steel posts. The post-and-beam structure from the 1950s framing negatives, 70 years later.
8 — Glass door detail. Floor-to-ceiling sliding panels with louvered shutters visible inside. Dark wood fascia, flat roof line.

Streetscapes

The community taking shape. Dirt roads, power poles, scattered ranch houses on open land. These show Sea Cove Drive, Clipper Road, and the surrounding area during the first years of Tract 14649.

3g — Same angle as 3b. Dirt pile in the street, house across the way, power pole, PV hills in background.
3c — Workers on the foundation slab. Houses and the street visible in background. Rebar and forms in foreground.
3d — Looking down the street from the foundation. Power poles, plumbing risers, houses under construction. "KODAK SAFETY FILM" on edge.
3h — The best streetscape. Power poles receding down the road toward the coast. Open land, scattered houses, sky.
3f — Plumbing stub-outs in foreground. Houses across the street with power pole, hills rising behind.
3j — Two women standing on the lot. House across the street, power pole, open grassland, PV hills.
3l — View between two houses. Block wall, vegetation establishing, another house beyond. The street filling in.
3m — Neighboring house with new foundation in foreground. Hills and open land behind. Early landscaping.
3k — Side of house under construction. Cinder block retaining wall going up, plumbing, hills behind.
8a — Looking at Clipper Road from Lot 69 (25 Sea Cove). 0 Clipper is upper left. Power lines in the distance mark the power easement through the lot at the end of the flood hazard zone.
8b — Wide view. House with power pole, open grass field. Very early development — one of the first houses in the tract.
8c — The widest view. Power lines strung, scattered houses, open fields, hills. The whole community in its first year.
8d — Single power pole standing in an open field. Distant structures on the horizon. Infrastructure arriving before houses.
8e — Ranch house on open land, hills behind. Could be one of the first completed homes, or possibly the pre-existing Arrowroot ranch house.
