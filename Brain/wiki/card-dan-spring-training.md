# Spring Training Baseball Camp (Dan Spring)

## Summary
The go-to youth baseball program on the Palos Verdes Peninsula. Run by Dan Spring — Brown University captain, All-Ivy League, Detroit Tigers draft pick. 15,000+ kids coached over 15+ years. Relentlessly positive culture.

## Details

- **Website:** stbaseball.com
- **Location:** Palos Verdes / South Bay
- **Founded by:** Dan Spring — grew up in Washington DC as a 3-sport varsity athlete, played baseball at Brown University (captain, All-Ivy League), drafted by Detroit Tigers, pitched professionally 2003–2004. Relocated to Hermosa Beach with wife Marissa
- **Origin:** Started volunteering at PV Little League practices after retiring from pro ball. Parents asked for private lessons, word spread, then parents asked him to run a camp. Grew organically
- **Programs:** 60+ private lessons/week, 11 weeks of summer camp, clinics year-round, Fall League (100+ players each year)
- **Ages:** Up to 12
- **Scale:** ~15,000 players and families served over 15+ years
- **Philosophy:** "Relentlessly positive sports culture that emphasizes effort, attitude, hustle, and sportsmanship." No tryouts, no club team model, no negativity-based coaching. Enjoyment and skill development weighted equally
- **Testimonial:** "Your camp was literally the highlight of my childhood"
