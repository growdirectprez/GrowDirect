# Coach Ken Taylor — Junior Lifeguard Prep

## Summary
The go-to junior lifeguard swim test prep instructor on the Palos Verdes Peninsula. On staff at PV Beach & Athletic Club since 1996. Runs three classes a day through spring — almost all his students pass.

## Details

- **Based at:** PV Beach & Athletic Club (PVBAC), 389 Paseo Del Mar, PVE
- **Role:** Junior lifeguard test prep instructor
- **On staff since:** 1996
- **Format:** Three classes per day, 25 kids per class, running through the spring season before JG swim tests (March–April)
- **Track record:** "Almost all the kids pass who complete his classes"
- **What he teaches:** Swim technique, speed, endurance — specifically targeting the 100-yard swim test times required by LA County Junior Lifeguards (1:50 for ages 9–11, 1:40 for 12–13, 1:30 for 14–17)
- **Profiled as:** "The South Bay's Junior Lifeguard Coach" by Easy Reader / Peninsula Magazine
