# Youth Sports, Summer & Enrichment — Palos Verdes Peninsula

Beyond the major organized sports (covered in the lifestyle guide), the Hill has a deep bench of youth programming — from Little League to dance to academic tutoring for student-athletes.

---

## Little League Baseball

### Palos Verdes Little League (PVLL)
- **Contact:** PO Box 2495, Palos Verdes Peninsula, CA 90274 — pvll.president@gmail.com
- **Website:** pvlittleleague.com
- **Registration:** Opens fall for spring season. League age based on age as of Aug 31 of prior year

### Rolling Hills Little League (RHLL)
- **Address:** 3011 Palos Verdes Dr N, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Website:** rhll.org

### Silver Spur Little League
- **Home field:** Ernie Howlett Park, Rolling Hills
- **Known as:** Premiere LL on the Hill — Saturday morning hub

### Palos Verdes Baseball Association (PVBA)
- Covers rec-league and travel-level baseball across the peninsula

- **Location:** Palos Verdes area

## Summer Camps

### Rolling Hills Country Day Camp
- **Ages:** 4½–13
- **Location:** Rolling Hills Country Day School campus
- **Activities:** Swimming, arts & crafts, dance, sports, nature, imagination classes, academic enrichment
- **Registration:** Opens late March

### Peninsula Bay Cities (PBC) Day Camp
- **Ages:** 4–14
- **Location:** Rolling Hills Prep campus — 20-acre facility
- **History:** 30 summers, ACA-accredited
- **Activities:** Fields, courts, archery, sports, Chaos Tag, GAGA Ball

### Other Camps
- PV Surf Camp — ages 6–14, weekly sessions (see peninsula-kids-water)
- Code Ninjas (RHE) — coding camps
- School of Rock PV — music camps
- Urban Youth Park — parkour camps
- TennisPower Camp at Peninsula Racquet Club — June–August
- PVPUSD Summer Camp 2026 — district-run programs

## Sports Coaching & Private Instruction

### Tennis Coaching
- **PVTC Junior Tennis Academy:** Ages 3–16, USTA curriculum, open to non-members
- **Bob Krueger:** USPTA certified at Ernie Howlett Park, (310) 541-4585
- **South Bay Tennis Center:** 30+ years, Directors Courtney and Ken Hance
- **Private coaches:** Many work the Hill — based at clubs or booking public court time

### Swim Coaching
- See [[peninsula-kids-water]] — Coach Ken Taylor at PVBAC is the JG prep anchor

### Academic Tutoring & Test Prep
- **Peninsula Academy:** Since 1990 — tutoring, test prep, college counseling
- **Blue Train College Prep:** SAT/ACT prep, works with prospective athletic recruits
- **PV Prep Tutoring:** Local PV-based, SAT/ACT and academic subjects
- **Study Hut Tutoring:** One-on-one tutoring, SAT/ACT prep, college essays. PV and beach communities
- **PV Tutoring Company:** Includes student-athlete tutors
- **IvySmart Education:** South Bay — SmartCamp, robotics, SAT prep, after-school

## Content Notes

- "The Saturday Morning Hub" — Ernie Howlett Park on Saturdays is where Little League, soccer, and equestrian converge
- Rec vs. travel commitment gap: AYSO + PVBA rec = 4–6 hrs/week; one travel sport = 20+ hrs/week + $5K–$15K/year
- City rec programs at Hesse Park (RPV), Farnham Martin Park (PVE), and Ernie Howlett (RHE) provide lower-barrier entry
