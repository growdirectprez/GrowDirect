# Foundation California Formation

The Abalone Cove Foundation forms as a **California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation** under Corporations Code §§ 5110 et seq. Not Wyoming. Not Delaware. California, because the mission and activities are entirely in California.

Distilled working position from a 2026-04-19 advisor memo. Final filings require separate 501(c)(3) counsel review.

## Why California (Not Wyoming)

Wyoming nonprofits are attractive for crypto-adjacent DAOs seeking privacy and low fees. Wrong fit here because:

- Every activity happens in California (research on CA declarations, inter-HOA convening among CA HOAs, advocacy on Coastal Specific Plan / Subregion 4, conservation easements to PVPLC)
- A Wyoming nonprofit still must register as a foreign charity with the California AG (Form CT-1, annual RRF-1) — same regulatory burden plus extra filings
- California Corporations Code provides clearer statutory authority for the exact activities planned (historic preservation, convening other CA nonprofits, accepting conservation easements, publishing educational materials on local governance documents)
- Peninsula foundations, PVPLC, and local donors expect California nonprofits — Wyoming raises credibility flags for conservative funders
- No meaningful privacy advantage — the Foundation's work is intentionally public (cove.org, Library of Evidence, position letters)

The deferred Governance Engine (Epic 3) could later use a Wyoming LLC subsidiary if truly needed for a DAO-style wrapper, but the parent 501(c)(3) should remain California.

## Formation Steps

1. **File Articles of Incorporation** (Form ARTS-PB-501(c)(3)) with the California Secretary of State
   - Expedited processing available
   - Charitable purpose statement must mirror the language in the Consolidated Design Spec (educational, literary, lessening burdens of government via historic preservation)
   - Dissolution clause: assets to another 501(c)(3) with similar purposes
2. **Obtain EIN** via IRS Form SS-4 (immediate online)
3. **First organizational board meeting** — adopt bylaws, elect officers, approve conflict-of-interest policy, approve gift-acceptance policy, approve compensation policy, ratify pre-formation work (see [[foundation-three-hat-compensation]])
4. **File Form 1023-EZ** ($275) with IRS — charitable purpose statement and disclosures; include the Library of Evidence as supporting evidence of educational purpose
5. **Register with California Attorney General** (Form CT-1, $25)
6. **Franchise Tax Board exemption** (Form 3500A)
7. **Open business bank account** in the Foundation's exact legal name, tied to EIN, at a bank separate from Community of Abalone Cove's account
8. **Set up separate books and accounting** — never commingle with Community of Abalone Cove or the LLC

## Registered Agent

- **Initial:** A director (cost control)
- **Transition:** Professional service ($50–$150/year) once activities increase to shield personal addresses

## Initial Directors

- Minimum 3 per Corp. Code § 5210
- Recommended 5–7 for resilience and for a pool of disinterested directors to approve founder compensation
- All should understand the three-entity structure and the charitable purpose
- Target: CA residents (simplifies notice, meetings, and tax treatment)

## Name Confirmation

Working name: **Abalone Cove Foundation** — brand-consistent with the abalonecove.org domain.

Alternatives if SOS or trademark conflicts:
- Abalone Cove Community Stewardship Foundation
- Abalone Cove Historic Preservation Foundation

Before filing Articles: confirm availability with California Secretary of State + USPTO trademark search.

## Strict Separation Rules

- Separate bank accounts — never share with Community of Abalone Cove or the LLC
- Separate counsel — Foundation counsel distinct from Community of Abalone Cove counsel
- Separate books and accounting
- No commingling of funds or assets
- No shared officers without full written disclosure
- Foundation bylaws explicitly prohibit exercising governance over Community of Abalone Cove or any HOA — see [[foundation-bylaws]]

## Timeline

Formation can be completed in 2–4 weeks once initial directors are confirmed:

- Week 1: Name confirmation + Articles drafted + filed (expedited)
- Week 1–2: EIN + bank account + counsel engagement
- Week 2: First board meeting (bylaws, officers, policies, pre-formation ratification)
- Week 2–4: 1023-EZ filed + CT-1 filed + FTB 3500A filed
