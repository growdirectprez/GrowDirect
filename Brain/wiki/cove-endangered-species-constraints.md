# Endangered Species Constraints — Species → Trigger → Project Map

Operational cross-reference: given a construction or land-use scope on or adjacent to WPBCA parcels, which listed species intersect, which regulatory trigger fires, and what is the consultation / mitigation obligation.

This is not a scientific summary (see [[coac-pv-endangered-species.md]] for the source-paper synthesis; [[cove-pvplc-partnership]] for the operational conservation partnership). This is the decision tool: "for this scope of work, what habitat / permit / consultation hits?"

## The five listed species on or near WPBCA parcels

| Species | Scientific name | Status | Habitat on Peninsula | PVPLC recovery role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Palos Verdes blue butterfly | *Glaucopsyche lygdamus palosverdesensis* | Federal Endangered (ESA) | Coastal sage scrub; deerweed (*Lotus scoparius*) + *Astragalus trichopodus* larval host plants | Captive rearing; 2020 first reintroduction onto PV Nature Preserve |
| Coastal California gnatcatcher | *Polioptila californica californica* | Federal Threatened (ESA); CA Species of Special Concern | Coastal sage scrub (*Artemisia californica*-dominated) | PVPLC partner; NCCP covered species |
| Coastal cactus wren | *Campylorhynchus brunneicapillus* (coastal pop.) | CA Species of Special Concern; ESA candidate historically | Prickly pear (*Opuntia littoralis*, *O. oricola*) + coastal cholla (*Cylindropuntia prolifera*) thickets | PVPLC habitat manager; Dalkey 2016 site-fidelity study |
| El Segundo blue butterfly | *Euphilotes battoides allyni* | Federal Endangered (ESA) | Coastal dunes; not primary Peninsula species but in PVPLC recovery portfolio | PVPLC partner species |
| (Coastal sage scrub itself) | *Artemisia californica* + associates | Sensitive natural community | The habitat-wrapper for the above | NCCP Subarea Plan covered community |

Sources: [[coac-pv-endangered-species.md]], [[cove-pvplc-partnership]], 2019 RPV NCCP/HCP.

## The regulatory triggers

| Trigger | Authority | When it fires |
|---|---|---|
| **Section 7 Consultation** (ESA §7) | 16 U.S.C. §1536 | Federal action, federal permit, or federal funding + may-affect-a-listed-species |
| **Section 10 Incidental Take Permit** (ESA §10) | 16 U.S.C. §1539 | Non-federal action with take of listed species (requires HCP) |
| **NCCP Subarea Plan compliance** | CA Fish & Game Code §2800 et seq. | RPV 2019-adopted Subarea Plan; compliance is a condition of covered activities within the planning area |
| **CA Endangered Species Act (CESA) §2081** | Fish & Game Code §2081 | Take of CA-listed species without federal ESA coverage |
| **Species of Special Concern review** | CA DFW guidance | Advisory; flags projects for additional review |
| **Coastal Act ESHA** (Environmentally Sensitive Habitat Area) | Pub. Res. Code §30240 | Coastal zone; development adjacent to ESHA must avoid significant disruption |
| **CEQA §15065 mandatory finding** | CEQA Guidelines | Threshold of significance if project may substantially reduce habitat or threaten a listed species |

## The species → trigger matrix

| Scope of work | PVB | Gnatcatcher | Cactus wren | El Segundo blue | CSS community |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New residential construction on existing pad** (Tract 14649, cleared lot) | Low if pad already graded; no native habitat present | Low | Low | n/a | Low |
| **New construction on vegetated lot** | **Section 7 / 10 trigger** if deerweed or *Astragalus* present | **Section 7 / 10 trigger** if CSS present | Section 10 / CESA §2081 if cactus thickets | n/a | **NCCP compliance required** |
| **Grading or vegetation removal in moratorium area** | **Section 7 / 10 trigger** | **Section 7 / 10 trigger** | Section 10 / CESA | n/a | **NCCP compliance** |
| **Trail work / repair on bridle trail easement** | Low if surface-only; rises if host plants affected | Rises if CSS edge disturbed | Low unless cactus pruned | n/a | Case-by-case |
| **Road / drainage maintenance (PVDS, Clipper, Sea Cove)** | Low unless adjacent fill or pipeline trench crosses habitat | Low unless CSS cleared | Low unless cactus thickets cleared | n/a | Case-by-case |
| **ARC review involving bluff setback or coastal frontage** | Applies 40-ft bluff setback (Dec. One-A 1950) — habitat largely below | Low — CSS rarely on narrow bluff bench | Low — cactus prefers upland | n/a | Consider ESHA adjacency |
| **0 Clipper / Lot H area development** | **HIGH** — Filiorum Reserve adjacent; PVB range | **HIGH** — CSS documented | **HIGH** — prickly pear thickets documented | n/a | **ESHA likely; HCP / NCCP required** |
| **0.57-acre creek / flood hazard area at 0 Clipper** | HIGH | HIGH | Medium | n/a | **ESHA — "habitat in excellent condition" per Coastal Specific Plan** |

**Default rule of thumb:** If the scope touches *native vegetation* anywhere near Portuguese Bend, Abalone Cove Reserve, Filiorum Reserve, or the Three Sisters corridor, **consult PVPLC and assume at least NCCP compliance review.** Pre-consultation is cheap; surprise enforcement is not.

## The five scopes of work and how to handle them

### 1. Single-family residence on an already-cleared Tract 14649 lot

- **Habitat triggers:** Low if the lot has been maintained and no native CSS present. Verify by site walk before any demolition or grading.
- **ARC obligations:** Standard architectural review per Declaration No. One (1949) + Declaration One-A (1950) for bluff lots. See [[cove-legal-framework]].
- **Coastal obligations:** If lot is in the Coastal Zone, Coastal Development Permit required through RPV (delegated by Coastal Commission). See [[cove-coastal-specific-plan-s4]].
- **Consultation default:** None required under ESA/NCCP unless native vegetation is present at the time of application.

### 2. New construction or substantial remodel on a vegetated lot

- **Habitat triggers:** Full species matrix above. Assume Section 10 ITP or NCCP compliance review.
- **Pre-application step:** Commission biological survey (can be triggered by coastal staff, but cheaper to do voluntarily). Identify host plants and CSS coverage.
- **Mitigation hierarchy:** Avoid → minimize → mitigate. NCCP Subarea Plan defines the mitigation-ratio framework.
- **PVPLC coordination:** PVPLC is the NCCP Habitat Manager for RPV's 1,400-acre Palos Verdes Nature Preserve. Engage early even when work is outside the Preserve but within NCCP area.

### 3. Trail repair / maintenance on the bridle-trail easement (Declaration of Easements 1949)

- **Habitat triggers:** Surface-only work is low impact. Any trail widening, cut/fill, or vegetation clearing rises to NCCP review.
- **Note on easement:** Private recorded easement per [[cove-legal-framework]]; operational protocol coordinates with WPBCA governance. **This matter is governance-sensitive** — see [[wpbca-ocean-path-easement]] (visibility: private). Do not include ocean-path specifics in Foundation public voice material.
- **Consultation default:** WPBCA internal review + PVPLC heads-up for anything beyond surface maintenance.

### 4. Emergency / moratorium area work (drainage, road, landslide mitigation)

- **Habitat triggers:** Default high. Moratorium area = Portuguese Bend + Abalone Cove slide complex = intersecting NCCP reserves + CSS + coastal ESHA.
- **Public-entity angle:** Any public-entity work that causes property damage triggers R9 *Albers* inverse-condemnation risk per [[cove-case-law-lessons]]. Species impacts are an additional vector.
- **Consultation default:** Section 7 if federal funding (FEMA, USACE); Section 10 otherwise.

### 5. New permit-track subdivision or upzoning (Builder's Remedy / HE-driven)

- **Habitat triggers:** Maximum. Any parcel being considered for increased density in the Portuguese Bend / Abalone Cove region intersects multiple species + NCCP + Coastal ESHA + Safety Element overlays.
- **Relevant authorities:**
  - R8 / R14 from [[cove-case-law-lessons]]: HE/BR does not preempt CC&Rs
  - R11: *Monks* landslide-moratorium defensibility
  - R9: *Albers* inverse condemnation if public action triggers further damage
  - CEQA §15065 mandatory significance finding if habitat impact
  - Coastal Act §30240 ESHA protection
- **0 Clipper test case:** See [[cove-0-clipper]]. The Foundation + PVPLC ask for the 0.57-acre habitat creek area to be added to the Abalone Cove Reserve conservation easement is the live template — [[cove-pvplc-partnership]].
