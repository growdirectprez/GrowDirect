# Professor Adrian Beck — Total Retail Loss Framework

> **Scope.** Reference card for the academic foundation of the Total Retail Loss (TRL) framework. Beck is the leading British academic on retail shrinkage and loss measurement. His work is the intellectual predecessor to the three-accountability-rail model in CATz.

---

## Who He Is

**Professor Adrian Beck** — Emeritus Professor, Department of Criminology, University of Leicester, UK.

30+ years of research focused on helping retailers understand and manage the impact of loss on their business. Academic advisor to the **ECR Community Retail Loss Group** for over 20 years. The most cited academic voice on retail shrinkage methodology in the UK and internationally.

Co-authored the defining shift in LP methodology: from "shrinkage" (a catch-all, inconsistently defined term) to **Total Retail Loss** — a structured typology covering every form of loss across the retail enterprise.

---

## The Core Thesis

Retail loss has been measured wrong for 100 years.

"Shrinkage" has no universally agreed definition. Retailers use it to mean wildly different things — some include fraud, some don't; some include supply chain loss, some don't. The result is benchmarks that don't benchmark, programs that don't close the right gaps, and executives who can't articulate their actual loss exposure.

Beck's answer: **Total Retail Loss** — a typology of 33 categories of loss spanning the entire retail environment, from shop theft in physical stores to fraud at corporate headquarters.

## The Total Retail Loss Framework — What It Is

**33 categories of loss across the full retail enterprise**, organized into four zones:

1. **Customer-facing (physical store)** — shop theft, self-checkout misuse, fraud at checkout, BOPIS abuse
2. **Supply chain** — vendor short-ships, DSD fraud, distribution center shrink
3. **Employee-facing** — internal theft, time fraud, returns abuse, waste
4. **Enterprise/digital** — payment fraud, corporate expense fraud, loyalty abuse, e-commerce returns fraud

**The measurement standard Beck proposes:**
- Total Retail Loss as % of sales (not shrinkage %, which excludes too many categories)
- Consistent category definitions so benchmarks actually work across retailers
- Separation of "known loss" (auditable) from "unknown loss" (residual SOH gap)

---

## Connection to CATz

Beck's TRL framework is the academic foundation the CATz methodology was built on top of, extending it in three ways:

| Beck (TRL) | CATz extension |
|---|---|
| 33 loss categories, typology-based | Three accountability rails (Operational · Financial · Evidentiary) — structured around closure, not just classification |
| "Total Retail Loss" as % of sales | Bitcoin-standard cost accounting — loss expressed in satoshis, not just percentage |
| Consistent measurement methodology | Hashed, provenance-stamped evidentiary record — not just measurement but proof |
| Academic/consultant delivery model | ALX agent delivery — the same methodology running at 10x the efficiency |

Beck asks: *what is the full scope of retail loss?*
CATz answers: *how do you close all three accountability rails to eliminate it?*
