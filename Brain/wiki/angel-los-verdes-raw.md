## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Western bluffs of PV Peninsula, adjacent to Los Verdes Golf Course
- Community dates back to 1962, continued development through 1970s
- Non-gated, no formal HOA or monthly dues
- Homes sit along and around the golf course — many with ocean views from Malibu to Catalina
- Ranch-style architecture from 1960s-70s era

### Housing Stock
- Single-family homes, 1,334–5,087 sqft, average ~2,134 sqft
- Typical home: 4 bed / 2 bath
- Zip code: 90275

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 31
- **Median close price:** $1,750,000
- **Median days on market:** 23
- **Price per square foot:** $922
- **YoY price change:** -5.4%
- Accessible entry with strong PPSF — demand outweighs supply at this price tier

## 6. Lifestyle

### Golf — Los Verdes Golf Course
William Bell-designed 18-hole par-71 course (est. 1964). LA County-operated, open to public. 6,605 yards with panoramic ocean views from most holes. **One of the top 10 busiest courses in the nation.** Active Men's, Women's, and Senior clubs. 5,000 sqft clubhouse accommodates up to 350 guests for weddings/corporate events.

### Hiking
48 trails in RPV area. Top picks: Point Vicente Beach Walk (4.7-star), Terranea Beach & Hudson's Bluff Sea Cave via Seascape Trail (5.2 mi), Portuguese Bend Reserve (424 acres).

### Nearby Attractions
- Point Vicente Interpretive Center — gray whale migration viewing
- Wayfarers Chapel — Lloyd Wright glass chapel in lush gardens
- Terranea Resort — 102-acre oceanfront with spa, pools, kayaking, multiple restaurants

Source: Los Verdes Golf Course, AllTrails, RPV Nature Preserve
