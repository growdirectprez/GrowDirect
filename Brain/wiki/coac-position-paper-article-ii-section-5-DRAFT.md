# Position Paper — Status of Recorded Covenants and Procedural Mechanisms

**DRAFT.** Visibility: private-until-board-authorized. Requires-counsel-review.

This is a draft position paper for review by separate HOA counsel and, after that review, by the Board of the Community of Abalone Cove. It is not for external publication or filing in any proceeding until both reviews are complete and the Board has voted to authorize the filing on a properly noticed agenda. Drafted in the calm legal register modeled on the November 23, 2009 objection of the West Portuguese Bend Community Association in RPV Case No. ZON2009-00416. Numbered, citation-precise, brief-not-complaint. Names no individuals beyond the role-only signature block.

## Preamble

The Community of Abalone Cove holds the verbatim text of Declaration No. 100 (recorded October 7, 1929, in Book 9436, Page 155, Official Records of Los Angeles County) and Declaration No. 101 (recorded October 14, 1929, in Book 9482, Page 37, Official Records of Los Angeles County), together with the August 15, 1930 grant deed from the Palos Verdes Corporation to the Filiorum Corporation (Book 10226, Page 170, Official Records), which expressly incorporates the provisions of those declarations into the chain of title for the parcels conveyed. The Community has also commissioned, through the Abalone Cove Foundation as a neutral research and documentation deliverable, a licensed land survey of the seven parcels described in Declaration No. 101 against the original recorded monuments.

This paper summarizes the present-day status of those instruments and the procedural mechanisms they contain, as the Community understands them on the documentary record. The Community is not seeking relief from any tribunal in this filing. The position is recorded to ensure that the present-day status of these recorded covenants and their internal procedural mechanisms is on the public record.

## Numbered Points

1. **Declaration No. 100 establishes the constitutional framework for the area to which it applies.** Recorded October 7, 1929, in Book 9436, Page 155. Establishes the Community Association of Palos Verdes, the Art Jury, the architectural standards, the general plan of development, and the procedural mechanisms by which the framework is to be administered. The verbatim text of the operative provisions is held by the Community of Abalone Cove and is available for inspection by qualified parties.

2. **Declaration No. 101 identifies seven specific parcels and incorporates Declaration No. 100 by reference.** Recorded October 14, 1929, in Book 9482, Page 37. Describes seven parcels by metes-and-bounds; binds those parcels to the Declaration No. 100 framework by express incorporation; and adds further restrictions specific to the seven parcels. The verbatim text and the metes-and-bounds descriptions are held by the Community of Abalone Cove.

3. **The August 15, 1930 grant deed incorporates both Declarations into the chain of title.** Book 10226, Page 170, Official Records. Conveys three portions of Lot H of the Rancho Los Palos Verdes from the Palos Verdes Corporation to the Filiorum Corporation, expressly incorporating each and every provision, condition, restriction, reservation, lien, charge, easement, and covenant of Declarations Nos. 100 and 101 — *as fully and completely as if herein set forth in full.* Under California law, recorded covenants of this character run with the land. Covenants do not extinguish on conveyance, change of ownership, or change of governmental sovereign over the land.

4. **Declaration No. 100 contains a self-executing procedural mechanism in Article II, Section 5.** Where the Community Association of Palos Verdes has failed to meet or act for more than ninety consecutive days, fifteen owners of record on fifteen separate parcels within the original Declaration No. 100 boundary may publish notice, hold a meeting, and elect three Trustees. The Trustees inherit the full enforcement authority set forth in Article II, Section 4, including the authority to record a Restated Declaration that re-affirms the surviving Declaration No. 100 and No. 101 covenants on the chain of title. The mechanism is self-executing under the recorded text and does not require external authorization to be invoked.

5. **The ninety-day condition precedent has been satisfied as a matter of historical fact.** On the records available to the Community, the Community Association of Palos Verdes has not met or acted in the Article II enforcement role for substantially longer than ninety consecutive days. The Community of Abalone Cove (formerly West Portuguese Bend Community Association), founded in 1949 under separate instruments, has functioned in its own governance role since that date; that body's continuity is not, and does not constitute, meeting or action by the Community Association of Palos Verdes for purposes of Article II, Section 5.

6. **The Community of Abalone Cove preserves all rights, procedures, and remedies set forth in the recorded covenants.** Including but not limited to the procedural mechanisms in Declaration No. 100 Article II, Section 5, and the substantive restrictions of Declaration Nos. 100 and 101 as carried forward by the August 15, 1930 grant deed. The Community reserves the right to invoke them in accordance with the recorded text and to coordinate with other parcel owners within the Declaration No. 100 boundary for that purpose. Any such invocation, if and when it occurs, will follow the procedure set forth in the instrument itself.

7. **This filing does not seek relief from any tribunal.** The Community of Abalone Cove is not pursuing litigation, is not asserting a grievance against any party, is not requesting any action by any external body, and is not, by this filing, invoking the procedural mechanism set forth in Declaration No. 100 Article II, Section 5. The position is recorded to ensure that the present-day status of the recorded covenants and the procedural mechanisms they contain is on the public record, and to prevent any later argument that the Community has slept on its rights with respect to these instruments.

## Reservation of Rights

The Community of Abalone Cove reserves all rights under the recorded covenants and under California law, including but not limited to the rights set forth in California Civil Code §§5975 et seq. (Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act), the rights and procedures set forth in Declarations Nos. 100 and 101 themselves, and the procedural mechanisms recited above. Nothing in this filing waives, modifies, or limits any such right.

## Editorial Decisions Captured

- **No individual names.** Per [[Brain/agents/condor]] voice rules and the volunteer-protection rule. The signature line uses role only.
- **No threats.** No mention of litigation, demand for performance, or specific deadline. The paper preserves rights and records status; it does not assert a cause of action.
- **No naming of "the reactivation."** The paper recites the procedural mechanism as written in the recorded instrument; it does not name a strategy or attach a label that did not appear in the 1929 text.
- **No reference to specific contemporary parcels (e.g., 0 Clipper Road, 24 Sea Cove Drive, 7 Sea Cove Drive, APN 7573-007-900).** The paper operates at the level of the recorded instruments. Parcel-specific application is downstream of the survey and the Trustee election.
- **The "we are not suing" posture is preserved.** Both in tone and in operative language.
