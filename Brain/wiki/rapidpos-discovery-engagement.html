RapidPOS Discovery & Diagnostic — Project Plan

v1 2026-05-04 Draft Project type: discovery & diagnostic Duration: 4 weeks (Week 0 + Weeks 1–4)

1 — Scope

Objective

Produce a structured, evidence-based picture of RapidPOS's commercial, product/technology, and operational reality. Deliver a prioritized transformation portfolio and roadmap. Install a queryable knowledge layer that persists post-engagement and is ready to support follow-on transformation execution.

In scope

  • SaaS diagnostic — commercial reality, product and technology reality, operational reality
  • Information systems planning (abbreviated) — target architecture, application portfolio, project portfolio
  • Strategic positioning (abbreviated, where engagement context requires it)
  • Deployment of the diagnostic platform on RapidPOS's GCP environment
  • Synthesis, decision log, audit trail, signed handoff package

Out of scope

  • Implementation of recommended transformation work (separate engagement)
  • Procurement and vendor selection beyond what diagnostic findings surface
  • Any modification of RapidPOS source systems beyond bidirectional connector design (no production writes during discovery)
  • Customer-side change management beyond what is required to support discovery interviews and access

8 — Acceptance criteria

Each criterion must be satisfied for handoff to be signed.

  • All required schema-fragments populated to the depth defined in the charter
  • Synthesized narratives rendered for every populated facet
  • Decision log signed by mission control; all decisions trace to authority and rationale
  • Audit log complete and exported to immutable storage; every finding traces to source
  • Knowledge layer accessible by RapidPOS-named operators with documented access path
  • Transformation portfolio approved by sponsor with priorities, dependencies, and investment plan
  • Handoff package contains: charter, imprints used, all findings, all schema-fragments, all syntheses, decision log, audit log export, transformation portfolio, roadmap, investment plan, operator runbook
  • No halt-severity issues open
  • Connector substrate documented for follow-on push of to-be changes

Data handling — explicit exclusions

  • No customer-of-customer data is exchanged or stored beyond what is strictly necessary for the diagnostic and authorized in the SOW.
  • No PCI-scoped data is accessed. Engagement is explicitly out of PCI DSS scope. Any system that touches payment card data is excluded from connector access.
  • No PHI or HIPAA-regulated data.
  • No GDPR Article 9 special-category personal data unless explicitly scoped in the SOW with appropriate controls.
  • All data handled remains within RapidPOS's GCP environment under RapidPOS's IAM control. Delivery-org operates under scoped, audited credentials.
