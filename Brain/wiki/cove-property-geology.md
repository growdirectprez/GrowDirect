# Property & Geology

The WPBCA community and the 0 Clipper parcel sit within one of the most active landslide complexes on the California coast. The geological evidence is devastating for the rezoning.

## The Landslide Complex

**Ancient Portuguese Bend Landslide (APBL):** 120,000-500,000 years old. The entire project area is within this boundary.

**Abalone Cove Landslide (ACL):** Reactivation of the APBL. Movement initiated 1974, continued through 1985, encompassed ~85 acres. Led to creation of the Abalone Cove Landslide Abatement District (ACLAD), which now operates 22 dewatering wells.

**Current movement (March 2026):** 2.14 inches per week — orders of magnitude faster than historical averages (0.3 in/year for Zone 2, 1.4 in/year on eastern edge vacant lots, 1.9 in/year at ACL toe).

**Wayfarers Chapel:** Dismantled in 2024 due to 2+ feet/month ground movement. Same formation, same forces.

## Geological Formation

The slide surface is bentonite clay within the Portuguese Tuff layer (50-75 feet thick), part of the Monterey Formation (2,000+ feet thick) over Altamira Shale. This clay has been sliding for a quarter million years.

## Critical Finding: Plumbing Caused Slides

The city's own Zone 2 Landslide EIR states that residential plumbing and septic systems were "likely catalysts" for slide movement. Groundwater is "the controlling factor." Robert Stone & Associates (1979) recommended against further development until slide movement stopped. It never stopped.

The city approved 16 units with full plumbing on the same formation. Its own environmental report contradicts the 2024 rezoning.

## Parcel Maps — What the Assessor Knows

The LA County assessor maps reveal details the city planning department missed:

**Flood hazard zone:** Marked on the 0 Clipper parcel. Reduces usable area from 1.56 acres to ~1.01 acres. The city calculated density on the larger number.

**Oceanfront lot carve-outs:** Bold diagonal lines run through EVERY oceanfront lot (7-15) with separate parcel designations for southwestern (bluff-face) portions. The assessor recognizes the terrain difference between buildable flat lot and steep bluff.

**"No plumbing" notation:** The 1985 assessor map for 0 Clipper shows: "250 ft no plumb add on other restriction a variance." The assessor knew about the geological restrictions. The planning department didn't check.

## Tract 14649 Layout

81 lots across 4 streets: Sea Cove Drive, Clipper Road, Barkentine Road, Packet Road. All APNs begin with 7573. Fire Station 53 sits at 6124 Palos Verdes Drive South, adjacent to the tract (1960s wooden structure, structurally inadequate).

## Slide Impact on Community

The landslide has been displacing the community's ocean access easement since the 1950s. Evidence includes: Nelson Taylor → Simes lot swap (1952-1959) forced by slide movement, creation of ACLAD, ongoing ocean path maintenance issues, and the 2024 Swank draft easement status agreement.
