## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Northwest RPV, along PV Drive East from La Vista Verde gatehouse to PV Dr South and up Crest Road
- ~758 homes. Community dates to 1958.
- Predominantly large SFR, many 4+ bedrooms. Avg ~2,593 sqft (range 1,530–19,460 sqft).
- Named for panoramic Catalina Island views; many homes also overlook LA basin and harbor
- Some of highest elevations on the peninsula
- Quiet, family-friendly, safe. Low turnover (only 14 sales in 2025).

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 14

- **Median days on market:** 48

## 6. Lifestyle

### Parks & Trails
- **Deane Dana Friendship Park** — 123 acres, 1.1-mile loop, nature center, harbor/Catalina views, BBQ/picnic
- PVPLC preserves: 1,600+ acres of trails across peninsula
- Abalone Cove Shoreline Park, Sacred Cove tidepools nearby

### Golf
- Trump National Golf Club (adjacent)
- Los Verdes Golf Course (public, affordable)

### Ocean
- Terranea beach cove, Abalone Cove, Portuguese Bend
- Surfing, tide-pooling, diving, kayaking

### Character
Quiet, established, views-focused. Residents skew older long-term homeowners. Feels removed from urban LA despite 30 min from downtown via 110.

Source: AllTrails, Outdoor SoCal, Stephen Haw Group
