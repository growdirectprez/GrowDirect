# Claim: Lot H Declaration Contains Only Racial Exclusionary Language

**Status:** CONFIRMED — verified against source transcription (1950-Lot-H-Declaration-Verbatim.md)

## The Correction

Prior research across multiple sessions and briefs assumed the 1950 Lot H Declaration imposed building-type restrictions (single-family residential only, architectural controls, etc.) on the broader Lot H parcel, including APN 7573-006-024 (0 Clipper Road).

**This was wrong.** The 1950 Lot H Declaration contains only:
- Article II, Section 1: Racial occupancy covenant (void under Cal. Civ. Code § 12955)
- Article II, Section 2: Enforcement mechanism for the racial restriction
- Articles III-V: Boilerplate enforcement, reversion, duration, modification

It contains **zero** architectural restrictions, building type limits, ARC authority, minimum square footage, setbacks, or development standards.

## How the Confusion Arose

The **2009 Restated Declaration** merged four founding documents (Declaration No. One, One-A, Easements, and elements from the original scheme) into a single instrument, stripped the racial language, and kept all architectural controls. This made it look like the building restrictions came from one unified source when they actually came from Declaration No. One (1949), which only covers Tract 14649 (81 lots).

Researchers reading the 2009 Restatement naturally assumed its architectural provisions traced back to the Lot H Declaration's geographic scope. They don't.

## What This Changes

| Prior Theory | Corrected Understanding |
|---|---|
| Lot H Declaration blocks multi-family at 0 Clipper | Lot H Declaration's only restriction is void |
| Declaration No. One protections extend to Lot H parcels | Declaration No. One covers only Tract 14649 |
| CC&R defense is the strongest argument | CC&R defense is significantly weaker for parcels outside Tract 14649 |
| ARC has review authority over Lot H development | ARC authority comes from Declaration No. One — does not extend beyond Tract 14649 |

## Remaining Questions

1. ~~Did PV Corp record any **other** instruments covering APN 7573-006-024 with building restrictions?~~ **ANSWER: Almost certainly not.** Research found protections for the Filiorum properties east of Tract 14649 (Declaration 100, Declaration 101, Grant Deed OR 10226 pg 170, plus the Red Tile District Art Jury under the Palos Verdes Community Association) — but these apply to the Abalone Cove parcels, NOT to the 0 Clipper lot.
2. What is the "other restriction" referenced in the 1985 assessor notation ("250 ft no plumb add on other restriction a variance")? — Still unresolved.
3. ~~Did the 2009 Restated Declaration intentionally expand its geographic scope beyond Tract 14649?~~ Likely no — it covers only the 81 APNs in Tract 14649.
4. **The deed gap:** No deed history for APN 7573-006-024 between PV Corp and Wong (1979). Fire station parcel map and various subdivisions of Parcel 106 found over time, but the chain of title for the 0 Clipper lot between PV Corp's original ownership and Wong's 1979 acquisition is missing. This is the most interesting remaining mystery — who owned it and under what terms for those ~27 years?

## What We Found About PV Corp's Broader Declaration Scheme

The research uncovered a layered system of protections PV Corp put in place across the peninsula:

| Instrument | Coverage | Protections |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration No. One (1949) | Tract 14649 (WPBCA, 81 lots) | Full architectural controls, ARC, single-family |
| Declaration No. One-A (1950) | Lots 1-5 of Tract 14649 | Additional bluff setbacks, vegetation limits |
| Lot H Declaration (1950) | Broader Lot H (excepts named tracts) | Racial covenant only (void) |
| Declaration 100, 101 | Filiorum properties / Abalone Cove | Protections for parcels east of Tract 14649 |
| Grant Deed OR 10226 pg 170 | Abalone Cove area | Additional deed restrictions |
| Red Tile District / Art Jury | Broader PV peninsula (PVCA) | Architectural review via Palos Verdes Community Association |

**The 0 Clipper lot falls in a gap.** It's outside Tract 14649 (Declaration No. One doesn't apply), the Lot H Declaration's only restriction is void, and the Filiorum/Declaration 100-101 protections cover the Abalone Cove parcels to the east, not this lot. The Red Tile District/Art Jury (PVCA) is a separate entity from WPBCA and its jurisdiction over this specific parcel is unclear.
