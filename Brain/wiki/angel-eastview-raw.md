## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Eastern portion of RPV, bordered roughly by Western Avenue
- ~2,268 residences, 5,017 residents (US Census)
- Home sizes range 741–4,034 sqft, overall average 1,725 sqft
- Predominantly single-family with some townhome/condo product
- Many homes offer harbor and coastline views
- Most affordable entry point on the Palos Verdes Peninsula
- Peaceful, family-friendly, well-kept homes
- Shorter commute times to Harbor area, Torrance, and 110 freeway vs western RPV — practical benefit for working families

### Dual-District School Advantage
Eastview is the only RPV neighborhood where residents can choose between PVPUSD (top-tier peninsula schools) and LAUSD. This is a unique selling point — PVPUSD access at Eastview prices.

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 49
- **Median close price:** $1,340,000
- **Median days on market:** 26
- **Price per square foot:** $721
- **YoY price change:** +1.2%
- Value market with strong demand, tight inventory

**H1 2024 comparisons:** 25 SFR sales, PPSF rose to $761 (up 5.7% from $720). Highest sale $1,900,000; lowest $960,000.

## 6. Lifestyle

### Eastview Park
1700 Westmont Drive. 9.9 acres — children's playground, picnic tables, walking path, permanent restroom, parking, dog park enclosures (open daily). Neighborhood's central gathering spot.

### Trails & Nature
PV Nature Preserve (1,500 acres, 15 reserves) accessible from multiple RPV trailheads. PVPLC leads free guided nature walks every 2nd Saturday with trained naturalists, historians, geologists. Guidebook covers family-friendly hikes, wildflower viewing, tide pools, bird-watching, sunset trails, wheelchair-accessible routes.

### Walkability & Proximity
Eastview Park, The Terraces shopping, Western Avenue amenities all within walkable reach. Eastern edge location = shorter commutes to Harbor area, Torrance, 110 freeway vs western RPV.

Source: RPV Eastview Park, PV Source, PVPLC Trails, RPV Hikes & Tours
