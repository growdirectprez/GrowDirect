# Document Retention — CoAC / WPBCA

What the Community of Abalone Cove (formerly WPBCA) is legally required to retain, for how long, and what members can discard from personal copies. Compiled from the 2012 Bylaws, current Davis-Stirling (Civil Code §5100–5240), and the California Nonprofit Mutual Benefit Corporation Law (Corp Code §7200 et seq.).

## Summary

The **association** is the record custodian. Members have statutory inspection rights under Civ §5200–5240, so personal duplicates of association-held records are disposable. The 2012 Bylaws pin most retention to pre-2014 Civil Code §1363.xx citations (renumbered by AB 805 to §4000+); the substance survived the renumber. See [[cove-lessons-learned]] L-gov-5.

## Statutory Floor

| Regime | What it covers | Key sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Davis-Stirling (post-2014)** | Common-interest development records, member inspection, election materials | Civ §5100–5145 (elections), §5200–5240 (association records), §5300 (annual reports), §5675 (liens) |
| **Nonprofit Mutual Benefit Corp Law** | Corporate records, minutes, membership register | Corp §7230 et seq., §8320 (record-keeping), §8333 (inspection) |
| **Federal tax / IRS** | Financial substantiation, 990/1120-H filings | IRC §6501 (3-year SOL, extended to 6 for substantial understatement); 7-year CPA best practice |
| **California SOL on contracts** | Vendor agreements, management contracts | CCP §337 (written contracts: 4 years from breach) |

The 2014 Davis-Stirling recodification (AB 805) renumbered every citation in the 2012 Bylaws: old §1363.xx → current §5100+. Always translate before quoting.

## What the 2012 Bylaws Say

Explicit retention and records clauses in [bylaws-full.md](../../Cove/docs/archive/originals/transcriptions/bylaws-full.md):

| Clause | Topic | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **§8.14(d)** | Ballot custody | Sealed ballots held by inspector until tabulation + Corp §7527 challenge window; then transferred to the Association and stored in a secure place **≥ 1 year** after election |
| **§8.18** | Retention of Ballots | Reiterates ≥ 1-year retention, citing old Civ §1363.03(h) (now Civ §5125) |
| **§9.12** | Board Meeting Minutes | Minutes (or drafts marked as such) must be made available to Members **within 30 days** of the meeting; distributable on request and cost reimbursement (old Civ §1363.05(d), now §4950) |
| **§13.10** | Financial Records | Referenced — delegates to statute and board discretion |
| **§13.11** | Membership Records | Referenced — delegates to statute |
| **Article 14** | Inspection of Books and Records | Member and director inspection rights; Board may adopt reasonable inspection rules |

The bylaws do **not** state most retention periods directly — they defer to the statutory floor below. Treat the 2012 Bylaws as the floor, not the ceiling.

## Retention Matrix

### Keep **permanent**
| Category | Why |
|---|---|
| Articles of Incorporation + all amendments | Corp §8320(a); proves legal existence |
| Bylaws + all amendments (including the 2012 restatement, any future amendments) | Corp §8320(b); governs operations |
| Recorded Declaration, CC&Rs, Declaration 100/101, Restated Declaration (2009) | Runs with land; binds successors in perpetuity |
| Recorded easements (Ocean Access Easements 1 + 2, 1952) | Real-property interest; permanent |
| Board meeting minutes (open + executive session, all years) | Corp §8320(a)(1); Civ §4950 |
| Membership meeting minutes (annual + special) | Corp §8320(a)(1) |
| Membership register (owner list, addresses, voting rights) | Corp §8320(c); reconstructable from county records but keep the running copy |
| Assessment roll + lien history per APN | Civ §5675; lien enforceability runs with title |
| ARC applications + approvals | Bind successors; need for title transfer |
| Litigation files (Case 24TRCP00352 and any future matter) | Until final judgment + all appeals + collateral SOL (typically 4–10 years after terminal order, but treat as permanent given ongoing public record) |
| Insurance policies — occurrence-based | Latent claims can surface decades later |

### Keep **7 years** (IRS/CPA best practice; Davis-Stirling floor is shorter)
| Category | Statutory floor |
|---|---|
| Tax returns (990, 1120-H) + supporting workpapers | IRC §6501 — 3 years normal, 6 years substantial understatement |
| Audits, reviews, compilations | — |
| Bank statements, cancelled checks, deposit slips | — |
| General ledger, check register, journal entries | — |
| Invoices, receipts, paid bills | — |
| Budgets + reserve studies + funding plans | Civ §5300 disclosure chain; keep prior studies for trend |
| 1099s, W-9s, vendor tax forms | IRS |
| Annual Policy Statement + Annual Budget Report | Civ §5300 |

Davis-Stirling floor (Civ §5210) is "current fiscal year + two preceding fiscal years" for member inspection — but that's a **minimum**, not a destroy-after target.

### Keep **term + 4 years** (CA contract SOL)
| Category | Why |
|---|---|
| Vendor contracts (gardener, insurance broker, accountant, attorney) | CCP §337 — breach claims run 4 years |
| Management-company agreements (when adopted per 2026-03-21 meeting discussion) | — |
| Bank signature cards | While account active + 4 years |

### Keep **1 year minimum** (Davis-Stirling election floor)
| Category | Source |
|---|---|
| Ballots (voted + unvoted), ballot envelopes (inner + outer), voter lists, tally sheets, proxies, sign-in sheets | Bylaws §8.18; Civ §5125 |

Board discretion to keep longer if an election was contested or challenged.

### Discard / short retention
| Category | Retention |
|---|---|
| Duplicate copies, drafts superseded by final | Destroy after supersession |
| Routine correspondence (non-dispute) | 2–3 years |
| Spam / unsolicited mail | Immediately |
| Expired insurance quotes not bound | When superseded |
| Old board-candidate campaign materials | After election certification |

## Member Inspection Rights

Under Civ §5200–5210, any member may request inspection of "association records" — a broad defined term including:

- Financial books for current + 2 preceding fiscal years
- Interim financial statements
- Minutes of Board + member meetings (past + current year minimum, but retain permanent)
- Membership list (limited format per §5220)
- Governing documents
- Tax returns (narrower — enhanced records under §5205(b), 5 years)
- Executed vendor contracts

The Board may adopt a written inspection procedure (Bylaws §14.3) but cannot restrict below the statutory floor. Requests must be responded to **within 10 business days** for current-year records, **30 days** for prior years (Civ §5210(b)).

Practical implication: because members have this right, the association must physically (or digitally) **locate and produce** records on demand. Whatever's kept must be organized enough to retrieve — a box of unsorted PDFs fails the practical test.

## Personal Copies — Member Perspective

Because the association holds the originals, a homeowner's personal copies of association records are disposable:

**Discard without concern:**
- Mailed copies of minutes, agendas, notices, annual budget packets
- Duplicate governing-document mailings (the recorded versions live with the LA County Registrar-Recorder; the association has the internal copy)
- Old ballots, return envelopes, proxies you submitted (inspector/association keeps for 1+ year)
- Assessment invoices after payment reconciled

**Keep personally (long-term):**
- Your **deed** and **title policy** — permanent, never discard
- Your **assessment payment records** — 7 years for IRS / lien defense
- **ARC approvals for your own lot** — life of ownership; transfer to buyer at sale
- Anything you **personally signed** that binds you (escrow docs, easement consents, waivers)
- Evidence you collected as a fact witness in active litigation (Case 24TRCP00352 etc.) — until case closed and all appeals exhausted

## Caveats

1. **2012 Bylaws use pre-AB 805 citations.** Every §1363.xx reference is superseded. When quoting to the board or a member, always translate to current §4000+ / §5100+ numbering. The Bylaws Modernization Package (docx in governance archive) is the vehicle to fix this — open as of the 2026-03-21 annual meeting.
2. **Electronic records count.** Civ §5260 and AB 2159 (2024) confirm PDFs, emails, and scanned images satisfy retention requirements. Paper + scan is belt-and-suspenders, not required.
3. **Privilege and executive session are separate concerns.** Attorney-client privileged communications and executive session minutes have stricter access controls (Civ §5210(b)(5), §4935) — retain but restrict disclosure.
4. **The association's "fiscal year" controls the 2-year minimum window.** Confirm the FY definition in §13.x or by board resolution before applying.
5. **Coming legislation to watch:** SB 900 (2025, effective 2026) mandates 14-day utility-repair response and permits emergency assessments without vote — associated paper trail (invoices, board resolutions, member notices) will be discoverable in future disputes.
