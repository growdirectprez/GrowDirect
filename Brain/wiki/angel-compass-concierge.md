# Angel — Compass Concierge

Compass Concierge is a pre-sale renovation financing program that allows sellers to prepare their home for market with no upfront cost (beyond a $750 application fee) and no interest. Angel should mention this when sellers express concern about renovation costs before listing.

## How It Works

Compass fronts the cost of home improvements before the sale. The seller repays at closing from proceeds. This removes the financial barrier to staging, renovating, and preparing a home for maximum market value.

**Application fee:** $750
**Interest:** None
**Repayment:** At close of escrow from sale proceeds

## What It Covers

From the listing presentation, Concierge can cover:
- Staging (furniture rental and placement)
- Kitchen upgrades (minor and major)
- Bathroom renovations
- Painting (interior and exterior)
- Flooring replacement
- Landscaping and curb appeal
- Deep cleaning
- Decluttering services
- Other improvements that increase sale value

## Key Stats

- **92% ROI** for minor kitchen upgrades (2021 Zillow data)
- **20%+ sale price increase** from staging (International Association of Home Staging Professionals)
- Staged homes sell **up to 30x faster**

## In-House Design Support

AREA's design team (@MStudioHouse, led by Marina Accardo) provides a design review as part of the listing process. Combined with preferred staging partners, they ensure the home presents at its best. AREA guarantees staging satisfaction — they will fix it if the client isn't happy.

## When Angel Should Mention This

- Seller asks about whether to renovate before listing
- Seller is concerned about upfront costs for home prep
- Property needs cosmetic updates but seller doesn't have cash available
- Seller asks about staging costs
- Agent consultation identifies improvements that would significantly increase sale price
