# Gyms & Fitness — Palos Verdes Peninsula & South Bay

The peninsula has a range of fitness options from luxury clubs to budget gyms, with boutique studios concentrated in the Peninsula Center / Deep Valley corridor in Rolling Hills Estates. Nearby Torrance and Redondo fill in the gaps for big-box and specialty fitness.

---

## On the Hill

### Equinox Palos Verdes
- **Address:** 550 Deep Valley Dr, Suite 212, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 5am–10pm, Fri 5am–8pm
- **What it is:** Luxury fitness club — pool, spa, kids club, cycling studio, group fitness studios
- **Vibe:** Premium, full-service. The default for Hill residents who want a high-end gym
- **Sources:** Equinox website, Yelp (80 photos, 199 reviews)

### Coreology — Palos Verdes
- **Address:** 704 Deep Valley Dr, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **What it is:** Boutique studio — pilates-inspired XFormer reformer classes, sculpt, treadmill interval (tread), indoor cycling
- **Format:** Small group classes, musically driven, trained instructors for personalized attention
- **Sources:** Coreology website, Yelp (53 photos, 139 reviews)

### Fitness 19
- **Address:** 51 Peninsula Center, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **What it is:** Budget-friendly gym, standard equipment and machines
- **Sources:** Fitness 19 website, Yelp

### PV Beach & Athletic Club (PVBAC)
- **Address:** 389 Paseo Del Mar, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **What it is:** 5-story clifftop clubhouse, competition 6-lane pool, children's pool, fitness center. Strong swim culture anchor — summer league and year-round
- **Notable:** Coach Ken Taylor on staff — junior lifeguard test prep instructor since 1996
- **Sources:** PVBAC website, PV Source

### Jack Kramer Club
- **Address:** 11 Montecillo Dr, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Facilities:** Indoor/outdoor gym, 25-yard pool with diving boards, 13 tennis courts
- **Sources:** Jack Kramer Club website

### Truth Fitness
- **Location:** Palos Verdes Peninsula, near Redondo/Torrance border
- **What it is:** Private personal training studio — personal, semi-private, small group, sport-specific athletic conditioning. Since 2007
- **Sources:** Truth Fitness website

### Alloy Personal Training
- **Location:** Rolling Hills Estates
- **What it is:** Premier personal training, healthier active lifestyles. Since 1992
- **Sources:** Alloy website
