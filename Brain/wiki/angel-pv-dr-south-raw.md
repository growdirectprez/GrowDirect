## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Southern RPV along PV Drive South, from San Pedro border to Hawthorne Blvd
- Encompasses areas near Trump National Golf Club and Portuguese Bend
- 1,368 residences. Home sizes 781–14,520 sqft (avg 2,416 sqft).
- Mix of large custom estates, ranch homes, some condos
- **Widest price range on the peninsula:** $873,500 (lowest SFR sale in all PV 2025) to $6,550,000 (one of RPV's highest recorded, June 2025)
- Dramatic coastline setting — Palos Verdes Scenic Drive is a renowned LA coastal route
- Western portion borders Portuguese Bend landslide complex

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 18
- **Median close price:** $2,000,000
- **Median DOM:** 51 (longer — larger/unique properties)
- **Price per sqft:** $762
- **YoY price change:** -9.0%

**PPSF decline:** Largest on peninsula at 19.3% ($847 → $684) — driven by property mix and landslide-adjacent discount.

Source: PV Source, South Bay Residential, Rubyhome

## 5. Community News (Recent)

- **Record sale:** $6.55M in June 2025 — continued ultra-luxury demand despite median declines
- **Landslide adjacency:** Western PV Dr South borders Portuguese Bend complex. Construction ban (Aug 2025), FEMA $42M buyout, gas shutoff since Aug 2024 affect properties in the slide zone.
- **Dewatering progress:** 11 wells installed, movement stopped near well locations
- **Trail closures:** Some Portuguese Bend Reserve and Abalone Cove trails remain temporarily closed
- **Wide price gap:** $873K-$6.55M in 2025 reflects extreme diversity — modest homes near San Pedro to oceanfront estates near Trump National

Source: Prestige Team, RPV Land Movement, PV Source

## 6. Lifestyle

### Dramatic Coastline
PV Drive South follows the most scenic stretch of the peninsula. Palos Verdes Scenic Drive is a renowned LA coastal route.

### World-Class Golf
Trump National — 18-hole public course on 261 acres between PV Dr South and the Pacific. Clubhouse, driving range, trails, native habitat preserves.

### Resort Living
Terranea Resort — destination-resort amenity set (spa, multiple restaurants, pool, ocean activities) directly in the neighborhood.

### Hiking & Nature
Seascape Trail (0.7 mi, gentle coastal bluffs near Point Vicente Lighthouse), Portuguese Bend Reserve, Abalone Cove tide pools, Forrestal Nature Reserve. Docent-led hikes available.

### Point Vicente Lighthouse
Historic lighthouse on peninsula cliffs, panoramic ocean views. Adjacent Interpretive Center (10,000 sqft museum) — natural/cultural history, sperm whale jaw display.

### Beach Access
Abalone Cove Shoreline Park (public), Portuguese Bend Beach Club (private, gated).

### Resident Sentiment (Nextdoor)
Beach, beauty, cleanliness, ocean, peacefulness, safety, trails, walkability, weather.

Source: Terranea, Hikespeak, RPV Point Vicente, Nextdoor
