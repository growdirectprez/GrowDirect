# Angel: 1926 Palos Verdes Estates Sales Brochure

The **founding sales brochure** for Palos Verdes Estates, published c.1926 by the Palos Verdes Project (Frank Vanderlip's development arm). Describes the 4-district plan (Valmonte, Malaga Cove, Lunada, Miraleste) with renderings of Lunada Bay Plaza, Spur and Saddle Club, Pacific Coast Yacht Club, Malaga Cove Terraces, and Margate Plaza.

This is the **century-old marketing artifact** that established the lifestyle vocabulary modern Angel content still uses: golf, ocean views, equestrian heritage, Spanish-mission architecture, 358 days of sunshine, deeded-to-residents amenities. Strategic value for Angel is:

1. **Heritage credibility** — direct citation of 1926 marketing claims for modern listings
2. **Neighborhood-specific anchors** — each district's surviving identity markers
3. **The "deeded to residents" lineage** — 100-year-old framing for private peninsula amenities
4. **Verifiable climate data** — 358 days of sunshine, 31 mph max wind
