# 15-Owner Network — Parcel Identification and Owner Recruitment

The Declaration 100 Article II §5 reactivation requires **15 owners of record title of 15 separate parcels within the original Declaration 100 boundary** (after negative-space exclusions). This card documents the parcel-identification process, the recruitment approach, the confidentiality requirements, and the coordination mechanism on `dev.abalonecove.org`.

**Not legal advice.** Distilled from a 2026-04-19 external-advisor memo. HOA counsel review required before any recruitment is framed around the reactivation.

## The Threshold

- **15 owners** — of record title
- **15 separate parcels** — one parcel per owner for the trigger (owners holding multiple parcels inside the boundary still only count as one of the 15 for threshold purposes, though their additional parcels may vote separately post-recording per [[coac-article-v-equity-modernization]])
- **Inside the original Declaration 100 boundary** — i.e., inside the master Lot H metes-and-bounds minus all recorded negative-space exclusions; see [[coac-negative-space-lot-h-boundary]]
- **Willing to attend the meeting and vote** to elect the three Trustees
- **Willing to remain confidential during the quiet lock-in phase** (Weeks -4 to -1 of the blitz timeline)

## Parcel Identification

Parcels inside the burdened land (subject to title-plant verification) include — but are not limited to — the following categories from the advisor synthesis:

### Priority 1 — Housing Element Site Overlaps

Parcels directly targeted by the April 16, 2024 Housing Element / RM-22 rezoning:

- **0 Clipper Road** (APN 7573-006-024) — core Lot H; never excluded; 1-acre west-of-creek bench on the fault-line shelf edge
- **Salvation Army campus parcel** — Housing Element listed
- **Silver Spur Canyon project area** — density pressure (482-unit Verdes Estates / Akhilesh Jha proposal contested by NVSS `savethecanyon.net` — see [[coac-yimby-opposition-landscape]])
- **City-owned parcels** surrounding Point Vicente, the Interpretive Center, Golden Cove, and vacant sites up toward Crest/Hawthorne

Including these in the 15 creates the strongest direct enforcement record against rezoning. Each Housing Element site inside the burdened land is presumptively covered by the revived Declaration 100/101 covenants (single-family, density, drainage, setbacks, Art Jury review) the instant the Restated Declaration is recorded.

### Priority 2 — Abalone Cove Residential Parcels (Tract 14649 Edge Cases)

Tract 14649 was expressly excluded from Declaration 100 by later subdivision recording. However, certain fringe or overlapping parcels — particularly those on the coastal or creek-side edges — may still reference Declaration 100/101 in their chain of title. Title-plant verification needed per parcel.

The Community of Abalone Cove (the current governing HOA for Tract 14649) is structurally aligned with the reactivation even where it does not directly participate: protecting single-family character and geologic safety inside Tract 14649 is reinforced by covenant revival on adjacent Lot H parcels.

### Priority 3 — Coastal / Portuguese Bend Corridor Parcels

Parcels along the Seacove Drive / Portuguese Bend corridor that fall inside the original Lot H master boundary but outside the named tract carve-outs. The 1950 Lot H Declaration adds additional restrictions on everything in Lot H not already in a named tract — these parcels are often dual-burdened.

### Priority 4 — Rolling Hills Estates Fringes

Certain fringe parcels or cross-boundary lots may still carry residual Declaration 100/101 references. The RHCA's own CC&Rs are the primary shield in Rolling Hills Estates proper, but the revived Declaration 100 covenants provide **supplemental evidentiary support** in Builder's Remedy or density litigation involving shared infrastructure or adjacency. These parcels are a secondary source for the 15 but can contribute to the network.

## Parcel Verification Process

Each candidate parcel must be verified before inclusion:

1. **Chain-of-title review** — trace back to PV Corp / Filiorum / the 1930 Filiorum grant deed (Book 10226). Confirm the parcel has not been expressly excluded by a later subdivision or conveyance.
2. **Negative-space audit** — confirm the parcel is not inside PVE, Tract 14649, the Filiorum conveyances, or other recorded carve-outs (see [[coac-negative-space-lot-h-boundary]]).
3. **Deed recital check** — look for "subject to Declaration 100" or equivalent recitals in the most recent conveyance.
4. **Assessor Parcel Number (APN)** — confirm current APN and ownership record.
5. **Conflict check** — confirm the owner is not currently in a litigation or financial distress posture that would compromise the meeting's standing.

Title-plant work (commercial title company or experienced HOA counsel) is required for each of the 15. `dev.abalonecove.org` maintains the working list in a private-access map overlay, with per-parcel verification status.

## Recruitment Approach

The user's strength is door-to-door onboarding ("I will go door to door and onboard the way I have onboarded thousands of people getting new tools at work"). The recruitment approach:

### Tier 1 — Personal Conversation (Weeks -4 to -2)

One-to-one with each candidate owner. No mass email. No public messaging. No social posts.

**Frame around stewardship, not confrontation:**

- "The Community of Abalone Cove board is stabilizing after the 2023 rename and post-COVID turnover."
- "We're modernizing with `dev.abalonecove.org` — community directory, map, document archive."
- "There's a procedural mechanism in Declaration 100 that 15 owners can use to add a layer of covenant protection on top of what the City has done. We're exploring whether that fits for our community."
- "Would you be willing to be one of the 15?"

**DO NOT frame recruitment around:**
- The ocean path easement (sensitive; see [[wpbca-ocean-path-easement]])
- 0 Clipper grading impact on individual neighbors
- Parcel 106 or other specific litigation-adjacent topics
- YIMBY hostility or "anti-SFR" rhetoric
- "Catching the City off guard" framing (even if that's the blitz design, it's not the recruitment frame)

### Tier 2 — Platform Coordination (Weeks -2 to -1)

`dev.abalonecove.org` private sections:
- Interactive map with boundary overlay
- Document archive (Declaration 100, 101, Filiorum deed, Wong map, 2009 WPBCA restatement)
- Coordination space for the network (not public)
- Confidentiality acknowledgment form

### Tier 3 — Meeting Logistics (Week -1)

Final confirmation of attendance. Meeting date, location, quorum-verification process. Counsel-reviewed meeting script. Pre-signed powers of attorney for absent owners where allowed.

## Confidentiality Acknowledgments

Every network member signs a simple confidentiality acknowledgment at first personal conversation. Content:

> "I, [name], as an owner of record title of [APN / address], acknowledge my voluntary participation in the Community of Abalone Cove's preparation for a potential reactivation meeting under Declaration 100 Article II §5. I agree to treat the timing, participant list, and substantive preparation materials as confidential until the public notice is published. I understand this is voluntary and I may withdraw at any time before the notice is published. I acknowledge that this agreement does not obligate me to attend or vote at the meeting, but if I commit to attending, I will provide reasonable notice if I must withdraw."

The acknowledgment is not legally binding as an NDA. Its purpose is to establish shared expectations and document the network's good-faith preparation.

## Handling Owner Hesitations

Expected objections and counter-framings:

### "This sounds confrontational."

The reactivation is **not** confrontational. It's a recorded-title procedural mechanism the Vanderlip drafters built in 1929 specifically for moments when public jurisdictions fall short of community stewardship. The Restated Declaration is factual, neutral in tone, and designed to coexist with current zoning. See [[coac-restated-declaration-blitz-plan]] post-recording narrative.

### "Won't this invite City lawsuits?"

Possible but manageable. The full evidentiary packet (1939 booklet, geologic reports, Coastal Specific Plan, 2009 restatement precedent, Nahrstedt / Citizens for Covenant Compliance) is assembled in advance. Courts presumptively respect duly noticed, recorded instruments. See [[coac-declaration-100-article-ii-section-5]] risks section.

### "I don't want to be publicly associated with this."

Participation is a signature on a recorded document that becomes public the day the Restated Declaration is recorded. Before that, confidentiality holds. The network is not about public advocacy — that's the 501(c)(4) role (see [[501c-advocacy-arm]]), which network members need not join.

### "What does this cost me?"

Nothing to participate. The Restated Declaration does not impose new assessments. ARC fees, if any, are minimal and comparable to existing HOA practices. Future enforcement actions are funded by the Trustees or the 501(c)(4) arm, not by individual owners.

### "What if I change my mind?"

Withdraw before the notice is published (Day 0). After notice, the owner's role is simply to attend the meeting and vote — no long-term commitment beyond that.

## Rolling Network Development

Beyond the initial 15, the network grows over time:

- **Additional Lot H owners** whose parcels qualify can voluntarily annex under Article V §5 of Declaration 100 after recording
- **New escrows** — the recent successful § 4525 disclosure delivery demonstrates the Board can integrate new owners into the governing-document regime; new owners of qualifying parcels can join the Association post-recording
- **501(c)(4) supporters** — broader neighborhood residents (including those not in the burdened land) can support the advocacy arm without being in the network itself

## Platform Integration

`dev.abalonecove.org` supports network development:

- **Interactive map** — negative-space boundary overlay with per-parcel status (inside/outside, verified/pending, participating/invited/declined)
- **Document archive** — foundational records accessible to network members (private access)
- **Coordination space** — private messaging, meeting logistics, confidentiality acknowledgments
- **Non-binding survey tool** — informal polls (e.g., preferred meeting dates) per Civ. Code § 5100 compliance (not official ballots until electronic balloting adopted per § 5105)

## Verification Before Publishing Notice (Day 0)

- [ ] 15 parcels verified inside master boundary
- [ ] 15 owners confirmed willing and able to attend
- [ ] Confidentiality acknowledgments signed
- [ ] Backup/alternate owners identified (in case of last-minute withdrawal)
- [ ] Meeting logistics set (date, location, quorum process)
- [ ] Powers of attorney for absent owners drafted where needed
- [ ] HOA counsel confirms threshold + procedure satisfied
