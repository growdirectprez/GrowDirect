# PV Surf Camp

## Summary
The local surf camp on the peninsula. Weekly sessions for ages 6–14 at Paseo Del Mar in PVE, run by Leith Emery — a veteran school teacher who grew up surfing the same beach. 4:1 instructor-to-student ratio.

## Details

- **Address:** 300 Paseo Del Mar, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (310) 908-8164
- **Website:** pvsurfcamp.com
- **Ages:** 6–14
- **Season:** Weekly sessions, June 9 – August 15
- **Schedule:** Mon–Fri, 8:30am–12:30pm
- **Extended hours:** Available until 2pm for $25/day
- **Cost:** $389/week ($50 sibling discount)
- **Instructor ratio:** 4:1 students to instructors (never more)
- **Run by:** Leith Emery — local veteran school teacher and father, grew up learning to surf on the very same beach where the camp runs
- **Operating:** 15+ years
- **What they do:** Surfing, ocean safety, variety of ocean sports. Staff's first priority is incorporating ocean safety while teaching participants in a fun environment
- **Vibe:** The default surf camp for Hill families. Not a chain — this is a local guy teaching kids in his own backyard
