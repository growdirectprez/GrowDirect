# LLC Rescue Status (Existing California LLC)

The commercial R&D hat in the three-entity structure maps to an **existing California LLC that needs rescuing** — not a new entity. This card tracks what the LLC is, what's lapsed, and what must be done to bring it back into good standing so it can receive revenue, pay the founder, and license to the Foundation or other HOAs on arm's-length terms.

## Why This Matters

Three things depend on a good-standing LLC:

1. **Commercial revenue** — Canary, governance-engine, Angel, and other products can only be licensed through a legally operative entity
2. **Founder compensation** — paying yourself through the LLC requires the LLC to be in good standing
3. **Arm's-length licensing to the Foundation** — if the Foundation ever licenses software from the LLC, the LLC must be a legitimate counterparty (not dissolved, not suspended)

The Foundation formation can proceed on its own timeline, but if the LLC isn't restored, the three-hat compensation model breaks for the LLC side. Canary R&D hours + Cove app-code hours then have nowhere clean to land.

## What to Verify (Rescue Checklist)

### Entity Status

- [ ] **Entity name** — exact legal name as filed with California Secretary of State
- [ ] **Entity number** — CA SOS file number
- [ ] **Good-standing status** — active, suspended (FTB or SOS), or dissolved
- [ ] **FTB status** — up to date on Form 568, tax payments, and any minimum franchise tax ($800/year)
- [ ] **SOS status** — Statement of Information filed on schedule (biennial for LLCs)
- [ ] **EIN status** — active, tied to correct entity name

### Members and Management

- [ ] **Member(s)** — who owns the LLC (single-member vs. multi-member)
- [ ] **Operating Agreement** — exists? up to date? matches current intent?
- [ ] **Manager structure** — member-managed or manager-managed
- [ ] **Registered agent** — current and in CA

### Operating Posture

- [ ] **Business purpose** — as registered; may need amendment if current activities are broader (software R&D, licensing, consulting)
- [ ] **Banking** — business checking account tied to LLC name + EIN, active
- [ ] **Books** — accurate, separate from personal and Foundation
- [ ] **Insurance** — general liability, E&O if licensing software

### Tax and Compliance

- [ ] **Annual franchise tax** paid for current year ($800 minimum)
- [ ] **Past-due franchise tax** — any years missed; back-taxes owed
- [ ] **Penalties** — any FTB penalties for late filing
- [ ] **Sales tax / CDTFA** — if applicable to the business model (likely not for software licensing)

## Likely Rescue Actions

Depending on what the verification turns up:

**If suspended by FTB** (common cause: missed franchise tax):
- Pay all back-taxes, penalties, and current-year tax
- File FTB Form 3557 (Application for Certificate of Revivor)
- Wait for certificate; LLC returns to good standing

**If suspended by SOS** (cause: Statement of Information overdue):
- File current Statement of Information
- Pay penalty
- Suspension lifts

**If dissolved** (more serious):
- May need to re-form the LLC entirely
- Or file Certificate of Revivor if within reinstatement window

**If active but operating agreement is outdated**:
- Draft updated Operating Agreement covering:
  - Current business purpose (software R&D + licensing + consulting)
  - Member structure
  - Management decisions
  - Distributions (how founder pays self)
  - Licensing authority (can the LLC license software to the Foundation or other HOAs?)
  - Arm's-length transaction provisions (for Foundation licensing)

## Wyoming vs. California for the LLC

The advisor's position: Wyoming was offered as an option for a new commercial LLC, but **use the existing CA LLC** if it can be restored. Reasons:

- Already exists — avoids new formation cost and time
- Already tied to California (which is where operations happen)
- Foreign-qualification of a Wyoming LLC in California costs more than rescuing
- Existing LLC may have useful banking, EIN, and IP history to preserve

If the CA LLC is beyond rescue (dissolved + outside reinstatement window), reconsider. Forming fresh in California would still beat Wyoming given CA operating footprint.

## Arm's-Length Licensing Readiness (To the Foundation)

Once the LLC is restored, the following should be true before any license from LLC to Foundation:

- LLC has a current Operating Agreement authorizing software licensing
- LLC maintains separate books from the Foundation (no overlap, no commingling)
- License terms are in writing, compared to market rates, approved by disinterested Foundation directors (founder recused)
- License agreement filed as exhibit to Foundation board minutes
- Annual review by the Foundation board

See [[foundation-three-hat-compensation]] arm's-length licensing section.
