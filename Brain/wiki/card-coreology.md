# Coreology — Palos Verdes

## Summary
Boutique fitness studio in Rolling Hills Estates specializing in pilates-inspired XFormer reformer classes, sculpt, treadmill intervals, and indoor cycling. Part of the Deep Valley Dr fitness corridor alongside Equinox.

## Details

- **Address:** 704 Deep Valley Dr, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Website:** coreologyfitness.com
- **What it is:** Boutique studio — pilates-inspired XFormer® reformer-based classes, sculpt, treadmill interval (tread), indoor cycling
- **Format:** Small group classes, musically driven, trained instructors for personalized attention
- **Signature workout:** XFormer® combines pilates principles and strength training to work the full body
- **Class types:** Reformer, sculpt, tread (treadmill intervals), cycle (indoor cycling)
- **Vibe:** High-energy boutique — the kind of studio where you book your spot and bring your grip socks
- **Context:** Part of the Deep Valley Dr fitness corridor in RHE — Equinox is at 550 Deep Valley, Coreology at 704, Club Pilates nearby. This stretch is where Hill residents go to work out
