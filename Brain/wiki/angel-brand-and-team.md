### Pricing Strategy (from Listing Presentation)
8-step pricing process:
1. Identify key attributes
2. Develop buyer profiles
3. Analyze the market
4. Build out marketing plan (active buyers, ready-to-move buyers, didn't-know-they-were-looking buyers)
5. Prepare property for photography (design review by Marina, Compass Concierge, calendar optimum date)
6. Tell the story (day/twilight photography, video content)
7. Celebrate the opportunity (pre-marketing: Private Exclusive, pocket listing networking, brokers open, premiere public open house)
8. Execute on plan

### Pricing Psychology
- Peak buyer activity in first few weeks on market (activity curve peaks at week 2-3)
- Price at market value → 95% of buyers see it
- 5% over → 50% of buyers
- 10% over → 30% of buyers
- 15% over → only 20% of buyers
- Competitive pricing generates most activity and offers early

## How This Feeds Angel

### Listing Presentation → Angel Data
AREA's 9-phase marketing timeline is powerful but generic — every listing gets the same sequence. Angel makes it **data-specific**:
- Phase 1 (First Consult): Angel provides APN-level property history, neighborhood comps, owner equity estimate
- Phase 3 (Obtain Assets): Angel generates community lifestyle content from the wiki
- Phase 4 (Pre-Listing): Angel identifies active buyers in the area from listing search data
- Phase 7 (Open House): Angel's iPad app captures leads with neighborhood intelligence
- Phase 8 (Digital Campaign): Angel's weekly content feeds the social media and retargeting pipeline

### Buyer's Guide → Angel Chatbot
The buyer's guide content maps directly to Angel Agent conversation topics:
- Neighborhood guides → `neighborhood_profile` tool
- School information → `school_info` tool
- Market analysis → `market_stats` tool
- Property search → `listing_search` tool
- Pricing strategy → `cma_summary` tool

### Team Structure → Angel Product Scope
Angel serves Angelique individually but benefits the whole AREA team:
- Alexa (marketing) can use Angel's content engine for social media and email campaigns
- Amy (operations) can use Angel's CRM pipeline for transaction tracking
- Marina (design) can reference Angel's neighborhood data for staging and positioning
