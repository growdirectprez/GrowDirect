# Kids Water Activities — Palos Verdes Peninsula & South Bay

Junior Lifeguards is the defining summer activity for Hill kids. Surf camps, private swim coaching, and year-round swim programs complete the picture.

---

## LA County Junior Lifeguards

The program that every peninsula family knows about. Run by LA County Fire Department Lifeguard Division.

### Program Structure
- **Ages:** 9–17
- **Season:** 6-week summer program (June–August)
- **Schedule:** Daily, 4 days/week (Mon–Thu), 9am–3pm
- **Beach stations:** Torrance Beach is the main South Bay/PV station
- **Cost:** ~$685 per child (includes uniform t-shirt and swimsuit)
- **Registration:** Opens February for returning JGs; new applicants must pass swim test
- **2026 registration:** Returning JGs Feb 2–Mar 9; swim test sign-ups open Feb 10 at 8am online

### Swim Test Requirements
Must pass a 100-yard swim test:
- **Ages 9–11 ("C" Group):** 1:50.00
- **Ages 12–13 ("B" Group):** 1:40.00
- **Ages 14–17 ("A" Group):** 1:30.00

### 2026 Swim Test Dates
- Saturday March 14 — George Mannon Aquatic Center
- Sunday March 22 — Loyola Marymount University
- Sunday March 29 — Palisades Charter High School
- Sunday April 12 — Greater Whittier Regional Aquatic Center

### Getting a Coach to Train for the Test
This is real — many kids need swim coaching before they can pass. The test is competitive and nerves matter. Options:

- **Coach Ken Taylor at PVBAC:** The go-to JG prep instructor on the Hill. On staff at PV Beach & Athletic Club since 1996. Runs three classes/day, 25 kids/class, through spring. "Almost all the kids pass who complete his classes." (Source: Easy Reader / Peninsula Magazine) — see [[Brain/wiki/card-coach-ken-taylor|Card — Coach Ken Taylor]]
- **South End Racquet & Health Club — ZAP Swimming:** Professional coaching specifically for JG test prep
- **Swimphi:** Private lessons tailored to JG test — swimming techniques and endurance to meet qualifying times
- **South Bay Swim Team:** Offers JG prep instruction
- **Waterworks Swim School (Torrance):** Junior Lifeguard Prep courses
- **LA County free JG Ready Training:** Free swim technique sessions with Lifeguard Staff before tryouts

### What Kids Actually Do
Daily activities include ocean swimming, body surfing, paddleboard, surf rescue techniques, first aid, beach runs, ocean safety education, and physical conditioning. The program builds teamwork, water competence, and an identity as an ocean kid.

### The Culture
JG is a rite of passage. Kids who do it come back every summer. It's the connective tissue between Hill families at the beach — kids from every neighborhood mix at the same station. Parents organize around the schedule. Returning JGs mentor younger kids. It's less "camp" and more "the thing you do."

## Surf Camps & Lessons

### PV Surf Camp
See: [[Brain/wiki/card-pv-surf-camp|Card — PV Surf Camp]]

- **Address:** 300 Paseo Del Mar, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (310) 908-8164
- **Ages:** 6–14
- **Season:** Weekly sessions, June 9 – August 15
- **Schedule:** Mon–Fri, 8:30am–12:30pm (extended to 2pm for $25/day)
- **Cost:** $389/week ($50 sibling discount)
- **Ratio:** 4:1 students to instructors
- **Run by:** Leith Emery — local veteran school teacher, grew up surfing the same beach
- **Sources:** PV Surf Camp website, Yelp, ActiveKids

### Other Surf Options
- **Redondo Surf Club:** Private coaching in South Bay (Redondo, Manhattan, PV). Max 4 students
- **Surf With Joey:** Private surf and skate coaching in PV & Malibu. Gentle approach, young kids (ages 5+)
- **Dive N' Surf:** Private lessons ages 4–84, all year, 7am–7pm daily, equipment included
- **Torrance Beach:** The go-to lesson spot — broad takeoff zone, consistent whitewater, low-risk conditions

- **Waterworks Swim School (Torrance):** Ages 2.5+, private and semi-private, also offers JG Prep courses
