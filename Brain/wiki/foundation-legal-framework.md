# Foundation Legal Framework — MOC

This is the map-of-content for the Abalone Cove Foundation (California 501(c)(3), forming) legal and governance framework. The Foundation operates within a five-entity landscape; see [[wpbca-compliance-framework]] for the Community of Abalone Cove (formerly WPBCA) operational side.

All cards here are distilled working positions from 2026-04-19 advisor memos. Nothing is legal advice until separate 501(c)(3) counsel reviews.

## Naming note

Filenames under `Brain/wiki/wpbca-*.md` are retained for continuity. Content uses the current legal name **Community of Abalone Cove** for all forward-looking references. **WPBCA** is reserved for historical references (1949 founding, 2009 restated declaration, 2012 Bylaws drafting, prior-board history, legacy counsel). The rename was the previous board's last action, filed with CA SOS with quorum on the governing vote, driven by the Portuguese Bend Abatement District forming and the desire to disassociate the community's HOA name from the landslide connotation.

## Core Three-Hat Compensation Structure

The compensation allocation model covers **three** entities (the three hats the founder wears). These are the entities that determine where compensation flows:

| Entity | Type | Role | Can Pay the Founder? |
|--------|------|------|----------------------|
| **Abalone Cove Foundation** | California 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation | Research, Library of Evidence, editorial sites (cove.org, abalonecove.org), inter-HOA research/convening partnership, neutral procedural support | Yes, reasonable compensation for Foundation-specific work, after formal board adoption |
| **Community of Abalone Cove** (formerly WPBCA) | California Nonprofit Mutual Benefit Corporation (HOA) | Governance of Tract 14649 under 2009 Declaration + 2012 Bylaws + Davis-Stirling | Volunteer board; formal director fees only if adopted under bylaws |
| **LLC (existing CA LLC, needs rescuing)** | California Limited Liability Company | Commercial software R&D and licensing (Canary, governance engine, Angel, ownpv, etc.) | Yes, per the LLC's own operating terms |

## Adjacent Entities (Not in the Compensation Model, but in the Landscape)

Two additional entities exist in the operating landscape and appear in the spec's corporate-separation table. They are not part of the three-hat compensation model — the founder is not compensated by any of them — but they shape the Foundation's working context:

| Entity | Type | Role | Foundation Relationship |
|--------|------|------|------------------------|
| **Rancho Palos Verdes Council of Homeowners Associations (CHOA)** | Existing inter-HOA coordination forum (peninsula HOA presidents / board representatives) | Peer-to-peer coordination among peninsula HOAs; Community of Abalone Cove board already participates | Potential future audience, partner, and commercial-software sales target. **Not** the Foundation's primary charitable-activity channel. Inter-HOA convening may route through CHOA where appropriate; Foundation supplies research support on request. |
| **Peer HOAs** (PV Bay Club, Portuguese Bend Beach Club, Portuguese Bend Community Association, Seaview Tract, Klondike Canyon area associations, and others) | Each an existing California Nonprofit Mutual Benefit Corp or similar | Governance of their respective member communities | Separate entities with their own governance; the Foundation may publish research relevant to shared issues and convene where requested |

If the §V §5 reactivation path is ever triggered, a sixth entity — the reactivated Community Association of Palos Verdes — enters the picture. That is deferred; see [[foundation-blockchain-proof-layer]] for the proof-of-record design and the original spec for the federation architecture.
