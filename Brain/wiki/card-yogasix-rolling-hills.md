# YogaSix Rolling Hills

## Summary

Boutique yoga franchise on the Rolling Hills Road corridor in Torrance — six class formats from restorative to hot power, open seven days. Part of the Xponential Fitness family. Technically Torrance but serves the Hill community and sits in the same commercial strip as other peninsula-adjacent fitness options.

## Details

- **Address:** 2919 Rolling Hills Rd, Unit 14, Torrance, CA 90505
- **Phone:** (424) 265-5115
- **Website:** yogasix.com/location/rolling-hills
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 6am–7:45pm, Fri 6am–6:30pm, Sat 7:30am–12:15pm, Sun 7:30am–1:30pm
- **Classes:** Y6 101, Y6 Restore, Y6 Slow Flow, Y6 Hot, Y6 Power, Y6 Sculpt & Flow
- **Membership:** 4x/month, 8x/month, unlimited, or drop-in
- **Neighborhood:** Rolling Hills Road corridor (Torrance)

## Highlight Angle

Six distinct class types under one roof — from true restorative to heated power flows. The Rolling Hills Rd location puts it minutes from the Hill, and the structured membership tiers make it easy to scale up or down.
