# Trustee Slate — Preparation and Vetting

The three Trustees elected under Declaration 100 Article II §5 inherit the **full Article II §4 authority** — 26 enumerated powers including enforcement, assessment liens, Art Jury interpretation, and the authority to record the Restated Declaration. The slate must be pre-vetted, fully briefed, and committed to immediate action on the day of the meeting.

**Not legal advice.** Distilled from a 2026-04-19 external-advisor memo. HOA counsel review required before any Trustee candidate is approached.

## What the Trustees Actually Do

The advisor memo captures the demand directly:

> "The Trustees need to be ready and willing to take the fight."

On the day of the meeting, the elected Trustees:

1. Accept election by majority vote of the 15+ owners
2. Immediately adopt the pre-drafted Restated Declaration
3. Sign and notarize on the spot
4. Authorize same-day (or next-business-day) recording at the County Recorder

Within the first week after recording:

1. Issue demand letters to the City, any pending Housing Element applicants, and Coastal Commission staff
2. Activate ARC preemptive reviews on pending architectural applications
3. Coordinate with ACLAD on the public-private partnership signal (see [[coac-aclad-partnership]])
4. Support the 501(c)(4) advocacy arm's public launch of the 2026 Civic Problems memorandum (see [[coac-2026-civic-problems-memorandum]], [[501c-advocacy-arm]])
5. Maintain Trustee minutes and a contemporaneous record of post-recording enforcement activity

Ongoing (weeks + months):

1. Respond to any declaratory-judgment or injunctive-relief actions filed by the City or developers
2. Coordinate with the Community of Abalone Cove Board (the 2012 Bylaws HOA governing Tract 14649) on shared enforcement interests
3. Exercise Article II §4 powers as specific issues arise (assessments, Art Jury decisions, enforcement votes)
4. Prepare for potential expansion of participating owners via Article V §5 voluntary annexation

## Selection Criteria

### Required

- **Willing** to accept election and act immediately on the day of the meeting
- **Able** to attend the meeting in person (or via counsel-approved electronic participation if the bylaws permit)
- **Committed** to the post-recording enforcement role ("take the fight")
- **Qualified** — typically an owner of a parcel inside the burdened land, or a resident in good standing of the broader Abalone Cove / Portuguese Bend community
- **Confidential** — able to hold the blitz timing confidential through Week -1

### Strongly Preferred

- **Legal, title, planning, or civic-engagement background** — at least one Trustee with directly-relevant expertise enhances the slate's credibility and capability
- **Resident commitment** — physical presence in the community (e.g., Seacove Drive, Barkentine Road addresses per the CoAC operational slate at [[wpbca-compliance-framework]])
- **Diverse professional backgrounds** — among the three, cover legal/compliance, geologic/planning, and community-organizing capacities
- **Not active in current litigation adverse to the City or developers** — Trustees should not be perceived as pre-committed litigants; they are newly elected governing-body members
- **Clean conflict-of-interest posture** — no financial interest in projects being opposed (e.g., not affiliated with the 0 Clipper applicant or with YIMBY-aligned groups)

### Disqualifying

- Unwillingness to sign the Restated Declaration on the day of the meeting
- Inability to maintain confidentiality through Week -1
- Current City Council, Planning Commission, or Coastal Commission role (conflict with the governance action)
- Financial distress or ongoing personal litigation that would compromise the Trustees' standing
- Known alignment with developers or YIMBY organizations (adversarial to the reactivation's protective purpose)

## Vetting Process

### Week -4 to Week -3 — Identification

Private conversations with 5–7 potential candidates. Door-to-door + phone + in-person meetings only. No platform messaging until a confidentiality acknowledgment is signed.

Candidate sources:
- CoAC Board operational slate (2BarkentineRd@abalonecove.org as President, 25seacove@abalonecove.org as Director, 11barkentine@abalonecove.org as ARC Chair — see [[wpbca-compliance-framework]]). Note: CoAC Board members may or may not serve as Article II §5 Trustees. The two roles are formally distinct; overlap is allowed but not required.
- Existing relationships among the 15-owner network (see [[coac-15-owner-network]])
- Peninsula residents with relevant professional backgrounds (attorneys, planners, geologists, civic leaders)
- Community of Abalone Cove members who have previously demonstrated protective instinct in neighborhood matters

### Week -3 to Week -2 — Briefing

Each candidate receives:

1. **Confidentiality acknowledgment** (same format as the 15-owner network)
2. **The Playbook summary** — this MOC + the mechanism card + the blitz plan
3. **The pre-drafted Restated Declaration** — complete text they'll be asked to sign
4. **Evidentiary packet** — 1939 booklet; 1949–1950 instruments; 1980 Wong map; 1978 Coastal Specific Plan; USGS 1946; Ehlig 1982; LGC 2011; 2009 WPBCA restatement precedent; Nahrstedt / Citizens for Covenant Compliance opinions
5. **Counsel's opinion letter** — HOA counsel's written analysis of the Article II §5 procedure, the Restated Declaration, and the defensibility of the reactivation
6. **Expected post-recording duties** — demand-letter templates, ARC review expectations, potential litigation defense posture

Candidate reviews materials. Follow-up conversations answer questions. No decision required yet.

### Week -2 to Week -1 — Commitment

Three Trustees confirmed. Each signs:

1. **Acceptance of nomination** — formal acknowledgment of willingness to be elected
2. **Post-election action commitment** — acknowledgment that, if elected, the Trustee will immediately adopt and sign the Restated Declaration and accept the associated enforcement duties
3. **Confidentiality through Day 0**

Alternates identified in case of last-minute withdrawal (at least one alternate per Trustee seat).

### Week -1 — Final Rehearsal

Dry-run of the meeting:
- Quorum verification of the 15+ owners
- Nomination and election procedure
- Acceptance speeches (brief, factual)
- Adoption of the Restated Declaration
- Signing and notarization logistics

- Conflict-of-interest disclosure
- Confidentiality acknowledgment

- Post-election action commitment

## Meeting-Day Logistics

On the day of the meeting (Day +1 to Day +7 of the blitz):

### Before the Vote

- HOA counsel present (or on standby via phone)
- Notary present for signing
- Recording courier on standby for immediate delivery to the County Recorder
- Quorum verification — 15+ qualifying owners present (or represented by pre-signed powers of attorney where permitted)

### The Vote

- Nominations called
- Three Trustees elected by majority vote
- Acceptances recorded in the minutes

### Immediately After the Vote

- Trustees formally convene as the Association's governing body
- Adopt the Restated Declaration by Trustee vote (per the modernized Article V)
- Sign and notarize
- Recording courier departs for the County Recorder

### Same Day or Next Business Day

- Restated Declaration recorded and stamped
- Post-recording press statement released (neutral factual register — see [[coac-restated-declaration-blitz-plan]])
- `dev.abalonecove.org` updated with the recorded instrument

## Post-Recording Trustee Duties (First 30 Days)

- **Demand letters** to City, any pending applicants, Coastal Commission (within 7 days)
- **ARC preemptive reviews** activated (within 14 days)
- **First Trustee meeting** — adopt operating procedures, confirm counsel retention, set regular meeting cadence (within 21 days)
- **501(c)(4) launch coordination** — support the public 2026 Civic Problems memorandum campaign (within 30 days)
- **ACLAD formal signal** — agree informal partnership language or initiate MOU discussion (within 30 days)

## Risks Specific to the Trustee Slate

### Risk: A Trustee withdraws at the last minute

**Mitigation:** Alternates identified and briefed. The meeting can proceed with substitute nominees if needed; the 15 owners' election authority is intact.

### Risk: A Trustee leaks timing

**Mitigation:** Confidentiality acknowledgments signed. Limited distribution of briefing materials. No platform messaging until Week -1.

### Risk: A Trustee refuses to sign on the day of the meeting

**Mitigation:** Pre-signed acceptance and post-election action commitments form the baseline. If a Trustee refuses to sign despite commitment, alternates step in; the meeting can be continued to the next day with the alternate seated.

### Risk: Post-recording litigation targets a specific Trustee personally

**Mitigation:** Trustees acting in their official capacity are protected by the standard HOA/nonprofit director-and-officer liability framework. HOA counsel advises on D&O insurance for the Association. Personal liability is remote for duly-elected Trustees acting within their Article II §4 authority.

### Risk: A Trustee's post-recording enforcement effort weakens ("doesn't take the fight")

**Mitigation:** Pre-selection criteria emphasize commitment. The 501(c)(4) advocacy arm and CoAC Board can provide complementary enforcement pressure. The Article II §5 framework allows for re-election or replacement of Trustees if needed under the Restated Declaration's modernized Article V.
