# Ice Cream & Frozen Treats — Palos Verdes Peninsula & South Bay

Three new shops opened on the Hill in 2024–2025, filling a long-standing gap. Handel's anchors the broader South Bay with locations in San Pedro and Redondo Beach.

---

## On the Hill

### Lunada Creamery
- **Address:** 711 Yarmouth Rd, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (424) 241-0620
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 12–8pm, Fri 12–9pm
- **What they serve:** Soft serve with weekly rotating flavors, one in-house made flavor each week (e.g., all-natural peanut butter), always at least one vegan option, homemade cookie sandwiches, take-home pints
- **Toppings:** Fresh fruit, vegan cookie dough from a local baker, apple pie filling, cinnamon compote
- **Founded by:** Julie and Jason Anthony — moved to PV nine years ago, spent 15 months developing the concept
- **Space:** 380 sq ft in Lunada Bay Plaza (former Bank of America ATM space)
- **Design:** Minimalist décor, woven pendant lamps, wooden benches, quartz counter
- **Sources:** OurSouthBay ("Two New Soft Serve Outposts"), Yelp, Lunada Creamery website

### Neptune's Frozen Treats
- **Address:** 55 Malaga Cove Plaza, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (424) 365-9575
- **Hours:** Sun–Thu 12–8pm, Fri–Sat 12–9pm
- **What they serve:** Frozen yogurt, custard, gelato, and sorbet with rotating flavors — cookies n' cream, espresso, non-dairy oat milk vanilla, vegan mango sorbet
- **Toppings:** Cheesecake bites, fresh blackberries, toasted coconut flakes, chocolate wafer straws
- **Founded by:** Katherine Kemps and her mother Gemma — Katherine is a 2024 SMU grad and PV native. Great-great-grandfather founded Beverly Dairy (LA) and Kemps Ice Cream (Midwest)
- **Tagline:** "Seas the Swirl. Savor the Flavor."
- **Space:** Former RE/MAX office in Malaga Cove Plaza
- **Design:** White subway tile, coastal hues, natural wood, subtle metallics, mosaic tile Neptune logo wall by Jonathan Cohen
- **Sources:** OurSouthBay ("Two New Soft Serve Outposts"), PV Pulse, Yelp, Neptune's website

### Golden Scoop Gelato
- **Address:** 31228 Palos Verdes Dr W, Rancho Palos Verdes 90275 (Golden Cove Center, corner of Hawthorne)
- **Phone:** (424) 327-2888
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 12–9pm, Sat–Sun 11–9pm
- **What they serve:** 24 flavors of premium small-batch all-natural gelato, affogato, soft serve, frozen yogurt, croffles. Flavors include Earl Grey, pistachio, salted caramel
- **Founded by:** Nathan Kang — wanted to bring a gelato shop to RPV because of the Mediterranean climate
- **Ingredients:** Real California Milk, no eggs, powders, or artificial ingredients. Everything made in small batches from natural, local ingredients
- **Sources:** PV Pulse (Lynne La Fleur article), Yelp, Golden Scoop website

## South Bay — San Pedro & Redondo Beach

### Handel's Homemade Ice Cream — San Pedro
- **Address:** 970 N Western Ave, San Pedro
- **Hours:** Daily 12–9:30pm
- **What they serve:** 45+ flavors made fresh daily on premises. Homemade recipes since 1945
- **Known for:** $2.50 scoop Tuesdays. Named one of Ten Best Ice Creams by National Geographic
- **Sources:** Handel's website, Yelp, Accardo Real Estate blog

### Handel's Homemade Ice Cream — Redondo Beach
- **Address:** 1882 Pacific Coast Highway, Redondo Beach
- **Phone:** (424) 247-8861
- **Hours:** Daily 11am–10pm
- **What they serve:** Same — 45+ daily flavors, cones, sundaes, shakes, pops
- **South Bay presence since:** 2010
- **Sources:** Handel's website, Yelp, Accardo Real Estate blog
