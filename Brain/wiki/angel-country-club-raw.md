## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Surrounds Los Verdes Golf Course on the western slopes of the Peninsula
- Built primarily 1960s-70s in ranch architectural style
- Non-gated, no formal HOA (individual condo communities may have their own)
- Golf course and ocean views from many properties
- Close to both Golden Cove and Peninsula Center shopping

### Sub-Communities
Monaco, Monte Verde, La Cresta, Palos Verdes Panorama, Peppertree Lane, Seagate, Alta Vista, Island View (condos)

### Housing Stock
- SFR: Oversized lots, many 3,000-5,000 sqft. Range: $1.3M-$2.2M
- Condos: Seagate, Island View communities. Range: $350K-$500K
- Mix of single-family and condo product

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 31

- **Median days on market:** 20
- **Price per square foot:** $759

- Larger homes/lots drive higher median but lower PPSF than compact neighbors

## 6. Lifestyle

### Golf
Los Verdes Golf Course is the neighborhood's centerpiece — 18-hole par-71 public course with ocean views. One of the busiest in the nation. 5,000 sqft clubhouse hosts weddings/events (up to 350 guests).

### Ryan Park
Robert E. Ryan Community Park (30359 Hawthorne Blvd) — RPV's original park (1973). Pirate ship playground, tot lot, baseball diamond, basketball courts, BBQ picnic areas, community building. Superior ocean views from elevated position. Open daily 9 AM–5:45 PM.

### Hiking
Portuguese Bend Reserve (424 acres), Point Vicente Beach Walk, White Point Nature Preserve. 48 trails in RPV area.

### Nearby
- Point Vicente Interpretive Center — whale watching
- Abalone Cove Shoreline Park — tide pools
- Wayfarers Chapel — Lloyd Wright glass chapel
- Terranea Resort — spa, dining, kayaking, coastal recreation

Source: Los Verdes GC, Ryan Park, AllTrails, RPV Nature Preserve
