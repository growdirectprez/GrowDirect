# Neptune's Frozen Treats

## Summary
Frozen yogurt, custard, gelato, and sorbet shop in Malaga Cove Plaza, PVE. Founded by Katherine Kemps — a PV native whose family has been in the ice cream business since 1914.

## Details

- **Address:** 55 Malaga Cove Plaza, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (424) 365-9575
- **Hours:** Sun–Thu 12–8pm, Fri–Sat 12–9pm
- **Founded by:** Katherine Kemps and her mother Gemma. Katherine is a 2024 SMU grad and PV native
- **Family legacy:** Great-great-grandfather founded Beverly Dairy (LA) and Kemps Ice Cream (Midwest, est. 1914 in Minneapolis)
- **Menu:** Frozen yogurt, custard, gelato, and sorbet with rotating flavors — cookies n' cream, espresso, non-dairy oat milk vanilla, vegan mango sorbet
- **Toppings:** Cheesecake bites, fresh blackberries, toasted coconut flakes, chocolate wafer straws
- **Tagline:** "Seas the Swirl. Savor the Flavor."
- **Space:** Former RE/MAX office in Malaga Cove Plaza
- **Design:** White subway tile, coastal hues, natural wood, subtle metallics, mosaic tile Neptune logo wall by Jonathan Cohen
- **Brand voice:** Neptune (Roman god of the sea) — wise, witty, charismatic
