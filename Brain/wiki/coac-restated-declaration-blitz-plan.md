# Restated Declaration — Blitz Execution Plan

The Declaration 100 Article II §5 reactivation must be executed as a **blitz**: quiet preparation during Phases 1–2, then a compressed notice → meeting → election → recording sequence that minimizes the window for City, developer, or YIMBY preemptive response. This card documents the timeline, the pre-draft requirements, and the post-recording enforcement launch.

**Not legal advice.** Distilled from a 2026-04-19 external-advisor memo. HOA counsel review required before any notice is published.

## Why a Blitz

The self-executing reactivation works entirely in the **private recorded-title layer**. No City approval, no Coastal Commission pre-review, no public hearing required before recording. Once the Restated Declaration is stamped at the County Recorder, any later challenger faces a duly noticed, self-executing, recorded instrument that courts treat as presumptively valid on its face.

The advisor captured the user's framing directly:

> "If this works it has to be a blitz that catches everyone off guard and then the cart goes sorry looks valid."

The blitz design:

- **Minimizes the preemption window.** The City cannot moot the mechanism by scheduling a Council vote or passing an urgency ordinance between notice and recording.
- **Avoids YIMBY mobilization.** California YIMBY, LA YIMBY, South Bay YIMBY, and YIMBY Law have established patterns of pressure (demand letters, threatened litigation, public campaigns). A blitz denies them the weeks of runway they normally use.
- **Denies developers vesting leverage.** A pending or filed Restated Declaration forecloses arguments that the old covenants are abandoned by non-enforcement.
- **Respects the Vanderlip design.** The mechanism was drafted as dormant and obscure for exactly this kind of decisive use.

## Blitz Timeline

### Week -4 to Week -1 — Quiet Lock-In

No public notice. No communication with the City. Nothing on `cove.org` or `abalonecove.org`. All coordination in private sections of `dev.abalonecove.org` + door-to-door.

**Deliverables ready by Day 0:**

1. **Final 15-owner list** confirmed, with each parcel verified to sit inside the master Lot H boundary after negative-space exclusions ([[coac-negative-space-lot-h-boundary]])
2. **Three Trustee candidates** vetted, briefed, and committed to accept election and sign on the spot ([[coac-trustee-slate-preparation]])
3. **Pre-drafted Restated Declaration** reviewed by HOA counsel, including:
   - Boundary Recital (master metes-and-bounds + all negative-space exclusions + Exhibit A plat overlay)
   - Article V modernization (one-parcel/one-vote; see [[coac-article-v-equity-modernization]])
   - Surviving Declaration 100/101 covenants (single-family use, density caps, drainage, setbacks, Art Jury review)
   - Creek/fault-line avoidance-corridor recital
   - ACLAD partnership recital
   - Contemporary references (1980 Wong subdivision, 1978 Coastal Specific Plan Subregion 4, 1946 USGS, 1982 Ehlig, 2011 LGC Valley)
   - Express strike of racial covenants (per Shelley v. Kraemer and the 2009 WPBCA restatement precedent)
   - Reversionary-interest recital
4. **Notice text** drafted and legally reviewed — 3× LA daily newspaper + 1× San Pedro paper format
5. **Meeting script** including Trustee election resolution
6. **Demand-letter templates** for post-recording enforcement (addressed to City, any pending applicants, Coastal Commission staff)
7. **Post-recording press statement** for the 501(c)(4) advocacy arm (neutral factual register — see [[coac-2026-civic-problems-memorandum]])
8. **Confidentiality acknowledgments** signed by all network members
9. **ACLAD coordination signal** — informal alignment or formal MOU pending (see [[coac-aclad-partnership]])

### Day 0 — Trigger

Publish the notice. Reciting the >90-day failure to meet/act (factually true; the Community Association of Palos Verdes has been dormant for decades):

- 3 publications in a Los Angeles daily newspaper
- 1 publication in a San Pedro newspaper

**No advance courtesy copy to the City.** The user's position on this is explicit: "we would not need to remind the city that these documents exist if they were being true to the stewardship they say they want to protect." The title record speaks for itself.

### Day +1 to Day +7 — Meeting, Election, Recording

Hold the meeting of owners as soon as the minimum statutory notice window permits. The 15+ aligned owners attend and vote.

1. Confirm quorum and boundary-qualification of each voter
2. Elect the three pre-vetted Trustees by majority vote
3. Trustees accept election
4. Trustees immediately adopt the Restated Declaration
5. Trustees sign and notarize on the spot
6. Recording courier delivers to the County Recorder same day (or next business day)
7. County Recorder stamps the instrument — **"cart goes sorry looks valid" moment**

The Restated Declaration is now in the direct chain of title. Any subsequent title search surfaces it automatically.

### Day +8 to Day +30 — Enforcement Launch

With the Restated Declaration recorded:

1. **Demand letters** from the Trustees to:
   - RPV City Planning (citing the revived covenants as equitable servitudes running with the land; noting that state housing mandates do not automatically preempt private recorded restrictions per Nahrstedt / Citizens for Covenant Compliance)
   - Any pending Housing Element applicants (0 Clipper Road 16-unit applicant; Silver Spur Canyon applicant if active; others)
   - California Coastal Commission staff (for sites in the Coastal Zone — Subregion 4)
2. **ARC preemptive reviews** — the Community of Abalone Cove ARC Chair (11barkentine@abalonecove.org) begins reviews on any pending architectural applications, citing both the 2009 Restated Declaration and the newly revived Declaration 100/101 covenants
3. **501(c)(4) educational launch** — viral 2026 Civic Problems memorandum campaign goes live on `dev.abalonecove.org` (or a separate advocacy domain) with the factual record, ACLAD partnership framing, and contrast to RM-22 precedents (Bay Club, Trump overlays). See [[coac-2026-civic-problems-memorandum]].
4. **Platform population** — interactive map on `dev.abalonecove.org` displays the negative-space boundary, the 15 reactivated parcels, ACLAD well locations, and Housing Element site overlays
5. **Door-to-door follow-up** — welcome the new escrow owner + extend outreach to additional parcels that might qualify for future inclusion under the Restated Declaration's Article V provisions

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: City files for declaratory judgment that Declarations 100/101 are unenforceable

**Response:** The Restated Declaration is prepared with a full evidentiary packet (1939 booklet; 1949 Declaration of Easements; 1950 Lot H Declaration; 1980 Wong map; Coastal Specific Plan; USGS 1946; Ehlig 1982; LGC 2011). Continuous reference in later deeds + the 1978 Coastal Specific Plan defeats abandonment arguments. See [[coac-declaration-100-article-ii-section-5]] risks section.

### Risk: Developer or YIMBY group challenges the 15-owner threshold or notice sufficiency

**Response:** Each of the 15 owners has been verified as holding record title inside the master boundary. Notice publishes exactly as the clause specifies. The meeting minutes and counsel's opinion letter form the first line of defense.

### Risk: One or more Trustees refuses to act or leaks timing

**Response:** Confidentiality acknowledgments signed at Week -4. Trustees pre-briefed and committed. Alternates identified in case of last-minute withdrawal. See [[coac-trustee-slate-preparation]].

### Risk: ACLAD objects publicly

**Response:** Same-side strategy. Informal coordination signal or MOU established before Day 0. ACLAD's mandate (public safety, slope stabilization) is structurally aligned with the private covenant overlay. See [[coac-aclad-partnership]].

### Risk: CoAC 2-of-5 Board fails to stabilize in parallel

**Response:** Phase 2 does not require CoAC Board quorum — the Article II §5 reactivation operates through the 15-owner trigger independently. Phase 1 stabilization is valuable but not blocking. If Phase 1 stalls, the blitz still proceeds on the Article II §5 timeline.

### Risk: Blitz timing leak via platform

**Response:** `dev.abalonecove.org` network-coordination sections use private access controls. Door-to-door outreach uses generic "community directory" framing until Week -1. Confidentiality acknowledgments cover network members.

## Post-Recording Narrative

The Restated Declaration is factual, not adversarial. The public framing post-recording stays neutral:

> "The Community of Abalone Cove and its Trustees, acting under the self-executing reactivation provision of Declaration 100 Article II §5 (recorded 1929), have restated the surviving private covenants to preserve single-family residential use, natural drainage protections, geologic avoidance corridors, and architectural-review oversight across the parcels within the original Lot H boundary. This complements the public safety mission of the Abalone Cove Landslide Abatement District and the historical stewardship principles documented in the 1939 Palos Verdes Estates Civic Problems booklet. The revived covenants run with the land and are enforceable alongside current municipal zoning and the California Coastal Specific Plan for Subregion 4."

No attacks on the City. No attacks on developers. No attacks on YIMBY. The record speaks. See [[coac-2026-civic-problems-memorandum]] for the viral campaign framing.
