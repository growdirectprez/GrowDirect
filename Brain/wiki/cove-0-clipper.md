# 0 Clipper Road

The immediate threat to the WPBCA community. A 1.6-acre vacant parcel proposed for 16 units of housing, rezoned from RS-4 to RM-22 in April 2024 as part of RPV's Housing Element compliance.

## The Property

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| APN | 7573-006-024 |
| Legal description | Lot 1, Tract 43725 |
| Size | 68,084 SF / 1.56 acres (but flood hazard zone reduces usable area to ~1.01 acres) |
| Zoning | RM-22 (rezoned April 2024 from RS-4) |
| Assessed value | $2,334,657 (2025) |
| Annual tax | $34,618.97 |

## Current Owner

**Clipper Development LLC** (Ali Vahdani), Vernon CA. Vahdani is a civil engineer and founder of Optimum Seismic (seismic retrofit firm). First-time housing developer. Political donor to RPV campaigns including former Mayor Cruikshank.

**Transaction history:**

| Date | Event | Amount |
|------|-------|--------|
| Dec 2005 | Wong Family Trust → Hartman Trust | $1,800,000 |
| Oct 2021 | Hartman Trust → Clipper Development LLC | $2,200,000 |
| Nov 2025 | Hankey Capital commercial loan | $2,100,000 |

Note: Thomas Hartman (prior owner) lives at 28 Sea Cove Dr — inside the WPBCA community. The Hankey Capital refinance at nearly double the original purchase loan suggests development financing.

## Proposed Development

14 townhomes + 2 cottages (16 units). Designated as Housing Element Site #16 — the only site in RPV's Coastal Zone.

**Critical issue:** The city calculated density on 1.56 acres. The assessor's own map shows the usable lot is ~1.01 acres (flood hazard zone). Actual density: ~16 units/acre, not the ~10 units/acre the entitlement implies. Nobody checked the county's records before rezoning.

**January 2025:** HCD confirmed the city can remove Site 16 and remain RHNA-compliant. The city does not need this site. But in June 2025, City Council voted to keep it anyway.

## Geological Hazards

The parcel sits within the Ancient Portuguese Bend Landslide boundary. The city's own Zone 2 Landslide EIR documents that residential plumbing systems were "likely catalysts" for slide movement. Groundwater is "the controlling factor." The Abalone Cove Landslide Abatement District operates 22 dewatering wells. Ground is currently moving 2.14 inches per week (March 2026). See [[Brain/wiki/cove-property-geology|Property & Geology]].

## Active Litigation

**Case:** Community of Abalone Cove v. City of Rancho Palos Verdes (24TRCP00352)
**Court:** LA Superior Court, Torrance Courthouse, Dept. B, Judge Douglas W. Stern
**Filed:** September 11, 2024

Four causes of action: General Plan violation, Coastal Act violation, Political Reform Act violation, common law conflict of interest.

**City's defense:** Demurrer arguing mootness (three identical ordinances — 678U, 680U, 681 — petition only challenges 681) and statute of limitations.

**Petitioners' response:** Supersession doctrine (681 replaced 680U replaced 678U; only 681 is operative). Specific statute § 65860 (90 days from zoning amendment) applies over general statute § 65009.

This litigation attacks the government process.

## CC&R Defense — Status Update (April 2026)

The CC&R strategy has been significantly weakened by closer examination of the source documents. See [[Brain/wiki/claim-lot-h-scope-correction|Lot H Scope Correction]] for the full analysis. In summary:

- The **1950 Lot H Declaration** contains only a voided racial covenant — no building restrictions
- **Declaration No. One** (1949) has the architectural controls but only covers Tract 14649 (the 81 WPBCA lots), not this parcel
- **Declarations 100 and 101** protect the Filiorum properties east of Tract 14649 in Abalone Cove (with the full Red Tile District / Art Jury protections under the Palos Verdes Community Association) — but do NOT apply to 0 Clipper
- The parcel's deed history has a **27-year gap** from PV Corp to Wong (1979). The fire station parcel map and various subdivisions of Parcel 106 over time are documented, but no deed restrictions for the 0 Clipper lot itself have been found

**The strongest remaining arguments are geological, environmental, and procedural** — not CC&R-based. The litigation (24TRCP00352) attacking the government's rezoning process, the landslide hazard evidence, the Coastal Act issues, and the FPPC complaint all stand independently.

## Counter-Proposal

The **Abalone Shore Community Campus** — an alternative public-benefit use:

1. **Modern Fire Station 53** — replace the adjacent 1960s wooden station. FEMA AFG grant eligible ($5-10M).
2. **PVPLC Welcome Center** — gateway to Portuguese Bend Reserve (399 acres). No current visitor facility.
3. **Annie's Heritage Garden** — preserves pre-1942 Japanese American farm genetics. Heritage tomato seedlings. Farm stand memorial.
