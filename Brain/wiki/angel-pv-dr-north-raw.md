## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Northern gateway to the PV Peninsula, running along PV Drive North through RPV and bordering Rolling Hills Estates
- Established single-family homes — predominantly 3-5 bedroom residences built 1940s–1960s
- Generous lots with partial to sweeping ocean, coastline, and Catalina views
- Intersection of suburban tranquility and easy access to greater South Bay
- ~74% of employed residents work in executive, managerial, or professional roles (top 3% nationally)
- Average rental price ~$5,700/month

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 51 (second-highest volume MLS area on peninsula)
- **Median close price:** $2,250,000
- **Median days on market:** 16 (fast-moving)

- Fast-moving market with strong demand despite modest price correction

## 3. Shopping

### Golden Cove Shopping Center
31176 Hawthorne Blvd at PV Drive West, RPV. Ocean-view outdoor center, 320 parking spaces. Anchored by Trader Joe's. 7-Eleven, various services, dining. Sunset views from parking lot are a local draw.

### Peninsula Shopping Center
67 Peninsula Center, RHE. Peninsula's primary retail hub (open since 1961). 53 stores: Pavilions (grocery), T.J. Maxx, Old Navy, Five Below, Petco, Rite-Aid, Bath & Body Works, Club Pilates, multiple restaurants. "Shop the Hill" campaign by RHE promotes local commerce.

### Malaga Cove Plaza
Historic Mediterranean-style village in PVE, short drive north. Boutiques, galleries, Malaga Cove Library.

Source: Golden Cove, Peninsula Shopping Center, Shop the Hill RHE, Yelp

## 6. Lifestyle

### Trails & Nature
PV Nature Preserve spans ~1,500 acres across 15 reserves. Hiking, equestrian, and bicycle trails. Pedestrian pathways line PV Drive North median between Via Valmonte and Malaga Cove Plaza.

### Parks & Recreation
- **Ernie Howlett Park** — 25851 Hawthorne Blvd (between PV Dr North and Rolling Hills Rd). City's major recreational facility — sports fields, playgrounds, community programming.
- **Ladera Linda Community Park** — Community events, trailhead access
- **Fred Hesse Jr. Community Park** — McTaggart Hall, community meetings and recreation
- **Point Vicente Interpretive Center** — Whale watching overlook, museum, events

### Outdoor Culture
Ocean views from residential streets, proximity to coastal bluff trails, equestrian communities in adjacent Rolling Hills, year-round temperate weather. Minutes from PV shoreline, Trump National Golf Club, Terranea Resort.

**RPV Recreation & Parks** — 30940 Hawthorne Blvd. Programs: youth sports, adult fitness, senior activities, nature education.

Source: RPV Recreation, RPV Parks, Nature Preserve Trails, PVPLC, Ernie Howlett Park
