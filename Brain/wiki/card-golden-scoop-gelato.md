# Golden Scoop Gelato

## Summary
Small-batch all-natural gelato shop at Golden Cove Center, RPV. Twenty-four flavors, plus affogato, soft serve, and croffles. Opened February 2025.

## Details

- **Address:** 31228 Palos Verdes Dr W, Rancho Palos Verdes 90275 (Golden Cove Center, corner of Hawthorne)
- **Phone:** (424) 327-2888
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 12–9pm, Sat–Sun 11–9pm
- **Founded by:** Nathan Kang — chose RPV for its Mediterranean climate and natural setting
- **Menu:** 24 flavors of premium small-batch gelato (Earl Grey, pistachio, salted caramel), affogato, soft serve, frozen yogurt, croffles
- **Ingredients:** Real California Milk, no eggs, powders, or artificial ingredients. Everything made in small batches from natural, local ingredients
- **Opened:** Monday, February 3, 2025
