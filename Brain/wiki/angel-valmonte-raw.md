## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- One of four neighborhoods in PVE (alongside Malaga Cove, Lunada Bay, Montemalaga)
- Defined by eucalyptus-lined streets — particularly "The Grove" area — creating a dense tree canopy giving the neighborhood a wooded, retreat-like character unlike most coastal communities
- No street lights or sidewalks in much of Valmonte, reinforcing quiet rural feel
- Most popular family neighborhood in PVE
- Northeast district of PVE, bordering Hollywood Riviera (Torrance) to north/east, Malaga Cove to west
- In 1948, city constructed barriers on Via Alameda, Via Pasqual, and Via Colusa at city limits to control through-traffic

### Housing Stock
- Primarily Cape Cod and ranch-style homes on flat lots
- Originally built late 1940s–early 1950s as modest 2-bed/1-bath starter homes
- Many significantly expanded or fully rebuilt in recent years
- Lot prices during initial development were ~$600, construction costs $6,000–$12,000
- Today among PVE's highest-priced neighborhoods

### Demographics
- Population: ~3,938
- Median age: 50
- Average individual income: $122,494

## 2. Dining & Coffee

Valmonte is residential with no commercial district. Residents gravitate to **Malaga Cove Plaza** (short drive or walk west):

**At Malaga Cove Plaza:**
- **The Cove Cafe** — Modern rustic cafe. Gourmet coffee, signature flatbreads, sandwiches, paninis, wraps, salads, pastries. A Valmonte staple. (thecovecafepv.com)
- **Yellow Vase** — Coffee, pastries, breakfast. Bright, inviting atmosphere. Popular morning gathering place.
- **Malaga Cove Ranch Market** — Family-run deli, bakery, market, wine & beer bar. American, Mexican, Armenian food. Also offers catering. Operating since 1952. (malagacoveranchmarket.com)

**Nearby (short drive):**
- **Bettolino Kitchen** — Italian, Promenade on the Peninsula
- **Gabi James** — Mediterranean/French bistro
- **Table Manners** — Casual neighborhood dining
- **Vida Modern Taqueria** — Contemporary Mexican

Source: Yelp, TripAdvisor, The Cove Cafe, Malaga Cove Ranch Market

## 3. Shopping

### Malaga Cove Plaza
Primary retail hub for Valmonte. Designed after Bologna, Italy architecture with replica Neptune Fountain. Buildings constructed 1924–1961.
- Boutique shops and art galleries
- Malaga Cove Ranch Market (general store/market, operating since 1952)
- Historic Malaga Cove Library
- PVE Police Department and city government offices
- Free parking

### Promenade on the Peninsula
Larger shopping center in Rolling Hills Estates with national retailers and additional dining. Hosts seasonal events including spring carnival.

Source: Yelp, TripAdvisor, Malaga Cove Plaza history

## 6. Lifestyle (Parks, Trails, Recreation)

### Valmonte Trail & Frog Creek Loop
- 1.3-mile easy loop starting on PV Drive North
- Elevation gain 203 ft, 30-60 minutes
- Open grassy area with Pacific Ocean views
- Dogs welcome on leash

### Eucalyptus Median Trails
- Pedestrian pathways along street medians on PV Drive North between Via Valmonte and Malaga Cove Plaza
- Shaded by towering eucalyptus trees

### Valmonte Canyon Trail
- Located at intersection of PV Drive North and Via Opata
- Trails on either side of the canyon

### PVE Passive Parks
Memorial Garden, Farnham Martin Park, Civic Center Park, Lunada Bay Plaza. PVE recreation oriented around self-guided nature enjoyment.

### Nearby Peninsula Highlights
- Point Vicente Interpretive Center and Lighthouse (whale watching Dec-May)
- Abalone Cove Shoreline Park and tide pools
- Palos Verdes Shoreline Preserve coastal trails
- Terranea Resort (oceanfront dining, spa, coastal trail)
- Trump National Golf Club Los Angeles

Source: AllTrails, PVE Recreation, Maureen Megowan, Hyperborea
