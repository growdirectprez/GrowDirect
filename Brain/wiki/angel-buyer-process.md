# Angel Buyer Process

Angelique's 9-step home buying process, Compass exclusive tools, financial obligations, and buyer representation agreement. Extracted from the 2026 AREA Buyer's Guide. Angel should use this to walk buyers through the process and answer questions about what to expect.

## The 9-Step Buying Process

### Phase 1: Prepare for Purchase (Steps 1-5)

**Step 1 — Buyer Consultation.** Initial meeting to discuss goals, lifestyle, and what the buyer wants in a home. Angelique uses this to guide the search strategy.

**Step 2 — Sign Buyer Representation Agreement.** A mutual business agreement establishing Compass and Angelique as the buyer's exclusive representative. Covers scope of services (property search, showings, negotiation, purchase assistance), duration (typically 6 months), property type and location criteria, and compensation terms. Commissions are negotiable. Angelique reviews the agreement together with the client before asking them to sign.

**Step 3 — Get Pre-Approved.** Pre-approval letter from a lender before submitting offers. Shows sellers the buyer can meet the proposed settlement date. Also provides projected down payment, closing costs, and monthly payment estimates.

**Step 4 — Get Early Access with the Compass Advantage.** Access exclusive listings before they appear on public websites — Compass Private Exclusives, Coming Soon, and Make Me Sell inventory (see Compass Tools section below).

**Step 5 — Submit and Negotiate Offer.** Collaborative crafting of a competitive offer — price, contingencies, closing timeline. Angelique guides clients through decision-making to ensure confidence in the offer terms.

### Phase 2: Contract to Close (Steps 6-9)

**Step 6 — Sign Contract.** The purchase agreement between buyer and seller defining price, settlement company, date, and earnest money deposit amount. Escrow deposit is good-faith money held in a non-interest bearing escrow account, deposited only after both parties sign.

**Step 7 — Home Inspection, Disclosures, Reports, and Repairs.** Angelique coordinates all inspections within the agreed timeline and contractual contingencies. Reviews disclosures and preliminary title report with the buyer. Buyer may approve or negotiate credits/repairs. Final walk-through before closing verifies condition and completed repairs.

**Step 8 — Loan Application and Appraisal.** Lender requires financial documents (bank statements, tax records, pay stubs). Mortgage contingency period to obtain commitment. Lender reviews docs and issues Clear-to-Close. Appraisal done on the property.

**Step 9 — Closing.** Buyer is a homeowner.

## Compass Exclusive Tools

Angel should know these when buyers ask about advantages of working with a Compass agent:

**Compass Private Exclusives.** Off-market listings shared only between Compass agents. Property details don't appear on public sites. Allows sellers to test pricing, gain insights, and generate early demand before going public. Buyers get access to inventory no one else sees.

**Compass Coming Soon.** List on Compass.com before going live on MLS/aggregate sites. Buyers can express interest, schedule viewings, and make offers before competing buyers see the listing. 2-3x more views, 7% fewer days on market, ~19% more open house traffic vs. straight-to-active listings.

**Make Me Sell.** Access to ~4 million additional properties nationally where sellers are open to the right offer but haven't formally listed. Expands inventory beyond what's on MLS.

**Compass One.** Integrated client dashboard where buyers can view exclusive inventory, discuss favorites with Angelique, and track next steps. All moving parts in one place.

## Financial Obligations for Buyers

Angel should be able to explain these when buyers ask about costs:

- **Earnest Money Deposit (Escrow):** Good-faith deposit, typically a percentage of sale price, held by listing brokerage or escrow company in non-interest bearing account
- **Insurance:** Homeowners insurance, private mortgage insurance (if down payment < 20%), title insurance. May also need flood or earthquake insurance depending on location
- **Loan Down Payment:** Determined by loan type — FHA financing or conventional financing
- **Inspections:** Home, radon, termite, and more — costs vary by home size
- **Appraisal:** Required by lender, paid upfront by buyer
- **Closing Costs:** Funds needed to settle, unless negotiated otherwise
- **Commission:** Sellers may pay all or part of buyer's broker commission. If seller doesn't pay, buyer may be required to pay
