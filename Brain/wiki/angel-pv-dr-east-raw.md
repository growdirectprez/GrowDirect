## 6. Lifestyle

### Outdoor Recreation
RPV features 44 hiking trails, 34 running trails, 20 walking trails. PV Nature Preserve offers 12 individual reserves with mapped trail systems. Trails wind through coastal sage scrub along dramatic bluffs.

### Notable Trails
- **Terranea Beach & Hudson's Bluff Sea Cave via Seascape Trail** — 5.2 miles, ~2 hours
- **Pirate and Flying Mane Loop** — Most popular RPV trail, 4.7-star rating
- **Sacred Cove Trail** — Short, family-friendly, beach access, 400 ft elevation change
- **Abalone Cove trails** — Coastal access, tidepools, marine preserve

### Ocean & Coast
Eastern RPV sits above dramatic sea cliffs. Abalone Cove Shoreline Park, Sacred Cove, Terranea oceanfront. Gray whale migration passes offshore (Dec-Apr). Catalina Island views defining feature.

### Terranea Resort
100 Terranea Way — luxury amenity anchor: spa, golf, multiple restaurants, oceanfront trails, event spaces. All within RPV city limits.

### Character
Quiet, semi-rural feel despite LA metro location. Large lots, equestrian-friendly areas, no sidewalks on many streets, strong community privacy. City incorporated 1973 to maintain low-density residential character.

Source: AllTrails, RPV Nature Preserve, TripAdvisor, Accardo Real Estate
