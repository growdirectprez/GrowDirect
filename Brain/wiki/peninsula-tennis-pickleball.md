# Tennis & Pickleball — Palos Verdes Peninsula

The peninsula has a deep tennis culture with four major clubs, public courts, HOA courts, and a pipeline from junior development through USTA league play. Pickleball has expanded rapidly — most clubs now offer dedicated courts.

---

## Private Clubs

### Palos Verdes Tennis Club (PVTC)
- **Address:** 3303 Via Campesina, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (310) 373-6326
- **Courts:** 11 lighted tennis courts, 8 lighted pickleball courts
- **Facilities:** Full-service pro shop, locker room with showers, clubhouse
- **Membership:** $2,000 initiation + $70/month residential dues. No waiting list for PVE residents
- **Founded:** 1959, opened 1963
- **Programs:** Private lessons, group clinics, leagues, Junior Tennis Academy (ages 3–16, USTA curriculum). Non-members welcome for instructional programs, leagues, clinics, and junior academy. Casual court access reserved for members
- **Pickleball:** Weekly open play sessions — $15 PVE residents, $20 non-PVE, free for members
- **Note:** Adjacent to Palos Verdes Golf Club (3301 Via Campesina, est. 1924) — separate entities at the same address
- **Sources:** PVTC website, City of PVE, Yelp, PV Source

### Peninsula Racquet Club (PRC)
- **Address:** 30850 Hawthorne Blvd, Rancho Palos Verdes 90275
- **Phone:** (310) 541-2523
- **Courts:** 12 fully LED-lighted tennis courts, 4 pickleball courts
- **Facilities:** Pro shop, fitness center (by reservation), outdoor rehab/performance training facility
- **Setting:** Cliffside location overlooking Pacific Ocean and Catalina Island — sunsets and migrating whales
- **Organization:** Equity-owned, non-profit membership club
- **Membership:** Equity Tennis: $750 initiation + $130/month. Non-Equity (short-term): $150 + $175/month. Junior (18 & under): $150 + $65/month. Pickleball only: $200 + $49–69/month. Fitness only: $100 + $30/month
- **Programs:** Weekly workouts, mixers, men's/women's league play, tournaments, social events, adult and junior programs
- **Hours:** 8am–8pm daily
- **Note:** Located on Hawthorne Blvd — sometimes referred to colloquially as "Hawthorne Racquet Club"
- **Sources:** PRC website (About page), Yelp, Pickleheads, Global Tennis Network

### Jack Kramer Club
- **Address:** 11 Montecillo Dr, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (310) 326-4404
- **Courts:** 13 tennis courts
- **Facilities:** Indoor/outdoor gym, 25-yard swimming pool with diving boards, clubhouse, sport court, playground, café (daily 9am–5pm)
- **Founded:** 1962, partnering with Vic Braden — focus on building tennis champions
- **Notable alumni:** Tracy Austin, Pete Sampras, Lindsay Davenport
- **Programs:** Private lessons, junior workouts, advanced/elite competitive training for all ages
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 6am–10pm, Sat–Sun 6am–8pm
- **Sources:** Jack Kramer Club website, Yelp, Global Tennis Network

## Public Courts

### Rolling Hills Estates Tennis Club at Ernie Howlett Park
- **Address:** 25851 Hawthorne Blvd, Rolling Hills Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (310) 541-4585
- **Courts:** 8 public tennis courts, open daily 8am–dusk. Pickleball overlays on some courts with portable nets
- **Pro:** USPTA certified tennis pro Bob Krueger (RHE resident) — private and group lessons
- **Drop-in pickleball:** $5/person/day. Weekday afternoon programming includes drop-in open play, court reservations, free Friday afternoon use
- **Sources:** City of RHE website, Yelp, Pickleheads, Global Tennis Network

### Ken Dyda Civic Center (RPV)
- **Courts:** 2 pickleball courts added to existing tennis court
- **Access:** Free, first-come first-served, one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset
- **Sources:** City of RPV news, Patch

### Additional Public Courts

## HOA & Private Courts

- **Rolling Hills Community Association:** 3 private tennis courts within the gated city. Contact: (310) 544-6222
- Various HOA communities maintain private courts for residents
- Larger-lot neighborhoods in Rolling Hills, Rolling Hills Estates, and RPV have homes with private courts — a real estate angle for buyers shopping for that amenity

## Junior Development & Coaching

- **PVTC Junior Tennis Academy:** Ages 3–16, USTA curriculum, age-appropriate equipment. Tennis & Me class (ages 3–4), drop-in play. Open to non-members
- **Jack Kramer Club:** Junior workouts through elite competitive training. Historic pipeline to pro careers
- **South Bay Tennis Center:** 30+ years, Directors Courtney and Ken Hance, after-school junior development
- **Bob Krueger at Ernie Howlett:** USPTA certified, RHE resident — the public-court teaching pro
- **USTA Junior Team Tennis:** Level-based team matches at local parks and clubs, weekly league play throughout SoCal
- **Private coaches:** Many work the Hill — based at clubs or booking public court time
