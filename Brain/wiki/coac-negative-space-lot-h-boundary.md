# Negative-Space Lot H Boundary — Technical Description

The Declaration 100 boundary is not defined by inclusion. It is defined by **negative-space exclusion**: the largest-possible master metes-and-bounds of Lot H, minus the recorded subdivisions and conveyances that were affirmatively carved out. This card documents the drafting technique, why it strengthens (rather than weakens) the reactivation, and how the Restated Declaration must describe it.

**Not legal advice.** Distilled from a 2026-04-19 external-advisor memo. HOA counsel + title-plant verification required before recording.

## The Drafting Technique

Palos Verdes Corporation used the largest possible metes-and-bounds description of its original holdings, then expressly excluded already-subdivided or conveyed parcels by reference. The pattern repeats across Declarations 100 and 101, the 1930 Filiorum grant deed (Book 10226), and subsequent tract-specific overlays (Tract 14649, One-A, Lot H, etc.):

> "All of Lot H except the parcels heretofore subdivided and recorded as Palos Verdes Estates…"
>
> "except Tract 14649 as shown on the map recorded in Book…"
>
> "except those portions conveyed to Filiorum Corporation or to the Portuguese Bend Community Association…"

This is not sloppy title work. It is **intentional, phased-development architecture** used by the Vanderlips across the peninsula. PV Corp retained unified master control while subdivisions were released; covenants in Declaration 100 automatically attached to everything inside the master boundary that had not been expressly carved out.

## Why It Matters Now

The exclusions are **not affirmative grants of new restrictions**. They are exceptions that leave the remaining land burdened by the original master covenants. Because the exclusions were recorded chronologically as subdivisions occurred, the title chains can appear "strange" or overlapping on modern title reports — especially where later deeds (post-1950) reference only tract-specific declarations (e.g., 1949 Declaration No. One or 1950 Lot H Declaration) without repeating the full master metes-and-bounds.

For reactivation purposes, this structure is **legally advantageous**:

- Any parcel whose chain of title traces back to PV Corp/Lot H remains subject to Declaration 100 unless that parcel was expressly excluded via a recorded subdivision.
- The 15-owner trigger in Article II §5 is keyed to *"owners of record title of fifteen separate parcels within the original boundary described in this Declaration."* The original boundary is the master metes-and-bounds **minus** the negative-space exclusions — exactly as set forth in Declaration 100 (Book 9436 p. 155) and cross-referenced in the 1930 Filiorum deed.

## Parcels Inside the Boundary (Current Understanding)

These parcels are understood to sit within the master boundary after exclusions, based on the advisor synthesis and the chain-of-title work to date. **Verification is required before relying on any specific parcel**:

- **0 Clipper Road** (APN 7573-006-024) — core Lot H; never excluded
- **Salvation Army campus parcel** — listed in the RPV Housing Plan; overlaps historic Lot H
- **Silver Spur Canyon project area** — density increases conflict with original single-family / drainage / setback / geologic-protection covenants; same master Lot H overlay
- **City-owned or city-controlled parcels** surrounding Point Vicente, the Interpretive Center, Golden Cove, and vacant sites up toward Crest/Hawthorne — retained or reacquired by public entities, but never released from the original PV Corp master restrictions via affirmative exclusion
- **Certain fringe parcels or cross-boundary lots in Rolling Hills Estates** — may still carry residual references to Declaration 100/101 in older title documents; independent RHCA CC&Rs remain the primary shield there, but the revived covenants can provide supplemental evidentiary support in Builder's Remedy or density litigation

Even parcels that appear to sit in later tracts (e.g., portions of Tract 14649) can be reached if their title chain contains the "subject to Declaration 100/101" recital or if they fall inside the master metes-and-bounds after subtracting the negative-space carve-outs.

## Parcels Expressly Excluded (Partial List — Confirm)

- **Palos Verdes Estates (PVE)** — first development area; excluded by original drafting
- **Tract 14649** — the current 81-lot Community of Abalone Cove tract; excluded by later subdivision recording
- **Filiorum Corporation conveyances** — parcels transferred via the 1930 Filiorum grant deed (Book 10226) where the deed affirmatively released them
- **Portuguese Bend Community Association conveyances** — certain portions conveyed to that entity
- **Other tract-specific subdivisions** recorded over time with affirmative releases

The exact list must be verified through a full negative-space exclusion audit by a title company or experienced HOA counsel. The result is a **remaining burdened land area** — the "negative space" — on which the Declaration 100 covenants continue to run.

## How the Restated Declaration Must Describe It

To eliminate title-chain confusion and make the reactivation bullet-proof, the Restated Declaration must include:

### Boundary Recital (Section 1 of the Restated Declaration)

A **single, comprehensive Boundary Recital** that:

1. Quotes the original Declaration 100 metes-and-bounds **verbatim** from Book 9436 p. 155 (or the cleanest certified copy obtainable; see smeared-page issue below)
2. Lists **every negative-space exclusion** ever recorded (PVE, Tract 14649, Filiorum conveyances, etc.), citing the recording book and page for each
3. Defines the result — the burdened land — as the parcels within the master boundary that remain subject to Declaration 100/101 after all exclusions

### Exhibit A — Modern Plat Overlay

A **modern plat overlay** attached as Exhibit A, showing:

- The original Declaration 100 master boundary (from the metes-and-bounds)
- All negative-space exclusions overlaid
- Current assessor parcels within the remaining burdened land, highlighted
- Wong 1980 subdivision map as reference
- Key Housing Element sites and ACLAD footprint for context

This exhibit is built from the `dev.abalonecove.org` interactive map (see [[wpbca-payments-platform]] and the Cove platform's `cove/map/` data).

### Identification Recital

A recital confirming that each of the 15 participating owners holds record title to a parcel inside the burdened land and that the reactivation is therefore properly triggered under Article II §5.

## The Smeared Page Problem

Page 155 of Declaration 100 — the property description page containing the master metes-and-bounds — is **partially illegible on the county recorder's microfilm**. The county recorder has been unable to produce a clean copy.

Fragments visible on the damaged page:

- References to Lot H and the Bixby partition (Case 2373)
- Bearings and long distances (2,304.72 feet, 1,779.84 feet, 2,209.30 feet — distances suggesting the full coastline, not a subset)
- Mean High Tide Line of the Pacific Ocean, with a general easterly direction along said line
- Triangulation Station "Redondo"
- References to Rancho Palos Verdes and Malo Pinito Ranch
- An acreage figure (illegible)
- A "true point of beginning"

**Mitigation options** (pending title-plant work):

1. **Vanderlip Papers** — locate a clean copy in the original family archives
2. **Title-plant search** — commercial title companies often retain cleaner scans than the county recorder
3. **Reconstructive survey** — cross-reference with the 1930 Filiorum grant deed (which incorporates the same description) and adjacent recorded documents to reconstruct the smeared portions
4. **Certified copy from an adjacent recording** — Declaration 101 (Book 9482 p. 37) and related instruments may reference the Declaration 100 boundary in ways that allow reconstruction

The Restated Declaration must cite the cleanest available version and include a recital explaining the sourcing (e.g., "Master boundary description reconstructed from Book 9436 p. 155 and cross-referenced with the 1930 Filiorum grant deed at Book 10226…").

## Relationship to Other Recorded Instruments

The negative-space boundary interacts with, but does not replace, other recorded instruments:

- **Declaration 101** (Book 9482 p. 37) — implementing instrument under 100, assigning specific parcels to use classes (A through K) defined in 100's Article IV. Without 100's master boundary, 101's class designations have no meaning.
- **Declaration No. One** (1949, restated 2009) — governs Tract 14649 (expressly excluded from Declaration 100's burdened land, but overlapping in community context)
- **Declaration One-A** (1950) — adds bluff setbacks and height limits on Lots 1-5 of Tract 14649
- **1950 Lot H Declaration** — adds additional restrictions on everything in Lot H not already in a named tract
- **1949 Declaration of Easements** — reserves the two ocean access easements (1952 recorded) and utility/drainage rights
- **1930 Filiorum grant deed** (Book 10226) — the conveyance from PV Corp to Filiorum that cross-references the Declaration 100 master boundary

The Restated Declaration should recite this layered structure so no one can credibly argue the boundary is ambiguous.
