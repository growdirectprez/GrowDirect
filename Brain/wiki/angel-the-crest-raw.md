## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Along Crest Road in RPV, top of the PV hill, near Crestwood area
- Mix of SFR and attached homes (condos/townhomes). Styles: Mediterranean to traditional, built 1958–2017.
- Central RPV location — easy access to shops, restaurants, recreation, ocean views
- Family-oriented with walkability to Peninsula Center amenities
- **Notable condo/townhome inventory:** 11 units available ($699K–$1.38M) — accessible entry point

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 22
- **Median close price:** $1,935,000
- **Median DOM:** 13 (one of the fastest on the peninsula)
- **Price per sqft:** $808
- **YoY price change:** -0.4% (most stable on peninsula)

**H1 2025:** 12 SFR sales, avg $2,015,167. Most expensive $2,755,000, least $1,501,000. PPSF steady $812.

Source: PV Source, Prestige Team, Neighborhoods.com

## 6. Lifestyle

- **Central hilltop:** Panoramic ocean views in multiple directions from elevated lots
- **Walkability:** Walking distance to shopping, parks, recreation — standout for car-dependent RPV
- **Family-friendly:** STEAM magnet school, strong PTA, quiet streets
- **Recreation:** Point Vicente, Abalone Cove, RPV trail system, Los Verdes Golf Course nearby
- **Housing mix:** $700K condos to $2.7M+ SFR = more economically diverse than other RPV areas

Source: Prestige Team, Accardo
