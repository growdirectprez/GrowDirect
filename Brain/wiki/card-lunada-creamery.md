# Lunada Creamery

## Summary
Soft serve creamery in Lunada Bay Plaza, PVE. Weekly rotating flavors, vegan options, cookie sandwiches, take-home pints. Opened 2024 in a 380 sq ft former ATM space.

## Details

- **Address:** 711 Yarmouth Rd, Palos Verdes Estates 90274
- **Phone:** (424) 241-0620
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 12–8pm, Fri 12–9pm
- **Founded by:** Julie and Jason Anthony — moved to PV nine years ago, missed having a local frozen treat spot, spent 15 months developing the concept
- **Inspired by:** The Big Chill (LA) and Penguin's (Redondo Beach)
- **Menu:** Soft serve with one in-house made flavor each week (e.g., all-natural peanut butter), always at least one vegan option, homemade cookie sandwiches, take-home pints
- **Toppings:** Fresh fruit, vegan cookie dough from local baker, apple pie filling, cinnamon compote
- **Space:** 380 sq ft in Lunada Bay Plaza (former Bank of America ATM space)
- **Design:** Minimalist — woven pendant lamps, wooden benches, quartz counter
