# Founder's Narrative (Private)

Private narrative file capturing the personal context that explains how this Foundation came to exist. Keep internal until the Foundation is seated and the board approves the public framing. Advisor guidance: "The personal story can be told later in a separate long-form piece (book, long interview, or Foundation origin story) once the entity is established and the research library is public."

The public voice on cove.org and abalonecove.org uses the restrained "I might be right" register. This file is the raw material, not the published voice.

## Why This File Exists

Three reasons:

1. **Documentation helps with the narrative** — the founder noted directly that "I am documenting like this because it helps me with the narrative." The act of writing it down sharpens the reasoning.
2. **Later long-form** — Foundation origin story, interviews, or book material will draw from this.
3. **Defense against mischaracterization** — if the Foundation is ever accused of being a personal vehicle or a cover for commercial interests, this file (combined with the three-entity separation and the logs) tells the real story.

## The Person Behind the Foundation

- **Third-generation Californian** who moved to New York City in his early career
- **10-year NYC grind**, then moved back to California in 2003 to raise children
- Brought family to Palos Verdes because of the public schools — ultimately ranked among the best in the country
- Children got through a CA high school during COVID and into four-year universities — Wolverines (Michigan) and Ducks (Oregon). Not UC schools; applicants' portfolios weren't selected. "We sent them to become Wolverines and Ducks and they are better for it and we are bummed it didn't work out the way we thought it would 20 years ago"
- **55 years old** with 30 years of professional work history
- **Out-of-work IT Manager** by recent status. 25 years of system implementation experience. Managed global teams. Watched the consulting world become outsource operations. Stressed-out remote lot manager before this chapter.
- **AI-enabled retraining** — started using AI, built systems to retrain himself using every piece of his digital life relevant to his professional life, started building tools he thought he could market as a reinvention
- **Bitcoiner through and through** — mined BTC in 2014 using cgminer on a Raspberry Pi. Long-term capital gains provide personal runway. The shelf photo (red heat sink miner, caps, a physical USD coin on a lanyard) is the visual shorthand for this identity.
- **Wife is a realtor** in Southern California — boom-bust income, historically
- **Largest asset is the house** — a seaside cottage on Tract 14649, Rancho Palos Verdes
- **The house is sliding** — Portuguese Bend landslide country; walls crack a little each day. The lot across the street, when graded, is expected to crack them faster.

## The Three Fronts

The founder's own words:

### Front 1 — Failing HOA

"The HOA that governs my neighborhood is failing. There are only two members; my wife is one, and we need three to be legitimate."

Community of Abalone Cove (formerly WPBCA) has 5 director seats; only 2 are filled. Board cannot achieve quorum on meaningful action. The wife is one of the two. Dues are under $20/month — severely underfunded. The community owns two rare 1952 private ocean access easements on Seacove Drive. These are the jewel and the vulnerability.

### Front 2 — Cove, Grassroots

"I started to build cove out of frustration because we are in quagmire with only two. The board can't vote to vote and nothing is tracked and no one is taking the charge. The board tries but if they try too much they get heard less. It has to come from the grassroots."

Cove as the platform emerged from this frustration. Parcel-keyed membership, secret-ballot separation, blockchain-proof governance, document vault, community archive. The tooling started as what he needed as a member, then became something potentially commercial.

### Front 3 — The 100-Year Story

"I found out this really interesting story that goes back 100 years and actually could challenge all of this stuff."

Declaration 100 (1929). The layered Palos Verdes declaration scheme (1923 / 1929 / 1950). Lot H. The Bixby chain. The Vanderlip loophole. Parcel 106. The creek drainage rights in Section 11 of Declaration 100 that should inform the 0 Clipper grading analysis. The smeared page on the county recorder's copy that obscures exactly where Lot H ends. The missing page 166. All of it hiding in plain sight.

The founder is also a student of architecture and a parent who took his kids to see Frank Lloyd Wright's Wayfarers Chapel before it was dismantled. The historic-research hat is authentic, not performance.

The 1952 ocean path easements in Tract 14649 are analogous to Hollister Ranch in Santa Barbara County — rare private ocean access that creates a k-shaped economy inside a single HOA. The founder is reluctant to emphasize this publicly because of the Hollister/Bixby-style pressure it could invite.

## The Voice, Public vs. Private

**Public voice** (cove.org, abalonecove.org, position letters, Foundation materials): restrained, factual, planning-commission appropriate, "I might be right" register. Let the documents speak. Let the reader see. Don't editorialize.

**Private voice** (this file, founder's journal, long-form later): direct, personal, contextual. Includes the frustration, the BTC identity, the kids, the sliding walls, the realtor wife, the retrained IT manager, the Raspberry Pi miner. The reason this exists at all.

The public voice is what the Foundation publishes. The private voice is what survived enough to build the Foundation.

## Ethics of Documentation

- Personal context belongs here, not in operational Foundation materials
- The Foundation's mission is preservation and education, not advocacy on any lot owner's behalf
- Specific lot-level grievances (0 Clipper grading, Tract 14649 easement disputes) go through Community of Abalone Cove or individual counsel — never through the Foundation's public channels
- The founder's personal BTC holdings, personal LLC revenue, and personal narrative stay with the founder. The Foundation's books, voice, and decisions stay with the Foundation.

## What This Is Not

- A Foundation press release
- Marketing copy for the commercial product
- Advocacy for or against any specific development proposal
- A political statement about California housing policy

It's a journal entry that helps the founder think clearly and gives future writers a complete source.
