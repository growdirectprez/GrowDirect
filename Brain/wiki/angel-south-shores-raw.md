## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Southern edge of PV Peninsula, technically City of Los Angeles (San Pedro), bordering RPV. ZIP 90732.
- ~3,590 residents
- Primarily medium-sized SFR (built from 1954), plus condos (Southpointe, South Shores Racquet Club) and PV Shores mobile homes
- Two sub-areas: Lower South Shores (closer to water, coastal trail access) and Upper South Shores (higher elevation, wider views)
- Peaceful, dog-friendly, walkable, wide streets, mid-century coastal homes
- Unobstructable Pacific and Catalina views — "a private escape perched above the Pacific"
- **SSCA** (South Shores Community Association) — active 50+ years

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 37
- **Median close price:** $1,350,000
- **Median days on market:** 17 (fastest on peninsula)
- **Price per square foot:** $693
- **YoY price change:** -4.7%
- Most affordable peninsula entry + fastest DOM = high demand value market

Source: Neighborhoods.com, South Bay Residential, Prestige Team, SSCA, J Daniel RE

## 6. Lifestyle

### Parks
- **Averill Park** — Dramatic hills, gazebo, artificial pond with waterfall, rose-lined walkways, harbor views. Popular wedding/party venue.
- **Point Fermin Park** — Lighthouse, cliffside views
- **Deane Dana Friendship Park** (shared with RPV) — Nature center
- **Joan Milke Flores Park**
- **Knoll Hill Off-Leash Dog Park**

### Coastal
- Coastal trails near Lower South Shores
- Cabrillo Beach (swimming, tide pools)
- Point Fermin tidepools
- Surfing, diving, jet skiing, sailing, sportfishing nearby

### Harbor
- LA Waterfront, Cabrillo Marine Aquarium, whale watching departures, harbor cruises

### Character
Working-class coastal meets upscale views. More diverse and accessible than RPV to the west. Strong community identity through 50-year SSCA. Racquet Club offers gated resort-style living (tennis, pools, clubhouse). Buyers seeking coastal lifestyle without full PV price tag — $1.35M vs $1.97M in Mira Catalina.

Source: SanPedro.com, Averill Park, Randazzo RE
