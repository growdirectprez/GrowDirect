## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Central RPV, above Peninsula Center shopping area, between Hawthorne and Crenshaw
- Geographic and commercial heart of the PV Peninsula
- Most walkable neighborhood on the Hill — shopping, schools, dining, entertainment all within walking distance
- More "town center" than rural estate character — suburban but convenient
- Active community identity centered around shopping centers, schools, and civic amenities

### Housing Stock
- Predominantly single-family, built mid-1960s, averaging ~2,400 sqft
- Ranch and split-level styles on standard lots — more modest and uniform than La Cresta
- **Condo/townhome inventory:** $699K (2BR/2BA 930 sqft) to $1,120K (3BR/3BA 1,358 sqft) — lower entry point

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 23
- **Median close price:** $1,840,000
- **Median days on market:** 40

**H1 2025 detail:** Avg sale $1,863,375 (vs $1,905,357 H1 2024). Range: $1,670,000–$2,290,000.

## 3. Shopping

### Peninsula Shopping Center (walking distance)
Target, Ulta Beauty, Old Navy, TJ Maxx, Bath & Body Works, Rite Aid, Bay Club fitness, Pavilions grocery, Chipotle, MOD Pizza, Jersey Mike's, Jamba Juice. Multiple services.

### Promenade on the Peninsula (adjacent)
Open-air lifestyle center in RHE. Pottery Barn, Williams-Sonoma, Equinox. Regal Cinemas, Ralphs. New: ZenesisX VR/e-sports, Level Up Bowl & Bistro. Coming 2026: Mega Looma Epic Adventure Park.

**Retail convenience:** Most retail-accessible neighborhood on the Peninsula. Virtually all daily shopping needs met without leaving the area.

Source: Peninsula Shopping Center, Promenade PV

## 6. Lifestyle

### Walkability
Peninsula Center's standout feature. Shopping, schools (Soleado Elementary, Chadwick), dining, entertainment all walking distance. Unusual for suburban RPV.

### Convenience Hub
Between Hawthorne and Crenshaw with direct access to Peninsula's two largest retail centers. Where Peninsula residents go for errands, socializing, dining.

### Family-Oriented
1960s homes, walkable schools, proximity to parks. Entry prices ($1.67M-$2.29M SFR, sub-$1.2M condos) most accessible on the Hill for families.

### Character
Active community identity around shopping/schools/civic amenities rather than views/estates. Suburban but functional — the Peninsula's practical heart.

Source: BCRE, Haynes RE, Accardo
