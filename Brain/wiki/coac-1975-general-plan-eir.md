# COAC: 1975 RPV General Plan + EIR — Annotated for Cove

The City of Rancho Palos Verdes' **founding planning document**. Adopted June 26, 1975 (Resolution 75-43), with amendments through at least 1988. Combines a General Plan and a Final EIR in a single integrated document — RPV used the CEQA exemption that allows a GP to satisfy the EIR requirement when both are properly cross-walked.

This article distills the parts of the 1975 GP/EIR relevant to Cove 501c (Abalone Cove Foundation) and the Community of Abalone Cove (CoAC) reactivation work. The full archive copy + page-delimited extraction lives at [`Cove/docs/archive/originals/city-records/general-plan/1975-general-plan-original.md`](../../Cove/docs/archive/originals/city-records/general-plan/1975-general-plan-original.md).

## Document Provenance

- **Adopted:** June 26, 1975 (Resolution 75-43)
- **City Council at adoption:** Marilyn Ryan (Mayor), Gunther Buerk, Ken Dyda, Francis Ruth, Robert Ryan
- **City Manager:** Leonard G. Wood (also acted as City Clerk)
- **Planning Advisory Committee chair:** Eleanore Wiedmann
- **Environmental Services Director / Planning:** Sharon Hightower
- **Inbox source:** UC Berkeley Institute of Governmental Studies library copy (stamped May 6, 1993), digitized by Internet Archive 2025 with CA State Library funding (IA item C124910957)

## Why This Document Matters for Cove

The 1975 GP/EIR is the bridge between **the Vanderlip-era Palos Verdes Project framework (1913–1973)** and **the modern RPV regulatory regime**. RPV incorporated Sept 7, 1973, so 1975 was its first comprehensive policy document. Everything since — the Coastal Specific Plan (1978), the LRPMP (2013/2015), the modern Housing Elements (1990, 2001, 2018), the FEMA buyout (2024) — flows from frameworks first laid down here.

For Cove 501c, the 1975 GP/EIR is foundational because:

1. **It establishes the city's recognition** of the Abalone Cove area's coastal-recreational character (Park, Beach Acquisition, Lifeguard, Bluff Trail).
2. **It documents the active landslide** as a pre-existing condition that constrains the entire Portuguese Bend / Abalone Cove zone — a fact the city cannot now claim ignorance of.
3. **It captures the Vanderlip transition** in primary-source narrative form — useful for the Restated Declaration recitals.
4. **It precedes the Coastal Act (1976)**, so its open-space and shoreline policies are independent of the Coastal Commission framework.

## Five Cove-Relevant Vectors

### 1. Abalone Cove Specific (11 direct mentions)

- **Tract 28750** = Former Abalone Cove School site, ~17 acres, declared surplus by school district. **GP Amendment 10** (Resolution 82-24, April 20, 1982) changed Land Use designation **from agricultural to commercial recreational**, allowing visitor-serving uses in the coastal zone. This is the city's formal disposition path for the parcel that today is part of the Abalone Cove park / Shoreline Park complex.
- **Abalone Cove Park** projected at 82 acres along the south side / shoreline.
- **Abalone Cove Beach Acquisition Final EIR (LA County, Dec 1974)** cited as source for the public-beach acquisition.
- **Drainage corridor:** Portuguese Bend slide-area drainage flows under PV Drive South and discharges into Abalone Cove. Mud-and-debris complaints documented in 1975.
- **Bluff-trail segments** at the County facility at Abalone Cove.
- **Lifeguard:** LA County Lifeguards responsible at Abalone Cove.
- **Fire station:** Plan to relocate Abalone Cove fire station to western section of city.

### 2. Vanderlip Lineage (Founders Narrative)

The 1975 GP/EIR is a primary-source historical text. Key passages (paraphrased from OCR; verify against PDF for any external citation):

- Frank A. Vanderlip purchased the Peninsula in 1913; estate **Villa Francesca**.
- "Plans were created for the Peninsula, which became known as the **Palos Verdes Project**."
- **Kelvin Vanderlip** developed Portuguese Bend area "around a club" (the historical antecedent to the Portuguese Bend Club).
- **Frank A. Vanderlip Jr.** + Great Lakes Carbon Corp. land deal (significant transfer; details in document).
- **John Vanderlip** named on a Coastal-area planning committee roster.

These augment [[cove-vanderlip-memoir]] and [[cove-community-history]].

### 3. Portuguese Bend Landslide Baseline (118 mentions)

The 1975 GP/EIR identifies "the Portuguese Bend Landslide [as] now active" with "horizontal dimensions of thousands of feet." Other slides characterized as "ranging from masses that may well be on the verge of renewed failure to some very old landslides that probably are quite stable."

This is the city's **own** characterization — useful when defending Cove positions against any city claim of "we didn't know" or "the science is new." See also [[coac-1967-portuguese-bend-clay-mineralogy]] for the underlying geology research that predates this characterization by 8 years.

### 4. Resource-Management Overlays (RM-1 through RM-9)

The 1975 GP defined nine resource-management overlay districts that became the basis for later zoning:

| Code | Resource |
|------|----------|
| RM-1 | Sea Cliff Erosion |
| RM-2 | Extreme Slope |
| RM-3 | High Slopes |
| RM-4 | Active Landslide |
| RM-5 | Old Landslide Area |
| RM-6 | Hydrologic Factors |
| RM-7 | Marine Resource |
| RM-8 | Wildlife Habitat |
| RM-9 | Natural Vegetation |

WPBCA parcels along the bluff fall into RM-1, RM-3, RM-4 / RM-5, and RM-7 simultaneously. The Restated Declaration recitals can cite these overlays directly as the city's own findings.

### 5. Amendment Trail (1976–1988)

The Amendments Summary Sheet at the front of the inbox copy (which the original 1975 archive copy may have been missing) lists GP Amendments 2 through 16. Cove-relevant entries:

| Amendment | Subject | Resolution | Date |
|-----------|---------|------------|------|
| 3 | Coastal Zone regulations (became Coastal Specific Plan) | 78-81 | Dec 19, 1978 |
| 10 | Former Abalone Cove School site, ag → commercial-recreational | 82-24 | April 20, 1982 |
| 12 | Golden Cove (PV Drive W / Hawthorne) commercial → residential 6-12 du/ac | 84-50 | Aug 7, 1984 |
| 14 | Eastview annexation | 84-48 | Aug 7, 1984 |

Amendment 14 is documented in detail at [[coac-1984-gp-amendment-14-eastview]].
