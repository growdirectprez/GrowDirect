## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Western RPV, encompassing Portuguese Bend — private, gated, rural/ranch setting near the ocean
- ~304 residents in Portuguese Bend proper (Census)
- Mix of ranch-style and custom homes, many on oversized lots. Some from 1920s, many 1950s. Horse-zoned.
- One of few gated communities in all of RPV
- **Strongest YoY appreciation on the peninsula: +15.3%**

### Landslide Context
Since spring 2023, accelerated land movement in Portuguese Bend complex (680+ acres). Governor Newsom declared state of emergency Sep 3, 2024. City permanently prohibited new construction in slide zone (effective Sep 18, 2025). Movement slowed to ~1.46 in/week (down from 1.8). FEMA $42M voluntary buyout announced Oct 2024. Gas shut off since Aug 2024. 11 deep dewatering wells installed — movement stopped near wells.
