# CoAC Reactivation Framework — MOC

Map-of-content for the **strategic reactivation of Declaration 100 Article II §5** as a private-covenant overlay on the Community of Abalone Cove (formerly WPBCA) governance structure. This is distinct from — and runs parallel to — the operational compliance work in [[wpbca-compliance-framework]] (third-director recruitment, payment platform, investment policy).

**Sensitivity:** This framework describes a deliberate "catch off guard" posture — the reactivation is designed to minimize the window for City, developer, or YIMBY preemptive moves against the recorded-title overlay. All cards marked `visibility: private-until-board-authorized` and `status: strategic-draft`. Not Foundation public voice. Not for cove.org or abalonecove.org publication. HOA counsel review required before any notice is published or instrument is recorded.

**Not legal advice.** Distilled working positions from a 2026-04-19 external-advisor memo. Archived verbatim at `Brain/raw/processed/advisor-memos/2026-04-19-reactivation-playbook.md`.

## The Premise

Declaration 100 (1929) contains a self-executing reactivation mechanism in Article II §5: if the Community Association of Palos Verdes has failed to meet or act for >90 consecutive days, 15 owners of record title of 15 separate parcels within the original Declaration 100 boundary may publish notice, hold a meeting, and elect three Trustees who inherit the full 26-power Article II §4 authority — including recording a Restated Declaration that revalidates surviving Declaration 100/101 covenants on the chain of title.

The mechanism has been dormant for ~95 years. The 90-day condition precedent has been satisfied for decades. The Vanderlip drafters wrote the clause intentionally as a constitutional-crisis reset, not a routine operating rule — latent, obscure, invocable only by residents who know about it.

The April 16, 2024 RPV City Council vote rezoning 0 Clipper Road (APN 7573-006-024, the 1-acre west-of-creek bench on the fault-line shelf edge) to RM-22 under state Housing Element pressure is the moment the reset button was designed for.

## Three Phases

### Phase 1 — Stabilize CoAC Governance (Track 1, Low Threshold)

Use the existing 2012 Bylaws + 2023 name-change + `dev.abalonecove.org` platform to seat a functional Board + ARC + Secretary (3 Board + 1 ARC + 1 Secretary = MVP). Ratify the rename as the new board's first act. See [[wpbca-compliance-framework]] for the operational details.

### Phase 2 — Reactivate Declaration 100/101 (Track 2, Self-Executing)

Trigger Article II §5 with 15+ owners whose parcels fall inside the original Declaration 100 boundary after negative-space exclusions. Elect three pre-vetted Trustees. They record the Restated Declaration with:

- Full negative-space boundary recital
- Modernized Article V equity/vote-share (one parcel / one vote)
- Explicit creek/fault-line avoidance-corridor language
- ACLAD partnership recitals
- Surviving Declaration 100/101 covenants (single-family, density caps, drainage, setbacks, Art Jury review)

### Phase 3 — Network Blitz + ACLAD + 501(c)(4) Advocacy

Pre-build the 15-owner + 3-Trustee network quietly in parallel with Phase 1. Use door-to-door + `dev.abalonecove.org` non-binding tools. Align the Trustees to accept election and record the Restated Declaration on the spot. Partner with the Abalone Cove Landslide Abatement District (same-side public-private defense). Stand up a separate 501(c)(4) for the viral 2026 Civic Problems educational/advocacy campaign. Execute the notice → meeting → recording sequence as a blitz to minimize the preemption window.

## How This Relates to the Three-Hat Compensation Model

This work is **volunteer CoAC governance** for Phases 1–2, with the Restated Declaration recording and enforcement as core CoAC Board activity. The Foundation 501(c)(3) role is limited to neutral research/documentation — it does not advocate, does not fund legal action, does not direct the reactivation. The new 501(c)(4) (if formed) handles the advocacy/viral campaign with its own separate funds and counsel.

Time spent on this work maps to the **CoAC volunteer hat** (unpaid), not the Foundation or LLC hats. See [[foundation-three-hat-compensation]] for the rules and [[foundation-time-logging-policy]] for the allocation methodology.

## Operational Gates

Before any notice is published:

1. Separate HOA counsel reviews the Article II §5 procedure, the negative-space boundary description, and the Restated Declaration draft
2. Separate 501(c)(4) counsel engaged for the advocacy arm (if that entity is being formed)
3. Certified copy of Declaration 100 p. 155 obtained (currently smeared on microfilm) — pull from Vanderlip Papers or title-plant search
4. PV Corp / Filiorum Delaware dissolution status verified — reversionary-rights successor analysis under California escheat
5. 15 final parcel list confirmed (inside the master boundary after negative-space exclusions; prioritize Housing Element site overlaps)
6. Three Trustee candidates confirmed, briefed, and committed
7. CoAC board ratifies the rename and `dev.abalonecove.org` platform (Phase 1 complete or in progress)
8. ACLAD coordination mechanism agreed (formal MOU or informal partnership signal)
