Strategic Positioning Execution Primer

Ruptiv · Five-D Method · 2026-05-04

A positioning engagement establishes who the company serves, what the brand stands for, where it wins against competitors, and what it should do next to bring its strategy to market. The result is a clear picture of the core customer, the brand promise, the competitive position, and a sequenced plan to act on them.

Method overlay — Merchandise fit test

Per assortment cluster, score the fit against the core customer. Surface gaps (under-served jobs) and stretches (clusters that don't belong). The fit-test agent reads the customer fragment and the assortment, emits a per-cluster score.
