# PV High School Drama — "Best Entertainment Value in LA"

## Summary
Both peninsula high schools run competitive drama programs, but PV High's department under Nicole Thompson (2017 Educator of the Year) produces professional-caliber musicals and plays. JRAY award winners. Ticket prices for a high school production — quality of a professional show.

## Details

### PV High School (Sea Kings)
- **Director:** Nicole Thompson — BA in Dramatic Arts (UC Santa Barbara), trained at NYU CAP21 Musical Theatre Conservatory, taught at La Jolla Playhouse and Old Globe Theatre. 2017 Educator of the Year
- **Choreographers:** Christopher Albrecht (UCLA), Niko Montelibano (UC Irvine, Disneyland Resort)
- **Vocal Director:** Sue Holmes Secrist (Stanford, choral conducting)
- **Awards:** JRAY Best Ensemble (Pippin), JRAY Musical of the Year + Jerry Herman Award for Best Director (Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat)
- **Website:** pvhsdrama.com

### PV Peninsula High School (Panthers)
- Full drama department with regular season of productions
- Recent: Metamorphoses (Dec 2025), Original Play Festival (Jan 2026), spring musical (Mar 2026)
- **Website:** pvphs.pvpusd.net/apps/pages/Drama

### The Real Estate Angle
This is a lifestyle amenity that doesn't show up in school rankings but matters to families who move here. Professional-level faculty, award-winning productions, and ticket prices that make it genuinely the best entertainment value in Los Angeles. A family moving from a city where theater means $150 tickets should know these shows exist.
