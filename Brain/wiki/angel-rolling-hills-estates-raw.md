## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Incorporated 1957. Adjacent to Rolling Hills — shares equestrian heritage but NOT gated, has commercial zones.
- ~8,200 population (2020). ~3.6 sq mi.
- Upscale suburban with strong equestrian identity — many properties zoned for horses with large lots
- Also includes standard residential subdivisions and commercial centers
- Mix of ranch homes, traditional two-story, newer custom builds
- Open streets with public access, unlike Rolling Hills
- Commercial districts along Silver Spur Road and Hawthorne Blvd
- **Retail center of the PV Peninsula** — three major shopping centers

## 6. Lifestyle

### Equestrian Culture
- **25+ miles dedicated equestrian trails** maintained by the city
- Many homes zoned for horses with private corrals
- Equestrian lifestyle embedded in city identity — neighborhoods named for horse culture (Dapplegray, etc.)
- **Peter Weber Equestrian Center** (26401 Crenshaw Blvd) — stall boarding, Western/English lessons, summer/winter pony camps, birthday parties, pony rides, access to 90 miles groomed trails
- **Ernie Howlett Park** (25851 Hawthorne Blvd) — three riding rings, dressage area, grandstand, multi-use trail, playground, sports fields. Home to Portuguese Bend Horse Show.
- **Dapplegray Park** — 1.5-acre equestrian facility with riding ring, lunging ring, dressage area connected to bridle trails

### Nature
- **George F Canyon Nature Preserve** — 51-acre preserve, 1.9-mile Stein-Hale Nature Trail through willow-riparian and coastal sage scrub. Only mainland exposure of Peninsula's Catalina schist bedrock.

### Character
Block parties, nature walks, trail rides, neighborhood gatherings define social fabric. More accessible than gated Rolling Hills but shares same natural beauty and equestrian heritage.

Source: RHE Equestrian, Ernie Howlett Facility, Peter Weber, George F Canyon AllTrails
