## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- South Redondo Beach, between PCH and the Pacific, 3 miles south of King Harbor
- Centered on S. Catalina Avenue — 300+ shops, restaurants, galleries, salons in a compact walkable grid
- Walkable, bicycle-friendly village one block from the ocean
- Residential areas quiet and tree-lined; commercial strip boutique-scale, no big-box
- **10-15 minutes from the Hill** — the natural "going out" destination for PV residents
- The anti-mall: one-of-a-kind boutiques, not chain-dominated

### Real Estate
- Starts ~$1.1M, $3-4M common, beachfront exceeds that
- The Esplanade and The Avenues are premium streets
- **Toll Brothers (2026):** 43-unit luxury townhome community (1,400-2,200 sqft, 3BR, up to 3.5BA). Sales open summer 2026.

### City Context
- Mayor Jim Light's March 2026 State of the City: "renaissance" — building permits now avg 10.5 days, two new tech companies moving in (Impulse Space doubling in size)
- Major waterfront revitalization underway: 11 acres open space, revitalized Seaside Lagoon, enhanced boardwalk, public market. Investment: $16M Sportfishing Pier, $20M Seaside Lagoon, $7.9M circulation.

Source: Riviera Village, South Bay Residential, Easy Reader, Toll Brothers

## 2. Dining & Coffee (Primary Section)

### Fine Dining / Elevated
- **Salt & Pearl** — Modern seafood & steak. Beach-casual, elevated execution. Top-rated Yelp/OpenTable.
- **Riviera House** (1708 S Catalina Ave) — Seafood-forward, craft cocktails. Open-air heated patio. Built 2019. 541 Yelp reviews. Classic cocktails.
- **il Posto** — Authentic Italian home cooking. Open-air dining. Branzino and Salmon standouts. New addition.
- **Bettolino Kitchen** — Modern Cal-Italian: housemade pastas, vibrant seafood, seasonal vegetables.

### Neighborhood Favorites / Casual
- **Gabi James** (1810 S Catalina Ave) — Spanish & coastal French. Gin, boutique spirits, wine. Tapas-style.
- **VIDA Modern Mexican + Tequila** — Tequila-focused, South-of-the-border meets East LA comfort.
- **Hook & Plow** — Beach-casual gastropub, local organic ingredients. Brunch.

## 6. Lifestyle

### The Esplanade
Mile-long oceanfront walkway, unobstructed Santa Monica Bay views. No structures blocking sunsets. Golden hour gathering spot. Connects to Torrance Beach south.

### Marvin Braude Bike Trail
22-mile paved coastal path, Will Rogers to Torrance Beach. Most popular bike path in LA. Final 2-mile stretch offers PV Peninsula and Catalina views.

### King Harbor / Redondo Pier
Shops, restaurants, marinas, live music, skate park, sport fishing fleet. Urban waterfront counterpart to the Village's boutique district.

### Torrance Beach Connection
South end of Village is steps from Torrance Beach — quieter, connects to RAT (Riviera to Airport Trail) and PV bluffs.

### Bicycle Culture
Village is extremely bicycle-friendly. Residents pedal between Village, Esplanade, and beach paths.

### PV Proximity
The Esplanade's south end meets the PV coastline. Riviera Village to Lunada Bay = 10-15 min by car. Natural "going out" destination for Peninsula residents who want walkable dining without driving to Manhattan or Hermosa.

Source: Marvin Braude Trail, Discover LA, Beach E Biking, Redondo Beach Pier
