## APPLICATION LAYER
**Square · Clover · Toast · Lightspeed**

> "Log in to see what happened"

Retrospective · Dashboard-mediated · Data as destination

Merchant goes TO the platform to learn what occurred after the fact. Reports export data. Intelligence is asynchronous. The store operates independently of the platform.

---

## OPERATING LAYER
**Canary Retail**

> "Agent in the store while it's happening"

Simultaneous · Event-driven · Data as operating infrastructure

The store IS the agent's environment. 166 MCP service junctions let agents reach physical store operations at packet-layer speed. The platform operates — it doesn't observe.

---

## Comparison Table

| Dimension | Application Layer | Canary Operating Layer |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Retrospective — after events occur | Simultaneous — at the speed of events |
| Agent Role | Merchant reviews dashboards | Agent executes in the store |
| Accountability | Reports show what happened | Four measurable rails (Ops · Financial · Evidentiary · Vendor) |
| Commercial Model | $25/seat subscription | Satoshi per-junction — aligned with merchant value flow |
