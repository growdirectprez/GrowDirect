## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Centered along Silver Spur Road, adjacent to Peninsula Center commercial district in RHE
- Geographic heart of the PV Peninsula — easy access to both hilltop residential and coastal preserves
- ~1,930 residences, development dating back to 1955
- Predominantly single-family, many with mid-century designs modernized over decades
- Home sizes 1,124–5,815 sqft, average ~1,910 sqft
- City-light views, select properties with panoramic downtown LA, San Gabriel Mountains, or coastline views ("Queen's Necklace" views)
- Distinguishing feature: **walkability** — Bristol Farms, Sunday farmers market, Peninsula Shopping Center, coffee, restaurants all along Silver Spur Road

### Real Estate (2025 — from CRMLS database, FACT)
- **Homes sold:** 34
- **Median close price:** $1,758,250
- **Median days on market:** 15 (one of the fastest on the peninsula)
- **Price per square foot:** $979
- **YoY price change:** +1.9%
- Fastest-moving value area on the Hill — PV schools/lifestyle without $3M+ entry price

**H1 2025 detail (PV Source):** Average sale price rose to ~$1,876,922 vs $1,778,647 in H1 2024. PPSF increased to $988 (up 7.3% from $921).

Source: Redfin, PV Source, Neighborhoods.com, RubyHome

## 3. Shopping

### Peninsula Shopping Center
67 Peninsula Center, RHE. Primary retail hub. Open-air power center. T.J. Maxx, Old Navy, Five Below, Pavilions (supermarket), Ulta Beauty, Bath & Body Works, Petco, Rite Aid, Club Pilates, Chipotle, MOD Pizza, Jersey Mike's, Jamba Juice. Free parking.

### Bristol Farms
837 Silver Spur Rd. Upscale grocery/gourmet market directly on Silver Spur Road. Anchor of the retail strip.

### Promenade on the Peninsula
Indoor/outdoor mall in revival phase. Regal Cinemas, Ralphs, growing new restaurant/entertainment roster. Coming: specialty ice cream shop, smash burger restaurant, Mega Looma Epic Adventure Park (2026).

Source: Peninsula Shopping Center, Shop the Hill RHE, Promenade PV

## 4. Events

### Weekly
- **PV Farmers Market** — 27118 Silver Spur Rd (Peninsula Center lot). Every Sunday, 8am-1pm, year-round. ~40 vendors: produce, eggs, fish, hummus, flowers. Walkable weekly ritual for Silver Spur residents.

### Annual
- **RPV Fourth of July** — Ken Dyda Civic Center. Since 1975. Live music, beer/wine, food trucks, inflatable rides, craft vendors, drone light show.
- **Summer Concerts in the Park** — Ken Dyda, Saturdays Jul-Aug, 4-6pm
- **PV Peninsula Chamber Annual Awards** — November. Recognizes outstanding businesses and community leaders.

Source: PV Farmers Market, RPV Recreation Events, PV Peninsula Chamber

## 6. Lifestyle

### Walkability
Silver Spur's distinguishing lifestyle feature. Bristol Farms, Sunday farmers market, Peninsula Shopping Center, coffee shops, restaurants all along Silver Spur Road — no freeway required. Most walkable neighborhood on the Hill.

### Outdoor Access
PV Nature Preserve: ~1,400 acres, 30+ miles of trails through coastal hills, canyons, rock outcrops. Pacific and Catalina views. Managed by PVPLC.

### Parks
- **Silver Spur Park** — SW corner Silver Spur Rd & PV Dr North. Half-acre open grass field. Small but walkable.
- **Hesse Park** — 29 acres on Hawthorne Blvd. Softball, soccer, running track, children's play, community room.
- **Ryan Park** — 10 acres south of Hesse. Baseball, grass areas, children's play.

### Buyer Profile
Families wanting PVPUSD schools (Blue Ribbon elementary, top-40 high school) and professionals who value peninsula lifestyle at below $3M estates price point. 15-day DOM signals sustained demand in a segment where inventory moves quickly.

Source: Silver Spur Park RHE, RPV Recreation, PVPLC, RPV Nature Preserve
