# Angel Listing Process

Angelique's approach to selling homes — pricing strategy, 9-phase marketing timeline, Compass exclusive tools, in-house design/staging, photography, video, and community involvement. Extracted from the 2025 AREA Listing Presentation. Angel should use this when sellers ask about the marketing plan or how to prepare their home.

## AREA Vision

The team's mission is to help everyone find their place. They strive to be available, conduct themselves professionally, and serve as consultants and trusted advisors. The goal is repeat business and referrals by impressing at every step — becoming the client's Realtor for life.

## Pricing Strategy — 8 Steps

Angelique's pricing approach is methodical and data-driven:

1. **Identify Key Attributes** — what makes the property stand out
2. **Develop Buyer Profiles** — who are the three buyer audiences (active buyers, buyers ready to move, buyers who don't yet know they want this home)
3. **Analyze the Market** — comparable sales, trends, inventory
4. **Build Out Marketing Plan** — targeted to the three buyer types
5. **Prepare the Property for Photography** — design review by Marina Accardo, trade services and Compass Concierge, calendar the optimum launch date
6. **Tell the Story** — daytime and twilight photography, video content
7. **Celebrate the Opportunity** — pre-marketing via Compass Private Exclusive and local pocket listing networking, brokers open event, premiere public open house
8. **Execute on the plan**

### Pricing Psychology

Peak buyer activity occurs in the first few weeks of listing. Getting the price right at launch is critical to maximizing position. The pricing pyramid shows the relationship between over-market pricing and buyer pool:

- At market value (0% over): 95% of buyers see it
- 5% over market: 50% of buyers
- 10% over: 30% of buyers
- 15% over: 20% of buyers

Competitive pricing using analysis, market research, and Compass AI tools generates the most activity and early offers before buyers move to newer listings.

## In-House Design and Staging

AREA's in-house design team (@MStudioHouse) combined with preferred partners ensures beautiful staging. Staging satisfaction is guaranteed — AREA will fix it if the client isn't happy. Key stats: 92% ROI for minor kitchen upgrades, staging helps sell homes up to 30x faster and increases sale price by 20%+.

## Compass Concierge Program

Pre-sale renovation financing with a $750 application fee and no interest. Covers staging to renovation. Invests in the home's potential to provide a faster, more profitable sale. Angel should mention this when sellers ask about whether to renovate before selling.

## Compass Exclusive Seller Advantages

**Private Exclusives.** Off-market listing shared only among Compass agents. Sellers control what information is shared while getting exposure to top agents. Allows price testing, critical insights, and early demand generation — all without accruing days on market. Stats: 2-3x more views, 7% fewer DOM, ~19% more open house traffic.

**Coming Soon.** Two launch opportunities — list on Compass.com before aggregate sites to build anticipation and pressure-test pricing strategy without accruing DOM.

## Photography and Video

AREA uses top photographers for daytime and twilight shoots. Cinematic property videos walk potential buyers through highlights. Instagram Reels and social media content extend reach beyond traditional marketing.

## Community Involvement

AREA gives back through Compass Cares and Baja Bound — 100% of transactions contribute a donation, with $100K+ donated and 8 homes built for families in need. Local sponsorships include PV High School football and baseball, Redondo Union baseball, and PV Little League baseball.
