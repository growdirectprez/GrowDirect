# Angel Transaction Timeline

Step-by-step escrow roadmaps for buyers and sellers, escrow document checklists, and moving checklist. Extracted from the AREA Escrow Roadmap. Angel should use this to answer "what happens next?" questions at any stage of a transaction.

## Buyer Escrow Roadmap

After the offer is accepted and escrow opens:

1. **Escrow Opens** — Buyer and seller agree to terms and sign contract. Buyer's deposit goes into escrow.
2. **Receive Opening Package from Escrow** — Paperwork to complete, sign, and return to escrow.
3. **Complete Due Diligence on the House** — Inspections are important. They're a small investment and can be used for negotiating later.
4. **Lock in Your Loan with Your Lender** — More paperwork with your lender.
5. **Renegotiations and Amendments** — May take place at this time based on inspection results.
6. **Receive Estimated Closing Statement** — Review all fees and costs of closing. Escrow will send more paperwork to sign if needed.
7. **Escrow Receives Loan Documents from Your Lender** — Escrow arranges for buyer to sign with a notary. Buyer deposits final funds. Escrow works with lender to fund the file, meeting remaining conditions.
8. **Closing** — You are now a homeowner. Welcome home!

## Seller Escrow Roadmap

After the offer is accepted:

1. **Escrow Opens** — Receipt of buyer's deposits opens escrow.
2. **Order Reports** — Provide escrow HOA contract information. Submit city report application and schedule required inspections.
3. **Appraisal + Inspection** — Expect requests for inspections and appraisal appointments from buyer's team.
4. **Submit Escrow Opening Package** — Completing the opening package allows escrow to start processing. Plan to discuss tax forms with a tax professional. Verify bank account matches how title is held. Notarization of some documents may be needed.
5. **Finalize Transaction Terms, Fees, and Costs** — Negotiations at this stage.
6. **Buyer Signs Loan Documents** — Escrow works with buyer and lender for notarized signing. Buyer deposits final funds. Escrow collaborates with lender on funding conditions.
7. **Buyer's Loan Funds** — One day prior to close of escrow.
8. **Close of Escrow** — Title insurer confirms grant deed has recorded. Complete accounting and balancing after all title funds revised. Agents arrange delivery of possession.
9. **Seller Proceeds Issued** — Disbursement occurs within 24 business hours after receipt of final title funds and written confirmation of recording. Retain the final settlement statement and tax forms for tax return.

## The Life of an Escrow (Full Flow)

Complete escrow process from both sides:

1. Buyer and Seller Sign Purchase and Sale Agreement
2. Agents Open Escrow and Buyer Places Deposit
3. Escrow Orders Preliminary Title Report
4. *(Parallel tracks begin)*
   - Escrow Reviews Preliminary Title Report
   - Buyer and Seller Complete and Return Opening Packages
5. Escrow Prepares Instructions and Documents
6. Statement of Information is Forwarded to Title
7. Escrow Requests Demands for Payoff
8. Escrow Provides Documentation to Lender
9. Escrow Reviews Demands and Documents
10. Obtain Loan Approval and Order Loan Documents
11. *(Tracks merge)*
12. Escrow Reviews File and Verifies All Conditions Have Been Met
13. Escrow Receives Loan Documents from Lender
14. Signing is Arranged for Buyer to Sign Loan Documents
15. Loan Documents are Returned to Escrow with Balance of Closing Funds Due
16. Escrow Reviews Returned Loan Documents to Verify Completion and Returns to Lender
17. Funds from Lender are Sent to Title Company and File is Setup for Recording with County Recorder
18. Upon Confirmation of Recording from County, File is Closed and Final Statements Prepared and Disbursed

## Escrow Document Checklists

### Seller Documents Needed
- Supplemental Escrow Instructions
- E-Signature Authorization
- Commission Instructions
- Disbursement of Proceed Instructions
- Property Information (HOA, existing loans, etc.)
- Grant Deed
- 1099 Certification
- CA Form 593
- Statement of Information
- Other Authority Documents required by Title Insurer (Trusts, LLC, Corp.)
- FIRPTA Affidavit or Remittance Statement
- Freeze Authorization (for lines of credit)
- Signed Closing Disclosure (if buyer obtaining institutional financing)
- Signature of Receipt for NHD Report
- Other documents specific to transaction needs

### Buyer Documents Needed
- Supplemental Escrow Instructions
- E-Signature Authorization
- Vesting Instructions
- Loan and Insurance Information
- Preliminary Change of Ownership Report
- Statement of Information
- Other Authority Documents required by Title Insurer (Trusts, LLS, Corp., FinCEN)
- Signature of Receipt for delivered reports and documents

### Buyer Documents to Expect Receiving
- Preliminary Title Report
- Items disclosed in the Title Report
- NHD Report
- Home Warranty Confirmation
- Pest Inspection / Notice of Work Completed
- Homeowners Association Documents
- City Report
- Other documents specific to transaction needs

## Moving Checklist

### 1 Month Prior to Move
- Change address with post office and IRS
- Hire moving company or rent vehicle
- Transfer/get copies of medical records (and school records for children)
- Get referrals from current doctors in new neighborhood
- Update insurance policies (home, auto, etc.)
- Check if insurance covers belongings during move
- Cancel plans with cable, telephone, newspaper, lawn, home security
- Change address with magazine/newspaper subscriptions and creditors

### 2 Weeks Prior to Move
- Cancel utility service for day after move date
- Purchase packing supplies (boxes, tape, markers)
- Make travel arrangements for moving day (special arrangements for children/pets)
- Start packing systematically — type general items per box for a master list
- Set aside items needed immediately, load last

### 1 Week Prior to Move
- Confirm reservations with moving/rental truck company
- Gather important documents (don't pack them for the move)
- Transfer prescriptions to local pharmacy
- Send change of address cards
- Return items that don't belong to you
- Clean (appliances, carpet, walls, etc.)

### Day Before Move
- Cash for movers' tips (if hired)
- Water bottles for everyone helping
- Pack last items, leave out one small suitcase with pajamas and toiletries

### Moving Day
- Write down utility meter readings for final bill
- Supervise movers, confirm final details (address, delivery time)
- Arrive at new house before movers
- Let a friend or family member know travel plans

### After You've Moved
- Change address with DMV — update driver's license and plates (2 weeks for out of state)
- Change address with employer
