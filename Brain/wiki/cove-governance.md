# Governance & Operations

WPBCA operates as a 501(c)(4) mutual benefit corporation (HOA) governing 81 lots. The Cove project proposes creating a parallel 501(c)(3) to support broader community and legal objectives.

## Current Structure: WPBCA

Governed by the 2009 Restated Declaration and 2012 Bylaws. Davis-Stirling compliant.

**Board:** 5 directors, 1-year terms, must reside in tract 35%+ of year.

**Voting:** Secret ballot required for director elections, assessments, bylaw/CC&R amendments, and director removal. No quorum requirement for secret ballot votes. General quorum: 1/3 of members. Assessment quorum: 1/2. Cumulative voting authorized. Proxy voting permitted.

**Assessments:** One membership per building site. Annual and special assessments authorized. Collection/lien procedures defined. Assessment amounts based on County assessed valuation.

**Architectural Committee:** 3 members. Approval authority over all construction, planting, and alterations. Derives authority from Declaration No. One.

**Ocean access:** Two deeded private easements to the Pacific Ocean. Maintaining them is the Association's core responsibility.

## The Problem: Decades of Underinvestment

Dues under $20/month. An association that treated the most valuable coastal property in California like a casual beach hangout. Ocean access paths need maintenance. Most members have never read the governing documents. The dormant HOA got away with being low-key because nobody was looking.

Now they are looking.

## Proposed: 501(c)(3) Foundation

A separate public benefit nonprofit ("Cove Foundation" or "Abalone Shore Heritage Foundation") to handle the broader mission that extends beyond WPBCA's 81 lots:

**WPBCA (501(c)(4))** handles: HOA governance, member assessments, community maintenance, board elections, CC&R enforcement for Tract 14649.

**Cove Foundation (501(c)(3))** handles: Digital document archive, Lot H parcel mapping (peninsula-wide), governance platform technology, heritage preservation, community coalition building, CC&R research and enforcement tools.

**Formation steps:** Articles of Incorporation ($30) → EIN (free, instant) → 501(c)(3) application ($275-$600, 2-6 weeks for 1023-EZ) → California tax exemption → AG registration. Total timeline: 2-4 months.

**Revenue model:** Tax-deductible donations, grants (historic preservation, coastal conservation, civic technology, environmental justice), platform licensing (other HOAs), data services (parcel research, Lot H verification, document retrieval).
