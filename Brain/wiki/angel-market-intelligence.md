# Angel Market Intelligence

## Summary

First comprehensive analysis of Angel's CRMLS dataset: 2,301 listings across 18 MLS areas on the Palos Verdes peninsula, covering July 2023 through April 2026. 1,468 closed transactions with prices. 1,250 market snapshots computed (850 monthly, 300 quarterly, 100 annual) across 25 geographic areas. This article captures what the data tells us — the foundation for market reports, neighborhood page stats, and Angel Agent tools.

## Peninsula-Wide Trends (Jul 2023 – Mar 2026)

### Median Close Price: Stable Around $2M

The peninsula median close price has oscillated between $1.75M and $2.35M for 34 months with no clear upward or downward trend. The market is not crashing and not surging — it's cycling around a $2M center of gravity with seasonal swings.

**Key months:**
- Peak: Sep 2023 at $2,225,000 (44 closings) and Sep 2025 at $2,250,000 (70 closings)
- Trough: Sep 2024 at $1,747,500 (36 closings — anomalous low month)
- Recent: Mar 2026 at $2,017,000 (46 closings)

**YoY comparison (Mar 2025 vs Mar 2026):** -10.4% on median price, but volume is nearly identical (45 vs 46 closings). The price dip reflects mix shift, not a market decline.

### Volume: Strong and Seasonal

Monthly closings range from 24 to 70. The pattern is seasonal — spring/summer volume peaks (May-Jul: 50-64 closings/month), winter dips (Dec-Jan: 25-37 closings/month). This is normal for Southern California coastal markets.

**2025 was the strongest year in our dataset:** 577 total closings (peninsula-wide), up from 530 in 2024. Sep 2025 was the single busiest month at 70 closings.

### Days on Market: Getting Slower

DOM has been trending up. Peninsula-wide median DOM moved from 8 days (Jul 2023) to 16 days (Mar 2026). The market is still fast by national standards, but it's not the 2023 frenzy anymore.

**Seasonal DOM pattern:**
- Spring (Mar-Apr): 10-16 days — fastest
- Summer (Jun-Aug): 15-27 days — steady
- Fall-Winter (Oct-Jan): 28-58 days — slowest
- Jan 2026 was notably slow at 58 days median DOM

### Price Per Square Foot: $800-$950 Range

Peninsula-wide PPSF has been remarkably stable: $830-$950 for most months. This is the most reliable pricing metric because it normalizes for home size.

### Inventory: Tight

Apr 2026 shows 97 active listings against 4 closings in the month — a 24.2 months-of-supply headline number. But April is still early; the spring closings haven't hit yet. Looking at rolling quarterly numbers is more meaningful: Q1 2026 had 107 closings, which would put real inventory around 2.7 months — still a seller's market.

### List-to-Close Ratio: Above 100%

Recent months show list-to-close ratios at or above 100% (101.8% in Apr 2026), meaning buyers are paying at or above asking. This is consistent with a supply-constrained market where well-priced homes attract competitive offers.

## Market Segmentation

The peninsula isn't one market — it's three overlapping markets:

### Segment 1: Sub-$2M (Entry/Starter)
- **Areas:** Eastview, South Shores, Silver Spur, Mira Catalina, Peninsula Center
- **Buyers:** First-time PV buyers, move-up from inland, downsizers from estates
- **Dynamics:** Fastest DOM (15-26 days), most competitive, negotiable but not soft
- **Content angle:** "You can live on the Hill for under $2M — here's how"

### Segment 2: $2M–$4M (Core Market)
- **Areas:** Lunada Bay, PV Dr North, Valmonte, Country Club, Los Verdes, PV Dr East
- **Buyers:** Families with school-age kids, established professionals, move-up from Beach Cities
- **Dynamics:** Moderate DOM (12-34 days), price-per-sqft premium for ocean views/walkability
- **Content angle:** "The sweet spot — where families want to be"

### Segment 3: $4M+ (Luxury/Estate)
- **Areas:** Rolling Hills, Malaga Cove, La Cresta, Monte Malaga, PV Dr South
- **Buyers:** Ultra-high-net-worth, custom build/teardown, equestrian, estate buyers
- **Dynamics:** Longest DOM (47-71 days), scarcity-driven pricing, unique properties
- **Content angle:** "One-of-a-kind properties that define the Hill"

## Data Infrastructure

This analysis runs from the `market_snapshots` table in the Cove database:
- **1,250 snapshots** (850 monthly + 300 quarterly + 100 annual)
- **25 geographic areas** (18 MLS areas + 6 cities + 1 peninsula-wide)
- **34 months** of monthly data (Jul 2023 – Apr 2026)
- **CLI:** `flask market snapshot`, `flask market trend`, `flask market summary`
- **Service:** `cove/angel/market.py` — `compute_all_snapshots()`, `get_trend()`, `get_snapshot()`
- **Model:** `cove/models/market_snapshot.py` — `MarketSnapshot`

Snapshots are recomputed after each weekly CRMLS import. YoY comparisons are computed inline. Low-confidence snapshots (< 5 closings) are flagged in `raw_data.low_confidence`.
