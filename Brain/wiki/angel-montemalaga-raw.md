## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Strictly residential, upscale hilltop neighborhood — no commercial or multi-family housing
- Highest elevation in PVE (up to 1,380 feet)
- Positioned between Malaga Cove (west) and Lunada Bay (south), RPV borders to east
- Covers roughly one square mile
- No ocean-frontage lots, but elevation delivers panoramic ocean, Catalina Island, and coastline views from many properties
- Most new construction in PVE is happening in Montemalaga

### Housing Stock
- Contains many of the largest homes on the entire PV Peninsula
- Lots are among the biggest in PVE — single largest home approximately 39,000 sq ft
- Architectural styles: Mediterranean to contemporary
- Typical listing range $1.1M–$3.2M, large estates well above

### Demographics
- Resident sentiment (Nextdoor): Beach access, beautiful, clean, dog-friendly, family-friendly, peaceful, quiet, safe, well-maintained

## 2. Dining & Coffee

Montemalaga is purely residential — nearest dining at two village plazas within ~5 min drive.

### Malaga Cove Plaza (west, ~5 min)
- **Malaga Cove Ranch Market** — family-owned 25+ years; deli, bakery, wine & beer bar; rotisserie chicken, enchiladas, baklava, lasagna. Community institution.
- Salt & Pearl, The Little Oliva, Bettolino Kitchen, Serendipity Restaurant nearby

### Lunada Bay Plaza (south, ~5 min)
- **Lunada Kitchen (Lunada Market & Deli)** — locally roasted coffee, brunch, handcrafted burgers, farm-to-table. Open daily 8am–8pm.
- **Black Bamboo Sushi** — hidden gem, small dining room and sushi bar
- Raffaello's Pizza, Salsa Verdes, Tomatillo Express Mexican Grill

Source: Yelp, Malaga Cove Ranch Market, Lunada Kitchen

## 6. Lifestyle (Views, Trails, Ocean Access)

### Views
Montemalaga's 1,380-foot elevation delivers some of the most commanding ocean panoramas on the Peninsula — sweeping views of the Pacific, Catalina Island, Santa Monica Bay, coastline from Malibu to Long Beach. Many homes have unobstructed sunset views.

### Ocean Trails Reserve
- 119 acres, RPV. Trailhead at Montemalaga Drive near Basswood. Open sunrise to sunset.
- Restored with 250,000 native plants; habitat for federally threatened California gnatcatcher and cactus wren
- Full hike: 4.5 miles, 800 ft elevation gain, ~2.5 hours, excellent ocean views and tide pool access

### Malaga Canyon Trail
- Reserve with coastal sage scrub, purple sage, spring fuchsia blooms
- Trailhead also at Montemalaga Drive near Basswood

### Malaga Creek Trail
- Easy, family-friendly trail along creek through lush vegetation with waterfowl habitat

### Bluff Cove to Lunada Bay Trail
- 1.7 miles, moderate, 173 ft elevation gain, 30-60 minutes. Spectacular coastal scenery.

### Beach Access
- **Bluff Cove** — steep trail descent, tide pools at low tide; parking at Paseo del Mar and PV Drive West
- **Lunada Bay** — rocky beach with tide pools; access via steep trails across Paseo Del Mar from Oakley Road and Avenida Mirola
- **Flat Rock Point** — accessible via steep spur trail from Bluff Cove descent

### Parks
- Farnham Martin Park (hosts summer concerts)
- Malaga Cove area parks and PVE Shoreline Preserve
- Ernie Howlett Park (RHE) — summer movie nights and community events

Source: AllTrails, PV/SB Sierra Club, PVPLC, RPV Nature Preserves, California Beaches, Outdoor SoCal
