## 1. Neighborhood Facts

- Incorporated January 24, 1957. One of four PV Peninsula cities.
- ~1,739 population (2020), ~700 homes. One of smallest cities in LA County.
- 2.99 sq mi. Population density ~581/sq mi.
- **Gated city** — one of only a handful of gated incorporated cities in the US. Three entry gates staffed 24/7. All roads private.
- **Entirely residential** — zero commercial, no multi-family, no sidewalks, no streetlights, no traffic lights. Minimum lot: 1 acre.
- Homes primarily large single-story ranch-style, traditionally white. Developed by A.E. Hanson (also developed Hidden Hills).
- **Equestrian mandate:** All properties must contain stables/barns or reserve space — prevents overdevelopment, preserves equestrian character.
- Median household income $250,001+. Third-highest median house value in US.
- Governed by City Council + RHCA (Rolling Hills Community Association) which manages amenities and trails.

## 6. Lifestyle

### Equestrian
- **26 miles maintained bridle trails**
- 2 riding rings (Caballeros Ring, Hix Ring — residents and guests only)
- Mandatory stable/barn provisions on every lot
- Horses and riders daily on trails

### Privacy & Exclusivity
Three staffed gates, private roads, no through-traffic. One of the most private residential communities in Southern California.

### Rural Character
No sidewalks, no streetlights, no traffic signals. Rolling hills with native vegetation, mature trees, panoramic ocean/canyon views.

### Amenities
- 3 RHCA tennis courts, Tennis Club open to all residents since 2014
- **Rolling Hills Country Club** — private, golf, dining, social
- Nearby: George F Canyon Nature Preserve, PV Nature Preserve, Portuguese Bend Reserve

Source: RHCA Bridle Trails, Rolling Hills CC, Jake Sullivan Homes
