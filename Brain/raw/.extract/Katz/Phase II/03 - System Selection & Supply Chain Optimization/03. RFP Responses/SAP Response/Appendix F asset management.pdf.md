ASSET MANAGEMENT AND THE ABILITY TO COMPETE

For manufacturers and distributors, getting products to market
quickly and cost-effectively is paramount. To do so, they have to
get the most out of their facilities and equipment – the assets
that make it possible to produce and deliver products.

In managing those assets, you have to ensure that they perform
at peak levels and, at the same time, keep capital and mainte-
nance costs down. You have to track an array of assets across
locations and over time to optimize investment, maintenance,
and repair. You have to make sure equipment and maintenance
procedures comply with safety and work regulations. And with
processes increasingly extending across corporate boundaries,
you have to work closely with suppliers and partners to manage
your assets properly.

In short, you have to keep close watch on a complex, changing
array of assets. In a competitive, cost-conscious world, doing it
right is critical. If assets are managed well, they can help shorten
production cycle times and boost profits. If not, they can add
costs, slow responsiveness and, ultimately, impair your compa-
ny’s ability to compete.

To help you manage your company’s assets as effectively as
possible, SAP offers mySAPTM Product Lifecycle Management
(mySAPTM PLM), an e-business solution that provides a full
range of asset life-cycle management capabilities. mySAP PLM
supports far more than classical enterprise asset management.
With it, you can manage physical assets and equipment through
the entire life cycle, from investment planning to operation,
obsolescence, and asset replacement. And you can keep assets –
and your company – performing at the highest levels.

END-TO-END ASSET MANAGEMENT
The asset life-cycle management capabilities of mySAP PLM
provide you with a comprehensive end-to-end solution. They
help you manage the selection, purchase, and installation of
equipment. With mySAP PLM, you can monitor and maintain
assets; perform equipment repairs, modifications, upgrades, and
refurbishment; and track costs for individual assets, production
lines, and entire facilities. You can share drawings, manuals,
service bulletins, and parts information, and you can collabo-
rate across the company and with external partners to optimize
your use of assets.

Manage All Your Assets with Precision
mySAP PLM lets you manage fleets, buildings, equipment –
virtually any asset – in detail. You can track equipment and
serial numbers; manage warranties, bills of material, and
change orders; monitor service level agreements; and analyze
equipment history.

Perform Preventive Maintenance
You can avoid malfunctions and breakdowns with preventative
maintenance features that help you identify potential problems
early on. The solution supports various maintenance approach-
es, including performance-based, time-based, condition-based
and predictive maintenance, test equipment management, and
automatic measurement transfer.

Ensure Safety During Maintenance Procedures
mySAP PLM helps you comply with safety and environmental
regulations so you can provide a safe working environment for
your maintenance staff. Work-clearance management enables

you to manage lockout or tagout processes, maintenance
processes, and work approvals, and you can draw on a time-
saving work clearance document library.

Perform Repairs with Speed and Accuracy
mySAP PLM provides work-order-cycle features that help you
repair malfunctioning equipment quickly and efficiently. It lets
you see the complete maintenance process flow, including all
the equipment, tools, and techniques involved. It also supports
notification processing, work order resource planning, work
order scheduling, work order costing, and execution and
completion.

Collaborate with Manufacturers and MRO Suppliers
With e-procurement tools and tight integration with supplier
relationship management (SRM) applications, you can work
with your business partners to check the availability of spare
parts, conduct business-to-business procurement of supplies,
use Web-based illustrated parts catalogs, and process returns
and repairs. You can also take advantage of online exchanges to
find and compare vendors to ensure quality sources of supply.

Stay in Touch with Field Personnel
mySAP PLM supports a wide range of mobile and wireless
devices, including PDAs, mobile computers, smart phones, and
pagers. That means you can confirm orders and collect data
and measurements directly from the plant floor, quickly dis-
patch employees to respond to problems, empower decision
makers in the field, and manage maintenance operations –
anytime, anywhere.

Deliver Asset-Management Information Where it’s Needed
You can use Web-based enterprise portal technology and con-
tent that’s tailored for asset-management processes. The portal,
which is part of mySAP PLM, is essentially an information cock-
pit that gives your employees and your supply chain partners a
single point of access to the full range of information, applica-
tions, tools, and services they need to work and collaborate.

mySAP PLM lets you draw on a wide range of information and
processes to manage assets as effectively as possible. With its
open, standards-based architecture, it can be integrated easily
with SAP and non-SAP systems. That means that you can link
it with everything from supply chain management, customer
relationship management (CRM), and finance solutions to
process control, CAD, and supervisory control and data
acquisition (SCADA) systems – and work with a comprehensive,
accurate view of assets and maintenance activities across the
enterprise.

PUTTING ASSETS TO WORK FOR YOUR BUSINESS
With the asset life-cycle management capabilities of mySAP
PLM, you can sort through the complexities of tracking and
maintaining equipment and facilities. You can share accurate,
integrated information and work with a holistic view of assets.
And you can collaborate across the enterprise and the supply
chain to ensure that you get the most out of those assets. And
that can help you:

ment expenses; closer tracking of operations, safety, and
maintenance; and better-informed capital investment
decisions

• Improve plant and equipment performance with preventive
maintenance and fast repair capabilities that help reduce

planning, operation, maintenance, and replacement

• Respond to change with mobile access to information,

improved management of upgrades and maintenance, the

based on accurate, up-to-date information and global enter-

• Support competitive strategies with assets that are performing

time- and cost-to-market, streamline product launches and
