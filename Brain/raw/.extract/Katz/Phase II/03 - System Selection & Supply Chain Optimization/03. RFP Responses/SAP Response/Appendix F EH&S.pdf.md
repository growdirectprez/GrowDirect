CHALLENGES
Today, companies around the world must contend with increas-
ing complexity – not only in products and processes, but also in
the growing number of regulations focusing on occupational
health and safety and environmental issues.

Keeping up with those rules and laws can be a time-consuming,
detail-laden effort. It involves the careful tracking of changing
regulations, shipping requirements, waste-disposal restrictions,
product documentation, and a wealth of other information.
And regulations can concern not only engineering, but also
production, sales, distribution, and other areas of the company.

It's not easy. And managing environment, health, and safety
(EH&S) issues is becoming more and more important. Failure to
comply with regulations can result in high fines and penalties.
And handling EH&S issues effectively can pay off in everything
from reduced risks to employees to lower costs and an
enhanced corporate image.

To help companies reap such benefits, SAP® offers mySAPTM
Product Lifecycle Management (mySAP PLM) – a powerful solu-
tion that gives you the tools and capabilities you need to manage
the entire range of today's sophisticated EH&S processes, in all
areas of your company and throughout the complete product
life cycle.

SIMPLIFYING A COMPLEX WORLD
With the environment, health and safety management capabili-
ties of mySAP PLM (mySAP PLM EH&S), you can keep track of
complex, changing regulations. Manage data and documents.
Support EH&S activities in everything from new product devel-
opment and manufacturing to service and maintenance. And
optimize and automate EH&S processes.

EH&S issues cross many corporate boundaries, and every com-
pany has its own unique processes and requirements. mySAP
PLM EH&S lets you manage EH&S processes globally, and it can
be easily adapted to meet your organization's specific needs – as
well as the requirements of various laws and regulations world-
wide. What's more, it can be integrated with non-SAP and SAP
solutions. That means you can easily use EH&S information and
processes in areas such as human resources, finance, and supply
chain management to boost the efficiency and effectiveness of
EH&S efforts across the company.

mySAP PLM EH&S is designed to help you excel at:
• Storing and managing EH&S data

mySAP PLM EH&S provides a flexible specification database
that lets you track substances, exposures, packaging, danger-
ous-goods classifications, and waste codes. Data can be easily
changed to reflect changes in the law, and the system
maintains an accurate log of such changes for record keeping.
Environmental protection and work safety data can be made
available across the company, and comprehensive search and
output functions help you find information quickly and easily.

• Managing documents

You can produce and maintain a variety of documents, such
as material safety data sheets, transport emergency cards
(tremcards), standard operating procedures, and labels. With
the product safety functions in mySAP PLM EH&S, you can
either create reports manually or generate them automatically
when certain events occur – when, for example, a customer
purchases a product that requires a material safety data sheet.
The system also creates and ships reports automatically when
specification data changes, helping ensure that customers
have up-to-date safety information. The ability to easily create
and send reports, combined with the central storage of infor-
mation, makes the EH&S capabilities of mySAP PLM especial-
ly well suited to hazardous-substance management.

• Keeping track of dangerous goods

With mySAP PLM EH&S, you can easily create accurate
dangerous-goods documentation. mySAP PLM EH&S can
automatically supply that data to other solutions for use in
delivery notes and packing lists. It also checks to determine
whether a planned means of transport – such as road, rail, sea,
or air – is appropriate, and helps ensure that only those ship-
ments that comply with regulations are allowed to leave com-
pany premises.

• Managing waste disposal

mySAP PLM EH&S helps you dispose of hazardous and
nonhazardous waste as efficiently as possible, while complying
with all relevant national and international rules and regula-
tions. It helps you choose suitable disposal firms, allocate dis-
posal costs among internal departments, and ensure that
authorized waste quantities are not exceeded. You can create
required disposal documents and reports, such as waste life-
cycle analyses, with a few clicks of the mouse.

• Keep up with change

The flexibility of the solution's EH&S tools lets you react
quickly to increasingly frequent changes to regulations – and
keep pace with those changes on a global basis across the enter-
prise.

• Control costs

mySAP PLM helps you optimize and automate EH&S processes
and operate with consistent, accurate information along the
complete product life cycle – saving you both time and money.

mySAP PLM EH&S provides industrial hygiene and safety and
occupational health functions that help you identify and min-
imize risks to employees' health – and comply with safety laws
and regulations in doing so. Drawing on information and
functions from the asset life-cycle management capabilities of
mySAP PLM, you can arrange, follow up on, and assess plant
maintenance tasks. And if an accident does occur, you can en-
ter and archive the relevant data and quickly create accident
reports, statistics, and analyses to meet legal requirements and
drive accident-prevention efforts.

• Supporting preventive healthcare

With mySAP PLM EH&S, you can track employee healthcare
treatments and examinations and monitor the results and
diagnoses. The system can also draw on information from
human resources and other areas to indicate when specific
employees are due for examinations, taking into account the
individual's exposures in the workplace, tasks, and age.
