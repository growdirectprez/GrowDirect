automated processes, their labor management techniques
remain mired in manual limbo. Like most large retailers, you’re
probably not even trying to manage your workforce in a
consistent, structured, and automated fashion. You’re letting
each individual store do it manually. This can lead to:
• Inconsistent scheduling techniques and policies
• Too many or too few employees on the floor at any given

moment

• Favoritism in the scheduling process
• Erratic submission of scheduling reports

The painful results: lost sales, lower profits, reduced customer
loyalty, and decreased competitiveness.

Controlling payroll expenditures, providing excellent customer
service while increasing sales revenue, and gaining overall
visibility are key objectives of effective workforce management.
SAP StaffWorks, an integrated and automated solution, can help
you achieve these objectives. Scheduling consistency, accuracy,
and objectivity can be enhanced via specific organizational
standards and comprehensive employee information that work
in conjunction with established optimization processes. These
processes eliminate ineffective labor practices and increase sales.
Simply put, the right people work at the right time within a
prescribed budget.

SAP StaffWorks integrates four task-specific functions to deliver
such a solution. Here’s how.

SAP StaffWorks works with SAP Business Information Warehouse
to allow enterprise-wide visibility into your key, store-level labor
metrics and sales data. It is the first decision-support solution
developed for workforce management purposes. You can review
information, such as planned versus actual sales, payroll percent
and dollars, sales per hour, and coverage statistics.  All this
empowers you make better, faster, and more cost-effective
operational decisions proactively.

The SAP StaffWorks parameter manager gives corporate-level
managers the power to control fast-changing labor parameters at
all store locations quickly and easily. You can control a range of
variables, including tasks, labor standards, operating hours,
organizational structure, and pay rules. With this information,
you can make the corresponding changes in store configurations
quickly.

SAP StaffWorks in-store function is an intelligent workforce
management system that evaluates millions of scheduling
variables, applies weighted factors to your business priorities, and
creates the optimum schedule for each location in your
operation. Using such criteria as employee skills and availability,
labor laws, point-of-sale (POS) data, workloads, budgetary
constraints, payroll objectives, and floor coverage, SAP
StaffWorks creates the optimum schedule for each location in
your operation. This dramatically reduces costs and improves
coverage.

Finally, StaffWorks’ time and attendance capabilities streamline
and simplify the collection and reporting of time data in the
stores. Seamlessly integrated into the SAP StaffWorks solution,
the time and attendance capabilities provide managers with
automated in-store control of employee time accounting and
payroll procedures.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The retail environment present some tough challenges. You
have to deal with changing economic conditions, product crazes,
cultural influences and environmental conditions. These factors
can lead to excessive payroll expenditures, inadequate customer
service, lost sales revenue, and an ineffective workforce. And it’s
exacerbated by a lack of visibility, lack of control, and poor
execution. Retailers need a complete workforce management
strategy and a consistent and accurate process that provides
visibility and control, and allows efficient execution of objectives.
SAP StaffWorks is the solution.

Despite fluctuating economic conditions, the unpredictability of
retail trends, and other environmental factors, SAP StaffWorks
has consistently demonstrated its ability to provide complete
visibility, deliver insights on demand, and allow control over one
of the most significant expenses associated with retailing – labor.
Working as an integrated suite of tools, SAP StaffWorks has
proven to be an effective method for achieving measurable and
significant results.
