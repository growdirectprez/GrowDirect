PHASE 2: MARKETING ANALYTICS
Marketing analytics is an intermediate solution that offers a full
range of tools to leverage raw customer data into more com-
plex, strategic insights, and decision support. It delivers a more
extensive suite of analytical methods, ranging from generic pro-
cedures to ready-to-run business solutions that you can apply
to any number of customer and market scenarios. It provides a
comprehensive answer to all your critical customer questions (cid:150)
from behavior modeling and value assessment to customer
portfolio analyses (cid:150) that lets you create a richer, more accurate
picture of your customers.

For example, you can use mySAP Retail Analytical CRM with a
third-party data mining tool to mine your anonymous per-
receipt POS data to answer critical consumer behavior ques-
tions. What are the association rules within buying patterns?
Can these rules be used for cross-selling or up-selling? What
new customer segments can you discover based on buying pat-
terns?

You can also apply the solution to known customer sales data (cid:150)
from loyalty card shopper data and Web site orders to data from
your mail order business (cid:150) to develop even deeper insights.
What customer segment will be most responsive to your next
promotion? Which customer buying patterns provide the high-
est average order? Which are most profitable?

With mySAP Retail Analytical CRM, you can analyze and
strengthen customer relationships in the context of retail mar-
kets. Market and competitor research can help you identify and

BUILDING LONG-TERM, PROFITABLE CUSTOMER

RELATIONSHIPS
mySAP Retail Analytical CRM delivers benefits in several ways.
First, you get the answers you need to make critical business
decisions about your customers and your relationship with
them. For example, you can calculate the value that each cus-

your resources on those with the greatest potential for long-
