Strategic Planning
JDA Consulting Services Group conducts Strategic Planning meetings to help you plan your business and IT initiatives.  This forum also provides JDA with a better understanding of your overall business and goals.  In this planning session, you will share your business vision for the next 1-3 years by presenting your high-level strategies and timelines.   Our senior management will respond with JDA’s future product direction, integration and product portfolio.   Product overviews may be requested to clarify an interest and we can coordinate discussions with specific JDA individuals as needed.  Once the visions are clear, we will facilitate a working session to hear your issues, priorities and begin the actual planning.   We will have the appropriate people on hand from Consulting Services, Business Development, Sales, Product Management and Product Development to offer input and insight.  As a team we will spend our time discussing and resolving any issues, validating assumptions, assigning priorities, and working toward the deliverable of a one to three year IT plan.   


Initial Planning & Training
During the Project Planning and Kick Off meeting we introduce key project team members discuss and ratify project assumptions, project scope, strategy, critical project dates and the overall project approach.  We will work toward agreement on staffing requirements, review communication and reporting requirements, administration, budget, and schedule the Business Process Analysis workshops.  At the conclusion of this meeting JDA will prepare a detailed project plan and scope document.  Subsequent to the Business Process Analysis phase, we can assist your organization with the initial load of the application and conduct software configuration and hardware orientation with your project team.   JDA also strongly advises that your management team attend the product overview courses offered by JDA University.  By developing a plan and attending the overview course, your team will be better prepared to participate in the integration of the business process and supporting system functionality discussed in Discovery and Design phase.   

Discovery and Design
This phase focuses around Business Process Analysis (BPA) which is a series of collaborative tasks between you and JDA.  The purpose of the BPA phase is to help align business activities and systems for optimal performance.  Key personnel are interviewed to understand the business workflow.  The interviews lead to team workshops that review current processes for strengths and inefficiencies, identify business performance improvement opportunities and determine your optimal system usage and configuration.  To create your future vision, JDA has developed baseline process models.  The models combine “best practice” business processes with the functionality of JDA’s supporting technology.  Through this cooperative effort the team will define processes for both business activities and optimal systems support.

Construction & Quality Control
JDA can lead your team in configuring your system based on the decisions made in the BPA.  Configuration services include option settings, customizing the user interface, application parameter set-up, recommendations for establishing organizational structures, and configuring the system data interchange module.  JDA can also provide assistance with the development of test scripts and the test plan.  During this phase the JDA project team works to ensure that the software, hardware and operating procedures are in place before training end users.  JDA can provide on-site support to validate that all operating procedures are in place, system data is correct, interfaces to other systems are exchanging information correctly and software enhancements are functioning according to specification.

Implementation
During this phase of the project JDA can provide planning for end-user training, assist with a cut-over migration plan, a pre-implementation review, and assist with the conversion of production data and validation.  JDA can also provide on-site go live and post implementation support, and a post system review.

Post Implementation Support & Audit
Post Implementation Support and Audit ensures that the client environment is running smoothly and to your satisfaction prior to the transition to JDA’s Client Support Services.  A post implementation audit can be done to review all pertinent project documentation, log any outstanding issues and plan a path to resolution.      

Project Management
Your JDA Project Manager will provide leadership in all phases of your project.  The JDA Project Manger will be responsible for project planning, status reporting, issues resolution, resource coordination, budget review and maintenance.  Your Project Manager will pro-actively work to keep the project on track, and will be available on a day-to-day basis to facilitate each aspect of the project.  In addition your Project Manager will ensure a plan is followed throughout the project to provide your organization with the skills and knowledge to be completely independent of JDA once the project is complete.
Change Management
With this service, JDA process consultants can drive the changes identified in Business Process Analysis sessions, develop communication plans and incorporate business process decisions into testing and training.  The lead change manager facilitates the implementation of new processes within the organization throughout all phases of your implementation.

Business Process Optimization
This engagement is an extension of the Business Process Analysis and Design phase in which JDA process consultants can assist in the alignment of your organization to support the new business processes and system procedures.  With this engagement JDA can provide job descriptions, develop performance models, constantly measure implementation results, and conduct periodic performance evaluations.

Risk Management
Risk Management is a service in which JDA’s risk management consultants can facilitate risk assessments and ensure the proper action plans are defined, and followed up on after each project milestone. This service can ensure that changes to the project team, business environment and other internal or external factors are recognized and addressed with a timely action plan.
