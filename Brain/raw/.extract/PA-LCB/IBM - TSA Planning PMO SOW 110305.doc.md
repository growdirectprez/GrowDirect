Overview 
IBM appreciates the opportunity to provide this Statement of Work (SOW) to continue to assist The Sports Authority (TSA) with the Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project.  IBM will support TSA with the Wave 1 launch, the planning of the future Wave launches, and the transitioning of the ASP Project Management role to a TSA Project Manager.  This document amends the prior SOW dated September 27, 2005, and outlines IBM’s involvement in the aforementioned activities over an estimated 13 week timeframe.

IBM Statement of Work 
This Statement of Work (SOW) describes the work to be undertaken by IBM (“Services”) under the terms and conditions contained within the Master Consulting Agreement dated 2005 (“Agreement”).  Described within this SOW is the project, which consists of the Deliverable Materials to be provided by IBM, and the IBM responsibilities and related TSA responsibilities to be provided in accordance with the terms of this SOW.  Changes to this SOW will be processed in accordance with the procedure described in Section 10.3.1 of the Agreement. The investigation and the implementation of changes may result in modifications to the Estimated Schedule, Charges, and other terms of this SOW and/or the Agreement.

To the extent there is any contradiction, inconsistency or ambiguity between the terms of this SOW and the Agreement, the SOW will govern. This SOW and the Agreement represent the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter and replace any prior oral or written communications.

Project Scope
This SOW describes the scope of IBM’s involvement in TSA’s Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project efforts.  The scope, timeline and charges for this project are agreed upon within this SOW.  For purposes of this effort, the following represents the project’s scope that can be defined at this time:
IBM will work with TSA Project Management Office, new Project Manager, Project Team, Merchandise Planning, Assortment and Replenishment Planning, Store Operations, Information Systems, Financial, Strategy, Human Resource, and Training teams as well as JDA to accomplish the project scope.
IBM will transfer the creation of the project charter, management of the project deliverables and deadlines, and management of the JDA relationship to the recently hired TSA Project Manager (Kelly Dwight).
Provide overall approach guidance on the Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project charter, project plans and roll-out strategy.

The investigation and/or implementation of any activities and/or deliverables not specified within this SOW may result in modification to the estimated schedule, charges, and/or other terms of the SOW.

Assumptions
This SOW and IBM's estimates are based on the following key assumptions, and those listed in the activities in Section 2.5 IBM Responsibilities. Deviations that arise during the proposed project will be managed through the procedure described in Section 10.3.1 of the Agreement and may result in changes to the Project Scope, Estimated Schedule, Charges and other terms. These adjustments may include charges on a time and materials basis using IBM’s standard rates in effect at such time for any resulting additional work or waiting time. If an assumption deviation is not resolved through the Project Change Control Procedure within 3 days then the issue will be resolved in accordance with an agreed escalation procedure. 

TSA will provide a full time Assortment and Space Planning Transformation project manager (Kelly Dwight) by October 31, 2005.  This manager will be 100% available during the design workshops, project status, checkpoints and recommendation meetings.
TSA will provide dedicated team leads for the business process, data and interface (application development), technical infrastructure, and change management (organizational change, communication management and training).
TSA Assortment and Space Planning Transformation team leads will have A) a detail knowledge of the target business process/ application and architecture requirements B) project implementation experience for all phases in order to identify dependencies/ integration points, create approaches and prioritize activities C) team leadership skills to ensure activities and issues are completed on time and D) time and skills to document gaps, issues, options, decisions and complete deliverables.
JDA and TSA IS will provide knowledgeable resources by the beginning of the project to determine database sizing, technical architecture requirements, and technical implementation resource requirements and associated costs. 
JDA will provide knowledgeable technical and functional resources in accordance to the project plan to assist with project planning, implementation approaches and general education the on the JDA applications for TSA and IBM team members.
TSA IS will provide knowledgeable resources to design and program interfaces, extract, cleanse, and convert required project data from existing TSA systems into Intactix or other TSA systems.
The existing data environment is sufficient and capable of providing appropriate and timely data feeds necessary to support this project.
TSA business team, with documentation support from the TSA IS team, will be responsible for developing the Assortment and Space Planning detail business requirements, process and training documentation in accordance to the work plan. 
It is anticipated that the 2006 roll-out plan may be adjusted for 2005 project findings after this SOW. 
All deliverables will be created using Microsoft Applications (e.g. Excel, PowerPoint, Project).
Chago Santiago, Tony Forde and Debby Flynn will be available for project planning checkpoints, approach sign-offs, deliverable evaluations, issue resolution/ escalation and other related discussions in alignment with the project plan.
TSA will provide on-site building access, office/cube space and normal administrative support such as phone lines, high speed connections, printers, etc for the use of IBM project team members. 
TSA will provide a conference room/ scrum room equipped with white boards, flip charts, conference call and network connection to conduct project team work sessions.

Project Deliverable Materials
During the 13 weeks effort covered by this SOW, IBM will continue to provide project management leadership, advice and guidance to TSA as well as transitioning the project management role to the new TSA project manager.  TSA will be responsible for creating and updating all required project deliverables.


Project Risks
Key personnel and resources are not available during critical times
Current budget constrains scope and needed external resources (e.g. JDA)
Alignment of TSA, JDA and IBM project methodology and approaches
Decisions don’t get made in a timely manner
Scope is not clarified and managed
Benefits are not clearly defined
Delays in getting necessary documentation and data or documentation is not available in a usable format
TSA and JDA personnel do not have the skill set to support their project role to accomplish project deliverables and business solution success
TSA personnel assigned to participate in the project are reluctant to change or are not on board with the project direction or roles

IBM Responsibilities
IBM will provide ongoing project management/leadership for the IBM responsibilities in this SOW.  The purpose of this activity is to provide direction for the IBM project team and to provide a framework for project planning, communications, reporting, procedural and contractual activity.

IBM Program Manager 
Review the SOW and the responsibilities of both parties with appropriate TSA Project Management.
Maintain project communications through appropriate TSA Project Management.
Provide deliverable templates and guidance on documentation and procedural standards for deliverables where necessary.
Jointly refine the project approach with TSA and JDA to assist TSA in its objectives.
Measure and evaluate progress against the project approach with TSA Project Management.
Work with TSA Project Management to address and resolve deviations from the project approach and timelines.
Transition project management activities to TSA project manager including:  project charter, work plan management, JDA relationship management and project team management.
Participate in regularly scheduled project status meetings.
Provide input into the Weekly Status Reports to TSA Project Management.
Help identify, document, and resolve project issues and escalate issues within TSA‘s organization, as necessary.
If necessary, administer changes to this SOW with TSA Project Management.
Resolve deviations from the project schedule caused by IBM.
Work with TSA Project Management to refine the overall project approach as appropriate.
Coordinate and manage the activities of IBM project personnel.

TSA Responsibilities
Included below are TSA responsibilities that are not described in other sections of this SOW.  These include responsibilities related to Project Management, facilities, and business contact information.

TSA Staffing and Key Responsibilities
Throughout the timeline of this project, TSA will designate two part time Assortment and Space Planning Transformation project managers (one from the business and one from IS).  In addition, appropriate members of TSA Management team will participate.  These staff will be involved in the following activities: 
Manage TSA personnel and responsibilities for this project.
Coordinate and facilitate work sessions to produce project decisions and deliverables.
Serve as the interface between IBM and all TSA departments and JDA participating in the project.
Conduct project status and checkpoint meetings.
Obtain and provide information, data, and decisions within twenty four hours of IBM's request if the request is identified as urgent unless TSA and IBM agree in writing to a different response time. 
Review deliverables and work products submitted by IBM and approve or submit requested changes in writing within twenty four hours. 
Work with the IBM Project Manager to resolve deviations from the estimated schedule, which may be caused by TSA or JDA. 
Help identify, document, and resolve project issues and escalate issues within TSA‘s organization, as necessary.
Review with the IBM Project Manager any TSA invoice or billing requirements. 
Make appropriate TSA staff available to provide such assistance as is reasonably required to enable IBM to provide the Services. 

Facilities
Most of the work performed under this SOW will occur at TSA’s offices.  TSA will provide IBM and its personnel with suitable office space, and other accommodations and facilities that IBM may reasonably require to perform the Services, in particular supplies, furniture, computer facilities (PCs), telephone/fax communications, DSL lines and other facilities for all IBM personnel, as needed, while working on the Project. Whenever possible, the IBM project team will be located in a common area that is close to TSA‘s subject matter experts and technical personnel.  All necessary security badges and clearance will be provided for access to TSA buildings and any other required work areas. TSA will be responsible for ensuring that it has appropriate backup, security and virus-checking procedures in place for any computer facilities TSA provides or which may be affected by the Services.
