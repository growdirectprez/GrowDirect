Overview 
IBM appreciates the opportunity to provide this Statement of Work (SOW) to assist The Sports Authority (TSA) on the Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project.  Specifically, we will work with you to develop the project charter, 2005 project plan, high level 2006 roll-out strategy, 2005 budget and estimated 2006 budget covering the critical process, organization and technology changes, including Intactix, to achieve the desired benefits.  This document outlines IBM’s involvement in these activities over an estimated 6 and half week timeframe.

IBM Statement of Work 
This Statement of Work (SOW) describes the work to be undertaken by IBM (“Services”) under the terms and conditions contained within the Master Consulting Agreement dated 2005 (“Agreement”).  Described within this SOW is the project, which consists of the Deliverable Materials to be provided by IBM, and the IBM responsibilities and related TSA responsibilities to be provided in accordance with the terms of this SOW.  Changes to this SOW will be processed in accordance with the procedure described in Section 10.3.1 of the Agreement. The investigation and the implementation of changes may result in modifications to the Estimated Schedule, Charges, and other terms of this SOW and/or the Agreement.

To the extent there is any contradiction, inconsistency or ambiguity between the terms of this SOW and the Agreement, the Agreement will govern. This SOW and the Agreement represent the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter and replace any prior oral or written communications.

Project Scope
This SOW describes the scope of IBM’s involvement in TSA’s Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project efforts.  The scope, timeline and charges for this project are agreed upon within this SOW.  For purposes of this effort, the following represents the project’s scope that can be defined at this time:
IBM will work with TSA Project Management Office, Merchandise Planning, Assortment and Replenishment Planning, Store Operations, Information Systems, Financial teams as well as JDA to accomplish the project scope.
Provide overall approach guidance to deliverable development of the project charter, project plans and budget.
Assist in creating and defining the following elements of the project charter: project scope, goals and objectives, team structure and roles, operating guidelines, dependencies, critical success factors, high level project communication plan (e.g. status reporting), deliverables and storage framework, issue documentation and issue management procedures.  Note:  Deliverables will be developed on an as needed basis due to understood priorities.
This 6 and a half week effort will NOT include the business case development, financial modeling, success criteria, benefit definition and metrics for the project.  This will be covered under a separate SOW.
Assist with the validation of project scope, objectives and operating guidelines for the Assortment and Space Planning Transformation Project.
Assist with the integration assessment of the Assortment and Space Planning process and organization initiatives currently managed and underway by TSA.
Assist with the validation of application functionality and data required to support the Assortment and Space planning processes and system capabilities / constraints.
Assist with the development of the 2005 project plan and validated 2006 roll-out strategy taking into account previous process planning, business rhythms, concurrent change initiatives, and system and resource constraints.  Note:  It is anticipated that the 2006 roll-out plan may be adjusted for 2005 project findings after this SOW. 
Assist with the adjustment/ development of the 2005 and estimated 2006 implementation budgets based upon desired business objectives and implementation plan.
Assortment and Space Planning business requirements will be completed by the TSA business team and assistance is NOT covered under this SOW. 
Cost estimates outside of consulting fees will NOT be the responsibility of IBM.  This includes but not limited to technical hardware, software, and resource cost estimates.

The investigation and/or implementation of any activities and/or deliverables not specified within this SOW may result in modification to the estimated schedule, charges, and/or other terms of the SOW.

Assumptions
This SOW and IBM's estimates are based on the following key assumptions, and those listed in the activities in Section 2.5 IBM Responsibilities. Deviations that arise during the proposed project will be managed through the procedure described in Section 10.3.1 of the Agreement and may result in changes to the Project Scope, Estimated Schedule, Charges and other terms. These adjustments may include charges on a time and materials basis using IBM’s standard rates in effect at such time for any resulting additional work or waiting time. If an assumption deviation is not resolved through the Project Change Control Procedure within 3 days then the issue will be resolved in accordance with an agreed escalation procedure. 

TSA will provide two part time Assortment and Space Planning Transformation project managers (one from the business and one from IS) by the beginning of the project.  These managers will be 100% available during the joint planning, gap analysis and project status, checkpoints and recommendation meetings.
TSA Assortment and Space Planning Transformation project managers will have A) a detail knowledge of the target business process/ application and architecture requirements B) project implementation experience for all phases in order to identify dependencies/ integration points, create approaches and prioritize activities C) team leadership skills to ensure activities and issues are completed on time and D) time and skills to document gaps, issues, options, decisions and complete deliverables,  
JDA and TSA IS will provide knowledgeable resources by the beginning of the project to determine database sizing, technical architecture requirements, and technical implementation resource requirements and associated costs. 
JDA will provide knowledgeable technical and functional resources in accordance to the project plan to assist with project planning, implementation approaches and general education the on the JDA applications for TSA and IBM team members.
TSA IS will provide knowledgeable resources to extract, cleanse, and convert required project data from existing TSA systems.
The existing data environment is sufficient and capable of providing appropriate and timely data feeds necessary to support this project.
TSA business team, with documentation support from the TSA IS team, will be responsible for developing the Assortment and Space Planning detail business requirements in accordance to the work plan. 
It is anticipated that the 2006 roll-out plan may be adjusted for 2005 project findings after this SOW. 
All deliverables will be created using Microsoft Applications (e.g. Excel, PowerPoint, Project).
Chago Santiago, Tony Forde and Debby Flynn will be available for project planning checkpoints, approach sign-offs, deliverable evaluations and other related discussions in alignment with the project plan.
TSA will provide on-site building access, office/cube space and normal administrative support such as phone lines, high speed connections, printers, etc for the use of IBM project team members. 
TSA will provide a conference room/ scrum room equipped with white boards, flip charts, conference call and network connection to conduct project team work sessions.
