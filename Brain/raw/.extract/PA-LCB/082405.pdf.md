Example - New Item Initiatives Based
Upon Robust Consumer Insights

(cid:131) Only 10% of DIY’ers install struts, since the coil spring assembly needs to
be compressed and mounts attached prior to installation. Installing strut
compressors in all stores would be expensive and have both employee
and customer liability.  Installers have to go through the same process.

(cid:131) Solution:  New Product – “Quick Strut” A loaded strut for DIY and

Commercial customers.  Included strut, new coil spring and new mounting
plate – compressed at the factory.  Can be installed as easy as a shock.

(cid:131) Retailer had 1 year exclusive.

(cid:131) Took competitors 2 years to follow.
