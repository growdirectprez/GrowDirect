Objectives
To accurately and efficiently process collections and payments resulting from transactions between PETsMART related companies and transaction-based vendors
Critical Success Factors
Ability to complete transactions in a timely and complete manner
Ability to provide an accurate accounting of inventory to decision makers
Ability to generate purchase orders before invoices are matched
Ability to generate invoices based on shipments
Assumptions
Intercompany transfers encompass special instances of transfers, purchase orders, and invoice payments
Requirements related generally to the transfer, purchase order, and invoice payment portion of the process are assigned to the Transfer - SSG, Purchase Order Management, and Accounts Payable processes
Sporting Dog and UK are considered customers in the receivable process
Sporting Dog Specialties/State Line Tack (including SLT catalog orders) are considered vendors in the payable process
Transactions are at arms length due to FASB reporting requirements
