Assumptions
SAP store terminal will be used for DC to store and store to store transfers while Stockright will be used for vendor to stores purchase orders.
Requirements*
Store receipt information needs to be able to be uploaded from the stores. All required fields need to be captured including(receipt date, freight carrier, cartons, pallets, receiver name, etc.)Sites should also have visibility into all their orders and/or transfers.
Ability to receive a purchase order “complete” or by individual line item at the store.  This functionality will need to be set at the Source/Site level with the ability to have an override at the purchase order level.
Support data entry via a hand held RF(radio frequency) device.  Device must allow the user to specify the document(s) they will be receiving against, scan the UPC codes, and enter the appropriate quantities.  Quantities would then be applied to the appropriate purchase order and line item.
Create purchase orders from goods receipt without reference.  Access will need to be defined at the Source/Site level.
Ability for a site to receive a transfer even if the DC has processed the delivery document in the system.  Currently this occurs when problems with polling occur.  The will not post the receipt quantities until the DC has processed the delivery document.  Exception report will be required to identify instances where the store has received the transfer but the DC has not processed the delivery.
When purchase orders are received/approved/uploaded by the store, unreceived lines should be cancelled unless the backorder flag has been set for that source/site combination.
System needs multiple status codes for purchase orders at the store level.  Status codes are:
Open
In Process
Complete
Approved
Uploaded (to SAP system from store system)
Receipt posting will occur at the line level, not the PO level. For example, 10 articles may be ordered for a given site.  The site receives all ten articles plus an eleventh article that is an invalid article number.  When the purchase order is uploaded from the store, all valid articles should post and an exception report should be generated to identify the invalid article.
When receiving at the store, an exception report should be available to identify discrepancies between the order quantities and received quantities.  This will enable a receiver to audit his performance and correct any errors before the purchase order is approved for upload to the host.
System must be able to combine multiple purchase orders for receiving as a whole.  This is required when a vendor or distributor mixes product from multiple purchase orders on a pallet(s) or truck.  The receiver can receive the entire load as a whole and the system will apply it to the selected purchase orders.  Quantities will be applied to the oldest purchase orders first.

GOODS RECEIPT
New Functional Gaps:
PETsMART needs the capability to recognize and age the liability of a goods receipt prior to invoice entry. SAP currently does not recognize the liability until the invoice is entered. 
Implementation Considerations:
SAP Goods Receipt process is cumbersome: Assumption is that PETsMART will not use the goods receipt entry functionality on the terminal. DC will use .EXE and store will use some other application not finalized yet. (e.g. stores will have the capability to receive via RF and interface to SAP (e.g. Stockright may stay in place or RF would be developed for SAP)
The overview is very good for providing information on inventory types, position, and non sales activity. It is not really a decision support tool because it lacks other key values (e.g. sales)
