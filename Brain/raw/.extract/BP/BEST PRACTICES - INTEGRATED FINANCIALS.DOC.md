Best Practice Option

World class organizations have a fully integrated financial environment where all the data needed to perform business analysis is centrally stored and analysts throughout the organization are able to access the information they need to make decisions.  Integrated financial information environments electronically store and distribute data so that there is no manual re-keying of data for analysis.  

Example:
In 1996, Seagrams Company Ltd began a comprehensive reengineering program where objectives included growth, effectiveness, and efficiency.  Specifically, Seagrams wished to automate and standardize its worldwide financial reporting. In order to achieve these goals, Seagrams reengineered the reporting process, defined IT functional requirements for the process, and selected a state of the art system.  

The new financial report system entails a pipeline which automates and standardizes Seagram’s worldwide financial reporting.  Seagram’s goal is to automate all the manual processes performed by decentralized financial groups.  The automation of the financial reporting system will enable management to have more efficient, timely, standard decision-support reporting while freeing up planning and reporting personnel to provide more analysis.  

The benefits of Seagram’s automated reporting process cover a broad spectrum.  In the future, specific business units will no longer have to prepare standard regional, group and corporate reporting.  The business unit needs only to close the ledger.  The transmission process allows the local finance director to validate the integrity of the data. All currency conversions, reconciliation, consolidations and reporting will be performed in the data warehouse or by the top-end management reporting tool.
