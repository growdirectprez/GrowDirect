Best Practice Option
Best practices for Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) and Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) focus on increasing the capabilities of organizations to support electronic commerce, for both receiving and transmission.  Analysis reveals the following best practices:

Increasing Capabilities to Support EDI/EFT
Maximize the number of invoices received electronically
Maximize the number of payments made electronically
Eliminate manual and paper activities
Automated matching of invoices to purchase order
Interfaces with G/L, AP, AR, and receiving

Key performance indicators and benchmarks include the following:

Performance Measures/Benchmarks	        	Best Practice
Percent of invoices approved through automated matching           	57%	
Percent of payments made electronically                                                                         71%
Percent of employees receiving direct deposit                                                                100%
Percent of invoices received EDI                                                                                        80%

Examples of organizations who have utilized best practices for accounts payable:

A multinational company implemented EDI technology in 6 months.  The results were decreased AP average processing time from 2 – 4 days to 8 hours, less mistakes and miscommunications, and a simplified and faster process for ordering goods.  Improvements in customer satisfaction were attributed to this implementation.

The world’s largest English book publisher, with over $2.3 billion annual revenues in electronic publishing transformed to Internet-based purchasing.  This move allows customers to use Web browsers to directly purchase products and services and integrate all systems from order through management reporting.  The results were accelerated transactions, reduced time and cost for purchasing materials and services, and immediate updating and reporting of data for management.
