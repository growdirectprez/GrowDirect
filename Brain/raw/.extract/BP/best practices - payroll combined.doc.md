Payroll Policy/Process
Best Practice Option
Best practice for payroll processing focuses on streamlining payroll processing.  Analysis reveals the following best practices for payroll processing:

Streamlining Payroll
Standardize pay cycle, i.e. weekly, bi-weekly, monthly
Standardize forms and cycles
Eliminate of all full time pre-payroll clerical activities
Standardize procedure to administer, track, and pay executives
Practice flexible time collection methods

Functional Area/ Process Impacted

Payroll Structure
Best Practice Option
Best practice for payroll processing focuses on centralizing payroll, integrating payroll with other support and operational divisions,  and outsourcing where possible.  Analysis reveals the following best practices for payroll processing:

Centralizing Payroll
Centralized processing for all employees
Oversight and reporting of exceptions only
Ratio of one payroll staff to 500 to 1000 employees

Integrating Payroll
Integrate payroll with human resources and IT divisions

Outsourcing
Effectively use outsourcing to process payroll where applicable

Examples of organizations who have utilized best practices for payroll:

Sears, Roebuck & Co. consolidated payroll, HR, and Systems under one manager.  Payroll centers were reduced from 5 to 2 and HR Centers were reduced from 24 to 2.  In addition, payroll staff decreased from 168 to 68 employees.  HR Center expenses were reduced by 74% and payroll expenses decreased 59%.  In addition, the focus of these employees shifted from data processing to problem solver and advisor.

A large electricity company has centralized and standardized payroll across its many business units under the shared services systems, resulting in a 40% reduction in the number of finance department workers.  This organization went from 34 different payroll systems to 5 regional centers, to one megacenter.

Payroll Technology
Best Practice Option
Best practice for payroll technology centers on improving technological applications, making payroll more accessible to employees, and eliminating manual or paper activities.  Analysis reveal the following best practices for payroll technology:

Improving Technology Applications
Integration of payroll with human resources, accounts payable, and general ledger systems
Flexible time collection methods incorporating technology
Allow remote on-line, real time entry and inquiries

Eliminating Manual or Paper Activities
On-line paycheck stubs
Utilization of automated time entry
Direct deposit payment for all employees
Direct deposit processing to multiple account types and separate institutions
Automatic accrual for vacation and sick leave for each pay cycle
Automatic decrease holiday/sick leave time when time is taken
Provide special hours categories for ad-hoc requirements
