Vendor Management
Objectives
To maximize profit and sales through strong partnerships with our vendors and suppliers.

Vendor Management is the setup and approval of vendors(ranging from first time supplier to full marketing and logistics partners), the management of terms and service standards, the measurement of compliance to established standards, and the development and management of vendor partnerships.
Critical Success Factors
Ability to develop and maintain profitable supplier relationships.
Ability to obtain the right product at the right price in the right place at the right time.
Ability to accurately track and manage vendor performance and allocate purchases accordingly.
Ability to provide assistance and support to suppliers in underdeveloped markets.
Ability to take advantage of a wide range of supplier promotions and discounts.
Ability to maintain complete and accurate vendor information for use across functional areas.
Assumptions
No vendor maintenance is, or will be, performed on legacy WMS or Dallas WMS systems.
Vendor guidelines will focus on accounting, traffic, store, and warehouse compliance standards.
Merchandise Planning will provide vendor-level plans and forecasts to the scorecard.
Requirements
System must have ability to receive long range forecast from vendors.
System must have ability to provide accurate forecasts to vendors.
System must have ability to support collaborative forecasting.
Performance Measures
Amount of coop, rebates, and discount participation from vendors.
Percent of vendor EDI participation.
Average vendor score(vendor scorecard).
Vendor Profitability.

Better Practices Recommendations
Evaluate vendors to determine the level of partnership potential. A minimal level of compliance to standards is required
Compliance standards are set with each vendor regarding:
Product quality
Availability
Net pricing
RTV policy
Carton quality
Packing standards
Carton marking
Unit load specifications
Transportation and routing
Discounts and rebates
Marketing support (include. co-op)
Shared product sales forecasts
Performance monitoring includes measures from all facets of the supply chain inventory management process including:
Order processing errors 
Order fill rate
Order timeliness (delivery/pick-up delays)
Invoice errors
Quality
Profitability
Customer satisfaction
Business reviews are conducted at least annually. The review should consider the performance areas list:
New products 
Promotions
Growing to partnership status
Increasing business without partnership status
Decreasing business
Termination of the relationship
For partnership vendors, reviews are ongoing. The formal review involves a joint planning session subject to continued performance and scheduled periodically.
Based on the vendor review process business decisions are made to either:
Increase business
Decrease business
Terminate the relationship
As vendor relationships mature, retailers strive for as many full partnerships as possible
In order for the partnership to be successful, both parties must realize:
Increased sales
Decreased costs
Improved profit margin
Vendor allowance management is instituted to track sales and purchases against the agreed terms, allowances, and rebates. In addition, special agreements are also tracked.
In the case where a category is sole source, the vendor can become the category manager (VMI) subject to meeting performance objectives.
For substitute products, vendor performance considers the difference between vendors with like times and selects the most appropriate vendor based on performance measures.
“Blanket” return authorization and return methods are used based on a pre-negotiated dollar amount or percentage amount that is agreed upon by both vendor and retailer. These pre-authorized returns are subject to periodic vendor audits.
Partnerships - Vendor partnerships are determined by joint evaluation of supply chain economics. The level of partnership increases as more responsibilities are shared and, in come cases completely managed by the vendor:
Implement enabling technologies (UPC & EDI)
Create fixed cycle replenishment programs
Use shared forecasts and POS data (collaborative forecasting)
Manage retail space
Develop new products jointly

Benefits of Better Practices
Increased sales volume
Increased product contribution to profit margin
Decreased stock-outs
Reduced order-to-available cycle
Greater accuracy and timeliness on purchase orders
Improved compliance
Increased time savings
