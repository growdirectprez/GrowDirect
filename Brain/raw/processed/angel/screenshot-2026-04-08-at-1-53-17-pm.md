### 03 Dinner & Drinks
- **Nelson's, Terranea Resort** — A local favorite for casual coastal dining, drinks and one of the best sunset views in Los Angeles.
- **Red Onion / Palos Verdes** — A staple restaurant for Palos Verdes residents offering authentic Mexican food and a delicious margarita.

### 04 Boutiques/Local Shopping
- **Lunada Bay Plaza** — A charming neighborhood hub with local shops, cafés, and everyday essentials. Known for its small-town feel within Palos Verdes.
- **Palos Verdes Peninsula Center** — delivers the ultimate shopping experience. Immerse your senses in a unique blend of specialty retailers and restaurants.
- **Malaga Cove** — A historic, village-style plaza featuring local cafés, a neighborhood market, and boutique shops, all centered around the iconic Neptune Fountain in a walkable, Mediterranean-inspired setting.
