**Header:** "Palos Verdes Neighborhood Guide — places to explore in the local area"

**Intro:** Palos Verdes offers a lifestyle rooted in coastal beauty, privacy, and space. From scenic oceanfront dining to hidden hiking trails and local neighborhood cafés, this is a community designed for long-term living.

### 01 School District
Palos Verdes is served by the top-ranked Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified School District, known for academic excellence and strong programs. Notable schools include:
- Palos Verdes Peninsula High School
- Palos Verdes High School
- Silver Spur Elementary
- Montemalaga Elementary

### 02 Neighborhoods
"Palos Verdes is not one market; it's a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each offering its own lifestyle, views, and overall feel."

- **Malaga Cove (Palos Verdes Estates)** — Historic charm with tree-lined streets, classic architecture, and close proximity to the beach and Riviera Village.
- **Valmonte (Palos Verdes Estates)** — One of the most sought-after areas for its flat streets, larger lots, and convenient access on and off the Peninsula.
- **Silver Spur (Rancho Palos Verdes)** — Centrally located with a mix of home styles, close to shopping, dining and top-rated schools.
- **Lunada Bay (Palos Verdes Estates)** — A true coastal neighborhood known for its walkability, ocean views, and strong community feel centered around Lunada Bay Plaza.
- **Miraleste (Rancho Palos Verdes)** — A more accessible entry point into Palos Verdes, offering hillside homes with city and harbor views.
- **Las Verdes (Rancho Palos Verdes)** — Known for ocean-view homes, golf course proximity, and a relaxed coastal lifestyle.
- **Rolling Hills (Gated Community)** — Private, guard-gated equestrian estates with expansive land, unmatched privacy, and a true estate-style lifestyle.
- **Rolling Hills Estates** — A blend of suburban convenience and rural charm, with larger lots, horse trails, and a more laid-back feel.
