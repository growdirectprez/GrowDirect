# Advisor Memo — HOA Payment Platforms vs. Strike for WPBCA Assessment Collection

**Not legal advice.** External advisor synthesis, preserved verbatim for the record. Final positions require separate 501(c)(3) counsel and/or HOA counsel review before adoption.

The working position distilled from this memo is in [[wpbca-payments-platform]]. The memo is archived here for traceability: if the working position later needs to be audited, this is the source.

---

## Short Answer

Yes — using a dedicated HOA-specific payment platform is the safest, most compliant, and most practical choice for WPBCA.

These platforms are purpose-built for common-interest developments. They handle ACH, credit/debit cards, e-checks, resident portals, automated reminders, late-fee tracking, and audit-ready reporting — all while staying firmly inside Davis-Stirling and banking regulations. Strike (or any general fintech) can be a supplemental rail, but it should not be the primary system.

## 1. Why HOA-Specific Platforms Are Preferable

HOA-specific platforms are designed for the exact regulatory environment WPBCA operates in:

- **Built-in Davis-Stirling Compliance** — Automatic generation of proper notices, lien tracking, payment histories that satisfy member inspection rights (Civ. Code § 5200), and clear audit trails for assessments.
- **Resident Portal & QR Codes** — Members get a secure portal or app. Generate QR codes or payment links that can be printed on index cards and dropped in mailboxes — low-friction member experience.
- **Multiple Payment Methods** — ACH (recurring or one-time), credit/debit cards, e-check, and sometimes Apple Pay/Google Pay. No requirement for members to have crypto wallets.
- **Accounting Integration** — Direct reconciliation with the HOA's bank account. Clear reports for the board, treasurer, and any future audit.
- **Insurance & Banking Friendliness** — Widely used by California HOAs, so D&O and fidelity carriers are familiar and less likely to raise coverage issues.
- **Automation** — Late notices, delinquency tracking, payment reminders — reduces board workload.

Popular HOA-specific platforms used in California:

- AppFolio
- CINC Systems
- Buildium (RealPage)
- TenantCloud (smaller HOAs)
- HOA Express / Spectrum Association Management tools

Most offer tiered pricing based on number of units. An 81-lot community would likely fall into a low-to-mid tier, often $1–$3 per unit per month or a flat monthly fee.

## 2. Strike (or Similar Fintech) Role

Strike can still be useful as a **supplemental** low-fee ACH option inside the HOA-specific platform or as a backup rail. Many platforms allow multiple processors. However, the core treasury and primary collection system should remain with the HOA-specific platform linked to the HOA's EIN-tied business bank account.

**Recommended Hybrid:**

- Primary system: Dedicated HOA platform (portal, automated billing, reporting, QR codes).
- Optional low-fee ACH rail: Strike or similar, routed into the same HOA bank account.
- Fallback: Paper checks and in-person payments for members who prefer them.

This gives the convenience of QR code / index card drops while keeping everything auditable, insured, and regulator-friendly.

## 3. Implementation Steps for WPBCA

1. **Research & Compare Platforms** — Request demos from 2–3 HOA-specific providers. Ask specifically about:
   - QR code / shareable payment link generation.
   - ACH recurring authorization.
   - Integration with current bank account.
   - Reporting for board meetings and member inspections.
   - Cost for an 81-unit HOA.

2. **Adopt a Formal Payment Policy** — Short board resolution that:
   - Authorizes the chosen platform.
   - Requires all funds to flow into the HOA's primary bank account.
   - Discloses options to all members.
   - Keeps traditional payment methods available.

3. **Test Before Full Rollout** — Pilot with a few volunteer households to ensure QR codes work smoothly and members understand the process.

4. **Board Approval** — Record the decision in minutes to demonstrate prudent care.

## 4. Recommendation

Move forward with an HOA-specific payment platform as the primary system. It solves the friction problem (QR codes, easy ACH, resident portal) without introducing the fiduciary, insurance, or compliance risks of running collections primarily through Strike or any crypto-adjacent service.

Strike can be one of the ACH rails inside the platform if the fees are attractive, but the platform itself should handle the member-facing experience and record-keeping.

This approach is:

- Fully compliant with Davis-Stirling.
- Lower risk for directors.
- More accessible to all members.
- Easier to insure and audit.

## Next Step Proposed by Advisor

Draft a short, board-ready Payment Policy and Board Resolution authorizing an HOA-specific platform (with optional Strike ACH) and requiring funds to flow into the HOA's bank account. Language about QR code cards if that is the preferred member communication method.
