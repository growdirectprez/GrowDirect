Katz Group Executive Visioning Session Meeting Minutes – June 12, 2003

Introduction – Larry Latowsky

No decisions/conclusions today
Direction is the goal for today
Session is designed to make better informed decisions and thinking outside the box
Not talking about today’s obstacles but where we need to be in the future
During lunch with Ed Kilroy, Larry asked if Katz is undergoing too much change.  Responded change will be good for the company because it will move them from “competitively neutral” to “competitively advantaged”
Goal is to build the company into a world class retail organization
Wal-Mart has done so with supply chain commitment and vision
Katz vision is to have multiple banners and multiple formats which will give a competitive advantage (Loblaws has a similar model)
Pharmassist rebranding receiving resounding approval from pharmacists
Will be the leader in terms of market share, total sales, particularly in Pharmacy
