Attachment G: JDA Client Support Programs


JDA can offer you a variety of support programs that will provide you with valuable assistance throughout your implementation and for years to come. Our goal is to ensure that you are truly benefiting from our software by helping you achieve optimal performance.

If you select JDA’s Passport Support (Client’s non-stop service to global support) program, you receive:

new releases
Service Packs
solution line coverage 9 hours each business day (8:00 am until 5:00 pm, client head-office time, Monday thru Friday)
toll-free phone numbers to reach JDA (some exceptions)
Access to  HYPERLINK http://www.jdauser.com www.jdauser.com website/user forum.

Additional Support Services are available, including:

Custom Passport Support
Oracle DBA Support Services
Annual 24 x 7, 24 x 5, 12 x 7, or 12 x 5 Support
On-request Extended Support

The following provides more detail about each of these Support Services:


Passport Support

New Software Releases
Our R&D team continuously enhances product functionality and adds new features.  We are also regularly incorporating new technologies, such as versions of our third-party development tools and operating system components.  The results of these efforts are new releases of our products that we provide to our clients so they can achieve a greater return on their software investment. Our existing client base drives many of these new enhancements.

All new releases undergo extensive testing by JDA’s Quality Assurance Group. We also test each release’s integration with other JDA products to confirm that they are providing a true enterprise solution.

New releases provide major system enhancements and are typically available once a year.  All of these updates need to be released simultaneously for all products so that we apply integration updates keeping our software synchronized.

Service Packs
Service Packs include product fixes and are available as needed by the product. These may be combined into one cumulative package for those clients who wish to reduce the installation frequency.

JDA supports the current version of the product and one prior release. If and when we can assist with earlier releases we will do so.

Release Documentation
The following documentation is provided with New Releases and Service Packs:

Release Letter - This document explains the functional and technical changes included in the release.
Program Change Log - Notes describing modifications are provided and can be viewed online or listed on a report.
Installation Guidelines - This document describes the steps necessary to install the new release.

JDA Solution Line
Both functional and technical analysts staff the JDA Solution Line. Our clients can call with any ‘how to’ questions or to report any software issues.  With our call tracking system, we have streamlined the prioritization, escalation and follow-up processes to provide each client with a timely resolution.  When appropriate, we diagnose your issue in the Client Solution laboratory or by remotely accessing your system.

Extended support is available 12 x 5, 12 x 7, 24 x 5, 24 x 7, or can be based on your particular time requirements.

Clients can also reach us via the Internet by emailing questions or issues using an Auto Ticket Format.  All questions/issues entered via Auto Tickets are automatically logged into the call tracking system and escalated to appropriate support analysts. Clients will automatically receive an acknowledgement of receipt along with a call number via email.  Any client on support can access the status of any of their issues by signing on with their ID and Password to our Client Web Page.  This web page includes a client profile and forum section so that clients can network with one another.







Support Staffing

Functional Support is staffed by support analysts who typically have a retail background and have been trained on the functional features of the applications.
Technical Support is staffed by personnel who have detailed technical knowledge of the application and environments.


Optional Support Services

Custom Passport Support
Custom Passport Support includes everything you get with Passport Support plus:

Modification support, where JDA will research and fix any client reported issues with customized code that was developed by JDA.

Annual 24 x 7, 24 x 5, 12 x 7 or 12 x 5 Support

Solution Line coverage for after normal business hours (may require pager support for certain time periods)
Clients must already be utilizing one of the Passport JDA support packages

On-Request Extended Support

Pre-scheduled on-request extended coverage on weekdays and weekends

For more information about our Portfolio Client Support, please contact Chris Moore, Vice President, Portfolio Client Support Services at JDA (480) 308-3040 or your JDA Customer Relationship Manager, Ravi Srinivasan at (403) 212-3076.

JDA Users Group
JDA actively supports and encourages the independent Users Group of its software.  This group, which meets once a year, was formed and is managed by the users with JDA’s assistance.

JDA is very responsive to the collective voice of this group.  We have made product and support changes as recommended by our users.

For more information about the users group, please contact Connie Brinks, Manager, Corporate Events and Tradeshows at JDA (480) 308-3026 or your JDA Customer Relationship Manager, Ravi Srinivasan at (403) 212-3076.
