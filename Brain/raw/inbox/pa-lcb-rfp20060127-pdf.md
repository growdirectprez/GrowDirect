Dear Prospective Proposer,

April 4, 2006

You are invited to submit a proposal to provide the Pennsylvania Liquor Control
Board  with  category  management  analysis,  in  accordance  with  the  enclosed  Request
For Proposal (RFP) 20060127.

Control Board, Procurement Division, Room 413, Northwest Office Building, Harrisburg,
Pennsylvania 17124-0001.  Proposals must be received at the above address not later

proposals will not be considered, regardless of the circumstances.

PART  I

GENERAL INFORMATION FOR PROPOSERS

I-1.  PURPOSE  -  This  Request  for  Proposal  (“RFP”)  provides  interested  parties

with sufficient information to enable them to prepare and submit proposals for

consideration  by  the  Commonwealth  of  Pennsylvania,  Pennsylvania  Liquor

Control Board (“PLCB”), to satisfy a need for category management analysis.

I-2.

ISSUING OFFICE - This RFP is issued by the PLCB.  The Issuing Officer for

this  RFP  is  Ms.  Debra  L.  Brinser,  Chief,  Procurement  Division,  Room  413,

Northwest  Office  Building,  Harrisburg,  Pennsylvania  17124-0001;  telephone:

(717)  787-9851;  facsimile:    (717)  783-8927;  e-mail:  dbrinser@state.pa.us.

The  Issuing  Office  is  the  sole  point  of  contact  in  the  Commonwealth  for  this

RFP.

I-3.  SCOPE  -  This  RFP  contains  instructions  governing  the  proposals  to  be

submitted and the material to be included therein; a description of the service

to be provided; requirements that must be met to be eligible for consideration;

general evaluation criteria; a proposed contract; and other requirements to be

met by each proposal.

I-4.  PROBLEM  STATEMENT  -  The  PLCB  recognizes

the  opportunity

for

efficiencies  and  resource  and  cost  savings,  as  well  as  increased  customer

satisfaction, within the structure and processes of category management as it

1

relates  to  the  sale  of  wine  and  spirits.    The  PLCB  is  seeking  a  contractor  to

assist  the  PLCB  with  analyzing  its  current  category  management  practices,

policies and procedures, and evaluating completed merchandising test results.

The PLCB wishes to ensure that policies, procedures and required tools based

on  industry  best  practices  are  put  into  place  to  increase  service  delivery,

customer  satisfaction  and  store  operational  efficiency  in  each  of  the  PLCB’s

wine and spirits stores.  The selected proposer will conduct detailed category

management  analysis  and  will  benchmark  the  PLCB  category  management

processes  against  proven  industry  standards  and  norms.    The  selected

proposer  will  provide  the  PLCB  with  detailed  process  workflows  and

recommendations  for  category  management  efficiencies  and  improvements.

Additional detail is provided in Part IV of the RFP.

I-5.

TYPE  OF  CONTRACT  -  It  is  proposed  that  if  a  contract  is  entered  into  as  a

result  of  this  RFP,  it  will  be  a  fixed-fee,  deliverables-based  contract  and  will

contain certain specifications and provisions shown in Appendix Q of the RFP.

The  PLCB  may,  in  its  sole  discretion,  undertake  negotiations  with  proposers

whose  proposals,  as  to  price  and  other  factors,  show  them  to  be  qualified,

responsible,  and  capable  of  performing  the  work.    If  negotiations  have  not

concluded  in  an  agreement  within  five  (5)  PLCB  business  days  of  their

commencement, the PLCB may break off negotiations and move to negotiate

with the other responsive, responsible proposer(s), or to negotiate separately

with competing qualified proposers.

I-6.  REJECTION OF PROPOSALS - The PLCB reserves the right, in its sole and

complete discretion, to reject any and/or all proposals received as a result of

this RFP.

I-7.

INCURRING  COSTS  -  The  PLCB  is  not  liable  for  any  costs  incurred  by

anyone prior to issuance of a contract.

I-8.  SITE TOUR - A site tour will be conducted on the day and time indicated in the

cover letter.  Interested proposers shall contact the Issuing Officer identified in

Section  I-2  to  arrange  the  visit.    Each  proposer’s  participation  at  the  tour  is

limited  to  two  (2)  representatives.    Questions  posed  by  proposers  during  this

tour  will  be  answered  officially  at  the  Pre-proposal  Conference.    Questions

must be submitted in the format described in Section I-10 and the RFP cover

letter.

I-9.  MANDATORY PRE-PROPOSAL CONFERENCE - A mandatory pre-proposal

conference will be held on May 9, 2006 at 10:00 A.M., as specified in the RFP

cover letter.  The purpose of this conference is to clarify any points in the RFP

that  may  not  have  been  clearly  understood.      Questions  should  be

forwarded  to  the  Issuing  Office  in  writing  prior  to  the  conference  to

ensure  that  sufficient  analysis  can  be  made  before  an  answer  is

supplied.  In view of the limited facility space available for the conference, it is

requested  that  representation  be  limited  to  essential  personnel.    The  pre-

opportunity  to  submit  additional  questions,  in  writing,  at  the  pre-proposal

conference.    Answers  furnished  during  the  conference  and/or  site  tour

shall  not  be  official  until  verified,  in  writing,  by  the  Issuing  Officer.    All

questions and written answers will be issued as an addendum to and will

become part of this RFP.
