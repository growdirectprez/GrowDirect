| 6 | What do your major customers and suppliers need? | Vendors do not have access to data warehouse (Microstrategy) which detail historical sales information from POS.\nCurrently collaborating with McKesson, in particular with promotions.\nMcKesson is not good at front shop wholesaling. | NaN |
