Sobey’s Reference Check

Clinton Keay – Executive Vice President and CIO
October 29th, 2003 10am

Footprint
Implemented HR in all company, finance is in the process of being implemented across the company
Retail functionality (Inventory, sales & distribution, category management, merchandise planning) in Atlantic region only – Atlantic region is happy with it
EXE for warehouse E3 for forecasting – Not comfortable with whole suite of JDA
Apollo for Space Planning

Implemented SAP 4.0b, they are stabilized on this now
Considering upgrade options to SAP 4.7
Sobey’s helped develop SAP software
Sobey’s has high confidence with upgrade

Important to determine how/where to store perpetual inventory (application and data warehouse were combined resulting in performance issues).  This is not the case anymore.

Running IBM DB2 on P Series (P670)

Spent the last year on process work

Clinton has been in IT for 1.5 years, VP finance & Treasurer for 5 years prior

Quality of Software

Good functionality in SAP software, areas that need improvement are forecasting and warehousing

Ease of Upgrade

Only performed a minor upgrade but are planning to upgrade 4.0b to 4.7

Implementation

Transaction volumes were an issue because data was combined with app
Didn’t understand the system
Performed a lot of customization for version 4.0



Education & Training

Training is moving from classroom to web based
Biggest challenge for users was moving from green screen to windows based
Flexibility of real time information versus the old batch processing
SAP and Sobeys performed

IT / Technical Infrastructure

Hardware is much better today
EDI has been pushed to vendors, GHX EDI translator used

Change Management Acceptance

First company to this in North American grocery, no one to get advice from
Make sure Finance is stabilized prior to implementation
Culture was to do it quick
No project management skills in IT initially which was a problem
Need to follow a methodology with top down commitment

Future Direction with Vendor

Happy with relationship with SAP
Have good contacts in Germany but deal with the Montreal SAP office
If they had to do it again they would focus on governance, realistic timelines, project management and incremental roll out.
