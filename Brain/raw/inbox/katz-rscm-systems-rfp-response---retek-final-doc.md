1.0 Executive Summary

Retek is pleased to respond to Katz requirements for a Retail Supply Chain Management System.

This proposal:
Reflects our understanding of Katz requirements for a merchandising and supply chain management solution and for a vendor partner in achieving the benefits of this technology;
Discusses how Retek solutions best meet these needs;
Includes a description of Retek’s implementation, training, and support services;
Provides technical information so that Katz can best assess how Retek solutions fit within its technology strategy; and
Describes the investment required by Katz.

Our objective is to deliver software, services, and associated business practices that not only meet or exceed today’s requirements but also provide a compelling case for further merchandising operations and supply chain integration as requirements arise.


Katz Objectives

Based upon the Request for Proposal, we understand that Katz is looking for a  new merchandising and supply chain solution that will:
Enable business process improvement
Deliver superior customer service
Allow efficient flow of merchandise
Enhance merchandise and inventory planning
Support a culture of performance measurement.

A new Retail Supply Chain Management System has the potential to allow Katz corporate, franchise, and independent affiliates to market as a single, cohesive unit to gain competitive advantage.

Katz primary focus is to implement the above technology for the 300 corporate stores and headquarters functions.  Once comfortable with the implementation, Katz would like to make the solution available to its affiliated franchise and independent store operators.


Business Offer Highlights

Building upon over 17 years experience in developing and delivering advanced software technology exclusively for retailers, Retek is pleased to propose:
Installation of a number of Retek’s retail-proven, advanced transactional and predictive technology solutions (described in Section 2.0);
Rapid integration with current financial and other legacy applications through Retek’s advanced Integration Solutions;
Implementation Services, tailored to meet the division of responsibilities most comfortable for Katz, using Retek’s comprehensive implementation methodology and professional services team – with an impressive track record of on-time, on-budget implementations of advanced retail technology with over 200 customers worldwide;
A training program to ensure that Katz associates quickly become conversant in the use of Retek applications; and
An ongoing, cost-effective maintenance and customer support offering.

3.1.4 Company History

Please provide a brief history of your company.  What is your company’s number of years of established business presence in Canada and in total?  Please list the countries that your company has an office in and is capable of providing technical support.

In 1986, three Australians founded what is today Retek.  They began to develop a client / server-based merchandising system, MerMan (later renamed to Retek Merchandising System), that would be able to scale to handle the large volumes of data found in a typical retail organization.  The company relocated to Vancouver, British Columbia and ultimately established its headquarters in Minneapolis, Minnesota.  Retek’s first North American customer, ShopKo, was signed in 1994.  In 1995, Retek added its first customer in Canada, North West Company.  In 1996, Retek was acquired by HNC Software, a business-to-business software company that developed and marketed predictive software solutions, enabling Retek to be the first company to integrate predictive forecasting technology with advanced merchandising software.  In the late 1990’s, as its applications suite of retail solutions and its functionality for additional retail segments grew, Retek was the first to exploit Internet technology across its solution set; and later integrated wireless technology.  In 1999, Retek completed its initial public offering (IPO) and became an independent public company in 2000, listed on Nasdaq (RETK).

Today, Retek has employees in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Japan, Australia, and other countries.  Its headquarters is in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and has regional headquarters in the United Kingdom, France, Japan, and Australia.

3.3.2 Product Integration

Please describe the integration capability of your solution with external applications.  What features of your solution make it a good fit in a multi-application environment?


Retek Integration Solution

The Retek Integration Solution (RIS) combines message-based integration and monitoring with the Retek Integration Bus (RIB) EAI solution; multi-threaded data extraction, transformation and low-level data loading capabilities with the Retek Extract Transform and Load (RETL) product and other point-to-point solutions providing data as needed by Retek and third-party applications supporting industry-standard formats.  RIS delivers all the benefits of an integrated footprint with enterprise wide visibility that retailers demand.

Retek Integration Bus (RIB)

The RIB provides near real-time publication and subscription message-based integration.  The Retek Integration Bus (RIB) is an information management application that delivers a set of common business objects and documents used for messaging communication to/from the Retek Merchandise System (RMS).  Instead of integrating each application with every other application, all applications may now be integrated via the RIB.  The RIB reduces implementation time and cost by providing easier integration with legacy and third-party applications.  Development, maintenance and upgrade are faster and cheaper because the resulting environment is standardized and comprehensive.  Business functions are exposed at a granular level for each integration point and standard messages are represented in a flexible tagged (XML) format that when modified, impact a small set of functions.  Subscribing applications may pick and choose the elements of a message that are needed for processing.  Redundant integration points are eliminated and status / feedback messages are implemented to report of success and failure on common business transactions and improve data management.  The RIB’s modular architecture enables retailers to maximize their investment in legacy systems while implementing the Retek modules that provide high-impact returns.  The RIB has been developed using the technology foundation provided by SeeBeyond’s e*Gate products.

Retek Extract Transform and Load (RETL)

Scheduled high-volume, multi-threaded data extraction, transformation and data loading capabilities are found within the Retek Extract Transform Load (RETL) product.  The RETL is a high-performance runtime product specifically designed for use in parallel processing systems where high volumes of data must be processed quickly.  By incorporating RETL into an application, the time required to process data from databases and flat files may be reduced.  By adding server processors, the RETL can scale to handle even larger volumes of data.  The RETL is targeted toward two primary uses:

Data extraction and loading for interfacing between Retek applications and / or legacy systems;
Batch processing of computations, such as pre-aggregating data for reporting applications, or calculating replenishment numbers for merchandising applications.
