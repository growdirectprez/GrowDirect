Proposed Workplan:

Our proposed approach in working with the Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board (PLCB) on the category management project is organized into four project phases: Project Discovery, Assortment Planning Assessment, Space Planning Assessment, and Project Rollout.  An overview of the project phases and delivery schedule for the project objectives follows:

Project Discovery:

The Project Discovery phase focuses on project startup activities and establishing a baseline understanding of the current state processes, organization and tools in place for assortment and space planning at the PLCB.

Objectives
Initiate project and confirm objectives
Identify key participants
Develop understanding of current state process, organization and tools
Key Activities
Develop detailed project plan and assemble team
Meet with project sponsors, key executives and managers
Hold kick off meeting with core members of the joint project team
Facilitate current state discovery interviews and workshops
Document current state processes, reports and tool sets
Document current initiatives, concerns and pain points
Gain understanding of organization, including market, customers, culture, strategic vision
Deliverables
Detailed work plan and staffing schedule
Project calendar, workshop and interview schedule
Project organization and governance model
Current State Process

Assortment Planning Assessment:

The Assortment planning assessment will focus on an evaluation of the current de-list and core assortment methodologies.  Our approach will aim to improve the overall efficiency and effectiveness of the assortment planning functions from a financial and procedural point of view.

Objectives
Form cross-functional teams and conduct joint workshops to gather goals and objectives, identify requirements, validate existing documentation and evaluate proposed solutions
Utilize IBM Subject Matter Experts to help assess the current processes while simultaneously developing better practices recommendations for the future
Understand what Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) drive the process
Preparation of a set of prioritized recommendations based on the PLCB’s current and strategic objectives

Key Activities
Interviews with key personnel at various organizational levels
First-hand observation of the execution processes at back oofice and store level
Analysis of process input/output (data)
Research IBM’s extensive knowledge library around better practices
Document requirements for a comprehensive and appropriate PLCB assortment planning function
Document better practices information flow among various planning functions and external data users
Identify ways to the use planning information in performance metrics, reporting and to aid communication

Deliverables
De-list Procedure report
Core Assortment Methodology report

Space Planning Assessment:

The space planning assessment will be broken down into two main deliveries.  The first component will focus on the future state recommendation for plannograming and baseline category adjacencies.  The second component will focus on an end to end presentation of the future state PLCB category management processes at the planning and execution levels.

Objectives
Form cross-functional teams and conduct joint workshops to gather goals and objectives, identify requirements, validate existing documentation and evaluate proposed solutions
Utilize IBM process accelerators and IBM Subject Matter Experts to help assess the current processes while simultaneously developing better practices recommendations for the future
Utilize existing PLCB research and additional market research if necessary to formulate recommendations
Understand what Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) drive the process
Understand the extent of tools currently available with the PLCB organization, and if appropriate recommend alternative solutions.
Preparation of a set of prioritized recommendations based on the PLCB’s current and strategic objectives

Key Activities
Interviews with key personnel at various organizational levels
First-hand observation of the execution processes at back office and store level
Analysis of process input/output (data)
Research IBM’s extensive knowledge library around better practices
Document requirements for a comprehensive and appropriate shelf set strategy,
Document requirements for a comprehensive and appropriate sku count and category right sizing strategy
Document better practices information flow among various planning functions and external data users
Identify ways to the use planning information in performance metrics, reporting and to aid communication

Deliverables
Shelf Set Strategy
Location SKU Count methodology
Category right sizing methodology
Wood Shelf Product Placement Strategy

Project Rollout:

The project rollout phase will prepare a detailed schedule and approach to implementing the future state recommendations in the Product Management, Marketing and Store organizations.

Deliverables
Future State Implementation Plan
Next steps, recommended follow on activites
