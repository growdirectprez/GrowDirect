Key Performance Indicators

Best Practice Option
Best practices for financial performance indicators focus on increasing strategic uses, improving the format and relevancy, and identifying key statistics.  Analysis reveals that best practice for performance indicators includes the following:

Increasing Strategic Uses
Regular measurement and publication of performance indicators and comparison with prior periods
Adoption of a continuous performance improvement culture, with an attitude and commitment to seeking improvement
Use of flexible reporting timetables updated regularly to reflect improved performance
Genuine partnership relationships with business units
Promote an external, competitive focus
Drive action to achieve goals

Improving Format and Relevancy
Performance indicators derived from strategies and linked to key result areas
Regular surveys of customer and business requirements
Reflect established mission and objectives for the Financial Management Department
Focus on user requirements for indicators
Be tailored to specific needs at different levels of the organization, but ensure consistency and linkage throughout

Characteristics of Key Performance Indicators:
Simple to use and understand
Used as a supplement to budget performance measurement
Objective
Replicable
Few in number
Congruent with organizational objectives
Measuring factors that are under the direct control of the function being measured
