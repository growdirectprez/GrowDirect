PARTICIPATING CUSTOMER AGREEMENT

Customer:  Katz Group Canada Limited
Date:

The Customer by virtue of being a party to an agreement with SAP Canada Inc. (“Supplier”) made as of the ____ day of __________, 200__ (the “License Agreement”) which provides for, among other things, the license to the Customer, and the support, by Supplier, of the Software components set out in the License Agreement, and by executing this Participating Customer Agreement, hereby agrees to become a party to the Basis System Source Code Escrow Agreement between Supplier and North American Trust Company through its successor Laurentian Trust Company of Canada Inc. (“Agent”) dated as of the 13th day of September, 1994 (the “Escrow Agreement”), and specifically the Customer, in consideration of the covenants contained in the Escrow Agreement in favor of the Customer, agrees as follows:

	1.	The Customer acknowledges having received and read a copy of the Escrow Agreement, and the Customer agrees to be bound, as of the date noted above (which is the Effective Date for the purpose of the Escrow Agreement), by all of the terms, covenants and conditions of the Escrow Agreement as they relate to the Customer.

	2.	The Programs (as defined in the Escrow Agreement) to which this Participating Customer Agreement shall apply, and for which the Customer shall have the right to receive a copy of the Basis System Source Code from Agent under and in accordance with the Escrow Agreement shall be the programs set out in the License Agreement, and any other software modules licensed by the Customer from Supplier hereafter.

	3.	In consideration of being granted the right to become a Participating Customer under the Escrow Agreement, the Customer agrees to pay all charges payable by the Customer under Section 5 of the Escrow Agreement. The Customer agrees to pay Supplier an initial fee of seven hundred and fifty dollars ($750.00) in full consideration of the establishment of the escrow (as required under subsection 5(b) of the Escrow Agreement).

In addition, the Customer shall pay an Annual fee of $650.00, an Update fee of $79.50 per update and an Attendance Fee for Verification for Agent Personnel will be charged at $100.00 per hour. The Customer recognizes that Agent may from time to time increase the charges payable by the Customer under the said Section 5.
