Attachment A: JDA Company History Timeline


Below is a recap of historically significant events for JDA by year:

2003:
JDA acquired Engage, Inc., an innonative provider of enterprise advertising, marketing and promotion (AMP) software solutions.
JDA won its second consecutive Microsoft® Retail Application Developer (RAD) Award for  VistaRetail™ software as the best solution in the Supply Chain category.
JDA acquired the intellectual property assets of Vista Software Solutions, an innovative provider of collaborative commerce solutions for the retail and consumer goods industry.
JDA Client, Guitar Center, was named a winner of Executive Technology magazine’s 2003 CIO/IT Leadership Awards. At the heart of Guitar Center’s IT infrastructure are multiple JDA Portfolio® applications.
2002:
JDA CEO, Jim Armstrong, was named “Master Entrepreneur” of the Year; Ernst & Young recognized Armstrong for outstanding business achievements, perseverance & goodwill.
JDA client, Rite Aid, won the 2002 VICS Collaborative Commerce Achievement Award by leveraging JDA CPFR® Solution; JDA Portfolio Delivered high ROI to “Best in VICS CPFR Implementation” winners for second consecutive year.
JDA formed a strategic relationship with Microsoft Corp.
JDA won a Microsoft R.A.D. Award for Store Portals.
JDA client, Sodimac, won a CIO/IT Leadership & Innovation Award from Executive Technology based on ROI from JDA Portfolio.
JDA client, Londis Holdings, won the Global Retail Technology Forum Award for collaborative success achieved using JDA Portfolio.
JDA acquired J. Commerce in order to add thin client in-store offering to JDA Portfolio.
JDA and Manhattan Associates announced worldwide strategic alliance to integrate advanced demand chain solutions.
2001:
JDA client, Anheuser-Busch, won Consumer Goods Technology’s Leadership Award for JDA partnership.
The University of South Carolina received additional software grant from JDA.
JDA won Drug Store News' Retail Excellence Award; industry leaders vote E3 best VIP for non-pharmacy technology .
JDA acquired E3, world’s leading provider of inventory optimization solutions.
JDA advanced acquisition strategy with new Portfolio solution to speed systems integration and streamline trading partner collaboration.
JDA significantly advanced hardware reselling strategy by signing agreement with IBM.
 JDA advanced product expansion strategy with acquisition of Accrue Software’s NeoVista data mining products.
JDA formed strategic alliance with Symbol Technologies to deliver complete M-commerce solution.
JDA named on America’s fastest growing companies index.
JDA acquired Zapotec to respond to retailers’ multi-billion co-op ad collection challenges.
JDA formed alliance with Equifax Check Solutions to provide retailers greater control over fraud.
Headcount totaled 1,400 associates.

2000:
JDA announced latest advancements to Arthur Enterprise Suite.
JDA ranked #1 On 'Top 50 Retail Software Vendor' List In Retail-Specific Category; JDA among cross-industry software giants in fourth place position overall.
JDA named to DM Review’s Top 100 Business Intelligence Vendors; ratings from JDA’s Retail IDEAS clients secure company’s inaugural placement.
SPS Commerce and JDA deliver joint B2B solution to optimize supply chain for retailers and trading partners: alliance breaks new ground in solving challenge of integrating multiple, disparate systems.
JDA announced availability of Intactix pro/space Plus 2.0.
JDA unveiled latest Portals, CRM and B2C advancements at NRF.com.
JDA announced availability of new version of Retail IDEAS data warehouse and decision support system.
JDA announced new product line, JDA Portfolio.
JDA announced availability of new versions of Intactix pro/space Plus 2.0, Retail IDEAS 5.0, Win/DSS 4.2, ODBMS 4.1 and MMS 4R5 which achieved 200% performance improvement running on IBM’s latest AS/400e server.
JDA launched Intactix pro/floor at FMI’s 2000 Supermarket Industry Convention.
JDA acquired Pricer AB’s Intactix subsidiary, expanding its retail supply chain business-to-business solution.
JDA partnered with Harbinger Corporation to deliver integrated business-to-business supply chain solution. Partnership will enable seamless integration between JDA's MMS and Harbinger TrustedLink™ data transformation and integration software.
JDA partnered with Accrue Software to offer comprehensive e-commerce solutions such as click stream web analysis and advanced inventory analytics.
JDA announced a strategic initiative with IBM to deliver a comprehensive solution for global retailers wanting an accelerated, yet low risk, implementation to support their e-commerce operations.

1999:
Launched MMS.com the industry’s first complete e-commerce merchandising solution and announced first client, Store of Knowledge.
Established new corporate headquarters in Scottsdale, AZ after consolidating seven locations in the greater Phoenix area to a newly built 136,000 square foot facility.
Welcomed the return of Jim Armstrong as CEO.
Released Arthur Planning 4.4, the undisputed leader in enterprise planning systems. Also announced updated releases of other Arthur Information Manager database, Arthur Performance Analysis decision support system, Arthur Assortment Planning and Arthur Allocation.
Reached our 45,000th cash register licensed to run our In-Store Systems: Win/DSS and Distributed Store System. Also, released version 3.14 of Win/DSS with enhanced features and an upgraded development platform.
Won a 1999 Microsoft’s Retail Application Developer (R.A.D.) Award for Retail IDEAS and also announced version 3.0.
Announced release 2.0 of WCC featuring seamless integration with ODBMS.
Announced Passport JDA, our centralized global support providing 24 hours/5 days or 24-hours/7 days support for clients.
Expanded or developed new partnerships with leading vendors including Microsoft, IBM, Sun, Telxon, and Symbol.
Opened new offices in Tokyo, Japan and Montreal, Canada.

1998:
Acquired the Arthur Retail Business Unit from Comshare.
Won Microsoft’s Retail Application Developer (R.A.D.) Award for Win/DSS.
Launched e-commerce division and formed OEM agreement with IBM integrating IBM Net.Commerce with JDA’s new solution.
Announced availability of Retail IDEAS on the Microsoft SQL Server 7.0 database operating on Windows NT Server.
Announced general release of Warehouse Control Center, the warehouse and distribution management component of ODBMS.
Announced DirectPath, our accelerated implementation methodology.
Opened Paris, France; Rio de Janeiro, Brazil; and Melbourne, Australia offices.
Graduated 2,700 students from JDAU in its first year.
Effected a Three-for-Two Stock Split.
Revenue totaled $138,500,000.
Headcount totaled 1,100 associates.

1997:
Announced the general release of Win/DSS.
Signed first Retail IDEAS client, Gart Brothers.
Purchased LIOCS Corporation and added client/server Warehouse Control Center to product line.
Earned Year 2000 certification from the Information Technology Association of America (ITAA).
Opened the Dallas, TX; Chicago, IL; Minneapolis, MN; Columbus, OH; Parsippany, NJ; and Sydney, Australia offices.
Revenue totaled $91,770,000.
Headcount totaled 683 associates.

1996:
Effected initial public offering on 3/15/96, followed by a secondary offering 11/20/96.
Acquired JDA Software Services, Ltd. of Calgary, Alberta (company founded initially by Jim Armstrong in 1978) with offices in Calgary and Toronto.
Released Version 3.0 of ODBMS.
Released Version 4.4 of MMS.
Signed first Win/DSS clients, Sun TV & Appliance and West Marine.
Began development of Retail IDEAS, data warehouse system.
Opened Santiago, Chile office and signed first South American client, Falabella.
Revenue totaled $47,839,773.
Headcount totaled 387 associates.

1995:
Signed first ODBMS client, Petro Canada.
Moved corporate headquarters to larger location in Phoenix, AZ.
Began development of Win/DSS, in store system.
Released Version 2.1 of DSS.
Sold 28% of JDA to TA Associates, putting company on track to go public.
Revenue totaled $30,084,729.
Headcount totaled 209 associates.

1994:
Opened Singapore office and signed first Asian client, Seiyu.
Began development of ODBMS.
Released Version 4.3 of MMS.
Revenue totaled $23,829,174.
Headcount totaled 148 associates.

1993:
Received an Application Enhancement award from IBM for excellence in client/server application development for IBM AS/400 platform.
Released Version 4.2 of MMS.
Signed first African client, an Egyptian retailer.
Revenue totaled $20,321,102.
Headcount totaled 124 associates.

1992:
Released Version 4.0 of MMS.
Broadened our product line to meet in-store needs with Distributed Store System (DSS) and signed first DSS clients, Kitchen Etc. and Chanel.
Opened Atlanta, GA office.
Revenue totaled $15,974,089.
Headcount totaled 95 associates.

1991:
MMS continues to gain global market acceptance. Just a few of the new clients signed this year include Staples, USA; ABC Carpet, USA; Perfumania, USA; Virgin Retail, UK; Savacentre, UK; Stockmann Oy AB, Finland.
Signed first Australian client, Brash’s.
Revenue totaled $9,649,284.
Headcount totaled 70 associates.

1990:
Opened first international office in the United Kingdom and signed first UK based client, HMV.
Signed first Continental European client, Illums, Denmark.
Released Version 3.0 of MMS.
Expanded Scottsdale headquarters.
Opened Stamford, CT office.
Revenue totaled $7,463,614.
Headcount totaled 55 associates.

1989:
First JDA Users Group Meeting held in Scottsdale, AZ.
Moved corporate headquarters to Scottsdale, AZ.
Opened Chicago, IL office.
Revenue totaled $4,910,009.
Headcount totaled 30 associates.
1988:
IBM ships first AS/400 to JDA in July.
JDA is the only retail merchandising system running native on the AS/400 on IBM’s announcement day.
Revenue totaled $3,556,575.
Headcount totaled 18 associates.

1987:
Relocated headquarters to Phoenix, AZ.
Began development of MMS Warehousing and Distribution modules.
Completed initial “version” of fully integrated system.
Revenue totaled $1,528,246.
Headcount totaled 9 associates.

1986:
Hired first US associates.
Signed first three clients.
Began development of MMS Merchandising and Financial applications.
Revenue totaled $941,774.
Headcount totaled 4 associates.
