Selection Project Consultant
Katz Group Canada Limited
5965 Coopers Avenue
Missisauga, Ontario

Subject:	Request for Proposal
Retail Supply Chain Management Systems

Dear Dear Alan:

Thank you for the opportunity to submit this proposal to Katz Group Canada for its Retail Supply Chain Management requirements.

Retek is proposing a comprehensive solution including a number of products within our Retek 10 solution suite.

We believe that the Retek solutions deliver a new merchandising and supply chain system that will:
Enable business process improvement
Deliver superior customer service
Allow efficient flow of merchandise
Enhance merchandise and inventory planning
Support a culture of performance measurement.

Retek delivers industry-leading, retail-specific technology that helps retailers create, manage, and fulfill consumer demand.  We believe that we assist our customers to master the art of retail with the science of Retek.

Our proposal is that Katz license the Retek solutions for its corporate store operations.  Retek has also provided a subscription approach for Katz franchise and independent Retail Drug store affiliates that, we believe, will further enhance Katz ability to operate as a single, cohesive unit to gain competitive advantage.

Retek solutions are the technology of choice for leading large and mid-size retailers that, in aggregate, generate over $650 billion in annual sales.  Many of the world’s most successful retailers use Retek solutions including three of the top five US Retail Drug chains – Eckerd, Longs Drugs, and Walgreens.

We would welcome the opportunity to deliver results for Katz Group Canada.
