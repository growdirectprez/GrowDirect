Secure Store Client Services Strategy and Process

Communication and Travel

-  Minimum of Two or Three trips on site during the installation in addition to regular webexes and

calls.

o  Trip one Strategy Sessions (two to three days)

General Intent:  Strategy sessions with Loss Prevention to define areas of concern beyond
standard questions.  Additionally, an overall knowledge exchange to understand the
Walmart business and to share analytics program design, construction and management
best practices.

*Preferred attendees would be the sponsor, superusers, one or two select field users, an
operational trainer and a t-log expert.

  Gain a full understanding of the Loss Prevention structure.

  Organizational Hierarchy
  Roles and responsibilities for each organizational level

  Knowledge exchange regarding any unique business practices that may open an

exposure.

  Review of policies and procedures
  POS functionality


Incorporate a store tour as part of the knowledge exchange

  Review Best Practices when implementing an analytics program

  Secure Engagement Business Elements Overview Document
  Risk Dictionary Workbook

  Focus on the familiar and immediate concerns or suspicions

o  Benefit:  Focusing on familiar metrics initially aids in decreasing the
ramp up time with the application.  Often users need to learn both
data mining and a new application.  Data mining metrics they are
proficient with allows them to focus on learning the application

  Strategy/Plan to integrate Secure into their investigative process

  Communication

o  Corporate to Field
o  Field to Corporate

  Expectations of use by varied LP or non LP organizational roles

o  Expected Home Office use of the system

www.sysrepublic.com

o  Expected Field use of the system

  Application overview (Demo) after strategy session.



Introduction to Secure using Demo application

o  Benefit:  Allows business partner to begin visualizing the application

design and use.

  Begin Risk Area and business requirements discussions

*Selecting your metrics involves understanding the exposures important to you and
your organization.  An endless variety of questions and monitors can be created in
the Risk Dictionary.  It is important to keep the questions and monitors targeted and
focused on those activities of the greatest concern to your organization.

  Current and Prior Year Case Statistics Review
  Understanding of the POS system and any exposures created due to the way

transactions are processed.

  Operational Policies and Procedures on:

o  Returns
o  Markdowns
o  Discounts
o  Front end authorizations for voids, no sales, etc.

  Review of any reporting currently used

o

Identify the features of the successful reports you wish to carry over
or improve upon using Secure.

o  For the reports that are not very successful, identify the reasons

why and determine if they can be altered to be successful through
Secure.

  Document desired metrics in the Risk Dictionary Workbook

o  Second Meeting (Call) – One Week after first on site visit or first call

  Continued discussion of targeted metrics and business requirements
  Begin identifying intended use of application by Loss Prevention organizational

Hierarchy

o  Third Meeting (Call) – Two weeks after initial on site visit

  Finalization of initial targeted/core metrics(questions)

  Additions will occur as a result of the validation phase

  Client Services to assign identified risk metrics to appropriate category and

distribute Risk Dictionary Workbook to the client group

  Continued discussions to establish roles related to use by all organizational level.

o  Second onsite visit to begin more formal training

  Utilize demo application as an intermediate training tool

questions.

  Benefit:  Gives business partner the opportunity to identify required
