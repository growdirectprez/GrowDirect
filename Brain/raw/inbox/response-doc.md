EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
IBM Business Consulting Services has been engaged to assist with the selection of a Retail Supply Chain Management System for a client, herein referred to as a Katz Group Canada Limited. This successful firm has sales in excess of $ 4.2 Billion with 310 corporate stores and 1264 independent stores.  The company is Canada’s leading integrated retail pharmacy network with more pharmacies across Canada than any other retail pharmacy network.

Business Objectives and Priorities in Retail Supply Chain Management System
The corporation has a strong desire to grow significantly in the next decade.  Accordingly, the ability to have the system grow in functionality over time and be able to add new stores, entities, and possibly distribution centers is an important consideration.

Katz Group Canada Limited is engaged in a process to review an entire suite of Retail Supply Chain Management systems that will allow the corporation to more effectively manage and reduce costs, enhance current business processes though automation and the implementation of system ‘best practices’ provide more timely information to management while facilitating collaboration with stores, suppliers and partners as well as employees. While the system must meet the autonomous needs of the stores it must also respond to business unit and corporate requirements.

Product Integration (3.3.2)
Please describe the integration capability of your solution with external applications.  What features of your solution make it a good fit in a multi-application environment?

J.D. Edwards has full integration capabilities with its product called eXtended Process Integration or XPI for short.

XPI is a suite of technologies that allow you to assemble a portfolio of applications, link processes with trading partners, and integrate workflow engines so that the right process links to the right application and the right information gets to the right person at the right time.

XPI provides the infrastructure for both internal and external (B2B) collaboration. The internal component, XPIe, provides all the enterprise application integration (EAI) components required for high performance, real time internal collaboration. XPIe integrates seamlessly with the external or B2B component, XPIx. XPIx provides all the external workflow, security and standards-based elements required for safe and secure collaboration beyond the traditional four walls.

J.D. Edwards has moved beyond EAI and created a solution that will not only integrate internal systems but also will allow B2B collaboration through disparate systems, applications, and platforms. XPI helps organizations catch the next wave of business — extending beyond the enterprise to collaborate more effectively with customers and suppliers.

The standard industry approach to collaborative commerce is to provide point-to-point solutions, such as trading communities, exchanges, and marketplaces. Point-to-point solutions can mandate undesirable third-party intervention and provide very little integration and no universal connectivity. To avoid these pitfalls, J.D. Edwards has built its collaborative commerce vision on both its OneWorld enterprise and supply chain software, and its extended integration capabilities provided through XPI.

XPI uses a component-based message broker technology to integrate various enterprise applications and enable internal and B2B interoperability. We are leading the industry by providing a unique combination of technology (XPI) and applications, so our customers have the distinct advantage of implementing a complete collaborative solution.
