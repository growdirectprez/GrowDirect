# Commercial - Executive Summary

Commercial’s role is to build profitable  customer driven ranges and promotions that deliver to agreed margin targets

Commercial covers:
Planning the category strategy: from determining range direction to execution instore
Buying the range:
developing new product
constructing the range
Managing cost and Buy for Less to determine margin
determining size of committed or seasonal buys
managing retail price
managing and developing our supplier base
planning and negotiating promotions
exit planning
Reviewing KPIs to measure success against targets and re-forecasting depending on sales performance

The key benefits are that:
We deliver the budgeted margin to the business
The ranges we buy meet customer needs and expectations profitably and drive sales
We drive down cost and make best use of suppliers with market/customer knowledge to optimise supplier performance
We meet company price strategy and fight against competitor activity
We maintain data integrity
We review category performance to measure success against targets set in the category plan and capture actions for continuous improvement on all key measures

# Commercial - Executive Summary
Our main dependencies are with:
Marketing: to provide customer insight, market share data, competitor activity, and trade plan
Ordering & Distribution: to support the delivery of ranges into stores in the most cost effective way
Finance: to provide accurate data and budgets to support efficient margin management
Space, Range, and Display: to provide space information for efficient space management, and to build planograms that are driven by commercial and customer insight
Decisions that need to be taken – Process & Operating Model scope
Promotions
We believe that Marketing own the strategy and operational delivery & Commercial own the delivery to this strategy
Marketing are accountable for delivering the end-to-end process within the operating model
Price
We believe that Marketing own retail price strategy & Commercial own adherence to this strategy as well as retail price maintenance. Marketing own the retail price basket.
Negotiating and managing Logistics income
Should it be in Commercial (as in UK) rather than in Forecast & Ordering (International)?
Critical Path for the range change process
The Exec. agree the rules for the business and timings then have to be agreed at Trading Meeting or Operations Mtg
Commercial Director’s accountability is to meet the established rules or to flag issues to the Trading or Operations Boards
Do Supply Chain own it for continuity lines & Commercial for non-continuity? (S Chain view)
Operating Model Scope – view taken at 1st review
Merchandise Planning, Corporate Purchasing & International Sourcing – all now in scope
Clothing specific processes & Joint Buying – out of scope

# Tesco Commercial Principles
We develop Commercial rules and we follow them
We think Tesco first, Commercial second
We always hit our targets and then beat them
We always buy at the lowest price for the right quality and service
We get it right first time
Nothing is more important than the range and prices in our stores
Every Commercial role has operational content
We are always trying to make things simpler for stores
Good people who deliver get on
Everyone has a clear plan
We are never afraid to deliver bad news
We act with integrity always

# Commercial - Benefits
1) There is a plan for each category that is driven by company strategy
We ensure all functions are clear on their role in delivering their elements of the category plan and the direction for the category end to end
2) We deliver great ranges that meet customer needs and expectations profitably
We develop new and unique products that give customers a reason to shop with us
We forecast accurately to allow Supply Chain to optimise stockholding & minimise value of obsolete stock
We construct ranges and work with Space, Range & Display to optimise ranges according to store profile
3) We deliver the agreed margin to the business
We execute robust Buy for Less plans that simultaneously deliver value back to the customer
We meet company price strategy against competitor activity
We drive cost out of the value chain and make best use of suppliers to optimise their performance
4) We maintain data integrity within the product file
We provide a central control of product management which ensures financial control
5) We review category performance to measure success against targets set in the category plan and capture actions for continuous improvement on all key measures
We constantly monitor and review at line level the category’s performance to measure success against targets set in the category plan and capture actions for continuous improvement on all key measures

Commercial build profitable and customer driven ranges and promotions, and forecast committed buys to the agreed tolerance, that deliver to agreed margin targets
