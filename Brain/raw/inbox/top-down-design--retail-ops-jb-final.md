# Retail Operations - Executive Summary

Retail Operations covers:
Service Productivity, continual improvement of staff productivity by work measured staffing targets
Managing checkout service and productivity, scheduling to match trade and achieve queue length targets
Price Integrity, ensuring the price on the label matches the price charged at the checkouts
Sell goods and services, customers can buy all goods, development and support of specialist services I.e counters
Manage tenders, we can accept all tender types, reconcile and deposit cash promptly
The key benefits are that:
We look for way’s to reduce costs and improve the productivity of our store operations.
We have productive checkout staff who deliver great service
Our customers are charged the correct price every time
We provide excellent customer service efficiently and productively.
We monitor all transactions and tender movements from tills to cash office to bank to protect the companies assets
Our main dependencies are with:
Commercial: to ensure prices and products are maintained centrally and the number of price changes is managed
Marketing: to provide policy on prices, promotions, shelf edge labels and centralised management of point of sale
Property: to ensure best practise equipment and facilities is designed and available e.g ergonomically designed checkouts
Replenishment: to order stock, replenish using three point check and forecast sales to enable us to schedule staff
I.T: to ensure the right systems, software and equipment is developed and available
Key decisions to be made include:
What is the Group I.T solution for productivity scheduling?
What is Group best practise for operating concessions?
What is Group best practise for central and local price changes?

# Retail Operations - Benefits

                                                Retail Operations supports the customer promises of
                                           “I don’t queue” “The prices are good” “The staff are great”
1) Store Productivity
We continually reduce the cost and improve the productivity of our operation by:
measuring, benchmarking and setting realistic targets for stores
ensuring operational improvements in process result in productivity gains
2) Manage Checkout Service and Performance
We have productive checkout staff who deliver great service by:
training our staff and continually reviewing performance
schedule to ensure customers don’t queue
monitor performance throughout the day & take action to ensure we meet our service promise and staff remain productive
3) Price Integrity
We ensure our customers are charged the correct price every time by:
checking price on shelf edge label matches price at checkout
display in store marketing material in line with marketing guidelines
being legally compliant on shelf edge labels, scales, product withdrawals etc

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# Retail Operations - Benefits
4) Sell Goods and Services
We deliver excellent customer service whilst serving customers by:
scanning products accurately and quickly so customers are correctly charged
offering excellent customer service tailored to meet the needs of all customers
serving customers quickly so they do not queue
5) Manage Tenders
We monitor all tender movements to protect company assets by:
maintaining an audit trail of all tender movements
efficient and secure handling of cash from till to safe to bank to reduce risk and maximise interest earned
