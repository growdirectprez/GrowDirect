Memo
To:	Ron Langford
	The Kroger Corp
From:	Tanya Kovacik
Date:	August 1, 2001
Subject:		Agenda Retek Procurement Project Information Gathering Meeting

Kroger Base Retek Procurement CRP Project
Information Gathering Interview
Cincinnati, Ohio
August x, 2001

Participants

Kroger:
Ron Langford
Paul Tudor

PricewaterhouseCoopers:
Drew Riegler, Partner
Tanya Kovacik, Principal Consultant
Garry Birkhofer, Director
John Farrell, Director
Jeff Goethals, Principal Consultant

Schedule

2 hours
Discussion of Project Scope:
Which Kroger business units are included in the scope of the CRP?
Which Retek modules are included in the scope of the CRP?
Has an initial roll-out strategy been determined (ie: by module, by business unit, by DC)?
Which current applications will be replaced/retired with the roll-out of the Retek Procurement solution (incl profile of retired applications, ie: age, platform, support)?
What model was used to develop the staffing?
What role are you expecting the consultants to play?
Advise Kroger project team in creating deliverables
Take ownership of a subset of deliverables and be responsible for delivery
Contributions of Retek advisors vs Grocery industry advisors
What constitutes Change Management in your view?
What role is the ½ time Change Management resource expected to play?
Advise Kroger project team in creating deliverables
Responsible for creating all Change Management work products
Is there a plan for the 7 months of the Design/CRP stage of the project?



Schedule (cont’d)
1 hour
Discussion of Business Objectives:
General Questions:
Can you share your initial DOS/DC inventory analysis with us?
What is the business case supporting the Enterprise Procurement projects?
Can you share your evaluation/selection criteria for the initial application selection?
What product lines/DC/Business Units will be included in the project?  What factors influenced the selection?
What is the scope of the process/business change that you envision with this project?
What is your supply chain management philosophy/strategy?
Are there any additional supply chain/inventory productivity initiatives running concurrent with the Retek projects?
What are your specific goals/critical success factors for this project?
DC to Store Level Questions:
What type transportation optimization to stores is currently used?
What are typical inventory delivery 'pain points' reported from the store level?
DC Level Questions:
What parties have control over bringing product into the warehouse
How accurate are actual service levels to target service levels?
What percentage of products are currently cross-docked?
How are the DCs configured for cross-docking?
How productive are the receiving and put away processes?  Are they benchmarked?
Does Kroger support DSD on standard warehouse items (i.e. promotional items)?
How is successful inventory management measured at the DC level? Days of supply, how measured? What categories?
Does Kroger make use if 'Slow Moving' distribution centers?
Does Kroger use slow moving slots for less than pallet (by layers ordering / receiving)?
Do you hold damage / reclaimed products at the DC level for consolidation or does it all go direct to a reclamation center?
Category Management Level Questions:
How are forecasts generated?  Are they based on POS data (product sell-thru) or DC ship data? Other?
Does the method of forecasting vary by product type / category?
What visibility to store level inventory data is used for DC level forecasts?
What is your sense of overall data integrity from the store level up?
What are typical shipping & receiving 'pain points' for category managers?
How are Category Managers measured?  What metrics are used to measure inventory productivity?
What are typical inventory receiving / shipping 'pain points' reported from category managers?
From Manufacturer to Kroger:
What are average lead-times on regular movement products and promotional movement products?
Are there differences in the ordering process and fulfillment process of regular  movement product and promotional movement product?
½ hour
