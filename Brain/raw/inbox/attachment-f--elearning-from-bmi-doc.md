Attachment F – e-Learning from BMI



What is e-learning?

E-learning is the use of technology (usually the Internet) to create, deliver, track, and encourage learning.

What will an investment in e-learning bring?

Talent empowers individuals to perform at their best. Education is a cornerstone investment in your organization’s talent. Shift some of the accountability for training to the people who want or need it. Encourage your associates to learn by allowing them to co-own the learning process.

E-learning has become a fundamental part of learning strategies for companies who plan for success.

JDA’s Business Management Institute manages JDA’s e-learning initiatives under the name JDALEARN.
E-learning Content

JDA offers online training that addresses its products and industries. Courses are written from an end-user perspective, including business processes and best practices.

Our development methodology combines:

JDA industry and product expertise
Interactive and engaging activities
Professional instruction model

Courses play through the Microsoft Internet Explorer browser (v5.0 and up). Plug-ins are required for some courses.

E-Learning Fact Sheet

Some of the key benefits of e-learning include:

Accessibility: Take courses any time in any place with multiple deployment options (Internet, CD, network)
Assessment: Assess and track progress of individual learners
Control: Deliver the same messages and policies or customize the message for a specific group
Ease-of-use: Use standard web browser features to train your associates
Flexibility: Blend e-learning with an instructor-led course or offer e-learning as a standalone course
Investment: Leverage your investment in corporate intranets with a distributable learning application
Scale: Offer more learning to more users for less cost than instructor-led training
Speed: Increase timeliness of information to improve business responsiveness; deploy updates fast


Pricing Options

JDA e-learning courses will be available in the following pricing options:
Subscription:
The subscription option allows defined learners unlimited access to a defined group of courses for one year. The pricing varies by the number of users and the number of courses selected.
Units:
Like our prepaid instructor-led packages, an assessment of training needs is conducted. Based on this assessment, a package of units is purchased upfront. Courses are assigned unit values according to the type and length of the course. As learners take courses, units are deducted from the package.
JDA is committed to a blended learning approach (combining e-learning and instructor-led training). This is the most effective strategy for educating your associates. To offer a blended solution, JDA has prepaid packages with both e-learning units and instructor-led student days.
On-Demand:
The on-demand option allows learners to pay with a credit card through the e-commerce function on JDALEARN.
Custom:
Custom courses are built on a time and expense model. Custom learning environments are priced on a case-by-case basis.
