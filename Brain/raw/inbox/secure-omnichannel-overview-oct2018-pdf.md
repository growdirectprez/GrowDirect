Buy Online Return In-Store (BORIS) Collusion
(Example)

What

A consumer returns goods purchased online to a collusive associate within a retail store.
The return is processed and the customer refunded, however the goods are retained by the
consumer.

Impact

Stock and cash loss

Bad Actor(s)

Combination of associate and customer

Identification

• Algorithms and models identify consistent behavior between associates and customers, looking

•

for patterns of outlying activity at multiple levels (item, basket of goods, time, frequency,
linking of consumer ID’s etc.)
Relationships that are deemed to be risky are highlighted for LP associates to investigate
• Detailed information about the transactions and why they are deemed to be risky
•

Profiles of associate and consumer behavior compared and contrasted to what is deemed
to be normal activity

Buy Online Return via Call Center
(Example)

What

Impact

Customer makes repeated fraudulent calls to call centers reporting damaged or undelivered goods
or invalid substitution products in the case of “Grocery Home Shopping”

Product and/or cash loss, depending on retailers policy of shipping or refunding items

Bad Actor(s)

The consumer is taking advantage of the customer service policies of the retailer

Identification

• Algorithms and models to track customer purchase, return and complaint data to identify

where excessive and outlying abuse is occurring

• Very often persistent abusers are “Social Engineers” repeatedly testing the call center system

with multiple calls and aggressive escalations
LP provided recommendations of customers to “Divorce” (not allow to purchase again)

•

• Consumer linking also continues to provide analysis if customer creates a new account or

tries to order by via different payment types

Buy Online Pickup In-Store (BOPIS), Warehousing
(Example)

What

Impact

A 3rd party reseller makes multiple single purchases online for store pickup of high demand or
time sensitive items.
The 3rd party lists the purchased items on eBay or similar at a higher price.
When a purchase is made of the item on eBay, the reseller picks up an item in store (which
initiates the billing for that item), repackages and ships it to the new customer.
Any items the reseller has not been able to resell are cancelled and returned to retailer stock.

In demand items are “reserved” by reseller and not available to legitimate customers
When stock is released it may no longer be in “demand” and therefore requires markdown in store

Bad Actor(s)

The reseller is using the retailer as a warehouse for their business

Identification

• Algorithms and models analyze patterns of sales, returns, pickups, item types and volume

related to other customers and normal profiles.
LP provided recommendations of customers to “Divorce” (not allow to purchase again)

Order Cancellation
(Example)

What

Impact

In a “Pick in Store” orders are cancelled after the items have been picked. This can either be
legitimate cancels or collusion with the associate in store who provides the goods to a customer
before the order is cancelled.

Repeat cancelled orders from a customer causes lost payroll hours for pick and de-pick process
Collusive behavior leads to product and cash loss

Bad Actor(s)

Customer and/or associate

Identification

• Algorithms and models analyze patterns of sales, returns, pickups, item types and volume

related to other customers and normal profiles.
LP provided recommendations of customers to “Divorce” (not allow to purchase again)
LP associates are provided recommendations of associate/customer relationships to investigate
