Proposed System Changes

NOTE: A lot of these suggestions can be found on Amazon’s site.  I’ll designate them with an “A”.  If it’s an example that I’ve seen on other sites, I’ll specify.

Make the T&K logo clickable, sending customers to the home page
Change the “shop” button on the shopping cart to “continue shopping” and have the link send the customer back to:
The product list page IF they put the item in the cart from the product list page (i.e. a listing of specific products) OR they put the item in the cart from the product detail page
The last page they visited IF they added an item to the cart from any other page OR if they went to the shopping cart from any other page.  jcrew.com does this the way I am suggesting
Add a link to the homepage from every page (perhaps via the home icon that’s on many of the pages)
Add more detail to the order confirmation page – the details of the order, total price, ship to address, credit card (last 5 digits) with “print this page” instructions.  Also, information / links on how to track the order.  “A”
Add parametric search options for the specific categories we are building out (this can be an “advanced search” link, immediately under the search field on the homepage.
For Swatch, these would be by Line (Irony, Skin, etc), Analog / Digital, and specific features Single-time, Multi-time, internet-time, day / date, alarm, multiple alarms, etc. (if you can think of others, cool).
Boo.com is the only site I’ve seen that does a halfway decent job with multiple-attribute parametric search for fashion goods.
Add descriptive text (brief descriptions) to the “specials” graphics on the right side of the page
Add a “help” button to the store services banners.  This should link to all other store services (warranty, policies, Q&A, FAQs, tips, etc) “A”
Change the homepage to highlight specials and offer profile-driven items on the main screen with categories across the banners on the top and company information details on the bottom.  The structure of category banners on the top and company information details on the bottom should be consistent on every page.  I think that we can probably fit the category banner AND some of the key customer service info (“shopping cart”, “help”, and “my account” are the most likely), especially if we vertically stack the info “A”
Add a checkout tracking graphic (something that tells the customer where they are in the checkout process) to the checkout page “A”
Add the ability for someone to shop and checkout without registering (i.e. only entering their bill to / ship to info and email address) or storing their credit card payment information NOTE: This is probably not necessary for Swatch, but will be required longer term
Remove some of the extra graphics / font tricks, especially the shadow text and italics on all the headings.  These just make it harder to read.
Add functionality to the direct entry fields so that the customer can hit “enter” after the final field rather than having to click “ok” (e.g. I should be able to type “soup” in the quick search field, hit enter, and have it start searching.  The same should hold true for setting up the customer profile or other direct entry pages)
