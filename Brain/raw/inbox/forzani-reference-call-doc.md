Forzani Reference Call
Debbie Gillis – CIO
November 12, 2003

Joined Forzani 2 years ago
Looking for a POS solution for corporate and franchise stores, merchandising solution was 10 years old
Unable to respond to business needs (work arounds)
7 to 8 years ago close to bankruptcy, no investment in technology for years
Doing well for last 3 years
Looking for state of the art tools for end to end processes
Final Frontier is advanced warehouse management and advanced merchandising (price optimization)
52 events to manage a year
Going live next year in May with merchandising
Implemented WinDSS POS in 120 stores last year
Implemented PMM Price Management (including promotions) & Store Master last year (June 2002)
Implementing PMM inventory management and replenishment next May
Integrate with JD Edwards financials (One World) next year
Have implemented centralized price book, STSR12 conversion
Considered JDA, SAS and Retek (implementation costs were too high for Retek)
Chose JDA because of the larger customer base, similar customers
Pay high maintenance support – want good support
JDA provides source code
Staples had JDA MMS
Benefit of having source code is control, can go to JDA for mods, JDA like vendors, hire own resources – Gives options
Phase 1 was vanilla (minimal modifications) – Biggest modification was to support reserving warehouse inventory for the 4 banners using specific rules
Phase 2 will have more customization
Do not foresee integration problems with JD Edwards
90% of the implementation spend is with JDA, hired 1 external project manager for Forzani
5-6 Forzani resources required for implementation
Will be up to 10 resources between Jan and May, business analysts, interface developers, systems analysts, project management, 2-3 in QA

Critical Success Factors for Implementation
User involvement all the way along – must have user buy in
Project manager must provide expectations for users time (have right people at right meetings), respect users time
Top people in organization must be involved
Will fail if you have too many mods or if you move too fast
Need basic stability before you deliver advanced functionality:  Phase 1 – Bronze (2002), Phase 2 – Silver (2003), Phase 3 – Gold (2004)
QA and user acceptance are necessary
Need a retail / JDA specific project manager

Education and Training
For education and training, used 1 merchant and 1 store operations person when POS implemented
Converted them to trainers
Impacts 50 people at Go-Live, looking to have more full time trainers

Issues
Difficult to find good Unix people, AS/400 is much easier
Finding individuals with knowledge on JDA PMM is tough
Performance issues with database – 600,000 to 1,000,000 UPCs even though only 80,000 active SKUs

Relationship with JDA
Issues with original project manager, didn’t fit in with Forzani culture, replaced him
Need to give JDA notice and plan ahead to get good resources
Need to push for what you want
Price management functionality not advanced enough (Buy 1 get one free, deals)
PMM has had decent support, had more issues with POS
No room for buffer on timeline, negotiated for a free JDA QA person on site
PMM is a rich product, more complex then MMS, merchants like it but hard to implement changes
Bugs can be a problem, put something in the contract to protect against this
Price management is weak
Biggest cost was integration between WinDSS and PMM
Need to get business attention that this is a priority – often times more concerned with meeting their own targets
