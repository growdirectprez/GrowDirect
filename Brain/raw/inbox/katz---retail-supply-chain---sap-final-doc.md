Executive Summary

Though not known to many, SAP is the largest retail software provider in the world.

In this response, SAP outlines its ability to provide Katz Group Canada Ltd. (Katz) with a comprehensive, fully integrated and proven, extensible retail supply chain management solution using the industry leading SAP software.

In 1994 SAP purchased DACOS, a best of breed retail software provider, to gain retail industry and business process expertise. For the past 9 years SAP has leveraged this expertise in conjunction with customer insights to build best-in-class retail solutions that have been deployed in 292 customers around the world on SAP’s robust technology platform.

Initially, SAP was challenged in understanding some of the unique requirements present in the retail industry regarding solution deployment and change management practices. This resulted in early SAP implementations that did not meet expectations.  Recognizing these challenges, SAP’s put a plan into place to ensure the success of the retail initiative.  First, SAP’s board worked very closely with its customer base to demonstrate commitment and ensure customer success. Second, SAP extended a team of industry subject matter experts to drive product strategy, ensuring a focused development program.  Third, SAP committed tens of millions of dollars to elevate SAP’s retail solution to the best in class status it now holds.  Finally, to ensure continued focus and success, SAP dedicated direct executive involvement by appointing a board member direct management responsibility for SAP’s retail industry business unit.  This is the only industry group that reports directly to a board member.

Recent retail implementations have more than met customer expectations not only from a functional perspective, but also strategically, providing SAP customers with unique competitive advantage.  In each of SAP’s last 3 software releases, the retail solution has seen significant improvements in existing capabilities and major new developments in the areas of merchandise and assortment planning, supply chain management, category management, POS data management, and consumer focused solutions.  In addition, SAP also provides a suite of best business practices for retail, thus shortening implementation time frames and increasing user acceptance.

To provide even greater benefit to its retail customers, SAP has greatly enhanced the user experience, especially at the store level.  Extensive effort has been committed to ensure that business processes flow within the SAP software to complement the users interaction and experience, Internet browser technology is leveraged where appropriate and ergonomic principles are adhered to, throughout the user interface design.

SAP is now uniquely qualified to provide an integrated Retail Supply Chain Management System that has been designed to deliver an attractive total cost of ownership and has the architectural flexibility to adapt to meet changing demands over time.

The solution proposed in this response is a result not only of the functional specifications Katz requires, but is also a result of SAP’s understanding of key business drivers faced by Katz and other retailers.  SAP’s solution will fully support Katz’s ‘need for speed’ and entrepreneurial approach to driving business by providing a flexible, proven solution that can be quickly adapted to meet changing business goals.  SAP’s technology platform and extensive customer and partner base will provide Katz with a unique opportunity to collaborate with its partners and suppliers extensively and in new, innovative ways.  As the SAP solution delivers true real-time supply chain visibility, Katz will gain control of its product mix and inventory positions, by putting the right information in the hands of the right people at the right time.

Furthermore, SAP’s retail solution benefits extensively from cross-industry best practice development investments in such areas as human resources, financials, supplier relationship management, customer relationship management and business intelligence.  SAP has over 2,000 developers in cross industry in these areas that benefit the retail solution.

This uniquely differentiates SAP from its competitors as SAP customers benefit not only from a best in class retail solution, but also from a supply chain solution that has been rated number 1 in the market by the analyst community.

Globalization at SAP

In general terms, globalization is the process of designing and developing products and services for a worldwide market.  It addresses all of the enterprise issues associated with making a company truly global.  For the globalization of products and services this involves integrating all of the internal and external business functions within logistics, marketing, sales and customer support in the world market.  It consists of two aspects – internationalization and localization.

Internationalization is the process of generalizing a product so that it can handle multiple languages and cultural conventions without the need for redesign.  Internationalization takes place at the level of program design and document development.  For SAP, this ensures that the SAP solution set is suitable for use in multiple cultural and technical environments, and that they support localization processes.

Localization is the process of adapting software to the needs of a particular country (or "locale") and it consists of two major aspects – functional localization and linguistic translation.

These definitions are derived from the standards of  HYPERLINK "http://www.lisa.com" www.lisa.com (Localization Industry Standard Association).


Language Version (Linguistic Translation) versus a Country Version (Functional Localization)

Language versions and country versions are not the same.

Language versions refer to the level of translation for SAP software shipments.
Country versions refer to the adaptation of SAP's software (coding and customizing) to cover legal requirements and common, local business practices.  The logic of the software solution works independently of the language version used.

SAP solutions follow global best business practices, with specific functions and features to account for varying national tax laws, accounting laws, and manufacturing regulations in over 40 countries.  Katz can run all country versions simultaneously in the same system and each user can logon in their preferred language, with almost 30 languages to choose from.
