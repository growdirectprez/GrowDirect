Develop Publishing Workflows

Overview
Developing publishing workflows is the process of integrating the designed authors, roles and workflow into the Publishing Center so that site content can be created, tagged, approved and published by the appropriate authors within the BroadVision system.


Approach
The approach to developing publishing workflows is to build the process identified to create, tag and approve content within the organization into the Publishing Center.  After the workflows are developed, the workflows are mapped to the Instant Publisher Forms which represent the appropriate corresponding categories of content (articles, products, etc...).  Additionally, inboxes and access groups are created to correspond to the authors and roles identified in the design phase so that these individuals and/or groups of individuals can participate in the workflow process as designated in the design phase.


Work Product
The typical work product is the integration of all publishing workflows, authors and roles into the Publishing Center so that content can be published within the BroadVision system.

Input
Required inputs are the finalized design of authors, roles and workflows.
