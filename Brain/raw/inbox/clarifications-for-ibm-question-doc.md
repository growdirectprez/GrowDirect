Clarifications for IBM question 3c:

We are seeking clarification about whether the PLCB has a preference for any tools to be used by the vendor.  In other words, does the PLCB have specific tools in mind that the vendors are required to use?  Shelf optimization refers to looking at the results found by placing for example, a mixer bottle on the bottom shelf of the Vodka shelving to determine up-selling or cross-selling results or opportunities.



Clarifications for IBM question 5a:

In some cases, retailers store their own data in a data warehouse.  The data is continuously updated and available at any time to be used for continuing analytics.  We are seeking clarification to understand how current the data is updated or if the vendor will be required to pull the data as part of the effort.  In other words, is the data for the retail stores current, is the data updated on a frequent basis, and will the updates continue to be maintained throughout the lifecycle of this project?

Additionally, we were interested in whether PLCB maintains buyer demographics either in-house or purchased from a third party.  Buyer demographics might include age, ethnicity or income of the person who made the purchase, as well as whether the purchase was via cash/Debit or credit.



Clarifications for IBM question 5b:

We are seeking clarification about PLCB’s "batch schedule" and how often the data is refreshed for analytical use.  In the PLCB’s legacy data sources, i.e., for sales, inventory, demand forecasting, replenishment, etc., how often is data updated?  As an example, when a Buyer gets a report or uses an application to obtain sales data, is that data from yesterday?  Last Saturday?  Last hour?  Etc.
