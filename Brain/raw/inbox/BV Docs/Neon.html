NEON and BroadVision Join Forces to Integrate e-Business Applications

DENVER and REDWOOD CITY, Calif., Nov. 1 /PRNewswire/ -- New Era of Networks, Inc. (Nasdaq: NEON) and BroadVision, Inc. (Nasdaq: BVSN) today announced the formation of a strategic alliance to enable businesses to integrate existing systems with Internet applications to perform business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2C) e-Business. The companies will use NEON's e-Biz Integrator to integrate BroadVision's web-based One-to-One(TM) Commerce solutions with applications such as Oracle(R), SAP, PeopleSoft(R), Siebel(R) and legacy systems.

BroadVision's line of One-to-One Commerce solutions allows companies to market and sell products over the Internet via highly personalized web-based communication with prospects and customers. When implemented, these e-Business applications need to inter-operate with existing operational systems, such as order entry and inventory management. For instance, orders placed by customers using BroadVision may use SAP R/3 for fulfillment and billing.

In the past, creating the necessary links between applications has been a time-consuming, risky and error-prone custom programming task. NEON e-Biz Integrator eliminates the need for custom integration coding by supplying an out-of-the-box software solution that provides tight integration between BroadVision and operational systems, such as ERP packages, custom and legacy applications.

"NEON's e-Biz Integrator will enable quick integration of BroadVision's One-to-One Commerce solutions with enterprise software applications," said Dr. Eric Golin, chief technical officer of BroadVision. "The combination of NEON and BroadVision will further enable our customers to provide the best possible experience for their customers."

"BroadVision has become the gorilla of B2C e-Business applications," added Rick Adam, NEON's chief executive officer. "Joining forces with an acclaimed leader in one-to-one marketing solutions further enables our customers to create competitive advantage through e-Business." The NEON/BroadVision Solution

BroadVision's One-to-One Commerce serves as the technology foundation for a family of applications designed for rapid deployment and personalization of high-transaction Internet business sites. The One-to-One Commerce suite includes BroadVision One-to-One Retail Commerce and BroadVision One-to-One Business Commerce. The NEON integration will also support BroadVision One-to-One Knowledge. NEON e-Biz Integrator performs application integration functions for B2B and B2C applications. The product provides an integration server engine, including the patented NEONRules and the NEONFormatter. NEON e-Biz Integrator also supports XML and EDI capabilities.

The combination of NEON's e-Biz Integrator and BroadVision's One-To-One Commerce family of products will offer businesses a comprehensive, web-based, customer interaction and relationship management solution that are tightly integrated with their operational systems. NEON and BroadVision will jointly develop and market the e-Business Integration solution to customers. About BroadVision
