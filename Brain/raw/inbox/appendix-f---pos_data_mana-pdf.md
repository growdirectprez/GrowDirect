KEEPING YOUR FINGER ON THE PULSE
In a market driven by the whims of the consumer, retailers need
to respond quickly to local, regional, and international shifts in
sales patterns. Stocking the wrong items, for example, can result
in unwanted inventory or an inability to cope with surges in
demand. Management decisions based on imperfect point of
sales (POS) data can be extremely damaging, creating a snowball
effect that can impact your entire organization. You may have
sophisticated systems analysis and reporting tools in place, but
do you have access to accurate, up-to-date information from
your stores?

DATA THE WAY YOU WANT IT
SAP® POS Data Management software improves decision making
at all levels by gathering facts and figures from your retail out-
lets and making them available throughout your enterprise. It
also provides standardized reports at the click of a mouse through
a Web-based user interface, enabling managers to access, explore,
and analyze incoming sales information faster and more easily
than ever before. And it offers vital support for longer-term
strategic and operational decision making, helping you capital-
ize on emerging business opportunities.

SAP POS Data Management gives you much more than the
basics. It enables you to examine all your key issues – events and
promotions, prices and margins, and reason codes for returns –
as well as a host of other parameters.

The application is integrated directly with SAP® Business
Intelligence (SAP® BI) software, allowing you to scrutinize your
stores’ performance from a variety of perspectives. It also links
to a range of follow-on processes such as inventory management,
financials, billing, and credit-card settlement. In addition, the
application supports trickle polling, giving your planners an
up-to-the-minute picture of everyday activities. This approach
reduces strain on other systems, such as merchandise manage-
ment, that often work through the night processing mission-
critical data.

READY TO RUN
Predefined templates accelerate implementation and return on
investment (ROI). The application comes ready to run, with
POS processing and analytics features tailored to a variety of
standard roles ranging from store managers to replenishment
planners. Its integrated sales-audit functionality ensures that all
data is accurate and complete. And because the application is

built for integration with SAP BI, retail data is delivered direct to
the heart of your company – there is no need to process it in
the merchandise management system first. This improves the
performance of all your IT systems and allows documents and
relevant data to be used throughout your organization.
