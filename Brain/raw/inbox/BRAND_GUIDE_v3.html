Animation Specifications

Idle (All Clear)

Gentle 3s vertical bob (2px). Two wave arcs in Health Green (#059669) at 10–25% opacity, soft pulse. Small green dot steady. The bird feels alive and monitoring — "all clear."

Chirp (Health Alert)

2.2s cycle. Three waves staggered 180ms. Wave 1 starts green (#059669), Wave 2 hits yellow (#FBBF24), Wave 3 ends red (#EF4444). Each wave transitions through the full green→yellow→red spectrum as it radiates outward. Alert dot cycles the same sequence.

Timing

Wave 1: 0ms, starts green, full opacity. Wave 2: 180ms delay, starts green, 70% opacity. Wave 3: 360ms delay, starts green, 50% opacity. Each wave color-shifts to red by the time it fades. Creates a "vital signs escalating" effect.

Don't

Don't skip the green start. The green→yellow→red progression is the core brand motion. Starting at yellow or red loses the health-monitor metaphor.

Usage Guidelines

Do

Use the dark background (#0D1117) as primary. The Signal Yellow bird pops beautifully against dark surfaces.

Don't

Place the yellow bird on yellow or light-amber backgrounds. It needs contrast to read.

Do

Use the health-color waves to reinforce the monitoring/alert narrative. Green = calm, yellow = watch, red = act.

Don't

Use red waves on a green bird or mix health colors on the bird body. The body stays yellow/amber; the waves carry the health status.

Do

Include the tiny health bar under the wordmark in dashboard contexts. It reinforces the vital-signs metaphor.

Don't

Loop the chirp animation forever. Fire → 2-3 chirps → return to idle green. Respect attention.
