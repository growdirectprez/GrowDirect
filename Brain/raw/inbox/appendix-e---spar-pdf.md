SPAR SWITZERLAND

OPTIMIZING THE SUPPLY OF GOODS TO STORES AND
CUSTOMERS WITH mySAPTM SCM

The SPAR Switzerland group trades under the name SPAR
Handels AG. Its head offices are in St. Gallen and it is one of
28 subsidiaries of the extremely successful Dutch retailing group
that operates in 5 different continents. With around 170 SPAR
and EUROSPAR supermarkets, plus 6 TopCC Cash and Carry
wholesale supermarkets, employing more than 820 people in
total, SPAR Switzerland recently achieved sales of around
772 million Swiss francs. The corporation continues to grow,
and to ensure safe and lasting support for all its business
processes, it relies on SAP® R/3® and the industry solution
mySAPTM Retail.

INTEGRATED AND FUTURE-PROOF
The central warehouse, which covers an area of more than
25,000 square meters, and from which all SPAR supermarkets in
Switzerland receive their supplies, is also in St. Gallen. One
hundred thousand orders and around 900 storage pallets are
processed there every day, all subject to various retail-specific
requirements. This means that specifications such as weight or
best-before dates have to be considered when stock is being
picked or moved to or around the warehouse. In short: the
central warehouse is a food distribution center that has to meet
all kinds of major logistical challenges and requirements.

One of the highlights of the SAP LES project included the direct
data transmission link, which enables data to be entered
remotely. Today, SPAR Switzerland employees use mobile hand
scanners to log goods receipts, track removal and storage of
stock within the warehouse, and facilitate picking. The scanners
exchange all online data with the central SAP LES system, which
ensures an integrated and predominantly paperless flow of
information in the warehouse.

SPAR Switzerland's goal was to meet all these challenges head-on
using a new, powerful, and future-proof warehouse management
system. After evaluating systems currently available on the
market, the retail company found good reason to opt for SAP®
Logistics Execution System (SAP® LES), a major component of
mySAPTM Supply Chain Management (mySAPTM SCM).

“SAP LES fulfilled our requirements with regard to functionality,
integration, and future orientation better than any of the
other systems we tried,” says CIO Wolfgang Mähr.

FOOD DISTRIBUTION AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL
“With SAP Logistics Execution System, we can offer our stores an
optimal and timely supply of goods of the highest quality. We
now also have much more transparent business processes that are
supported by workflows, which make us much more competitive
as a food distribution center,” says
Egon Baumgärtner, the head of
logistics. Thanks to SAP LES, SPAR
Switzerland has witnessed an in-
crease in the quality of picking and
lasting improvements have been
made in stock availability.

“With SAP LES, we have a highly

effective instrument with which to

achieve further success in this tough,

competitive industry.”

Wolfgang Mähr, CIO, SPAR Switzerland

HIGHLIGHT: SAP MOBILE DATA COLLECTION
The company wanted to use SAP LES to implement a whole
range of improvements, which it summarizes under the motto
“increasing  competitiveness  and
raising customer orientation.”  That
meant introducing new, optimized
business processes for stock distribu-
tion, increasing picking quality, and
avoiding  stock-outs,  while  at  the
same time minimizing stock levels,
cutting costs, improving customer
service by reducing delivery times,
and substantially increasing the level of automation.

During the SAP LES implementation project, SPAR Switzerland
saw one success after another, starting with the definition and
mapping of a whole range of integrated business processes in
SAP LES and culminating in the production start-up that was
accompanied by training and testing.

In addition, the speed of stock

turnover – in particular, incoming goods inspection at individ-
ual stores – has increased considerably due to improved data
quality.
