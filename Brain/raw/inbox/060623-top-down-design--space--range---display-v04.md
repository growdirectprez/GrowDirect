# Space, Range & Display - Executive Summary

Space, Range & Display covers:
Creating and maintaining space allocations for all new and existing stores
Building ranges & planograms for all store formats, sizes and profiles
Implementing space, range & planogram changes in store
Managing & maintaining all central and store processes, systems & information needed to support these activities
The key benefits are that:
We use our space optimally, ensuring we have the right product categories available to customers
We have ranges which are relevant for customers
The displays on our fixtures make shopping easy for customers and support the operation
Processes are simple for stores and enable stores to implement changes accurately and on time
Our main dependencies are with:
Commercial: to ensure that space, range & display systems link with the product file & product hierarchy
Supply chain & distribution: to ensure the right ranges get delivered to the right stores on time, and that the ordering systems use the correct capacities
Store operations: to ensure stores have all the information they need to carry out all space, range & display activity
Property: to ensure all new stores, extensions and refits receive the right space, ranges & planograms
MIS: to ensure that group management information solutions include space, range & display reporting, and to ensure that management information is available to space, range & display systems
Key decisions to be made include:
What are the best practice systems for space, range & display?
What is the impact of Central European buying and any regional buying in China on space, range & display processes?
What is the right hand-over point with Property for creation of floor plans for new stores, extensions & refits?
Who owns ‘planning new & discontinued lines’ - Supply Chain or Space, Range & Display?
Where will depot & store range records be held?  Where will capacities be calculated & held?

# Space, Range & Display - Benefits
1) Set space allocations
We optimise the use of our space to ensure we:
can carry the right breadth and depth of product categories to satisfy our customers
give the right amount of space to each category to support efficient replenishment/stockholding
2) Build ranges and planograms
We ensure that we are Number 1 for range through:
Creating ranges which appeal to all customers
Targeting our ranges to match the customer profile of each store
We build planograms which:
Make shopping easy for customers
Support efficient replenishment/stockholding
Enable good availability and support the customer promise of ‘I can get what I want’
3) Implement space, range & display changes in store
We ensure ranges and fixtures in store remain relevant for customers through scheduled range reviews
We ensure ranges and fixtures in store remain fit for the replenishment operation

Space, range & display drives sales and enables efficient replenishment
