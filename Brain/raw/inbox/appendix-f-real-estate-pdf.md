AN URGENT NEED FOR PROFESSIONAL MANAGEMENT
In the past, efforts to increase efficiency , for the most part, have
concentrated on production and human resources, with
improvements to production and personnel management com-
manding center stage. The arrival of global networks and
Internet technology has brought a whole new dimension to
supply chain management and e-commerce. Maximizing the
business benefits from real estate management has stubbornly
remained a secondary consideration, despite the fact that real
estate accounts for up to 30% of your balance sheet total.

In a business environment characterized by fierce competition
and rapid change, the need for professional real estate manage-
ment is becoming increasingly urgent. Property managers and
owners alike want to get the most out of their real estate. At the
same time, real estate management processes have to be
integrated into your overall business strategy. Whether your
customers are commercial tenants, residential tenants, or inter-
nal departments, you need to respond quickly and efficiently to
their needs, make better use of your available resources, and
reduce operational costs while increasing the value of your real
estate portfolio.

THE COMPLETE REAL ESTATE MANAGEMENT SOLUTION
mySAP Financials (cid:150) Real Estate provides the expertise and
resources you need to master these challenges. It offers a com-
prehensive software solution that gives you the flexibility you
need to react to changing circumstances and to meet the
demands of both your customers and the market.

REAL ESTATE E-BUSINESS

into the existing business processes and are available around the

BUSINESS ANALYTICS
SAP is currently developing an asset management solution
based on SAP Strategic Enterprise Management (SAP SEM).
This new solution will deliver flexible tools for efficiently
analyzing and managing your real estate portfolio. It will
provide several methods to determine the market value of real
estate assets, plus KPIs, simulation scenarios, and what-if
analysis to help you make strategic decisions. The solution will
deliver the results in detailed reports, overview lists, or portfolio
graphics. These tools will enable you to analyze business
alternatives such as buy, sell, or leaseback. Standard reporting
provides decision makers with up-to-the-minute facts and
