Communication Plan Development for Katz

Communication Principles

To ensure managers feel that they are well informed,
   communication to them will precede communications to
   people who work for them.
Notice of upcoming communication messages will be presented to steering committee members in advance
Communication delivery will be done by the most credible
  source(s) for that particular message, including executives and
  other members of the management team.
 Each message will be based upon specific objectives and will be relevant to the target audience.
At regular intervals, communication effectiveness will be evaluated, and follow
  up measures will be taken as appropriate.
 The communication plan will focus on delivering critical
  messages to any audience no fewer than three times.
 The messages will be written to honestly portray
  important information, even if it will be viewed negatively.
