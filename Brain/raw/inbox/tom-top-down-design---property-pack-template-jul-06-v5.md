# Property Services - Executive Summary

Property Services covers:
Planning and building new trading space
Refitting, expanding and maintaining existing trading space
Managing store blueprints and design standards
Managing energy consumption and engineering standards
The key benefits are that:
We enable company growth through new trading and mall space
We seek to minimise building capital investment whilst maintaining standards
We minimise the impact of our facilities on the environment through energy efficient design
Our customers have a great place to shop
Our staff have a safe and simple workplace
Our main dependencies are with:
Property Acquisition and Site Research
Range Space and Display
Marketing / Property Insight
Retail Operations
Asset and Mall Management
Key decisions to be made include:
Who owns the Site Acquisition, Site Research and Corporate Purchasing processes within TOM?
What are the best practice Systems for Property Services – International, UK, or both?
How will other best practice Systems impact Property Services (e.g. Space, Range, Display)?

# Tesco Property Services Principles
Customer first – we listen to customer feedback and do everything we can to improve their shopping experience.
We always challenge “Can we design it better, simpler and cheaper?”
Our stores are functional but not extravagant.
Our stores are planned to operate in the simplest way.
We promote corporate responsibility by minimizing our impact on the environment.
We value our consultants, suppliers and contractors, treating them with respect.
We always consider the cost to Tesco in capital investment and life cycle maintenance.
We create once and replicate many times.
Our Property Services Teams are World Class, making people our best asset.
We always act with responsibility and integrity.
We always strive to beat our targets.

# Property Services – Benefits
1) Enable company growth
We maximise return on investment by optimising site usage
We create new trading space by building new stores and extending existing stores
We refit stores which provide like for like growth by ensuring latest initiatives are implemented to support customer plans
We provide opportunity for additional income by creating new mall lease area
2) Minimise building capital investment
We set year on year targets to reduce standard build costs
We manage projects to minimize abnormal building costs
We challenge design specifications to ensure they are fit for purpose and use off-the-shelf versus bespoke equipment
3) Minimise the impact on the environment
We reduce energy costs to invest in customers and staff by investing in energy initiatives
We track energy consumption at store level to target improvements in operations that reduce consumption
We protect the brand and support Corporate Responsibility

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# Property Services – Benefits
4) Customers have a great place to shop
We make shopping easier for customers by planning layouts with good aisles for circulation, the right number of checkouts, and the right ranges
We provide the facility to deliver the “Every Little Helps” shopping trip
We provide mall space and offer to create one-stop-shopping destinations for our customers
5) Staff have a safe and simple workplace
We make workplaces safe and legal by ensuring design standards comply with trading law and health & safety regulations
We provide the right amount of working space to support Retail Operations.
We provide the right equipment to support Commercial and Retail requirements by testing and trialing to ensure fit for purpose
We enable growth in a sustainable way
