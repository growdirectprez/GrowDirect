Secure Store Project Roles

The following company resources are typically required for a secure engagement:

  Customer Project Manager – Provides day to day coordination of time and resources for
internal staff.  Is the primary liaison for the Sysrepublic Project manager and works
closely with the management team through the life of the project.

  Customer IT Management – Provides Project Management for Customer IT resources,

facilitates access to systems and process required, manages Customer timeline.

  Customer IT Development Resource(s) – Responsible for Development of external data
feeds.  Depending on the specific skills and systems knowledge one of more developer
would be required to develop interfaces for external data feeds.

  Customer IT Networking – responsible for working with Sysrepublic networking team to

agree standards and establish connectivity.

  Customer LP Management – Provide Business requirements and signoff key milestones

as delivered.

  Customer LP Analyst(s) – Depending on the number or regions / formats supported by

the implementation project the appropriate number of resources determined by
Customer LP management should be assigned to develop the risk dictionary, and assist
in the configuration of the systems.

  Customer Process / Systems SME(s)-  Subject matter expert(s) from each functional
area including POS, Back Office, HR, Loyalty Programs maybe be required to assist in
the discovery and design phases of the project.

  Customer Technical Infrastructure – For on premise solutions IT resource for

procurement of hardware, sizing, and set up of the server and storage infrastructure is
required.

  Customer Database Administrator – For on premise Solutions a DBA will be required to

manage the SQL server databases and monitor for performance and tuning
opportunities.

The following Sysrepublic resources are required for a Secure engagement:

  Steering Committee – Joint committee of client and Sysrepublic executives responsible

for overall strategic vision, decision making and guidance of the program.

  Sysrepublic Project Manager - As liaison with Customer project manager on the
integration. This project manager will source extra technical resources from the
Sysrepublic side as needed for technical discussions and Testing discussions.

  Sysrepublic Senior Technical Lead – Lead architect of the solution manages the

development plan and is responsible for assignment of development activities required
for design, build and test of the solution.

  Sysrepublic Technical Developer(s) – Responsible for data mapping, interface

development and application installation and configuration of the Exception Based
Reporting application

  Sysrepublic Infrastructure Analyst – Responsible for system sizing, hardware

procurements, and physical server installs operating system and network preparation.

  Sysrepublic Client Services – Responsible for developing the customized risk dictionary,

validating data with business resources, training and on-going functional support.
