# People - Executive Summary

People – what we do:
Deliver our people promises
To be treated with respect – make everyone welcome at Tesco, listen and share information, solving problems at work
A manager who helps me – manage performance and attendance, maintain personal information and manage leavers
An interesting job - design lean, effective structures and jobs
An opportunity to get on – recruit, train and coach, manage our talent and reward our people

The key benefits are that:
Tesco is a great place to work and we deliver against our people promises
Our people are equipped to deliver the Every Little Helps shopping trip
We have the right people information to inform strategic decisions and ensure we are legally compliant
Our main dependencies* are with:
People managers throughout the business as they carry out the majority of our people processes.
Leadership teams as they influence the priority and importance of following the people processes.
People Matters Group to provide governance to the business.
Business Planning which informs our resource and talent planning processes.
Budgeting process for investing and managing people costs.
Payroll processes and systems to pay our people and to administer a number of our processes e.g. performance related pay, bonus and benefit payments.
IT and Support Services to provide our people with the equipment they need to do their job.

* Need clearer definition of the criteria

# Principles
We design interesting jobs that help us deliver the Every Little Helps shopping trip
Everyone is welcome at Tesco and we aim to mirror the diversity of the local population
Everyone has a clear job description, agreed objectives and a personal development plan so they know what’s expected of them
We give great training so people can carry out their job effectively and we develop people to help them grow personally
Everyone has a manager who is supportive and can be trusted
Managers are trained and competent to follow our processes and make fair and consistent decisions
We are flexible, supportive and fair in dealing with any difficulties staff may have attending work and we deal with unacceptable absence
Staff enjoy working for Tesco and look forward to coming to work
Everyone receives regular feedback and coaching so they know how they are doing
Everyone receives an annual performance review and managers have two a year
We pay fairly and reward people based on their on their contribution
We give everyone the opportunity to get on by discussing their career development, agreeing when they are ready to move and providing development opportunities
We select, train, develop and promote people based on capability to do the job and we do not discriminate
We listen to staff ideas and seek feedback to help us make better decisions
We treat people how we like to be treated
We aspire over time to pay amongst the best retailers
