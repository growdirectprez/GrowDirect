1.3 Evaluation Criteria
The process for satisfying Katz’ RSCMS requirements will be conducted via a software-driven approach.  The Katz’ selection team consists of a core team of representatives from each of the major business areas: executive stakeholders, and subject matter experts (SMEs). They will use the following decision criteria in evaluating the vendor’s software solution(s):

Ability to Execute
The primary objective is to implement the most fully integrated suite of software modules that meet Katz’s retail supply chain requirements and the vendor’s ability to demonstrate their ability to execute in the drug chain industry.
Additionally, the evaluation team will focus on the following three aspects during the RSCMS software vendor and product evaluation:

Integrated Functionality The primary objective is to implement the most fully integrated suite of software modules that encompass the entire range of merchandise planning, managing allocations, pricing & promotions, managing vendors, replenishing store inventory, receiving merchandise, managing perpetual inventory, support for POS & dispensary systems, and integration with financial systems.
Ease of Expansion The software must be modular and flexible to accommodate future growth and the handling of increased numbers of transactions, and additional branch offices.  This modularity must support a business release implementation approach.
Flexible inquiry, reporting and data transfer capabilities The system must provide user-defined inquiry/report writing capabilities, with multiple selection, sort, roll up & drill down criteria.  Immediate viewing and/or printing of the information are required.  The ability to down load data to desktop applications is desired in some application areas.
Relational Database  Reliability, accuracy and complete data integrity is a must. An industry standard relational database environment is preferred.
