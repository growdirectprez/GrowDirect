Resources

This project takes advantage of standards initiatives currently underway within the retail industry. In particular, the XML schema definitions published by ARTS ixRetail initiative and the Microsoft Activestore RBI initiative. Through these links you can learn more about these organizations and their work.

You will need to establish an Internet connection to follow these links.

  National Retail Federation - The world’s largest retail trade association, NRF’s mission is to conduct programs and services in research, education, training, information technology, and government affairs to protect and advance the interests of the retail industry. NRF's membership includes the leading department, specialty, independent, discount, and mass merchandise stores in the United States and 50 nations around the world. NRF represents more than 100 state, national and international trade organizations, which have members in most lines of retailing. NRF also includes in its membership key suppliers of goods and services to the retail industry.

  The Association for Retail Technology Standards (ARTS) of the National Retail Federation is a retailer-driven membership organization dedicated to creating an international, barrier-free technology environment for retailers.  ARTS was established in 1993 to ensure that technology enhances a retailer's ability to develop store level business solutions without limiting a retailers' ability to implement change. Effectively ARTS works to define standards along these lines.

Microsoft ActiveStore - A Microsoft-led, worldwide collaborative initiative that began in late 1996, ActiveStore aims to reduce the cost, time, and risk associated with deploying line-of-business (LOB) technology solutions in retail enterprises. Through their Retail Business Interface (RBI) initiative, ActiveStore is publishing XML schemas for common integration scenarios.
