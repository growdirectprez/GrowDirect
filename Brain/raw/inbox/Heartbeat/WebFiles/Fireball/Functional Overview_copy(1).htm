Functional Overview

The Consumer Demand Signal Service detects out-of-stock conditions in a live store environment and notifies store personnel of these issues. The service is driven by near real-time data feeds of the store's transaction data. This movement and pricing data is supplemented by item and promotional data feeds. A subscription service manages delivery of the events and alarms to the users.

There are two functional domains of this offering:  

    Retail Integration & Operations Services (RIO)

RIO integrates the retail enterprise and its data into Consumer Demand Signal Service.  RIO is broken into two subsystems: RIO Inputs and RIO Outputs. RIO Inputs focuses on integrating Point-of-Sale, item, and promotion batched data into the hosted ASPEN service via secured XML data transfers over HTTPS or VPN connections. RIO Outputs focuses on message routing to the endpoint devices and systems supported by Consumer Demand Signal Service.  RIO Outputs currently supports Motorola, Blackberry, and other SMTP pager devices only. Future versions of this offering will support Symbol’s Retail Commercial Infrastructure systems. Both Motorola and Blackberry devices were used in the offering pilots at Meijer and Food Lion.
 

    Application Service Provided Enterprise Notification System (ASPEN)

ASPEN is a hosted application service that is responsible for accepting RIO Inputs, sending them to the Out-of-Stock Notification algorithm for processing, and accepting and routing outbound messages as a result of that processing to RIO Output Devices, Web Services, and Retail Commercial Systems. ASPEN is an application service; it is constructed to support multiple retail enterprises with thousands of retail grocery stores and data.  A web service, ASPEN Slopes, is designed to maintain retail, store, user, device, and subscription data as well as provide the required accounting and operational facilities for managing the business of the Consumer Demand Signal Service.
