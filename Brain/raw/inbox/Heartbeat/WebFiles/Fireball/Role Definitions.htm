RIO Role Definitions

Microsoft Consulting Services

Managing Consultant (Program Manager):

  •  Return On Investment Owner

  • Budget / Timeline Owner

  • Develops Features and High Level Requirements

  • Resource Owner - Decision Maker

  • Communicates Project Vision to the entire team

  • Risk Owner

  • Manages Customer Expectations; Reports status to Customer Director

  • Scope Control

Senior Consultant (Product Manager):

  • Manages Project Specific Requirements

  • Manages Development Consultants

  •  Manages MC expectations and requirements

  • Aids in the Development of Timelines, scope, and budget

  • Develops low level project requirements (Planning, Analysis, Design)

  • Reports Status to customer's Project Manager

  • Owns Project Specific Risks

  • Identifies what customer resources are required to complete tasks 

  • Communicates low level project requirements to Development Consultants

Consultant (Software Development):

  •  Assists Senior Consultant with Design of Software

  • Develops and Constructs software based on Requirements developed by the SC

  •  Works with Infrastructure Consultant in Developing Scalable and reliable systems

  • Works with Customer Resources given internal policies, security, and Infrastructure constraints

  • Documents delivered product and enables Customer Operations for ongoing support

  •  Educates Customer resources

  • Scalability Testing and Load Balance

  • Testing

  •  Reports status up to Senior Consultant

 Consultant (Infrastructure Development):

  • Assists Senior Consultant with Design of Hardware and Platform Requirements

  •  Analysis & Design of Internal network hardware constraints

  •  Designs & Constructs Physical Hardware based on Requirements developed by the SC

  •  Works with Software Consultant in Developing Scalable and reliable systems

  •  Works with Customer Resources given internal policies, security, and Infrastructure constraints

  •  Documents delivered product and enables Customer Operations for ongoing support

  •  Educates Customer resources

  •  Scalability Testing and Load Balance Testing

  •  Reports status up to Senior Consultant

Customer

Director:

  •  Process Ownership

  • Business Decision Maker

  • Return on Investment Owner

  • Internal Human Resources Owner

  • Risk Assessment Development & Owner

  • Work with Program Manager (MCS) in High Level Business Planning and Requirements

  • Communicates Vision and Mission to entire team

 Project Manager:

  •  System Owner

  • Code Owner

  • Operations Owner

  • Hardware and Infrastructure owner

  • Long Term System Support

  • Communicates Requirements to Senior Consultant

  • Communicates Status to Director Customer

  • Contact Point for System Access, Internal Resources, and Internal Third Parties

  • Subject Matter Expert

  • Required person for RIO Outputs Project

 Internal Infrastructure Support:

  • Works with MCS Infrastructure and Development Consultants

  • Communicates Internal Hardware systems and Corporate Policy

  • Aids in the Design of Hardware and software systems

  • Aids in Security and User Policy development

  • Aids in transition of support and maintenance of hardware long term

  • Assists in Training and support strategy

  • Assists in Hardware Scalability Testing

  •  Reports and provides status to Project Manager Customer

Internal Testing/Logistics/User Training:

  •  Aids Customer and MCS in Developing Testing Plans

  •  Executes tests and checks for data integrity and consistency

  •  Reports test results back to MCS and Customer Development Resources

  • Long Term System Support Strategy

  • Trains Operations staff and develops manuals and user documentation

  • Aids in Customer corporate Policy, systems policy, and change control policy

  •  Reports Status to Customer Project Manger

  •  Required person for RIO Outputs Project

Operations Support:

  •  Works with entire Development Team in developing plans to operate and Support the hardware and software of RIO Input system.

  •  Aids MCS Development Team in Scalability Testing, logistics, and Corporate Policy

  •  Provides Network Expertise of Internal Systems to MCS Development team

  •  Provides access to systems, firewalls, Internet Policy, Data Security Policy to Development team

  •  Provides testing services and user education long term

  •  Reports Status to Customer Project Manager
