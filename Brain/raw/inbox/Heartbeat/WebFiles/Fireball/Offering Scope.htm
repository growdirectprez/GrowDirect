Offering Scope

Microsoft’s Supply Chain Orchestration Service Offering delivers next generation efficient replenishment capability to retail organizations by coordinating their supply chain’s reaction to real-time consumer demand signals.

 

This proof of concept offering is targeted at tier 1 grocery and mass retailers who are collecting transaction data centrally and have issues with maintaining a consistent in-stock position with their high turn (>1 turn/day) merchandise and who have a desire to reduce their order cycle times and inventory investment.

 

The Supply Chain Orchestration services are delivered using an ASP model with BizTalk Server 2000 orchestrating and integrating the various internal and external systems. The first installment of these services is the Consumer Demand Signal Service. This service is the catalyst for the supply chain orchestration process. Through this engagement, the customer will gain a thorough understanding of the business impact of deploying the Consumer Demand Signal Service and how to best integrate it into their existing operational framework. This pilot also serves as a proof of concept for the larger offering of orchestrating their supply chain's real-time response to current store conditions.

 

Successful execution of this proof of concept clearly demonstrates Microsoft’s ability to deliver both the technical infrastructure, industry expertise, and the business partner relationships required of an enterprise class, vertical industry application. In addition, the solution architecture and technical infrastructure position Microsoft well for other BI and EAI opportunities.
