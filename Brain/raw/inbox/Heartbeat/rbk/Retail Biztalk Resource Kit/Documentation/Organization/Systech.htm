Systech in Retail:

 As the retail industry's premier independent single source supplier of fully integrated point-of-sale and e-business solutions, Systech helps leading chains leverage technology products and services to operate more efficiently and profitably. Leading retailers choose Systech because they value the flexibility of selecting Systech-developed or other best-in-class solutions that best meet their business needs and the convenience of working with a single vendor that is dedicated to serving the retail industry. Systech offers a wide range fully integrated solutions that address the unique requirements of the supermarket, general retail and hospitality segments.

Systech in the Supermarket:

 In the high volume, low margin food retail industry, non-stop operations efficient customer service and anywhere, anytime access to information for rapid decision-making are keys to success. Systech offers a wide range of best-in-class products and services that provide chains the flexibility to apply technology using the approach that best meets their needs. From implementing ground-up a new IT infrastructure through OPENFIELD SMS, Systech's open architecture POS, Back Office and Enterprise Management solution, to strategic enhancements, services and staged migration for existing IBM 4680/4690 systems, Systech has a proven track record of providing products and services that deliver tangible business benefits to supermarket chains.

Systech IBM 4690 Applications:

Systech's IBM 4690 Operating System applications allow retailers to maximize their investment in the 4690 general retail and supermarket platforms. Through its 1998 acquisition of MGV, Systech now has a team of 100+ individuals with over 470 combined-years of experience with IBM hardware, operating environment and applications. Our professional staff brings a thorough understanding of the retail industry and related technologies to help you achieve your strategic objectives faster, with less disruption.

Systech's support and utility applications allow you to extend the power of your 4690 systems. Systech also offers specialized programming services to retailers and OEMs for all IBM POS applications (SA, GSA, ACE).
