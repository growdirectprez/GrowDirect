National Retail Practice MCS

 Background:

The mission of the National Retail Practice (NRP) is to advance the visibility, acceptance, enhancement, and penetration of the Microsoft dot-Net framework in the retail industry through innovative customer solutions and excellent service.

Utilizing a unique combination of retail industry experience and Microsoft technology expertise, NRP helps customers directly impact and enhance their business by using Microsoft technologies while also enhancing Microsoft’s internal comprehension of the Retail industry. The services provided by NRP are intended to assist and compliment the efforts of field sales and MCS organizations in driving Line of Business (LOB) wins and Microsoft platform adoption in the retail industry

Relevant Experience:

 The National Retail Practice has experience developing similar technologies to the proposed Biztalk SDK for Retail.  NRP played critical roles in the Fireball Project which was implemented 9/15/2001.  NRP managed, architected, and developed the following core solutions for the Fireball Project:

·         4690 POS Adapter. Delivered via NRP partner Systech. The adapter generates XML format data from IBM 4690 panel files. Systech has provided software and support free of charge for the pilot. The adapter is a Java application that was not originally designed to be a BizTalk adapter. As such NRP will consider changes / enhancements to improve the solution.

·         4690 BizTalk Preprocessor. This preprocessor was required to map the 4690 POS Adapter output into the binary input format required by the Data Ventures algorithm. The amount of data and the complexity of the schema necessitated its development due to limitations in the existing BizTalk mapping function.

·         Preprocessor Whitepaper. This white paper will explain the role of the preprocessor and why it was required.

·         Item File Preprocessor. This BizTalk preprocessor is breaking the chain wide item maintenance feed (one large file) into individual files for each store. No data transformation is being performed.

·         Standardize Transaction Interface to Heartbeat.  While not XML, the interface to the Heartbeat Algorithm has been standardized and should not change except to support new functionality. This is a key first step to isolating this business function from change as new retailers are engaged.

The National Retail Practice has an established and cooperative relationship with Systech.  Systech provides the software to convert the IBM 4690 transaction log data from a proprietary and archaic format into XML schemas (Systech Fragmentizer).   NRP also offers an excellent understanding of those schemas and data definitions.  The Systech Fragmentizer will also benefit in future versions of this SDK in providing XML data from other POS operating systems.  NRP also has established an excellent relationship with the Systech human collateral necessary to deliver this solution.
