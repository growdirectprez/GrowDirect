# QuickBase Overview
The US Property Team is evaluating QuickBase to enable project and workflow management for the following Site Acquisition and Construction activities:
Development Programme scheduling and progress tracking
Issue logging and tracking for construction RFI’s
Construction Project communication and reporting
Quick base has been recommend by one of the construction development partners
Web Based application hosted by Intuit; templates, reports and issue logs would be custom developed and maintained by QuickBase
Vendor has provided an initial product demo and high level cost proposal to the Property Team
The Property Team is working with Development Partners and Attorneys to coordinate functional requirements gathering for custom templates and reports
