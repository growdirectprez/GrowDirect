PROJECT SUMMARY
Gemkey wishes to engage BroadVision in a standard five day scoping planning workshop as outlined below to determine the scope and methodology to implement BroadVision into Gemkey’s environment.  This project will involve three workshops to cover Strategy, Content, and Technical areas of the implementation.

The goal of this project will be:

To establish best practices for designing and implementing BroadVision
To map the business strategies of Gemkey into the design of the site
To assess the needs and requirements for managing content through BroadVision
To determine resources required for implementation of BroadVision based upon site requirements.

DELIVERABLES
BroadVision will deliver a Vision Document including ongoing business planning, content management, and application development and systems integration.
Gemkey will engage consultants BroadVision’s WPSO to conduct a 5-day series of workshops in order to define the requirements.  WPSO consultants will lead the Gemkey team through a methodology that enables BroadVision customers to develop a one-to-one application that optimally utilizes BroadVision’s functionality.  The methodology is applied through a series of definition workshop sessions with BroadVision strategy, content and technical consultants.  WPSO will develop and deliver an integrated post-workshop document entitled “Gemkey One-To-One Vision and Requirements”.  The following table briefly describes each of the workshop sessions.  The topics covered in each workshop will form the basis for the post-workshop deliverable.
This is a fixed price engagement.

One-To-One Business Planning and Design:
BroadVision’s Strategic Services Group (SSG) will work with the Gemkey team to essentially “translate” your vision and business requirements into a one-to-one application, which optimally utilizes the features of the BroadVision product and follows best practices. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES
In order for BroadVision to fulfill the commitments of this Statement of work, Gemkey must provide for the consultants the following resources:
Any documentation prior to workshop sessions necessary for BroadVision to fully understand the project.  This includes, but is not limited to:
Strategic plans or other documentation outlining the vision, goals and objectives of the project
Site user or current customer segmentation, including the apparent need each user or customer segment will have when visiting the site
Analyses completed identifying planned or proposed content and/or features that will be available to site visitors
Overview of current or anticipated network architecture and design, including the identification of required systems for integration
Appropriate workshop participants for each WPSO session. Participants should be present for the entire session and must actively contribute ideas and requirements.  The traditional audience for each workshop is noted below.  Please work with your WPSO contacts prior to each workshop to develop an appropriate team for each session
SSG: Executives, business managers, and online service (non-technical) planning team. CCSG: Marketing managers, marketing communications managers, site producers and executive producers.  The ideal group size is two to five people.
ISG: Executives, business managers, online services planning teams, senior software architects.
