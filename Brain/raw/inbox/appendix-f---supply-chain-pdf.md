mySAP RETAIL SUPPLY CHAIN PLANNING: THE

COMPONENTS
mySAP Retail Supply Chain Planning is a fully comprehensive
approach to supply chain issues that deploys a powerful set of
tools and capabilities to address each step in the retail supply
chain in the most efficient and cost-effective way.

STORE REPLENISHMENT ENGINE
The Store Replenishment Engine is a sophisticated optimization
program that helps balance your entire ordering process,
enabling you to cut waste and overstock situations yet avoid
costly out-of-stock situations. Incorporating data on everything
from event handling and promotions to weather and future
sales, the Store Replenishment Engine calculates future
demand, compares that demand against current stock status
and expected issues and receipts, then generates an order for the
appropriate volume of products.

Deployable either in-store or centrally for all stores, the Store
Replenishment Engine is highly adaptable and can easily handle
sudden changes in purchase-order data at any point in the
ordering process. The engine can also generate long-term
ordering forecasts (cid:150) up to 12 weeks or more in advance (cid:150) and
automatically alert all partners in your supply chain. This
means your suppliers, distribution centers, and other supply
chain partners can improve their own planning, improving
your performance, cutting costs, and increasing customer satis-
faction.

DISTRIBUTION CENTER REPLENISHMENT ENGINE
The core of efficient supply chain planning, the Distribution
Center Replenishment Engine aggregates and analyzes all pur-
chase-order data and store planning data to help your distribu-
tion centers forecast and meet future demand. By comparing
demand forecasts with current distribution center stock and
expected inflow and outflow, the engine can calculate precisely
how many new orders are needed, then optimize those orders
depending on your criteria.

Do you need to process large numbers of separate orders in one
distribution center each day? Fast and powerful, the Distribution
Center Replenishment Engine can handle your high-volume,
high-performance supply chain challenges. It can also take data
from your suppliers to create long-range resource plans, help-
ing you avoid imbalances in product stock while keeping costs
down.

PORTALS
mySAP Retail Supply Chain Planning enables retailers to estab-
lish a leading network supply chain with your business partners.
Using the powerful, user-friendly, Web-based interfaces provided
by SAP’s enterprise portal technology, you can share sales data
and provide accurate replenishment services quickly and pre-
cisely, acquiring a competitive advantage in any of your different
sales channels. Portals also give your employees and the part-
ners the tools and access they need to support every step in
your supply chain operation.

Based on a personalized, role-based model, portals give
employees or partners access to the specific programs and data
they require to do their jobs. Suppliers, for example, can gain
access to your planning data early in the ordering process. This
allows suppliers to adjust their own production schedules (cid:150)
even those suppliers that lack the technical capability to auto-
matically transfer your planning data into their systems. Portals
also operate on an exception-based principle, meaning that the
planning process is largely automatic, and users do not inter-
vene unless a problem arises.

The mySAP Retail Supply Chain Planning solution also includes
functions for the following roles: stock planner, store manager,
merchandise administrator, and supply chain manager. And to
help you cope with the retail industry’s high employee
turnover, portals are easy to use and easy to learn, reducing the
time and cost of training new employees.

PROMOTIONS
mySAP Retail Supply Chain Planning uses data from past pro-

MASS HANDLING
A sophisticated mass-data management function lets you swift-

strategies as conditions change, enabling you to easily react to
changes in the daily business. For example, if one store alters its
replenishment orders based on a new forecast, the solution
automatically adjusts all other related plans.
