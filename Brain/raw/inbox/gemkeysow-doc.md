The purpose of this document is to describe the activities to be performed by BroadVision as part of the GemKey's implementation and deployment of BroadVision One-To-One Enterprise and Commerce solution. BroadVision understands that Phase one is to provide the infrastructure for the environment. Subsequent releases will incorporate the addition of Auction, C2C, Inventory management, CRM, and accounting capabilities through additional third party software such as Moai and Onyx, and Epicure. This document describes the current situation, identifies the implementation and integration approach and specific set of deliverables to implement the support for these additional services. In addition, this Statement of Work documents our planning assumptions and describes the proposed cost structure for implementing and deploying GemKey.com Phase 1.


BACKGROUND


GEMKEY currently expects approximately 20,000 daily visitors whose needs initially consist of business to consumer and business to business functionality where the approximate number of concurrent user is anticipated to be 2000. Also the number of products that will initially exist is approximately 200,000 to 300,000. These products will be listed in a product catalog. Given the anticipated growth of its product and content inventory, GemKey is looking to make it easier for its members to know what they want across their full range of products. In addition the need to be able to negotiate better pricing based on delayed delivery is a needed feature. By providing relevant content and user recommendations, GemKey can enhance the buyer's experience and encourage a greater use of the GemKey site. With a current customer base of approximately 20,000 active accounts, GemKey plans to use personalization as a means to grow the active online accounts above and beyond the current levels.

The GemKey initiative represents the company’s expansion of Internet activities into a wider array of e-commerce initiatives and the delivery of One-To-One content and information services.  Using BroadVision® One-To-One Enterprise and BroadVision One-To-One Commerce, the enhanced GemKey Web site will improve the level of service offered to current and potential GemKey customers as detailed below.  The initial focus is to migrate the existing GemKey clientele to the new site, with an immediate follow-on strategy of supporting the users who have never done business with GemKey.  This online initiative will also deliver efficiencies to GemKey by reducing costs associated with customer service and the management of targeted marketing programs.  Revenue is expected to be generated by the growth of product / supply sales to existing customers and by attracting new, customer-specific products to sell into emerging market niches.

Goals and Objectives
The goal of GemKey.com is to provide an open market for Gems and Jewelry that sets a high standard for quality of service and highly personalized presentation of products and services. In addition this site will capable of supporting increased utilization and very high levels o integration to existing systems internal to GemKey and at other organizations as well. These criteria will be met through the execution of GemKey business strategy of centralizing the Gem and jewelry trading market at the GemKey site and the utilization of the BroadVision platform (a time proven, extensible   environment of unsurpassed performance and scalability). By leveraging the One-To-One capabilities of BroadVision, GemKey can deliver its expertise and price advantage to a broader range of customers by being the only Internet provider of Gem trading and inventory management services and products with a truly personalized platform. The phase 1 objectives of GemKey's BroadVision implementation are as follows:

To improve the purchasing process with GemKey
To provide fast and reliable processing of high-volume and secure transactions and privacy protection.
To empower Business Managers to use rule and content wizards to dynamically alter content and target user communities.
To increase customer satisfaction by presenting a personalized interface for all site transactions.

The rollout of GemKey.com will be accomplished in multiple phases, accomplished in an initial 60 day infrastructure release to be followed by subsequent feature rich release. The first phase is currently targeted for a Go-Live date of early June 2000. The timing is being driven by an urgency to provide e-commerce abilities, timelier fulfillment, demonstrate personalization and integration with existing systems, and to promote the system as the go-to place for all Gem related transactions.

GemKey has requested that BroadVision provide experienced technical resource(s) that are proficient in the areas of  C++ component development, JavaScript development, BroadVision architectural, systems integration, installation configurations and performance tuning. In addition, BroadVision resource(s) will provide knowledge transfer to GemKey staff members on the BroadVision environment so that they can become self-sufficient over time. A BroadVision project manager will manage the BroadVision resource(s) with assistance from the GemKey project manager. The BroadVision project manager will have a hands-on role in the project and will lead the team. All BroadVision consultants will have prior BroadVision training and implementation experience.  A BroadVision program director will oversee the work effort. The BroadVision project manager is responsible for scheduling the resources and ensuring the project remains on schedule. The BroadVision consultant(s) will report weekly progress to the project managers, keeping them informed of task completion.


4.	 IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH AND DELIVERABLES

BroadVision uses its own project approach and project management methodology – One-To-One: LiveCycle – to deliver BroadVision Enterprise solutions to its clients.  The LiveCycle methodology has been tailored to accommodate implementation and deployment of web-based software solutions and, particularly, BroadVision Enterprise software.

LiveCycle is a repeatable and scaleable project approach and management discipline that BroadVision has tailored for BroadVision Enterprise deployments and has been utilized on all knowledge, commerce and financial service engagements to date.  It is structured in phases to ensure the necessary information is gathered and well understood by all parties, the required deliverables are completed, and progress is measured throughout the project lifecycle.  It utilizes rapid development techniques, storyboards, prototypes, usability labs and effective training techniques.  LiveCycle also includes templates for all deliverables, a detailed work break down structure and detailed project management processes.

To facilitate the implementation of GemKey's site, BroadVision recommends an accelerated design and development approach, which leverages GemKey’s existing business requirements and functional specifications and incorporates that knowledge into a comprehensive BroadVision configuration design. This approach takes advantage of the momentum gained from prior analysis efforts, leverages the experiences from the existing commerce web implementations and will incorporate the findings from joint GemKey/BroadVision team meetings. BroadVision envisions this project to be a joint development effort that blends the business and technical skill sets from both organizations. Using this strategy, the project team can make significant progress in achieving a successful implementation by the June 2000 timeframe.

In order to meet their aggressive time constraints, BroadVision suggests parallel execution of the definition, design, and development phase with lagged start dates.  The project team will install the BroadVision software; validate and update the requirements; create the proof of concept JavaScript templates.  After the initial foundation is established the team will build out additional GemKey Phase 1 functionality in preparation for the needs of the forthcoming backend integration components, which are expected to be part of phase 2
