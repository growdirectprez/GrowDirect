MISSION

The Special Interest Groups will provide a forum for product users, designers and
developers to share information and ideas on the product direction and enhancements.

The purpose of JDA User Group Special Interest Groups (SIG) is to give unified cross-
company input to JDA on product enhancements for their Products.

Special Interest Groups are typically organized following JDA’s product/tool set and will
be revised as required.

OFFICIAL TITLE

The official title of SIG Chairperson (SIG Chair) will be assigned to those individuals
appointed to oversee and organize the specific SIG areas.

ORGANIZATION

The SIG Chairs will report to the JDA User Group Vice President.

The SIG will be facilitated by a Global SIG Chairperson nominated by the SIG
membership or appointed by the Executive Committee of the JDA Users Group from
JDA’s maintenance paying customers.  The SIG Chairperson can appoint a person(s) to
assist in the facilitation of any meeting or communications upon notification to the
Executive Committee.

Each SIG Chair will have a JDA counterpart who will be the JDA Product Manager and
thus represent that product area on the JDA Design Council.

Regional SIG groups may be formed and must be self-organized (only by JDA clients on
support) with regional JDA assistance and coordination.  Any regional activities must be
communicated and approved by the global SIG chairperson and all voting on
enhancements must be done on a global level.

RESPONSIBILITIES

The SIG Chairperson will:

1.   Maintain a SIG member list and profiles (optional) for the members. Profiles should
be kept confidential and shared only with JDA or designated JDA representatives
unless specifically released by the member.  Profiles should only be used for
purposes consistent with User Group activities and not be used for individual or
company gain.

2.  Conduct two SIG conference calls a year with SIG members and report the minutes

of these meetings back to the JDA Product Managers and the Executive Committee.

3.  Ensure that the members submit the SIG enhancement requests following the JDA

Design Enhancement Process to JDA Client Support Services.

SIG  GUIDELINES For Meetings and Conference Calls

1.  An agenda prepared by the SIG Chair will be provided to the members, the JDA

Product Manager, and the Executive Committee in advance of each
meeting/conference call.  Revised agendas will be provided at the beginning of the
meeting/call.

2.  The meeting/call will be facilitated by the SIG Chairperson.

3.  A SIG Chairperson’s representative will take minutes.

4.  SIG Chairperson will consolidate and send the minutes of the meeting/call to the

JDA Product Manager and the Executive Committee for posting to the SIG’s Web
Page.

5.  A list of prioritized enhancements will provided to JDA’s Product Manager and to the

membership via the Web Site.

6.  The JDA Product Manager  will provide an update of the current and future

enhancements/issues being addressed by JDA.

7.  Meetings/calls will normally consist of two parts:

•  The first part will be a presentation by the JDA Product Manager to provide an
update on the status of new issues affecting the development of the product.
This will also entail an opportunity for questions from the membership.

•  The second part will consist of a presentation by the SIG Chair of the current and
new enhancement list followed by an open discussion that will lead to a new
prioritized enhancement list.

8.  Meetings and conference calls must remain focused on product enhancements and
issues that will affect the next release planning cycle.  Long term product direction
issues and questions as well as any industry specific issues must be deferred to the
end of the meeting or taken off-line.  Any industry specific issues should be
communicated to the JDA Product Manager. Cooperation and Communication are
the key to a successful meeting.

9.  Each new product enhancement request discussed during the meeting/call will be

assigned a volunteer sponsor from the membership at the meeting.  The sponsor will
provide the business rational for the request and will submit the appropriate design
enhancement request form to JDA.

10. SIG Chairpersons are responsible for SIG succession planning to ensure the on-

going success of the SIG.

11. The SIG Chair will oversee bi-annual the election process at the annual JDA Users

Conference.  Each nominee must agree to run for the position before being
proposed.  A SIG Chair may be nominated or volunteer for re-election for an
indefinite number of terms.

Pre-conference Planning Meeting

The objective of the Pre-conference Planning Meeting is to finalize plans for the Users
Conference SIG meeting.  This meeting, typically, consists of reviewing the overall
plans for conducting the SIG meetings, and finalizing the agenda for the SIG meeting at
the Users Conference in collaboration with the JDA Product Manager, and succession
planning in an election year.

Annual User Conference SIG Meeting

The objective of the Annual User Conference SIG Meeting is to:

•  welcome new members to the SIG

•  meet and network with the SIG members

•

•

review the latest enhancements being added to the product

review the status of the outstanding Enhancement Requests

•  determine if new requests need to be added

•

re-examine the priorities of the outstanding Enhancements

•  hold elections for new SIG chairpersons during the odd years

Agenda Development

The Users Conference SIG Meeting is the main SIG event each year.  The success of
this meeting is based on the Chairperson’s preparation for an organized meeting and
the participation of the attendees.  The Chairperson provides a formalized agenda
before the conference with a finalized agenda at the SIG Meeting.  The next two
sections provide some guidelines for preparing the agenda and facilitating the meeting
to maximize participation and success.

The SIG Chairperson’s key responsibility at the Users Conference is to ensure that the
SIG meeting achieves its objective.  Meetings are usually successful if:

•  attendees stay focused on the objective of the meeting and stick to the agenda; and

•  conversation and ideas keep moving forward.

Below are some techniques successful facilitators use to maintain the focus and
momentum of meetings.

•  You will need some basic facilitator tools in the room:

•

flip chart on a stand (ability to tape completed pages on the walls)

•  markers in various colors

•  At the start of the meeting, after the introductions are made, review the purpose of

the meeting and check if other topics need to be covered.

•  At the end of each section of the discussion repeat back the results and ensure

consensus.

•  Use basic facilitation techniques; ask questions of the group, make clarifying
statements and ask for agreement.  Leading the discussion ensures affective
decision-making.

•  For discussion purposes you may want to break down into smaller groups to

brainstorm on specific issues.

•

If one or two people are dominating the conversation, try to bring others in by directly
asking them to validate what was being said.

•  Have someone other than the Chairperson record the discussion

topics/ideas/questions on a flip chart.  Someone must take minutes and pass the
attendance sheet and surveys around.  Not only does this document the
proceedings of the meeting, but also it provides additional focus for the group and
can be used to clarify statements and positions.

•

If the meeting is wandering off track, regain focus by restating the purpose of the
meeting/agenda items or the time frame.  In most cases, the group will return to the
topic.  In those few instances where a new direction is needed, ask the group to
quickly summarize what they want to achieve in the time remaining.

•  Meetings occasionally stagnate (discussion goes in circles or no one is contributing).

Should this happen, try the same technique for groups that wander off track

•  When you encounter seemingly endless discussions or issues that do not pertain to
the agenda/mission of the SIG, legitimize the issue by writing it down on a separate
flip chart page.  This page is often called the Parking Lot or Group Memory.  The
issue/idea can be legitimized without agreeing or disagreeing.  Return to this page at
the conclusion of the meeting to see if the issue has been addressed.  If not, ask
someone in the group (perhaps the person who raised the issue?) to do some
further research and report back to the group.  You may also ask to take the
discussion “off-line” after the meeting or at another convenient time.  If appropriate,
the Chairperson should report the results of the off-line meeting back to the group.

Communication Guidelines:

Since the membership is spread across the world, communicating to, from, and among
the membership is challenging.  In addition, this is further complicated with issues
concerning multiple products, members participating in multiple SIGs, and geographic
issues.  However, the SIG Chair needs to ensure communication occurs between SIG
members, JDA User Group, JDA Product Managers and the Executive Committee.
