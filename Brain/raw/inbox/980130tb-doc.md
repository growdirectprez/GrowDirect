Objective and Definition
Minimize cost on freight and cost of goods while controlling weeks of supply.

TruckBuilding: process by which suggested orders from any of the order generation processes are adjusted for minimums, pack size, unit loads, discount or allowance levels and transportation limits (e.g. truck load rounding) before being approved as purchase orders and released to vendors.
Assumptions
Traffic is charged with optimizing the delivery method and any routing decisions.
Truck Building does not perform Traffic functions within SAP or otherwise.
If an order meets certain minimums which are designated in the master file, Truckbuilding will occur.
Minimum quantities for articles are managed within Replenishment or Purchase Order Management.
Minimum order size for a store is the minimum amount for a Purchase Order.
Replenishment of DC to DC transfers will not run through Truck Building.
Once the initial order quantities have been truckbuilt, Traffic must accept the quantity provided and ship the merchandise.
Only exceptions which are based on set tolerance levels are reviewed within the “Review Truck Load Results” process.
Requirements*
Ability to review (accept/decline authority assumed) any quantity that the system automatically determines within the process “Approve quantity(SOQ)”
MTB020
Systematically consolidate and combine multiple orders from one vendor or a number of vendors and send to multiple sites based on specific delivery parameters set at the vendor/article level.  System must be able to designate whether a vendor's order is limited to consolidation and delivery for a specific site or sites only or available for consolidation and delivery to any site.  Delivery combinations include the following: {20}
Single or multiple vendors to multiple distribution centers
Single or multiple vendors to multiple stores
Single or multiple vendors to multiple mixing centers
Any combination of the above
Example:
SKUs (Vendor A) ---> associated with delivery to sites 111, 112 113 and 114 only
SKUs (Vendor B) ---> associated with delivery to sites 115, 116 and 117 only
SKUs (Vendor C) ---> associated with delivery to any sites
Systematically combine Vendor B's SKUs with Vendor C's SKUs and send to the appropriate sites.
Article/Vendor combinations that are pre-determined as truckbuild eligible must be triggered as "available for truckbuilding."  In order to systematically group and build Article/Vendor orders, a flag is necessary which designates that an item is appropriate for truckbuilding.  This trigger must be maintained dynamically.  After a specified number of weeks, new article should be up for truckbuild review.  If the article meets certain criteria, including inventory turn and profit measures, it should automatically become eligible for truckbuilding.{40}

MTB090
Provide the capability to manage Truckbuilding through forecasted or historical weeks of supply.  Systematically maintain Truckbuilding profiles of all available Truckbuilding SKUs by plant, vendor, and proximity (i.e., pool SKU).  Combine multiple vendors/SKUs from plant, vendor, or proximity until the truck is built or the pre-assigned weeks of supply limit (max) is met.  Systematically add or cut as follows: {90}
Add:  based on weeks of supply, provide the ability to first add days of supply  to “current order” SKUs until max weeks of supply  is met or until weeks of supply equals the weeks of supply for the lowest “pool” SKU.  Then, utilize lowest “pool” SKU as an order SKU and continue the process until the truck is full.
Cut:  based on a set velocity code and/or characteristic attribute (i.e., event) and associated service level (i.e., 90%), provide logic that will begin cutting SKUs with low service levels.  Continue deleting SKUs from truck until either truck parameters are met or only “no cut” SKUs remain.  “No cut” SKUs can include event SKUs, SKUs which have a zero on-hand at site, or SKUs which are currently below presentation quantity at site.

Support the capability to systematically perform "Truck-splitting."  Truck-splitting occurs when a purchase order is broken down into more than one truck.  This situation exists when the purchase order created is larger than one full truckload.  The system must be able to truck-split purchase orders as outlined below:{80}
Utilizing truckbuilding parameters (weeks of supply), attempt to manage this situation by rounding the purchase order to one truck.
When purchase order contains more than one SKU, group like SKUs by truck

Support the capability to combine a manually created purchase order with replenishment orders that have been previously truckbuilt.  Last minute situations exist which facilitate the need to combine manual orders with replenishment orders.  In these situations the system must be capable of truckbuilding the manual purchase order with the original truckbuilt replenishment orders.{110}
MTB100
Ability to identify Truckbuilt orders by "submission number."  The submission number will identify the original multiple item/vendor/site combinations before and after they were Truckbuilt.  The submission number can then be used for linking orders when transmitting to vendor and for internal tracking purposes.  System must be capable of recognizing that orders have been combined by "submission number," while maintaining the ability to recognize the combined orders as one truck.{100}

Better Practices Recommendations
Suggested orders are adjusted for:
Pack size
Full case
Inner pack
Unit loads
Pallet layer quantity
Tier rounding
Full pallet rounding
Weight
Cube
Transportation rounding with tolerance includes:
Minimum order size (for prepaid freight)
Minimum to obtain a concession level (for purchase volume rebates)
Full truckload
Full truck loads are built with multiple destinations as an option.
Transportation rounding requires the use of reaching for a minimum tolerance.  If time supply exceeds the allowed limit, no order is produced and must be generated manually if required.
Final SOQ is subject to exception rules to determine if the order needs manual review.  Rules include:
Time supply (min. and max.)
Minimum quantities ($s and units)
Maximum quantities ($s and units)
Orders that fail edits are cycled for manual review.
