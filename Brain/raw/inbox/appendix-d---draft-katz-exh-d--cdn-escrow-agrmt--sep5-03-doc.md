BASIS SYSTEM SOURCE CODE ESCROW AGREEMENT


THIS AGREEMENT made as of the 13th of September, 1994;

AMONG:

		SAP AG, a corporation incorporated under the
		laws of Germany (hereinafter referred to as
		“SAP AG”)

				- and -

		SAP CANADA SYSTEMS, APPLICATIONS
		AND PRODUCTS IN DATA PROCESSING INC.
		a corporation incorporated under the laws of the
		Province of Ontario (hereinafter referred to as the
		“Supplier”)

				- and -

		NORTH AMERICAN TRUST COMPANY, A
		company incorporated under the laws of the
		Province of Alberta (hereinafter referred to as
		“Agent”)

				- and -

		Each corporation, partnership or other entity
		who becomes a party to this Escrow Agreement
		by executing a copy of the form of Participating
		Customer Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit
		1 (hereinafter referred to as the “Customer”,
		individually, and “Customers”, collectively)



WHEREAS:

	(a)	Supplier has entered into prior to the date of this Agreement, and will from time to time to time after the date hereof enter into, agreements with customers which provide for Supplier licensing to a customer the use of, and Supplier agreeing to provide maintenance service for, certain R/3 computer software products owned by SAP AG and distributed by Supplier (hereinafter referred to as “R/3 Software”).  For the purpose of this Agreement, “Programs” shall mean, in the case of each Customer, the R/3 Software that has been licensed by Supplier to such Customer, under any agreement(s) between Supplier and such Customer.

	(b)	To assure the continued maintenance of the Programs for customers of Supplier in accordance with the aforementioned agreements, Supplier and SAP AG desire to deposit and maintain in escrow for Customers one copy of the Basis System Source Code  and

	(c)	Agent, as escrow agent, is agreeable to maintain in escrow for each Customer such source code and to make it available to the Customer under the terms and conditions of this Agreement.


1.	Definitions

	In this Agreement the following terms shall have the meanings indicated below:

Customer Agreement:	For each Customer, the Participating Customer Agreement executed by the respective Customer in substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

Effective Date:	For each Customer, the date set out in the respective Customer Agreement comes into force in respect of each such Customer.

License Agreement:	For each Customer, the agreement between such Customer and Supplier referred to in the recital of the respective Customer Agreement.

Programs:	The R/3 Basis System and any other R/3 application system which currently is, or from time to time will be, licensed by Supplier to a customer of Supplier pursuant to an agreement that requires Supplier to provide maintenance services for such application systems.

Basis System Source Code:	The source code for the Basis System which will be comprised of one machine-readable copy in the appropriate computer language on storage media which shall be capable of being transformed into a hard copy source listing.


2.	Deposit and Updating of Basis System Source Code

	Forthwith after the execution of this Agreement by Supplier and Agent, Supplier shall deposit with Agent one copy of the Basis System Source Code.  Supplier shall provide to Agent from time to time one copy of any updates to the Basis System Source Code in order that Agent shall have in its possession the most current version of the Basis System Source Code within 120 days of the date that Supplier releases such update.  Agent shall keep the Basis System Source Code in a safe place in Toronto and shall deal with the Basis System Source Code only in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.


3.	Verification

	The Customer may, at any time on or after the Effective Date but no more than once every six months, request to verify that the Basis System Source Code in the possession of Agent is executable on the appropriate computer and is capable of compiling the object code of the R/3 Basis System.  Should the Customer wish such verification, it shall send written notice of such request to both Supplier and Agent specifying the date and time (on normal business days and during normal business hours) when it requires the verification to be conducted.  The location at which the verification is to take place shall be the premises of Supplier in the Toronto area.  At the date and time specified in the notice, or, if Supplier or Agent is unable to attend at such date and time, at such other time as the parties may agree (which date shall not be more than 30 days after the date initially requested by the Customer), Agent shall deliver the Basis System Source Code in its possession to the possession of Supplier in the presence of the Customer at Supplier’s premises.  In the presence of Agent and the Customer, Supplier shall perform such series of tasks and verification procedures as the Customer may reasonably require to enable the Customer to determine that the Basis System Source Code is executable on the appropriate computer and is capable of compiling the object code of the R/3 Basis System.  Upon completion of the verification, Supplier shall return the Basis System Source Code to Agent and Agent shall return the Basis System Source Code to its secured premises.


4.	Terms of Agreement.

	This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect for so long as any customer of Supplier continues to use any Program pursuant to an agreement that requires Supplier to provide maintenance services for such system; provided that either Agent or Supplier, on 90 days notice to all other parties hereto (including all the Customers), may terminate this Agreement, in which case Supplier shall appoint a new escrow agent in Toronto, Ontario on substantially the same terms as this Agreement prior to the date that this Agreement is terminated.  With respect to each Customer, the obligation of Supplier and Agent hereunder shall be terminated upon delivery to the respective Customer of the Basis System Source Code in accordance with the provisions of Section 7 of this Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.


5.	Charges

	(a)	Supplier, as agent for the Customers, shall pay Agent for services that are set out in Schedule A attached hereto including applicable taxes.  The fees set forth in Schedule A are for Agent’s ordinary services as escrow agent.  In the event Agent is required by a single Customer to perform additional or extraordinary services as a result of being an escrow agent, including intervention in any litigation or proceeding, Agent shall receive reasonable compensation for such services and be reimbursed for such cost incurred, including reasonable legal fees from such Customer which required Agent to perform such services.  All such fees may be increased by Agent upon 90 days written notice, provided that the fees charged by Agent to Supplier hereunder from time to time shall be no greater than the then published standard list prices for such services generally charged by Agent.  Supplier shall pay all such fees within 30 days of receipt of an invoice from Agent.

	(b)	All the Customers collectively shall reimburse  Supplier for all fees paid by Supplier to Agent under paragraph 5(a) or otherwise in respect of this Agreement, and for the related services performed by Supplier such as deposit and updating of the Basis System Source Code, and for any taxes, levies or similar amounts howsoever designated, paid or payable by Supplier or Agent based on such charges or on this Agreement or the services rendered hereunder, exclusive of taxes based on Supplier’s or Agent’s net income (which fees of Agent and Supplier and such taxes are hereinafter referred to as the “Collective Fees”).  Supplier shall take all reasonable measures to apportion equitably the Collective Fees among all the Customers on the basis of the number of Programs licensed by each Customer and any other relevant criteria.  Each Customer agrees that Supplier’s decision as to the allocation of the Collective Fees among the Customers shall be final and binding on each Customer and each Customer agrees to pay its respective portion of the Collective Fees so determined by Supplier.  The initial amount to be paid by each Customer in respect of the Customer’s portion of the Collective Fees is set out in the Customer Agreement.

	(c)	If the Customer requests a verification under Section 3, all of the fees of the Supplier reasonably incurred by Supplier and Agent related to performing, or assisting in, such verification shall be borne by the Customer unless the verification was not successful in which event the Supplier shall be responsible for all such fees.  The Customer shall also pay all reasonable third party fees incurred by Agent in respect of any request by the Customer under subsection 7(b) to obtain the Basis System Source Code.



	(d)	The Customer shall pay all required fees under clauses (b) and (c) hereof within 30 days of receipt of an invoice from Supplier.


6.	Default of Supplier.

	Provided the Customer is not in default under its License Agreement and that Supplier’s maintenance obligations have not expired or terminated under the License Agreement, an event of default under this Agreement shall be deemed to have occurred upon the occurrence of any one or more of the following:

(a)	If  Supplier makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors or if a petition in bankruptcy is filed against Supplier and said petition is not dismissed within 30 days, or if a receiver or trustee in bankruptcy is appointed for Supplier whose appointment is not vacated within 30 days, and as a result of any of the above, the personnel of Supplier are unable to execute their normal business routines and Supplier is unable to fulfill, in respect of any Customer, its support and maintenance obligations under the Customer License Agreement;

(b)	Supplier ceases generally to carry on business or Supplier ceases to carry on the business of licensing and maintaining the Programs; or

(c)	Failure of Supplier to provide any material maintenance services under the respective License Agreement and such failure continues for a period of 30 days after the Customer gives Supplier written notice of such failure,

provided that no event of default under this Agreement shall be deemed to have occurred if, within 30 days after the occurrence of any of the events noted in clauses (a), (b) or (c) of this Section 6 which is exclusive of and not concurrent to the 30 day periods described in clauses (a) and (c) of this Section 6, or if within 30 days after receipt by Supplier of the Customer’s notice under Section 7(a), SAP AG agrees in writing to assume Supplier’s maintenance obligations under the License Agreement, and Supplier’s obligations under this Agreement, and in particular, under Section 2 of this Agreement, and immediately cures any default in the performance thereof  and otherwise commences to perform such maintenance obligations and escrow obligations provided that the above provisions shall thereafter apply in respect of SAP AG.


7.	Obligations of Agent Upon Supplier’s Default.

	(a)	A Customer shall give written notice to Agent of the occurrence of a default by Supplier as set forth in Section 6, which notice shall specify the nature of such default and shall include a request to have released to the Customer the Basis System Source Code (such notice shall include a list of all the Programs then used by the Customer and for which the Customer desires to have a copy of the Basis System Source Code).  Within five business days of receipt of such notice, Agent shall send a copy of such notice to Supplier and SAP AG.


	(b)	If  Supplier gives Agent written notice of its concurrence in the Customer’s notice of default, and SAP AG gives Agent written notice of its intention not to assume Supplier’s maintenance obligations under the License Agreement, Agent shall promptly deliver to the Customer the Basis System Source Code in accordance with the Customer’s instructions (and for this purpose Agent may retain a third party to make a copy of the Basis System Source Code from the master copy of the Basis System Source Code).  If Supplier wishes to dispute the Customer’s notice of default, then within 30 days subsequent to receipt of the copy of the Customer’s notice, Supplier shall deliver to Agent an affidavit executed by a representative of Supplier stating that no such default has occurred, or that the default has been cured and the manner in which it has been cured.  Regardless of whether Supplier files such an affidavit, within 30 days subsequent to receipt of the copy of the Customer’s notice, SAP AG shall be entitled to file a notice with Agent indicating that if a default by Supplier is found to have occurred, SAP AG shall assume Supplier’s  maintenance obligations.  If Supplier fails to file such an affidavit, and SAP AG fails to file such notice of assumption, then Agent, in accordance with the instructions of the Customer, on the 31st day subsequent to receipt by Supplier of the copy of the Customer’s notice of default, shall deliver to the Customer the Basis System Source Code (and for this purpose Agent may retain a third party to make a copy of the  Basis System Source Code from the master copy of the Basis System Source Code.)  In the event that Supplier files an affidavit disputing the Customer’s claim that a default has occurred, Agent shall not deliver the Basis System Source Code to the Customer until directed to do so by Supplier and the Customer jointly or until Agent is ordered to do so by an arbitrator in accordance with Section 11 hereof .  As well, Agent shall not deliver the Basis System Source Code if SAP AG gives written notice that SAP AG is assuming all maintenance obligations under the License Agreement.


8.	Obligations of Agent Upon Termination of Maintenance.

	Upon the expiry or termination of the maintenance obligations under a License Agreement, Supplier shall give written notice of such expiry or termination to Agent.  Within five business days of receipt of such notice, Agent shall send a copy of such written notice to the Customer.  Following the expiration of 30 days from the receipt of such notice by the Customer, Agent shall cease to have an obligation to the Customer with regard to the Basis System Source Code unless, if the Customer wishes to dispute Supplier’s notice, within 30 days of receipt by the Customer of the copy of Supplier’s notice, the Customer files with Agent an affidavit executed by a representative of the Customer stating that the maintenance obligation under the License Agreement has not terminated or expired.  In the event that Agent receives such an affidavit from the Customer, Agent shall not terminate its obligations to the Customer until directed to do so by Supplier and the Customer jointly or until Agent is ordered to do so by an arbitrator in accordance with Section 11 hereof.



9.	Concerning Agent.

	Agent shall not by reason of its signing this Agreement, assume any responsibility for any transaction or liability for any transaction between Supplier and the Customer other than the performance of its obligations in accordance with this Agreement.  Agent makes no representation or warranty regarding the quality of the Basis System Source Code.  Except for a breach of the obligations set out in clause (b) hereof, in no event shall Agent be liable to Supplier, SAP AG, the Customer or to any other party for consequential, special or incidental damages.  If for any reason (except for a breach of the obligations set out in clause (b) hereof), Agent shall be found liable to Supplier, SAP AG, the Customer or to any other party, then in such event the parties agree that such liability shall not exceed the amounts paid to Agent hereunder during the year immediately preceding the event giving rise to such liability.  The party on whose behalf, or pursuant to whose direction Agent acts, shall indemnify and hold harmless Agent from any and all liability, damages, costs or expenses, including reasonable fees that shall be sustained or incurred by Agent as a result of third party claiming that Agent is in wrongful possession of the Basis System Source Code.

(b)	Agent recognizes and agrees that the Basis System Source Code is the valuable proprietary and confidential information of Supplier and SAP AG  and agrees to hold same in strict confidence and to take all appropriate actions to preserve its confidentiality and to prevent loss or damage to it.  Except as expressly provided for in this Agreement, Agent agrees that it will not use or disclose, divulge or make available the Basis System Source Code or any copy thereof to any person, association, firm, partnership or corporation either directly or indirectly in any manner whatsoever without the prior written consent of Supplier.

(c)	Agent may retain such independent counsel or other advisor as it may reasonably require for the purpose of discharging its duties hereunder, and may act on the advice or opinions so obtained.  The reasonable fees, expenses or disbursements for any such counsel or other advisors so retained shall be paid in accordance with Section 5(a).

(d)	In the exercise of its rights, duties and obligations hereunder, Agent may rely as to the truth of the statements and the accuracy of the opinions expressed therein and shall be protected in acting upon any resolution, direction, statutory declaration, opinion, report, notice, certificate or other paper or document reasonably believed by it to have been signed, sent or presented by or on behalf of the proper parties.  However, Agent may in its discretion require reasonable evidence of the due execution thereof before acting or relying thereon.




10.	Basis System Source Code License; Nondisclosure by the Customer.

	Customer recognizes and agrees that the Basis System Source Code, and all parts thereof, is the valuable proprietary and confidential information of Supplier and SAP AG and shall remain so, even after a release, if any, of the Basis System Source Code to the Customer pursuant to Section 7.  The Customer agrees to hold same in strict confidence and to take all appropriate action to preserve its confidentiality during any verification procedures under Section 3 or in the event that it obtains the Basis System Source Code from Agent pursuant to this Agreement.  If the Customer obtains the Basis System Source Code under Section 7, the Customer shall have a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use and modify the Basis System Source Code solely for its own use and the use of its affiliates in order to maintain the Programs licensed by the Customer and for no other purpose whatsoever, and the Customer shall use the Basis System Source Code only at its own premises or that of its affiliates on its own data processing equipment.

The Customer further agrees not to copy the Basis System Source Code, nor to disclose the Basis System Source Code to any third party, except to agents retained by Customer to assist in maintaining the Programs, provided that no such agent is in the business of marketing or developing software competitive with the Programs and the Customer shall secure from any party to whom such disclosure may be made a written covenant that it will not disclose the Basis System Source Code or make use thereof for any purposes except for the maintenance of the copies of the Programs licensed by the Customer.


11.	Arbitration.

	In the event that Supplier and Customer are unable to resolve a dispute arising under Section 7 or 8 within 30 days of either party’s written notice of a default as described in Section 7 or 8, such dispute shall be resolved by arbitration as follows:

i)	Either party may, after such 30 days, serve a notice on the other party of its intention to arbitrate.  Within 10 days of service of such notice, the parties shall meet and attempt to appoint a single arbitrator.  Should the parties be unable to agree on a single arbitrator, then either party may apply to a judge of the Ontario Court, General Division (the 	“Court”) under the Arbitration Act, 1991, S.O. 1991, Chapter 17 (the “Arbitration Act”) for the appointment of a single arbitrator.

ii)	The arbitrator selected to act hereunder shall be qualified by education and training to pass upon the particular question in dispute.



iii)	The single arbitrator so chosen shall proceed immediately to hear and determine the question in dispute.  The decision of the single arbitrator shall be communicated to the parties not later than 30 days after the close of argument in the arbitration, subject to any reasonable delay due to unforeseen circumstances.  Should the single arbitrator fail to communicate his decision to the parties within 60 days after the close of argument then either party may elect to have a new single arbitrator chosen in like manner as if none had previously been selected.  Such new single arbitrator shall proceed immediately to hear and determine the question or difference in accordance with this Agreement.

iv)	Every submission to arbitration pursuant to this Section 11 shall contain a provision requiring the arbitrator, if so requested by either party, to state in the form of a special case for the opinion of the Court any question of law arising in the course of the reference.

v)	The decision of the single arbitrator shall be drawn up in writing and signed and shall, subject to the specific provisions of and limitations in this Agreement, be final and binding upon each of the parties hereto as to any question or questions so submitted to arbitration, and the parties shall 	perform the terms and conditions thereof.

vi)	All costs of the arbitration shall be borne by the losing party on a solicitor and its own client basis.

vii)	In all respects not provided for elsewhere in this Article, the provisions of The Arbitration Act shall apply to any arbitration undertaken hereunder.
