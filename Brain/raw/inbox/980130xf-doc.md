Manual Transfers - SSG
Objective and Definition
To improve profit and turnover, fulfill customer needs, support advertising, and refine merchandise presentation through manual transfers.

Manual Transfers:  the process in which transfers between sites including store-to-store, DC-to-DC, store-DC, and DC-to-store are manually initiated and tracked.
Critical Success Factors
Ability to fulfill customer needs
Ability to effectively and efficiently process transfers between sites
Ability to provide information to decision makers who evaluate the utility of the transfer
Ability to manage and track transfer documentation
Ability to fully integrate with Logistics
Assumptions
Procedural rules are established to govern who initiates transfers and when transfers are allowable
SAP will provide a terminal in every site
Target replenishment requirements will be such that manual transfers will occur on an exception basis only
Transfers between sites in different companies will be handled in the process Inter-company Transfers, but will ideally be similar to the Manual Transfer process
Style to style transfers are not addressed by the Manual Transfer process
DC to DC transfers are used on an exception basis only specifically for  import merchandise or when vendor proximity to a DC creates a transfer situation (reasoning: about 50% of these take more than one day and one or more trucks to complete)
Orders cannot be deleted by the DCs
Cross-border, store-to-store transfers are not permitted
Transfers are non-maintainable once status is flagged “sent”
Transfer order must be received at the assigned receiving location
PETsMART transfer procedures and policies include the following:
One purchase order per truck
Mandatory daily store review of transfer orders
Mandatory DC/Traffic notification of large shipments
Standards regarding weight, cube, pallet and TUM (transfer unit of measure)
Short ship policy where store receives against quantity shipped.  Store must enter amount received only.  Contact must then be made to sending site regarding discrepancy.  Short ship will automatically trigger a cycle count for that item in both the sending and receiving site.
DCs will initiate transfers (DC to DC and DC to store) on an exception basis only
Stores will request DC transfers on an exception basis only
Requirements*
Ability to review transfer documents at both the receiving and sending site.
Support store to store, store to DC, DC to DC, and DC to store transfers.
Automatically generate order documents for the receiving site
MTR040
Support the creation, tracking and reporting of orders based on the following order status codes:{40}
Open - (order remains flexible for updates, order has not been confirmed by source, articles remain in the sending store's on-hands)
Sent - (order has been approved by source, articles are in-transit and commited to the receiving site)
Late - (order has not been received based on due date assigned)
Received - (order has been received at the site, articles are included in receiving site's on-hands)
Closed - (complete order has been received and all articles accounted for)
Canceled - (order has been deleted from the system)
Paid - (invoice associated with an order has been paid)
Ability to override the default GL account, but managing those overrides by limiting access and utilizing a pre-approved account list (i.e., Grand Opening expenses)
Automatically handle cost and inventory adjustments for the sending and receiving sites.  Also interface to unit inventory accounting.
Maintain integrity of transfers and transfer documents that take multiple days or shipments to fulfill.
Limit store access during receipt to only items which are listed in their assortment
Allow sites to be picked outside of replenishment schedule (forced picks)
Support seamless, inter-company transfers between distribution centers in one country and sites in another.  The system must have the ability to transfer merchandise between sites without creating intermediate billing documents.  Distribution center inventory must be relieved at the home country's moving average cost (MAC), while the receiving site's inventory must be charged as home country's moving average cost X receiving site's exchange rate. Example: US distribution center transfers merchandise to Canadian sites.  {50}

MTR010
The system should identify location candidates to receive inventory as well as locations with excess inventory.  The system should generate specific transfer recommendations (e.g., transfer x units from location A to location B).{10}  Recommendations must be based on:
Inventory levels
Projected demand
Sell Thru
The user should be allowed to specify the domain be evaluated for transfer opportunities:
Locations or groups of locations.
Items or groups of items.
A combination of both.
The user must also be able to control whether to accept or reject the system recommendations either singly, in groups, or in its entirety. The user must also be able to manually generate location to location transfers.  Either store to store, or store to DC.
Transfer recommendations should consider site specific projected demand and past sell thru.
Other potential considerations include:
Transfer and freight costs to handle and move inventory.
Markdown costs if goods are clearanced.
Transfer timing versus forecast demand.
Excess inventory carrying costs.
Expiration dates versus transfer timing.
Costs to purchase and move new inventory to some locations (i.e., new purchase order inventory costs versus excess inventory carrying costs).

Initiate Manual Transfer
Objectives
To fulfill customer needs, support advertising and refine merchandising while maintaining simple, accurate, and complete documentation of merchandise transfers.
Requirements
MTR060
Support the creation, tracking and reporting of transfers by the following reason codes at the line item level:{60}
Promotional (ad/event merchandise)
Aged inventory (DC inventory which has reached a certain holding date)
Discontinued items (articles which have been canceled)
Customer request (special order)
Overstock (inventory overages)
Out of stock (articles which are missing in site's assortment))
Transfer from a single site to multiple sites
Ability for SSG to initiate any type of transfer
MTR070
Support the automatic issue of a transfer notification report  to the appropriate inventory manager at the home office.  Based on the transfer reason code, systematically generate an on-line report to a user of the system.  This report must be triggered by line item reason code and provide the user insight into all transfers with a particular reason code.  Example: Report on all transfers which are tagged with the reason code "promotional."{70}
Support one-step and two-step transfers
Support real-time processing such that transfer request, initiation, delivery, and final acknowledgment take place in a single day (example: manager store-to-store transfer of items)
MTR030
Support the identification and transfer of "all" quantities.  The system must be able to recognize all articles as a group in addition to recognizing articles as a numeric quantity.  Certain situations necessitate the transfer of all articles from one site to another.   Example:  Store Closing - during a store closing the system must have the ability to transfer "all" items from one location to another in one transfer order.{30}
MTR100
Support the the ability to transfer and track expense articles (non-salable merchandise including services and supplies).  The system must be able to differentiate salable articles from non-salable articles.  Salable articles are maintained in a site's inventory and on-hands are managed.  Some non-salable (expense) articles must also be maintained in a site's inventory and on-hands managed for replenishment purposes.  (example:  bags)  Still other articles can be both either a salable and non-salable artricle. (example:  pet shampoo)  When the transfer of these items occurs the system must differentaite between salable pet shampoo and non-salable pet shampoo by designating one type as an expense article and the other as a salable article.{100}
MTR110
Support the creation of a "transfer request" which is automatically sent to multiple sites for potential approval.  Once received at a site, this "transfer request" must be systematically approved or not approved.  An approval then triggers a notification to the receiving store regarding shipment and closes the order.  The "transfer request" is then systematically updated regarding quantities that the sending store has shipped.{110}
MTR120
Ability to generate a system warning and block on any article/site combination that is not allowed to be transferred based on a "transferable/non-transferable" field which is flagged/maintained in the article/site master.  System should not allow a transfer request to be created for article/site combinations which are flagged as "non-transferable."  Example: National retailers typically carry articles which are regional in nature.  Due to state or local legislation, a site may be banned from carrying certain articles because they are illegal for sale.  The system must be able to block the transfer of a "banned" article from one site to another if that article is not allowed for a particular site.{120}
Maintain Manual Transfer
Objectives
To fulfill customer needs, support advertising and refine merchandising through the update, delete or change of the transfer order.
Requirements
Systematically create a new document which shows original transfer quantity versus transfer quantity received
Ability for sending or receiving site to view all transfers orders for any given location
Ability to view transfer history by document number
MTR090
Support the ability to limit distribution center deletion of transfer orders.  DCs must not have the ability to cancel a transfer order once it has been approved.{90}
Ability to modify or delete transfer order by line item or entire order.  Limit the ability to modify or delete transfer orders by status.  If status = sent, then transfer order cannot be modified or deleted.
Create audit trail which tracks changes made before and after order generation
Automatic error generation if sending DC is not appropriate for a given store
MTR020
Systematically support order creation, tracking and reporting by line item based on the following dates: {20}
Vendor ship date (truck load date)
Due date at ship via point (mixing center or crossdock date)
In-store date (estimated arrival date at store)
Cancel date (order cancelation date)
