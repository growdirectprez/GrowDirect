What Makes PVPUSD Special

PVPUSD is more than just test scores. The district emphasizes:

  • Academic Excellence with Flexibility: Rigorous curricula balanced with student choice and personalization
  • College Preparation from Day One: Advanced Placement, IB programs, and honors tracks begin in middle school
  • Engaged Community: Parent involvement, school foundations, and active PTAs are hallmarks of PV schools
  • Well-Resourced Schools: Strong bond measures and community support mean well-maintained facilities and current technology
  • Safe, Tight-Knit Communities: Low crime rates and strong neighborhood cohesion create secure learning environments
  • Exceptional College Placement: Graduates matriculate to UC schools, Ivy League universities, and top institutions nationwide

High School Feeder System

In PVPUSD, elementary students do not necessarily feed to the same high school based on neighborhood. The district uses a choice/controlled choice model for some assignments. Both high schools draw from the entire district, creating competitive enrollment.

Understanding School Feeder Patterns

PVPUSD uses a combination of neighborhood assignments and controlled choice for student placement. Understanding feeder patterns helps families know where their child's education journey will take them.

Elementary to Middle School Patterns

Elementary schools feed students to specific intermediate schools based primarily on neighborhood and residential location:

Lunada Bay, Montemalaga Elementary
→
Palos Verdes Intermediate (PVIS)
Cornerstone, Silver Spur, Soleado, Vista Grande, Rancho Vista, Mira Catalina, Point Vicente Elementary
→
Miraleste or Ridgecrest Intermediate

Middle to High School Transition

Students can apply to either high school through PVPUSD's choice process. High school assignment is NOT automatic based on middle school; families may request their preferred school, though enrollment is dependent on capacity.

PVIS, Miraleste, or Ridgecrest Intermediate
→
Palos Verdes Peninsula HS or Palos Verdes HS

Important: Feeder patterns can change. When planning a move, verify current attendance boundaries with PVPUSD directly at www.pvpusd.net.

Important Note on School Assignments

While neighborhood assignments are the primary method, PVPUSD does offer limited choice and has implemented controlled choice policies. Additionally, families can request transfers for specific programs or circumstances. Always verify current boundaries with PVPUSD Enrollment at www.pvpusd.net before making a home purchase decision.

Frequently Asked Questions About Palos Verdes Schools

Are Palos Verdes schools really as good as everyone says?
Yes. PVPUSD is ranked #45 out of 1,907 districts in California and #3 in the Los Angeles area. With 74% math proficiency (vs. 34% state average) and 80% reading proficiency (vs. 47% state average), the numbers back up the reputation. But more importantly, these are schools where educators and families are genuinely invested in student success. The culture of academic achievement runs deep.
What's the difference between Palos Verdes High School and Palos Verdes Peninsula High School?
Both are excellent, but they're different schools with different histories and communities. Palos Verdes Peninsula High School (in Rolling Hills Estates) is ranked higher nationally (#34 in CA, #285 nationally) with slightly higher test scores and a 98% graduation rate. Palos Verdes High School (in Palos Verdes Estates) is ranked #118 in CA and also offers excellent college prep with a 97% graduation rate. Both can get your student into great colleges. Consider location, school culture, sports offerings, and where your friends are going when making a choice.
How much does school zone affect home values in Palos Verdes?
Significantly. Homes in top-performing school zones (especially those feeding to highly-ranked elementary schools and PVPHS) command a premium. Parents will choose neighborhoods specifically to get their kids into certain schools. When you're choosing a home, knowing your school assignment should be a key part of your decision—along with the home itself, neighborhood, and family lifestyle.
Can I choose which high school my child attends, or is it based on neighborhood?
PVPUSD has a choice/controlled choice process for high school. Most middle school students can request either Palos Verdes High School or Palos Verdes Peninsula High School, though enrollment is capacity-dependent. Neighborhood does influence the likelihood of getting your choice, but don't assume you'll automatically attend one school based on where you live. Always check the district's current policies when planning a move.
What makes PVPUSD elementary schools different from each other?
While all PVPUSD elementary schools are strong, some rank higher on standardized tests (Montemalaga, Soleado, and Cornerstone consistently rank among California's top elementary schools). Others may emphasize different approaches or serve tighter-knit neighborhoods. The quality of a school also depends on the specific cohort, principal's leadership, and parent involvement during any given year. If possible, visit schools, talk to current families, and ask about the school culture and programs that matter to you.
Should I consider private schools over Palos Verdes public schools?
For most families, PVPUSD public schools offer exceptional education at no tuition cost. However, private schools like Chadwick offer different philosophies, smaller classes, and K-12 continuity that some families prefer. Private school tuition ranges from $44,000-$51,000+. Only you can decide if the private school difference is worth the cost for your family. Many successful Palos Verdes families choose excellent public schools and redirect the savings toward other opportunities.
