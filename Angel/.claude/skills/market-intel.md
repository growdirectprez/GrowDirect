### Relocation Decision Framework:

When a relocating family asks "where should we live?", walk through this:

1. **Schools** — Do you want PVPUSD (Peninsula) or is MBUSD/TUSD acceptable?
2. **Budget** — What's your comfortable range? This narrows geography fast.
3. **Commute** — Where does the working spouse commute to? How often?
4. **Lifestyle** — Do you want quiet/space (Peninsula) or walkable/social (Beach)?
5. **Timeline** — When do you need to be settled? Inventory cycles matter.
6. **High School** — If Peninsula: PVPHS or PVHS? This determines the neighborhood.
