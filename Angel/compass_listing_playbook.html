Section 1

Compass Private Exclusives

The most powerful listing tool in your arsenal. Here's exactly how it works after the 2025–2026 NAR policy changes.

What Changed: NAR Clear Cooperation + MLOS

In March 2025, NAR introduced the "Multiple Listing Options for Sellers" (MLOS) policy alongside Clear Cooperation. This was a game-changer: sellers can now legally choose to delay public marketing. MLSs have until September 30, 2026 to implement.

Before (Old Rule)Now (New MLOS Policy)
Listings had to enter MLS within 1 business day of any "public marketing" Sellers can sign a disclosure choosing to delay public marketing
Private Exclusives were in a legal gray area Private Exclusives are now policy-compliant with seller consent
Compass was in tension with NAR rules Compass's strategy is now aligned with NAR policy

The key requirement: Seller must sign a written disclosure documenting informed consent to delay public marketing. No disclosure = no Private Exclusive. This is non-negotiable for compliance.

How Private Exclusives Work

A Private Exclusive is a listing marketed only through Compass's network before (or instead of) going on the public MLS. The property is visible to 340,000+ agents across Compass and partner brokerages, but does not appear on Zillow, Realtor.com, or IDX feeds.

Benefits for the Seller

  • Test pricing without public Days on Market accumulating
  • Gather market feedback before full launch
  • Complete renovations/staging during private phase
  • Maintain privacy (celebrities, divorces, estates)
  • Create exclusivity and urgency among buyers
  • Compass data shows 2.9% higher close price on pre-marketed listings vs. direct MLS

Risks to Communicate

  • Reduced buyer pool during private phase
  • May miss buyers not connected to Compass agents
  • Seller must sign informed consent disclosure
  • Some local MLSs still have restrictive rules
  • Price discovery may be slower with limited exposure

Compass + Redfin Syndication (March 2026)

As of March 2026, Compass rolled out a three-phased syndication strategy with Redfin. Coming Soon listings now appear on Redfin with priority placement, and Private Exclusives will follow. Sellers must provide written consent for any syndication beyond the Compass network. This expands reach while keeping listings off Zillow and Realtor.com if the seller chooses.

Repayment Triggers

The loan must be repaid when any of these happen first: the home sells and escrow closes; the seller terminates the listing agreement; Compass terminates the agreement; 12 months pass from the Concierge start date; or Notable Finance suspends the loan per their terms. If the listing agreement expires without renewal, repayment is due within 10 days.

California DRE Compliance

DRE Required Every piece of marketing Angelique produces must meet these rules. Non-compliance = DRE disciplinary action against her AND the broker.

License Disclosure Rules (RE 559)

All "first point of contact" materials must include three things:

Required ElementSpecifics
Agent's Name As it appears on the DRE license — "Angelique Lyle"
8-Digit License Number CA DRE# 01475592 — must be readable, not tiny/hidden
Broker's Name & Identity "Compass" — must be equally prominent as agent/team name. Cannot be buried in fine print.

What Counts as "First Point of Contact"

Business cards, letterhead, postcards, flyers, door hangers, property brochures, yard signs, website, social media profiles (Instagram bio, Facebook page, LinkedIn), email signatures, text message signatures, voicemail greetings, online ads (Google, Facebook), virtual tour landing pages, and any initial advertising material a consumer might see.

For Angelique specifically: The website (OwnPalosVerdes.com), all Instagram posts about listings, her email signature, and the school guide all need "Angelique Lyle · CA DRE# 01475592 · Compass" clearly displayed. We already have this on the site — just need to verify it's on all social content.

Team Advertising Rules

Since Angelique operates as "Accardo Real Estate Associates" (a team name), these rules apply: at least one team member's name and license number must appear on all team advertising; the broker name ("Compass") cannot be diminished or made less prominent than the team name; and the team identity can be featured but broker oversight must be clear. The safe standard: "Accardo Real Estate Associates · Compass" side by side, same font weight.

Content Rules

  • No misleading or false information about properties (square footage, views, features)
  • Use only current, accurate photos — no old photos that misrepresent current condition
  • Cite reliable sources for claims (assessor records, builder specs, appraiser)
  • Never use discriminatory language — "No Section 8" is illegal (source of income discrimination); avoid phrases like "family neighborhood" or "bachelor pad" (familial status/sex)
  • All marketing materials must be reviewed under broker's supervision policies
  • Fair housing disclaimer on all advertising is best practice

Section 5

Listing Appointment Pitch

Pitch Scripts The full sequence for a listing appointment — from intro through close. Weaves together Private Exclusives, Concierge, and Angelique's personal story.

[Opening — Establishing Authority]

Angelique: "Thank you for inviting me. Before I walk through the numbers, let me tell you a little about my approach — because how I sell your home is just as important as what we list it for."

Angelique: "I've been on this Peninsula for over 20 years. I raised both my kids here — they went through PVPUSD, played sports at the rec centers, and both ended up at Big Ten universities. When I tell a buyer about this community, I'm not reading a brochure. I lived it."

Angelique: "I'm in the top 1.5% of agents nationally, and I'm with Compass — which matters because of the tools I'm about to show you."
[The CMA — Data First]

Angelique: "Let me show you what the market is telling us."

[Present branded CMA: comparable sales, active inventory, days on market, price trends. Use real data. Be honest about pricing — don't overpromise to win the listing.]

Angelique: "Based on recent comps, I see your home in the [$X – $Y range]. Now, there are things we can do to push toward the top of that range — and I have a way to do it that costs you nothing upfront."
[Compass Concierge — The Differentiator]

Angelique: "Compass has a program called Concierge. We front up to $50,000 at zero percent interest for home improvements — staging, painting, landscaping, flooring, kitchen touch-ups. You pay nothing until close. It comes out of escrow."

Angelique: "On this home, I'd recommend [specific improvements], probably [$X] total. That investment could move your sale price by 3–5%, which on a [$price] home means [$X] more in your pocket. The flat fee is $750. That's the entire cost of the program."

[Let them absorb. Answer questions. Then transition to the marketing strategy.]
[Private Exclusive — The Strategy]

Angelique: "Here's where my Compass tools really work for you. I want to use a phased marketing strategy."

Angelique: "Phase one: while Concierge improvements are being done, I list your home as a Private Exclusive — visible to 340,000 agents in the Compass network, but NOT on Zillow, NOT on Realtor.com. No Days on Market clock ticking. We test the price, gather feedback, and build anticipation."

Angelique: "Phase two: we go Coming Soon on Redfin — priority placement, more eyes, still no full MLS pressure."

Angelique: "Phase three: full MLS launch. By then, we may already have offers. And every buyer on the Peninsula has heard about your home."

Angelique: "Compass data shows pre-marketed homes close at 2.9% higher on average. On your home, that's [$X]."
[Handling the #1 Objection: "Another agent said they'd list it for more"]

Angelique: "I'll be direct with you — any agent can promise a high price to win your listing. That's called 'buying the listing.' What happens next? It sits. The Days on Market build. Buyers see the price drops. You end up selling for less than if you'd priced it right from the start."

Angelique: "My job is to get you the most money in your pocket, not to tell you what you want to hear today. The CMA data doesn't lie. And with Concierge improvements and a Private Exclusive strategy, I can actually get you above what the comps suggest — with a real plan, not a wish."
[The Close]

Angelique: "So here's what I'd like to do. Let me get the Concierge application started — it takes about a week for approval. While that's processing, I'll order professional photography and start preparing the Private Exclusive listing. We could have you in front of 340,000 agents within two weeks."

Angelique: "Does that timeline work for you?"

[This is an assumptive close. If they say yes, you have the listing. If they hesitate, ask: "What would you need to see to feel comfortable moving forward?"]
