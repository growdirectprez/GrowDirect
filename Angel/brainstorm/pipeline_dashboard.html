Compass Toolkit

How each Compass tool feeds the pipeline.

Compass CRM
Pipeline Stage: All
Central hub for all contacts. Segment by buyer/seller/sphere. Track every touchpoint. Set reminders for follow-ups. Run email campaigns.
→ Clean database, segment contacts, set up automated touchpoints
Likely to Sell
Pipeline Stage: Attract + Capture
AI scans your contacts + CoreLogic data to flag homeowners likely to sell. Surfaces warm leads you already know — the highest-conversion outreach you can make.
→ Check weekly, make 2–3 personalized calls per week
Collections
Pipeline Stage: Nurture
Visual property boards shared with buyer clients. Real-time collaboration. When a client opens and lingers on a listing, you get notified — that's a buying signal.
→ Create a Collection for every active buyer within 24 hours
Compass One
Pipeline Stage: Convert + Referral
Client dashboard for the full journey — before, during, and after the deal. Keeps docs, timeline, and market updates in one place. Stays active post-close for referrals.
→ Invite every client to Compass One at first meeting
Marketing Center
Pipeline Stage: Attract + Nurture
AI-powered design studio for listing flyers, social posts, brochures, and digital ads. Generate listing descriptions and social captions in minutes.
→ Create listing materials same-day, share across social channels
Business Tracker
Pipeline Stage: Convert
Deal dashboard with pipeline visualization, transaction checklists, and milestone tracking. See where every deal stands at a glance.
→ Review pipeline every Monday morning, update deal stages

Weekly Rhythm

Angelique's pipeline routine.

This isn't about spending hours on marketing. It's about 30–45 minutes of focused pipeline work each morning, plus the client-facing work Angelique already excels at.

Monday
8:30 AM · 15 min Review pipeline in Business Tracker. Update deal stages. Business Tracker
9:00 AM · 20 min Check Likely to Sell flags. Make 2–3 personalized calls. Likely to Sell
9:30 AM · 10 min Review weekend website leads. Respond within 5 min. CRM
Tuesday
8:30 AM · 20 min Create/update Collections for active buyers. Collections
9:00 AM · 15 min Respond to social DMs and comments. Instagram
Afternoon Showings, client meetings, tours.
Wednesday
8:30 AM · 30 min Record neighborhood Reel or YouTube short. Content
9:00 AM · 15 min Sphere outreach: 5 personal texts or calls. CRM
Afternoon Client work + new lead follow-ups.
Thursday
8:30 AM · 20 min Review blog post draft (VA-prepared). Approve + publish. OwnPalosVerdes.com
9:00 AM · 15 min Create listing materials in Marketing Center. Marketing Center
Afternoon Showings, listings, client meetings.
Friday
8:30 AM · 10 min Invite any new clients to Compass One. Compass One
9:00 AM · 15 min Schedule next week's social posts. Social
9:30 AM · 10 min Review week's leads. Tag and segment in CRM. CRM

Total pipeline time: ~35–45 minutes each morning, 5 days a week. The rest of Angelique's day is what she does best — meeting clients, showing homes, and closing deals.
