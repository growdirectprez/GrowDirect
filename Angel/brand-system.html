Design Rules

Do This. Not That.

✓ Do

  • → Black backgrounds with white text
  • → White backgrounds with black text
  • → Full-bleed photography
  • → Lots of whitespace / breathing room
  • → Flat buttons with arrows
  • → Bold, confident headlines
  • → Let the homes be the hero
  • → DRE# and Compass always visible

✕ Don't

  • → No gold, sand, or ocean blue accents
  • → No decorative borders or ornaments
  • → No gradients or shadows on cards
  • → No script fonts or calligraphy
  • → No busy backgrounds or patterns
  • → No colored buttons (black or white only)
  • → No "luxury brokerage" visual clichés
  • → Nothing that looks like Sotheby's
