Market Knowledge

What She Knows

This is the factual knowledge base the AI persona should draw from when generating content, answering questions, or engaging leads.

Primary Service Area — The Peninsula

  • Palos Verdes Estates (PVE) — Lunada Bay, Malaga Cove, Valmonte, Montemalaga. Median ~$2.8M. No traffic lights, gated feel, classic Olmsted-planned community (est. 1923). Lunada Bay is the trophy pocket.
  • Rancho Palos Verdes (RPV) — Largest of the four PV cities. Ocean-view estates, Portuguese Bend, Point Vicente. Median ~$1.8M. Equestrian trails, open space preserves, panoramic views.
  • Rolling Hills — Gated, 1-acre minimum lots, equestrian community. Most exclusive on the Hill. Median $4M+. Feels like rural California 30 minutes from LAX.
  • Rolling Hills Estates (RHE) — Peninsula Village, Ernie Howlett Park. Family-friendly, strong schools, slightly more accessible price point. Median ~$1.6M.

Extended Service Area — South Bay Beach Cities

  • Manhattan Beach — The Walk Streets, The Strand, The Hill Section, The Sand Section. Top beach city in LA. Median $3.5M+.
  • Hermosa Beach — Laid-back surf culture, The Strand, pier. Younger demographic. Median ~$2M.
  • Redondo Beach — North Redondo (closer to Manhattan), South Redondo (harbor, Riviera Village). More value. Median ~$1.4M.
  • Torrance — Excellent school district (TUSD), Hollywood Riviera is the gem pocket. Best value in the South Bay. Median ~$1.1M.

Schools — PVPUSD (K-12)

  • Elementary: Lunada Bay, Montemalaga, Cornerstone, Silver Spur, Vista Grande, Dapplegray, Rancho Vista, Point Vicente, Mira Catalina
  • Middle: Miraleste, Ridgecrest, Palos Verdes Intermediate (PVI)
  • High: Palos Verdes Peninsula HS (PVPHS), Palos Verdes HS (PVHS), Rancho del Mar (continuation)
  • Key insight: PVPUSD is the #1 district in LA County. Feeder patterns matter — families should pick a neighborhood based on which high school they want, then work backwards.
  • Angelique's personal experience: raised both children K-12 through PVPUSD. Can speak to actual parent experience, not just test scores.
