# Listing Playbook — Win, Launch & Market

## Listing Appointment Pitch Scripts

### Opening (Warm — Referral or Sphere)

> "Thanks for having me over. Before I dive into numbers, I'd love to hear your
> story — what's prompting the move, and what does the ideal timeline look like
> for your family? That helps me figure out the right strategy, because it's
> never one-size-fits-all."

### Opening (Cold — Market Inquiry)

> "I appreciate you reaching out. I know there are a lot of agents on the
> Peninsula — I'd rather earn your trust with information than a sales pitch.
> Can I ask a couple questions about your goals first?"

### Why Compass (When Asked)

> "I chose Compass for three reasons. First, Private Exclusives — I can market
> your home to 340,000 agents before it ever hits Zillow, which means we can
> test pricing and build demand without burning Days on Market. Compass data
> shows those listings close 2.9% higher. Second, Compass Concierge — if your
> home needs work before listing, Compass will front up to $50K at zero interest.
> You don't pay until close. Third, I'm in the #1 brokerage in LA by volume.
> That's not bragging — it means more buyer eyeballs on your home."

### Concierge Pitch (When Home Needs Work)

> "I can see this home has great bones, but let's talk about what would
> maximize your return. New paint, updated flooring, maybe staging — Compass
> will front up to $50,000 for that at zero percent interest. The only cost is
> a $750 flat fee at close. I've seen a $15K investment add $80K to a sale
> price. It's not charity — it's math. Want me to walk through what I'd
> recommend?"

### Private Exclusive Pitch

> "Here's what I recommend: we start with a Private Exclusive. Your home goes
> live inside the Compass network — 340,000+ agents see it — but it doesn't
> appear on Zillow or Realtor.com yet. This gives us 2-3 weeks to test our
> pricing, gather feedback from agents and serious buyers, and build
> anticipation. If we're getting strong interest, we know our price is right
> and we launch to MLS with momentum. If we need to adjust, we do it without
> anyone seeing Days on Market accumulate. The data shows this strategy closes
> 2.9% higher than going straight to MLS."

### Handling "Why Not Just Go to MLS?"

> "You absolutely can, and I'll support whatever strategy you choose. Here's
> the trade-off: MLS gives you maximum exposure from Day 1, but your Days on
> Market clock starts ticking publicly. If we price aggressively, great. But
> if the market tells us we need to adjust, everyone sees that. Private
> Exclusive lets you gather that intel without the public scoreboard. Most of
> my sellers choose the staged approach — Private Exclusive first, then MLS —
> because it gives them control."

## Marketing Assets Checklist

### Pre-Listing
- [ ] Professional photography (30-50 images)
- [ ] 3D Matterport walkthrough
- [ ] Drone/aerial photography (ocean views, lot size)
- [ ] Video tour (2-3 minutes, lifestyle-focused)
- [ ] Floor plan (measured, professional)
- [ ] Property description (Angelique's voice — see angel-voice skill)
- [ ] Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)
- [ ] Seller disclosure review

### Launch Day
- [ ] Compass listing entry (Private Exclusive or MLS)
- [ ] Compass Marketing Center collateral (print + digital)
- [ ] Email campaign to buyer database
- [ ] Social media posts (Instagram, Facebook)
- [ ] Agent networking (Compass Private Exclusive blast)
- [ ] Open house scheduling and promotion

### Ongoing
- [ ] Weekly seller update (activity, feedback, metrics)
- [ ] Price strategy review (DOM milestones: 7, 14, 21, 30 days)
- [ ] Social media refreshes (new angles, lifestyle content)
- [ ] Broker open / agent caravan
- [ ] Targeted outreach to likely buyers in Compass Collections
