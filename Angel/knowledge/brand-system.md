# Brand System — Angelique Lyle · Compass

## Design Philosophy

Black and white. Photography provides color. The brand is editorial, clean,
and confident — it lets the homes and the content speak. No gold, no sand,
no ocean blue, no gradients.

---

## Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Black | #000000 | Primary brand color, headings, buttons, section backgrounds |
| Near Black | #1A1A1A | Body text, dark sections |
| Gray 800 | #333333 | Secondary text |
| Gray 600 | #666666 | Supporting text, descriptions |
| Gray 400 | #999999 | Labels, metadata, subtle UI elements |
| Gray 200 | #E0E0E0 | Borders, dividers |
| Gray 100 | #F2F2F2 | Light backgrounds, alternating rows |
| Off White | #F7F7F7 | Section backgrounds |
| White | #FFFFFF | Primary background |

**Never use:** Gold, sand (#C8B99A), ocean blue (#1C3A4A), gradients, shadows,
ornamental elements. These appeared in early brainstorm documents but are not
part of the final brand system.

---

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Usage |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| Headings | DM Serif Display | 400 (regular) | All headings, hero text, property names |
| Headings (italic) | DM Serif Display Italic | 400 | Emphasis within headings |
| Body | DM Sans | 300–600 | All body text, UI, navigation |

### Scale
- Hero: 2.5rem+ (DM Serif Display)
- H2: 1.5rem (DM Serif Display)
- H3: 1.15rem (DM Serif Display)
- Body: 0.9rem (DM Sans)
- Labels: 0.6-0.7rem, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.12-0.2em (DM Sans 600)

---

## Buttons

- **Primary:** Flat black background, white text, right arrow (→)
- **Secondary:** White background, black border, black text, right arrow (→)
- **Ghost:** Transparent, gray-200 border, black text
- **Border radius:** 4px
- **Font:** DM Sans, 0.85rem, weight 500
- **Padding:** 0.75rem 1.5rem

---

## Layout Principles

- Lots of whitespace
- Alternate black and white sections for rhythm
- Full-bleed photography — the homes are the hero
- No ornamental dividers — use spacing
- Section labels: small uppercase, letter-spaced, gray-400
- Card borders: left border accent (3-4px solid black)

---

## Photography Direction

- Full-bleed, coastal light, interior warmth
- The homes are the hero — not the agent
- Lifestyle shots: families, neighborhoods, ocean views
- No stock photography — everything should feel specific to the Peninsula
- Agent headshot: professional, warm, approachable (not glamour shot)
