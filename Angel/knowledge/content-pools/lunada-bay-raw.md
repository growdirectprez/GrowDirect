## 1. Neighborhood Facts

### Character & Identity
- Lunada Bay is a blufftop coastal neighborhood in Palos Verdes Estates
- Known for walkability, ocean views, and strong community feel centered around Lunada Bay Plaza
- Population: ~4,519 residents
- Typical home: 4 bed / 3 bath, avg 3,028 sq ft (range 671–12,805 sq ft)
- One of PVE's four neighborhoods (with Malaga Cove, Valmonte, Montemalaga)
- Angelique's description: "A true coastal neighborhood known for its walkability, ocean views, and strong community feel centered around Lunada Bay Plaza."

### Real Estate (Current as of April 2026)
- **2025 full-year sales (Lunada Bay/Margate):** 61 homes sold (up from 45 in 2024, +35.6%)
- **Average sale price:** $3,175,747 (up from $3,131,777 in 2024)
- **Average price/sq ft:** $1,044 (up 2.8% from $1,016 in 2024)
- **Highest sale 2025:** $9,550,000
- **Lowest sale 2025:** $1,620,000
- **Current median (Redfin):** $2.38M (down 6.3% YoY)
- **Current median price/sq ft:** $988 (up 2.7% YoY)
- **Active listings:** 12 homes, $1.945M–$9.4M range
- **Days on market:** ~79 days (up from 53 prior year)
- **Market competitiveness:** Somewhat competitive (Redfin 45/100)
- Source: [PV Source Jan 2026](https://palosverdessource.com/2026/01/30/palos-verdes-homes-sales-lunada-bay-margate-2025/) | [Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/neighborhood/39748/CA/Palos-Verdes-Estates/Lunada-Bay/housing-market)

## 6. Community News (Recent)

### Lunada Bay Surf Policing (Feb 2026)
Winter swell brought 12-foot waves to Lunada Bay. PVE police patrolled cliffs with billy clubs to enforce zero-tolerance on localism. Bay Boys surf gang previously disbanded after legal action and fort demolition.
Source: [Surfer Magazine](https://www.surfer.com/news/lunada-bay-surfing-big-waves-localism-police)

### Lunada Bay Coastal Access Settlement
PVE resolved decade-long litigation, agreeing to ensure public access to Lunada Bay.
Sources: [PVE News](https://www.pvestates.org/Home/Components/News/News/3611/) | [LAist](https://laist.com/brief/news/drawing-a-resolution-to-an-ugly-chapter-palos-verdes-estates-agrees-to-ensure-access-to-lunada-bay)

### PVE City Updates
- Fire safety goat grazing contract awarded (Feb 2026)
- General Plan Safety Element in public review
- New police activity map launched
- Afternoon traffic control at PV Dr W & Via Corta shifted to 3–6pm M-F
Source: [PVE News](https://www.pvestates.org/community/news)

### Peninsula Center Library Renovation
Groundbreaking March 6, 2026. Main entrance becoming "civic front porch." Completion expected June 2026.
Source: [Easy Reader](https://easyreadernews.com/category/palos-verdes/)

### Wayfarers Chapel Relocation
Lloyd Wright-designed chapel in RPV planning rebuild one mile west of landslide-impacted site. Disassembly completed July 2024.
Source: [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/wayfarers-chapel-finds-new-potential-home-rancho-palos-verdes-landslides-closed-doors/16638586/)

### Java Wave Opens in Lunada Bay (Aug 2025)
New coffee shop in former St. Honore space. Founded by Beth Parwar, former art teacher.
Source: [PV Pulse](https://www.palosverdespulse.com/blog/javawave) | [LBHOA](https://www.lbhoa.org/2025/07/19/java-wave-opening-soon/)

### Lunada Bay Diwali Celebration (2024–2025)
Community Diwali at Lunada Bay Park — Indian cuisine, Bollywood DJ, community gathering.
Source: [PV Pulse](https://www.palosverdespulse.com/blog/diwalilunadabay)
