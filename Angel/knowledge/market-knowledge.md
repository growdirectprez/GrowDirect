# Market Knowledge — Peninsula & South Bay

## Primary Service Area: The Peninsula

### Palos Verdes Estates (PVE)
- **Neighborhoods:** Lunada Bay, Malaga Cove, Valmonte, Montemalaga
- **Median:** ~$2.8M
- **Character:** No traffic lights, gated feel, classic Olmsted-planned community (est. 1923)
- **Trophy pocket:** Lunada Bay — ocean-view estates, walking distance to the cove
- **Schools:** Lunada Bay Elementary, Malaga Cove Middle (feeds to PVPHS or PVHS)

### Rancho Palos Verdes (RPV)
- **Neighborhoods:** Portuguese Bend, Point Vicente, Terranea area, Miraleste
- **Median:** ~$1.8M
- **Character:** Largest of the four PV cities. Ocean-view estates, equestrian trails, open space preserves, panoramic views
- **Key features:** Trump National Golf Club, Terranea Resort, Point Vicente Lighthouse
- **Schools:** Multiple elementaries feed to different middle/high schools — feeder pattern matters

### Rolling Hills
- **Character:** Gated, 1-acre minimum lots, equestrian community
- **Median:** $4M+
- **Feel:** Rural California 30 minutes from LAX. Most exclusive on the Hill.
- **Access:** Gated entries, private roads, horse trails
- **Note:** Very low inventory — homes rarely come to market

### Rolling Hills Estates (RHE)
- **Neighborhoods:** Peninsula Village, Ernie Howlett Park area
- **Median:** ~$1.6M
- **Character:** Family-friendly, strong schools, slightly more accessible price point
- **Key features:** Peninsula Center (shopping/dining hub for the Hill)

## Extended Service Area: South Bay Beach Cities

### Manhattan Beach
- **Neighborhoods:** The Walk Streets, The Strand, The Hill Section, The Sand Section, The Tree Section
- **Median:** $3.5M+
- **Character:** Top beach city in LA. Walkable downtown, excellent restaurants, strong community
- **Commute advantage:** Close to El Segundo tech/aerospace corridor

### Hermosa Beach
- **Median:** ~$2M
- **Character:** Laid-back surf culture, The Strand, pier. Younger demographic
- **Lifestyle:** Nightlife, beach volleyball, pier concerts

### Redondo Beach
- **Areas:** North Redondo (closer to Manhattan Beach feel), South Redondo (harbor, Riviera Village)
- **Median:** ~$1.4M
- **Character:** More value than Manhattan/Hermosa. Riviera Village is the gem pocket
- **Commute:** Good access to 405, aerospace corridor

### Torrance
- **Neighborhoods:** Hollywood Riviera (gem pocket), Old Torrance, South Torrance
- **Median:** ~$1.1M
- **Character:** Excellent school district (TUSD), best value in the South Bay
- **Secret weapon:** Hollywood Riviera has the beach city feel at Torrance prices

## The Peninsula vs. Beach Cities Decision

This is the core decision for relocating families. Angelique should guide
based on the family's priorities:

| Factor | Peninsula | Beach Cities |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Space | Larger lots, more land | Smaller lots, denser |
| Schools | PVPUSD (#1 in LA County) | Varies — MBUSD, HBCSD, RBUSD, TUSD |
| Quiet | Very quiet, residential feel | More urban energy, nightlife |
| Views | Ocean, Catalina, coastline | Ocean, but primarily from Strand/Walk Streets |
| Commute | Longer to westside/DTLA | Closer to LAX, El Segundo tech, westside |
| Price | Mid-range $1.6M–$4M+ | Higher per sq ft, $1.1M–$3.5M+ |
| Vibe | Established, family-oriented | Younger, more social, walkable |

## Relocation Expertise

### Primary Inbound Corridors
- Bay Area / Silicon Valley (tech families)
- East Coast (NYC, NJ, CT — finance and corporate)
- International (Asia-Pacific)
- Texas (Austin, Dallas — tech/defense)
- Pacific Northwest (Seattle — tech/Boeing)

### Key Decision Factors for Relocating Families
1. School quality (PVPUSD is almost always the driver)
2. Commute to aerospace corridor (El Segundo/Manhattan Beach) or DTLA
3. Coastal lifestyle and safety
4. Community feel and family-friendliness
5. Budget alignment with expectations

### Target Persona: The Relocating Mom
- Age 32–48
- Household income $300K–$1M+
- Spouse in tech/aerospace/medical
- Timeline: 3–9 months before move
- Decision driver: schools first, then neighborhood feel, then house
- Research: 15–25 hours online before contacting any agent
- Pain point: overwhelmed by options, doesn't know the Hill
