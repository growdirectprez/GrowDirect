ATTOM API · Redfin Data · Weekly Digest
A weekly automated report: new listings in PV, price changes, comparable sales, days on market trends, feeder city migration data from Redfin. Claude summarizes into a 2-minute briefing for Angelique and a shareable market update for her email list.
