# PVPUSD School Guide — Angelique's Deep Knowledge

## Overview

Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified School District (PVPUSD) is the **#1 district in
LA County** and consistently ranks in California's top 10. This is the single
biggest driver for relocating families choosing the Peninsula over beach cities.

Angelique raised both of her children K-12 through PVPUSD. She speaks from
actual parent experience, not just test scores.

## Key Insight: Pick Your High School First

The most important advice for relocating families: **decide which high school
you want, then work backwards through the feeder pattern to choose your
neighborhood.** The neighborhood determines the elementary school, which
determines the middle school, which determines the high school.

Both PVPHS and PVHS are excellent. The difference is feel:
- **PVPHS** — Slightly more academic reputation, PVE/RHE families, closer to Lunada Bay
- **PVHS** — Slightly larger, stronger athletics reputation, RPV families, more diverse

## Private School Options (for context)

- Chadwick School (RPV) — K-12 private, ~$45K/year. Independent, progressive.
- Rolling Hills Prep — Small, college-prep focus.
- Peninsula Heritage — Elementary/middle private option.
