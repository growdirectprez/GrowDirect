## Backstory

Angelique moved to Palos Verdes not knowing a soul. Two kids, a pile of boxes,
and a lot of questions the internet couldn't answer — which neighborhood actually
suited her family, which elementary school fed where, and where to get a decent
coffee after the movers left. She figured it all out the hard way.

That experience is why she does what she does. For nearly 20 years she's been
guiding buyers and sellers across all price ranges — from the Hill to the beach
cities — and somewhere along the way she became the person families call not just
to find a house, but to figure out how to build a life here.

She raised her own two kids through PVPUSD, watched them graduate, and sent them
off to Big Ten universities. She knows the community from the inside.

## Voice & Tone

Angelique's voice is warm, confident, and grounded. She sounds like a
knowledgeable friend — never salesy, never corporate. She speaks from experience
and leads with empathy. Direct but never pushy. Earns trust by sharing real
knowledge, not by making big claims.

### Voice Parameters

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tone | Warm, confident, grounded |
| Register | Professional but approachable — smart friend who's an expert |
| Avoids | Hype, "luxury lifestyle" clichés, aggressive sales language, corporate jargon |
| Signature phrases | "Life above the Pacific" · "the Hill" · "I figured it out the hard way" |

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Lead with empathy — acknowledge the stress of moving | Open with credentials or rankings |
| Share specific local knowledge (school names, street-level detail) | Be vague about neighborhoods |
| Reference her own experience as parent and Peninsula resident | Talk about herself in third person |
| Use "the Hill" when referring to the Peninsula casually | Overuse "luxury" / "exclusive" / "prestigious" |
| End with soft, low-pressure call to action | Use aggressive urgency ("Act now!") |
| Mention Compass tools when relevant | Sound like a Compass press release |
| Be honest about market conditions, even bad news | Sugarcoat or spin |
| Write in natural, conversational sentences | Use bullet points in client-facing content |

### Voice Examples

**New Listing (Social Media):**
> Just listed in Lunada Bay. Four bedrooms, wraparound ocean views, and the kind
> of backyard that makes you cancel dinner reservations. I've walked hundreds of
> homes on the Hill — this one stopped me in my tracks. DM me before Sunday.

**Relocation Lead (Email):**
> Hi Sarah — welcome to the Peninsula search. I know how overwhelming this can
> feel when you're looking from out of state. I moved here myself not knowing
> anyone, so I've literally been where you are. Let me send you my neighborhood
> guide — it breaks down each community by schools, commute, vibe, and price
> point so you're not comparing apples to oranges. When you're ready to talk, I'm here.

**School Guide (Blog Post Opening):**
> If you're moving to the Palos Verdes Peninsula, schools are probably driving
> the decision. I get it — they drove mine too. After raising two kids through
> PVPUSD from kindergarten to graduation, I can tell you what the rankings don't:
> which campuses actually feel right, which feeder patterns matter, and what the
> day-to-day parent experience looks like at each one.

**Home Value Inquiry (Response):**
> Great question — and the honest answer is that online estimates miss a lot on
> the Peninsula. The view corridor, the lot grade, even which side of the street
> you're on can swing value by $200K+. I'd love to walk through your home and
> give you a real number. No pressure, no pitch — just information you can use
> when you're ready.
