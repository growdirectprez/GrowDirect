# Compass Platform Knowledge Base

## Compass Private Exclusives

### What Changed: NAR Clear Cooperation + MLOS

In March 2025, NAR introduced the "Multiple Listing Options for Sellers" (MLOS)
policy alongside Clear Cooperation. Sellers can now legally choose to delay
public marketing. MLSs have until **September 30, 2026** to implement.

| Before (Old Rule) | Now (New MLOS Policy) |
|---|---|
| Listings had to enter MLS within 1 business day of any "public marketing" | Sellers can sign a disclosure choosing to delay public marketing |
| Private Exclusives were in a legal gray area | Private Exclusives are now policy-compliant with seller consent |
| Compass was in tension with NAR rules | Compass's strategy is now aligned with NAR policy |

**Key requirement:** Seller must sign a written disclosure documenting informed
consent to delay public marketing. No disclosure = no Private Exclusive.
Non-negotiable for compliance.

### How Private Exclusives Work

A Private Exclusive is a listing marketed only through Compass's network before
(or instead of) going on the public MLS. Visible to **340,000+ agents** across
Compass and partner brokerages, but does not appear on Zillow, Realtor.com, or
IDX feeds.

**Benefits for the Seller:**
- Test pricing without public Days on Market accumulating
- Gather market feedback before full launch
- Complete renovations/staging during private phase
- Maintain privacy (celebrities, divorces, estates)
- Create exclusivity and urgency among buyers
- Compass data shows **2.9% higher close price** on pre-marketed listings

**Risks to Communicate:**
- Reduced buyer pool during private phase
- May miss buyers not connected to Compass agents
- Seller must sign informed consent disclosure
- Some local MLSs still have restrictive rules
- Price discovery may be slower with limited exposure

### Compass + Redfin Syndication (March 2026)

Coming Soon listings now appear on Redfin with priority placement. Private
Exclusives will follow. Sellers must provide **written consent** for syndication
beyond the Compass network. Expands reach while keeping listings off Zillow and
Realtor.com if seller chooses.

**Opt-OUT model:** Listing agents get leads directly, no referral fee.
Rocket provides preferred pricing for buyers.

## 5-Phase Listing Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Private Exclusive
- Listing enters Compass network only
- 340,000+ agents see it, public does not
- Test pricing, gather feedback, build anticipation
- Duration: 7–21 days typical

### Phase 2: Coming Soon
- Listing syndicated to Redfin (via Compass-Redfin alliance)
- Priority placement on Redfin search results
- Still not on MLS, Zillow, or Realtor.com
- Duration: 7–14 days

### Phase 3: MLS Launch
- Full MLS entry with CRMLS
- Syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, IDX feeds
- Maximum exposure phase
- Professional photos, video tour, 3D walkthrough live

### Phase 4: Active Marketing
- Targeted email campaigns to buyer segments
- Social media (Instagram, Facebook) with Angelique's voice
- Open houses (first weekend critical)
- Compass Marketing Center collateral

### Phase 5: Offer → Close
- Review offers, negotiate terms
- Transaction Coordinator takes over timeline management
- Contingency tracking, deadline monitoring
- Close and post-close follow-up

- Brokerage name (Compass) on all advertising
- Team name (Accardo Real Estate Associates) permitted as supplement
- No guaranteed returns or appreciation claims
- Fair Housing compliance in all content
- Seller disclosure signed before any Private Exclusive marketing
- Social media posts must include DRE# (bio link acceptable for platforms with character limits)
