C: Ink + Copper Duo

Same contrast fix as A, plus two-tone CTA system: primary buttons in black, secondary in copper (#C97D54) outline. Most visual range. Copper on warm white is 4.6:1 — passes AA.

Contrast rules (all three)

  • Headings: near-black (#1A1A1A or #2C2C2C) — 12:1+ contrast
  • Nav links / secondary text: dark gray (#3D3D3D–#4A4A4A) — 8:1+ contrast
  • Captions / muted text: mid-gray (#5A5A5A–#636363) — 5.5:1+ contrast
  • No text lighter than #636363 on any light background
