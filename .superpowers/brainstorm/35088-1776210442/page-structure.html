Page Structure

How should the page flow from merchant hook to partner pitch?

A

Two-Act Page

Act 1 — Merchant: Hero → Problem → How it works → Health check email capture

Act 2 — Partner: "What Canary actually is" platform deep-dive → Capabilities → Custom solutions pitch → Partner contact form + Calendly

Clear break between audiences. Merchants can convert and leave. Partners scroll deeper.

B

Interleaved

Hero → Problem → How it works → What Canary detects → Platform architecture → Weekly report preview → CTA section with both merchant + partner paths side by side

Single narrative that builds from "you're losing money" to "here's the platform behind it." Both audiences served by one flow, diverge at the CTA.

C

Merchant Page + Partner Anchor

Full merchant-focused page (like the sample) but with a distinct "For Operators & Partners" section anchored at the bottom — almost a page-within-a-page with its own headline, capabilities grid, and partner CTA.

Merchant experience isn't diluted. Partner section feels intentional, not tacked on. Nav link jumps straight to it.
