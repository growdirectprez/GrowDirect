# The Rollout
*Spine: ACT 7D | Manifesto: VII.5*

**Status:** 📝 DRAFT — Extracted from Strategic Thesis v1.0 + Manifesto VII.5. Awaiting Jeffe review.

---

## Three Phases. Zero Salesforce.

GrowDirect's go-to-market is built on a simple principle: by the time the company approaches a merchant, every question has already been answered. There are no cold pitches. There are no demos of software that might work. There is a product that runs, proven on the founder's own Square account, with real data and real Bitcoin receipts.

## Phase 1: GrowDirect Lab (Now)

The founder operates his own Square merchant account. The full pipeline runs against his own transactions. OAuth, webhooks, Chirps, evidence seals, Bitcoin inscriptions — all exercised on real data. Not a sandbox. Not test mode. Production.

The Lab serves two purposes. First, it validates every component of the protocol pipe end to end, with no dependency on an external merchant's cooperation or timeline. Second, it means the founder walks into the first merchant conversation having already done everything the product asks a merchant to do. No questions — only answers. The merchant asks "what happens when..." and the founder opens his phone and shows them.

## Phase 2: Friendly Merchant (Next)

One local merchant. NDA and Beta Tester Agreement. Confidential. This is a co-development partner, not a customer.

The friendly merchant provides the first external validation — real merchant data, real merchant workflows, real merchant feedback. The product adapts to their reality before it faces the market. The relationship is collaborative. The merchant sees Chirps firing on their own register data. They walk the resolution wizard on a real event. They see the Bitcoin receipt.

The friendly merchant also becomes the first external proof point for investors. The product is not theoretical. A real merchant is running it.

## Phase 3: Blitz (When Ready)

Everything goes live simultaneously. No soft launch. No waitlist. No "early access."

- **Square Marketplace** — the distribution channel merchants already use for payroll, loyalty, and inventory add-ons
- **Google Business Profile** — local discovery for merchants searching "loss prevention for Square"
- **SEO and content marketing** — the space is empty, so organic ranking is achievable without paid acquisition
- **Social media and community** — targeted at Square merchant communities and forums

The merchant searches for loss prevention. Canary is the only result — because nobody else exists in this space. There is no sales team. No implementation. No training. A merchant connects their Square account via OAuth and sees live Chirps within hours. Near-zero customer acquisition cost. Near-instant time to value.

The distribution model is the competitive advantage. Enterprise LP vendors have sales teams that sell to 500-location chains. GrowDirect has a marketplace listing that reaches 4.5 million merchants — and the product sells itself.
